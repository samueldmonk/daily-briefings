The Lead

As of ~12:09 PM ET: S&P 500 +2.2%, Nasdaq +2.6%, Dow +2.0% as AI stocks rebound — Nvidia the exception

New Yahoo Finance at ~12:09 p.m. ET: the S&P 500 was up 164.18 points (+2.23%) at 7,541.04, the Dow up 1,029.30 (+2.01%) at 52,315.03 and the Nasdaq Composite up 632.46 (+2.57%) at 25,211.89 — all reconciling with Friday’s closes. The Russell 2000 was up 2.71%, the VIX down 19.82% at 20.56 and the 10-year yield at 4.882% (+2.3 bp). December WTI crude was down 3.48% at $87.67 and gold up 1.07% at $4,118.50; Bitcoin was down 1.15%. Nvidia was down 5.00% at $177.31 (Yahoo Finance live blog, strip as of 12:09 PM ET).

Yahoo says the rebound follows a volatile post-election week in which investors dumped AI and chip stocks, sending the Nasdaq into correction territory before Friday’s partial recovery. Tech and industrial stocks led; chip names including AMD, Micron and ASML surged, and the rest of the “Magnificent Seven” gained, with Tesla up 5% in morning trading. At the open the Dow rose 1%, the S&P 500 0.9% and the Nasdaq 0.8% (Yahoo Finance).

The Nvidia overhang: Nvidia’s board is opening an independent investigation after CEO Jensen Huang admitted in a Sunday memo to employees to an extramarital affair with a woman whom U.S. intelligence believes was recruited to spy for China; “I was unfaithful to my wife,” he wrote. The stock fell as much as 8% before selling eased (Yahoo Finance). Yahoo’s Jared Blikre notes Nvidia is worth about $4.5 trillion and makes up nearly 7% of the S&P 500; Huang has run it since co-founding it in 1993 with no named successor. China’s Foreign Ministry dismissed the spying allegations as a smear campaign (AP via BNN Bloomberg). President Trump called Huang “a fine person” and said he should remain CEO (Bloomberg, via TheStreet).

Oil: crude fell after President Trump said he was highly unlikely to order fresh attacks on Iran as long as talks progressed; early Monday WTI was down 2.99% at $88.11 and Brent down 3.63% at $94.75. Saxo Bank’s Ole Hansen: oil “continues to trade lower on hopes of a US-Iran peace deal rather than concrete signs of progress.” Secretary of State Marco Rubio said talks were not “at the verge of a breakthrough” (TheStreet, 7:13–7:49 AM ET).

Context: on Friday the S&P 500 rose 0.3%, trimming its weekly loss to 2.7% — its worst week since April 2025’s “Liberation Day” tariffs — while the Dow fell 0.5% and the Nasdaq rose 1.1%; AP said before the open that the S&P 500 was 5.7% below last month’s all-time high (AP via BNN Bloomberg, 6:22 AM ET). Overnight, Taiwan’s Taiex jumped 4.9% on a 4.7% TSMC rebound and Tokyo’s Nikkei 225 closed up 2.3% at 58,775.50, while Seoul’s Kospi slipped 0.8% (AP).

Movers & Drivers

New

Nvidia −5% on board probe of CEO

Down 5.00% to $177.31 as of 12:09 p.m. ET, after falling as much as 8%; Yahoo says the damage across the semiconductor sector was largely contained to Nvidia. Yorkville’s Dan Ives called the investigation a “black eye” for Huang and expects him to remain CEO with no near-term interruption (Yahoo Finance).

New

AMD +3.35% premarket on Oppenheimer upgrade

Oppenheimer’s Rick Schafer upgraded AMD to outperform from perform with a $600 target, citing its position as a durable supplier of AI computing power (TheStreet, 8:37 AM ET); chip stocks including AMD, Micron and ASML later surged in regular trading (Yahoo Finance).

New

Ionis −18.82% premarket

Ionis Pharmaceuticals tumbled 18.82% before the bell after cutting its full-year sales guidance for flagship drug Tryngolza by roughly 50% (TheStreet, 8:37 AM ET).

New

NRG Energy +4.80% premarket

The retail electricity company rose after strong third-quarter earnings and a $4 billion asset-sale agreement; AST SpaceMobile gained 4.30% ahead of its earnings report (TheStreet, 8:37 AM ET).

New

Akamai −5.28% premarket on convertible offering

Akamai fell after proposing $2.2 billion of convertible senior notes, split evenly between $1.1 billion due 2028 and $1.1 billion due 2031 (TheStreet, 8:37 AM ET).

New

Alphabet unveils two new TPUs

Google stock rose nearly 3% premarket after Alphabet announced two new AI chips, its 8tx and 8i Max tensor processing units, per Bloomberg (Yahoo Finance).

New

Citi cuts U.S. stocks to neutral, upgrades Europe

Citi strategists led by Beata Manthey cut U.S. equities and global tech to neutral from overweight and raised European equities ex-UK to overweight, citing lower AI exposure; they warn Nasdaq positioning “could reset lower into net-short territory” (Bloomberg, via TheStreet).

New

SpaceX +1.70% premarket

SpaceX rose to $136.55 ahead of the bell after Stifel reiterated its Buy rating and $190 target, arguing it could acquire stalled data-center projects that already have power (TheStreet, 9:15 AM ET).
