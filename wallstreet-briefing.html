The Lead

New Tech rout deepens: Nasdaq −2.2%, Intel −28% as Amazon and Intel reports land (as of ~11:05 AM ET)

US stocks fell for a second day on the last trading day of October as Amazon and Alphabet’s rare earnings misses this week rekindled concerns about Big Tech’s AI spending. Yahoo Finance’s market strip, about 4 hours 56 minutes before the close (≈11:05 a.m. ET), showed the Nasdaq Composite down 2.24%, the S&P 500 down 1.38%, the Dow down 0.51% and the Russell 2000 down 1.30%; the VIX jumped 10.7% to about 19 (Yahoo Finance). At the open, Yahoo had the Nasdaq −2.5%, the S&P −1.3% and the Dow −0.3%.

Intel was down 28.09% as of 11:04 a.m. ET after its fourth-quarter outlook fell short; CEO Lip-Bu Tan said customers began signaling a PC slowdown tied to memory prices and availability. Amazon shares fell 4% in Yahoo’s update after its cash burn hit a record — free cash flow was an outflow of more than $42 billion over the 12 months through September (Yahoo Finance). Capital.com’s Daniela Hathorn told TheStreet the message from investors is that “strong AI demand is no longer enough on its own” (TheStreet).

Also weighing: oil rebounded — WTI was up 2.26% to about $104 on Yahoo’s strip — amid tanker attacks around the Strait of Hormuz, and crypto slid after Binance disclosed a $6.3 billion hack (see movers). The 10-year Treasury yield eased to about 4.95% (Yahoo Finance; TheStreet).

Movers & Drivers

NewDown ~28%

Intel (INTC)

Down 28.09% as of 11:04 a.m. ET (Yahoo); TheStreet had it −25.57% in premarket trading after a much weaker-than-expected Q4 revenue and earnings forecast, citing a severe PC slump and a factory accident in Israel (Yahoo Finance; TheStreet).

NewDown 4%

Amazon (AMZN)

Fell 4% after a Q3 earnings miss; it spent about $77 billion on property and equipment in July–September, more than double a year earlier, pushing trailing-12-month free cash flow to an outflow of more than $42 billion — nearly double the previous record (Yahoo Finance).

NewSocial & gaming

Reddit, Roblox, Pinterest

Reddit fell 24% on weaker-than-expected daily active users and a soft revenue forecast; Roblox fell 16% as daily active users dropped 9% to 138 million; Pinterest fell 6% after Q3 revenue of $1.17 billion missed the $1.21 billion expected (Yahoo Finance). Twilio sank 20.20% premarket on a disappointing Q4 organic growth forecast (TheStreet).

NewCrypto

Binance hack hits crypto stocks

Binance says a hack had an estimated $6.3 billion impact, with about $3 billion frozen on-chain; TRM Labs and Elliptic linked it to North Korean hackers. Bitcoin fell to around $77,000, and Circle, Coinbase, Robinhood and Strategy each dropped more than 6% in premarket trading (Yahoo Finance).

NewEnergy up

ExxonMobil (XOM) & Chevron (CVX)

Both beat estimates as refining margins soared; Exxon rose 2.09% and Chevron 1.70% in premarket trading, and Exxon is now earning more from refining than from upstream production for the first time in at least six years (Reuters via TheStreet).

NewPremarket gainers

Mondelez, Regeneron, Rivian

Premarket: Mondelez +7.39% on stronger-than-expected Q3 results; Regeneron +6.87% on an earnings beat and raised outlook; Rivian +5.24% on its Q3 beat and improved full-year outlook (TheStreet).
