The Lead

As of ~10:25 AM ET: S&P 500 +0.80%, Nasdaq +1.16%, Dow −0.27% as chips rebound

New Yahoo Finance’s market strip, with U.S. markets closing in 5 hours 36 minutes (≈10:24 AM ET), showed the S&P 500 at 7,581.18, up 60.25 points or 0.80%; the Nasdaq Composite at 25,357.07, up 291.20 or 1.16%; and the Dow at 52,092.93, down 138.96 or 0.27% — all three reconcile exactly with Tuesday’s official closes. Yahoo’s Nasdaq quote stamped 10:25:19 AM ET read +1.22% and its Dow quote at 10:25:24 AM ET −0.25%. The Russell 2000 was up 0.48% and the VIX down 6.21% to 18.25 (Yahoo Finance). At the open, the S&P 500 climbed 0.9% and the Nasdaq 1.5% while the Dow slipped 0.1%, per Yahoo; before the bell Nasdaq futures had been up 1.9%, S&P 500 futures 0.9% and Dow futures 0.2% (Investopedia via Yahoo Finance).

Why it’s up: chip stocks surged after CoreWeave’s quarterly results eased worries about AI demand and financing. Nvidia rose for the first time since CEO Jensen Huang admitted to an affair with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy — it was up 2.66% at $173.29 as of 10:25 AM ET — though Yahoo notes the stock is still down about 14% in November and has lost about $700 billion in market value; the bounce came despite a Baird downgrade to Neutral (Yahoo Finance). Investopedia said the market has been especially volatile in the week since Democrats’ decisive midterm victory, which sparked investor concerns that AI development could stall under new leadership in Washington and key statehouses (Investopedia).

Rates backdrop: the stock market is open for Veterans Day but the bond market is closed. On Tuesday the 10-year Treasury yield briefly spiked above 5% again as traders recalibrated bets on higher rates after the hot October CPI; Fed Governor Michelle Bowman said, “There is still work to do to bring inflation closer to 2%” (Yahoo Finance). It is a quiet day for earnings and economic data, with an MBA mortgage-rate update due (Yahoo Finance).

Movers & Drivers

Up New

CoreWeave jumps after beat-and-raise

Shares popped 13% after the Nvidia-backed cloud provider’s better-than-expected revenue outlook, per Yahoo’s main live-blog text, and a later Yahoo update said the stock “jumped 19%” (Yahoo Finance). Q3 revenue was $3.66 billion vs. expectations of $3.55 billion; CoreWeave cited about $9 billion in expected customer prepayments and deposits over the next three months and said it does not intend to issue more parent-level unsecured debt through 2027 (Investing.com).

Up New

Nvidia +2.66% despite Baird downgrade

At $173.29 as of 10:25 AM ET; Yahoo’s Ines Ferré reported shares rising roughly 5% after two straight days of declines. Baird’s Tristan Gerra cut the stock to Neutral in a note titled “Icarus’ Rise and Fall; Company Will be Fine,” citing a coming congressional hearing, government investigations and uncertainty around CEO tenure (Yahoo Finance).

Up New

Nebius surges 15% ahead of Thursday’s report

Neocloud stocks rallied across the board on CoreWeave’s results; Nebius, which reports before the bell Thursday, surged 15% in early trading (Yahoo Finance).

Up New

Chip stocks rip in premarket

Marvell was up nearly 9%; AMD, Intel and Micron each rose more than 5%; Broadcom climbed 4%; and the iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) was up 5% before the open (Investopedia).

Up New

Fossil +20.2% on surprise profit

The watchmaker jumped 20.2% in extended trading after an unexpected third-quarter profit — adjusted EPS of $0.19 — and a raised full-year revenue forecast; net income was $11.7 million vs. a $39.9 million loss a year earlier (Investing.com).

Up New

Tandy Leather +10.7% premarket

The specialty leather retailer rose after posting third-quarter net income of $500,000, vs. a $1.4 million loss a year earlier (Investing.com).

New

Dow lags the rally

The blue-chip average wavered near the flat line while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq rose; it was down 0.25% at 10:25 AM ET (Yahoo Finance).
