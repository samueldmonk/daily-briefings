The Lead

New

Pre-open, as of ~6:13 AM ET: Dow futures up, Nasdaq 100 futures down as oil climbs and the AI rebound stalls

U.S. stock futures swung between gains and losses early Tuesday. At Benzinga’s 6:13 AM ET check, Dow futures were +0.19%, S&P 500 futures −0.05%, Nasdaq 100 futures −0.43% and Russell 2000 futures −0.03%; SPY was down 0.02% and QQQ down 0.39% premarket (Benzinga). Reuters’ 5:20 AM ET read was similar: Dow E-minis up 115 points (0.22%), S&P 500 E-minis down 0.09% and Nasdaq 100 E-minis down 0.5% (Reuters via Investing.com). These are futures readings before the 9:30 AM open, not index levels.

Why. Reuters says renewed weakness in AI-linked stocks and a rebound in oil weighed on Nasdaq futures after Monday’s brief reprieve: CoreWeave fell 3.1% and Nebius 2.9% premarket, Apple 1%, and Nvidia slipped marginally. “I see this as a dip inside a selective market rather than a genuine AI buying opportunity or the start of a deeper repricing,” ActivTrades analyst Ion Jauregui told Reuters. Brent rose 1.7% to around $111 a barrel, near a five-month high, on a geopolitical risk premium from Houthi attacks on Saudi infrastructure and reports of vessel attacks in the Gulf (Reuters). Benzinga also flagged an Iranian IRGC spokesperson’s threat, reported by Al Jazeera, to target undersea communication cables in the Strait of Hormuz, and French curfews in parts of Paris, Lille and Nice after a weekend of riots (Benzinga).

Overseas. Japan’s Nikkei 225 closed 1.30% lower and Hong Kong’s Hang Seng fell 1.32%, while the Shanghai Composite gained 0.12%; European stocks opened lower on tech weakness (TipRanks).

Monday recap. The S&P 500 closed up 0.41% at 7,690.73 and the Nasdaq Composite up 1.11% at 26,430.89, while the Dow slipped 0.07% to 51,966.03 (Benzinga; AP via KRMG). Consumer discretionary, tech and materials led; utilities, real estate and financials lagged (Benzinga).

Movers & Drivers

New Earnings

General Motors down more than 5% premarket despite a beat

GM posted adjusted EPS of $3.70 vs. the $3.69 estimate and revenue of $50.0 billion vs. $48.77 billion expected, but narrowed full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $12.75–$13.75 — a $13.25 midpoint below the $13.42 consensus. It raised adjusted automotive free-cash-flow guidance to $11.5–$12.5 billion, completed its $6 billion buyback and approved a new $20 billion authorization (Investing.com, 6:39 AM ET).

New Earnings

Steel Dynamics −6.3% to −6.4% premarket on a Q3 miss

The steelmaker fell after missing third-quarter profit estimates (Reuters; Benzinga put the premarket drop at 6.43% (Benzinga)). Crown Holdings was down 4.04% and W.R. Berkley 2.13% premarket despite both beating estimates (Benzinga).

New Deal

3M +3.9% on an expanded Amazon relationship

3M said it is expanding its commercial relationship with Amazon and issued a warrant giving an Amazon subsidiary the right to buy 3M common stock worth over $521 million (Reuters via Investing.com). 3M also reports before the open (Reuters).

New AI trade

Memory and AI-infrastructure names slip again

Premarket, Micron was down 2.5%, SanDisk 3%, Intel 1.7% and Qualcomm 1.1%, with CoreWeave off 3.2% (TipRanks). Yardeni Research noted the semiconductor index had plunged 15.7% over eight straight sessions before Monday’s 1.6% rebound (Benzinga).

New After the close

Netflix and SpaceX report tonight

Netflix was 0.36% higher premarket ahead of results, with analysts expecting EPS of $0.82 on revenue of $12.88 billion; SpaceX (SPCX) was 0.56% lower, with EPS of $0.14 on $12.74 billion expected, after Elon Musk hinted its market opportunity could be larger than the $28.5 trillion in its pre-IPO prospectus (Benzinga). TipRanks notes Netflix is down about 26% year to date (TipRanks).
