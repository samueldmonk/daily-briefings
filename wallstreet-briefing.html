The Lead — official close, Wed Nov 18

New

Dow drops 561 points as insurers crater and Fed minutes turn hawkish; S&P 500 slips below its 200-day average

The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 1.11% and the S&P 500 lost 0.23% — its fifth losing session in a row and its longest slide since June, per CNBC's live coverage cited by StockTitan — while the Nasdaq Composite rose 0.42%. The Motley Fool noted the S&P 500 closed below its closely watched 200-day moving average.

The Fed's October minutes showed almost all officials judged further rate rises would likely be appropriate; the Fed held its range at 3.75%–4% in October, with Lisa D. Cook and Beth M. Hammack preferring a quarter-point rise. Earlier in the day, Governor Christopher Waller told CNBC a half-point rise is probably needed at the December 8–9 meeting. The dollar hit a 19-month high (Motley Fool).

Markets are closed; figures are the official 4:00 PM ET close. After-hours moves are listed separately below.
