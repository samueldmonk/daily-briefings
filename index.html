⛨ The Cyber Wire · The Wire

Macs left listening on port 5900 are being rooted and turned into Monero miners

The Netherlands' national cyber centre has warned that CVE-2026-65400 — a critical authentication flaw in the macOS Screen Sharing daemon, CVSS 9.8 — is under active exploitation, with attackers reaching Macs that expose port 5900, obtaining root and dropping a Monero miner. The most urgent clock still belongs to Ray: CISA's federal deadline for the actively exploited CVE-2025-62593 falls on Thursday, two days out. On the breach side the month's pattern is stolen credentials rather than exploited code — ShinyHunters claims 4.9 million Salesforce records from Brinks Home after an Entra vishing attack, and France's finance ministry confirmed staff logins were used to copy tax and property data on 678,000 people and businesses.

▲ The Closing Bell · The Tape

Futures lower before the bell as Brent hits $91 and the long bond sits at a 19-year high

July housing starts fell 12.4% to a 1.239 million annual rate against the 1.350 million expected, the sharpest signal yet of a construction pullback, even as building permits rose 5.0% to 1.443 million. Futures were already pointing lower — S&P 500 contracts down 0.41% and Nasdaq 100 down 0.76% — after President Trump rejected extending the 60-day Iran ceasefire that expired Monday and a senior Iranian official threatened a "fully offensive" posture, sending Brent to $91.27 and WTI to $84.20 and holding the 30-year Treasury yield at a 19-year 5.31%. The offset: Home Depot beat on both lines and reaffirmed full-year guidance.

Makhachev's record night in Philadelphia, and a champion sets a date to come back

Islam Makhachev's unanimous decision over Ian Machado Garry at UFC 330 was his 17th straight win inside the Octagon — a new all-time record, passing Anderson Silva — on a sold-out night that drew 19,238 people and a gate of about $6.7 million, the highest-grossing event in Xfinity Mobile Arena history. Mackenzie Dern made her first strawweight title defence underneath him. The September card just firmed up: Arman Tsarukyan returns from a nine-month layoff to face Mauricio Ruffy in the five-round UFC 331 co-main, a −400 favourite. Next up, though, is UFC Sacramento on Saturday.
