⛨ The Cyber Wire · The Wire

A past-due KEV bug turned into a list of 13,689 hardware-wallet owners and their home addresses

Logistics provider ShipMonk told Trezor on August 10 that an intruder had reached its customer order data; Trezor confirmed the breach on August 13. ShipMonk attributes the intrusion to a flaw in Metabase — the same product whose SQL injection bug, CVE-2026-72898, has sat in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue since August 11 with a remediation deadline of August 14 that has already passed. 11,742 customers had name, email, phone and shipping address exposed and 1,947 had name, city and email, all from orders received between May 10 and August 8 across seven countries. No keys or devices were compromised; what leaked is a verified, addressed list of people who own a hardware wallet. Separately, Have I Been Pwned has now indexed the leaked RingCentral data — 1.6 million accounts of names, emails, phone numbers and addresses taken by ShinyHunters in July. The nearest clock still ahead: CISA's deadline for the actively exploited Ray flaw, CVE-2025-62593, falls Thursday.

▲ The Closing Bell · The Tape

Stocks open lower a third straight day as the 30-year hits its highest since 2007

As of roughly 10 AM ET the Nasdaq Composite was down about 1.2%, with the S&P 500 off about 0.6% and the Dow about 0.3%, after President Trump rejected extending the 60-day Iran ceasefire that expired Monday and a senior Iranian official threatened a "fully offensive" posture. Crude stayed bid — Brent around $91 — and the US 30-year Treasury yield rose about two basis points to 5.32%, its highest since 2007, which is what did the damage to technology. Memory chipmakers were marked down as a group, Micron and SK Hynix each off more than 4%. Two offsets: Home Depot beat on both lines and reaffirmed guidance, and Reddit jumped 12% as it joined the S&P 500 before the bell, replacing AvalonBay Communities. Duolingo added about 3% on a D.A. Davidson upgrade. July housing starts, released at 8:30 AM ET, missed badly at a 1.239 million rate against 1.350 million expected.

Makhachev's record night in Philadelphia, and five more contracts on the line tonight

Islam Makhachev's unanimous decision over Ian Machado Garry at UFC 330 was his 17th straight win inside the Octagon — a new all-time record, passing Anderson Silva — on a sold-out night that drew 19,238 people and a gate of about $6.7 million, the highest-grossing event in Xfinity Mobile Arena history. Mackenzie Dern made her first strawweight title defence underneath him. Tonight, Dana White's Contender Series returns to the Meta APEX at 7:00 PM ET on Paramount+, headlined by Namo Fazil against Kaik Brito at welterweight; Week 1 produced four contracts. Edson Barboza retired after his UFC 330 loss. The next UFC card is Sacramento on Saturday, with Anthony Hernandez against Gregory Rodrigues at middleweight.
