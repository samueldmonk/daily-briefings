About this page

Each briefing is rebuilt from live web search every thirty minutes between 8 AM and 6 PM Eastern. Every claim, figure and name is checked against a source fetched during that same run — anything that cannot be confirmed is left off the page rather than carried forward. Past editions are kept in the Archive as point-in-time snapshots.
