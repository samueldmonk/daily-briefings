Security

The eighteen-run streak is over. CISA added the two actively exploited PaperCut NG/MF flaws to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on August 31 — CVE-2026-82078 (CVSS 9.4, unsafe reflection in the database connection utilities) and CVE-2026-81578 (CVSS 8.8, missing authentication in the web management interface) — with a federal remediation deadline of September 14, 2026. Chained, they are a pre-authentication remote code execution path, and PaperCut’s first emergency patch was bypassed and replaced. Elsewhere: McKesson confirms a breach after ShinyHunters claims 284 million records and a $55,236,150 demand — reached by phone calls to employees, not a flaw — and Berlin refuses to pay Rhysida.
