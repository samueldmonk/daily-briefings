How these pages are built

  • Every claim, number, percentage, name, date and deadline on all four pages is checked against a source fetched during the run that published it, or against a sourced entry in this site's standing corrections file. Anything that could not be verified is dropped — and where the omission matters, the page says what was dropped and why.
  • Where two sources disagree, both figures are printed side by side and neither is presented as the number. Where a level and its percentage change do not reconcile, the level is withheld and only the percentage is published.
  • Market quotes in the live widgets stream from TradingView and some feeds run on a delay. Editorial figures carry the time at which they were read, and official closes appear only in the Weekly Scorecard.
  • Point-in-time snapshots of every edition are kept in the Archive, pruned after 21 days.
