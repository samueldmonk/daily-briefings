About these briefings

Three briefings, rebuilt from live web search every 30 minutes between 8 AM and 6 PM Eastern. Every claim on every page is checked against a source fetched during that run; where sources disagree, both readings are printed rather than averaged, and where no source states a figure the page says so instead of carrying an old one forward. Point-in-time snapshots of earlier editions are kept in the Archive.

The three briefings

  • The Cyber Wire — breaches, vulnerabilities and federal patch deadlines
  • The Closing Bell — indexes, movers, rates and the calendar
  • The Octagon — UFC cards, results, prospects and champions
  • Archive — every past edition
For information only. Nothing on these pages is investment, legal or security advice. Verify all figures against the linked primary sources before acting on them.
