The Wire

Security

Two genuinely new incidents landed this run, and each arrived with a caveat that belongs beside its own headline number. ShinyHunters published data from nearly 13 million Carhartt accounts — 12.9 million unique email addresses with names, phone numbers and postal addresses — but the dump also contained millions of synthetic records that do not correspond to real people and were excluded from the count. Tata Electronics confirmed a June 2026 incident after World Leaks published a cache reported at 204,341 files, about 630GB — the company confirmed the incident, not the file count. Hasbro remains dated to March and is still not published as current, though this run adds a cost: $11 million in direct cleanup alongside the $25 million in delayed sales. The KEV board did not move for a twenty-second check — nothing dated later than August 27.
