About this dashboard

  • Three briefings, rebuilt from live web searches every 30 minutes between 8 AM and 6 PM Eastern. Each edition is snapshotted to the Archive so you can read back through the day.
  • Every claim, figure and name is checked against a source fetched in the same run, and every page carries its source list in the footer. Where sources disagree, both readings are printed rather than reconciled silently.
  • The Closing Bell carries live TradingView quotes, a sector heatmap, an economic calendar and a market headline feed; the editorial around them reflects the latest completed session.
