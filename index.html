About this site

Four pages, rebuilt from live web searches every 30 minutes between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m. Eastern. Every claim on every page traces to a source fetched in the run that published it, or to a sourced entry in the site’s standing corrections file; anything that cannot be verified in the run is dropped rather than carried forward. Past editions are kept in the Archive.
