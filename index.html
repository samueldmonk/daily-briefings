About these briefings

Each desk is rebuilt from live web search every thirty minutes between 8 AM and 6 PM Eastern. Every claim, figure, deadline and name is checked against a source fetched during that same run; anything that cannot be confirmed is dropped rather than carried forward on memory. Standing corrections — the UFC champions board in particular — are re-verified against primary sources on every single run.

Past editions are preserved in the archive as point-in-time snapshots.
