Two federal patch clocks are running — and the flaw actually under attack has no deadline at all

Two federal patch clocks are running — the WinSock flaw CVE-2026-68820 is due today and the maximum-severity Oracle flaw CVE-2026-21962 (CVSS 10.0) is due Thursday, August 27 on the tightest window CISA can set — while the flaw actually being exploited right now carries no deadline at all: GitLab CVE-2026-19478 (CVSS 9.4) lets an unauthenticated attacker rewrite public projects through a GraphQL directive and came under attack within days of disclosure; alongside it Cisco has patched nine Crosswork and Secure Workload flaws, five of them scoring a perfect 10.0, and malware is still being caught reading its orders out of FTP welcome banners to stage the E4del and PINHOLE trojans.
