About this edition

Three briefings, rebuilt from live web searches every 30 minutes between 8 AM and 6 PM Eastern. Each summary above is the lead sentence of its own page, carried across verbatim — nothing is asserted on this front page that is not supported on the briefing behind it.

Every claim, number, name, date and deadline is checked against a source fetched in the same run that publishes it. Where sources disagree, both readings are printed and neither is adopted. Where a figure could not be verified, it is left out rather than estimated — and where an earlier edition got something wrong, the correction is stated on the page. Point-in-time snapshots of past editions are kept in the Archive.

Markets content is for information only and is not investment advice. Security content is situational awareness, not incident-response advice. Fight cards and bouts are subject to change. Live market widgets on the Closing Bell page stream independently of the editorial text and may be delayed.
