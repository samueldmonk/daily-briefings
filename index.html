A phishing kit is beating MFA on Microsoft 365 — and three federal patch clocks are running

A commercial phishing-as-a-service kit called Mirage2FA has been relaying Microsoft 365 logins through an adversary-in-the-middle proxy and stealing the authenticated session cookie after the victim completes two-factor authentication — ANY.RUN counts 9,426 targeted addresses and 4,532 potentially compromised, about 48%, across 94 countries — while three federal patch clocks run in the United States: the WinSock flaw CVE-2026-68820 is due today, the maximum-severity Oracle flaw CVE-2026-21962 (CVSS 10.0) is due Thursday, August 27, and the Zimbra flaw CVE-2026-73570 is already past its August 24 deadline.
