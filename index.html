About these briefings

Three briefings, rebuilt from live web search every 30 minutes between 8 AM and 6 PM Eastern. Every claim, figure, name and date on each page is checked against a source fetched in the same run; anything that cannot be verified is dropped, and conflicts between sources are printed as conflicts rather than resolved silently.

Point-in-time snapshots of every edition are kept in the Archive.
