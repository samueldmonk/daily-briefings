⛨ The Cyber Wire · The Wire

Adobe ships an emergency hotfix for a CVSS 10.0 Magento zero-day that had been backdooring stores for three days

Adobe published APSB26-146 on 7 September at 20:20 UTC at priority 1, closing CVE-2026-75650 — the flaw Sansec named StyleSmuggler, scored CVSS 10.0, an unauthenticated remote-code-execution bug in Adobe Commerce and Magento Open Source that Adobe confirms is being exploited in the wild. Sansec dates exploitation to 4 September and reproduced the full chain on clean 2.4.7, 2.4.8 and 2.4.9 installs; payloads seen so far are a Rust Linux backdoor and a PHP web shell. The fix is a composer hotfix, not a release. The nearest open federal deadline still belongs to PaperCut NG/MF on 14 September, six days out, with the SonicWall group already three days overdue.

Markets reopen after Labor Day with the Dow lower, energy the only green corner, and Brent through $99

Regular trading resumes at 9:30 AM ET today after Monday’s holiday closure. Pre-open, Dow futures are down about 0.6–0.7%, S&P 500 futures −0.2% and Nasdaq-100 futures +0.1%. Brent is up 2.3% at $99.19 and WTI up 3.3% at $94.49 — crude at a three-month high — after Iran warned Gulf oil and gas infrastructure could be targeted and said an agreement with Oman on Hormuz shipping is nearing completion. Valero +1.9%, Exxon +1.8% and Chevron +1.5% lead the pre-market. Canada’s tariffs on ~$20bn of U.S. goods take effect today; Oracle reports after the close and August CPI lands Friday.

Parnasse needed 2:35 of his UFC debut to finish Dan Hooker — and the next main event is a short-notice replacement

Salahdine Parnasse, 28, a former two-division KSW champion signed this summer, stopped Dan Hooker at 2:35 of round one in the UFC Paris main event on 5 September, took a $100,000 Performance of the Night bonus and called for Max Holloway. Next up is Noche UFC: Silva vs. Delgado in Glendale on 12 September — Jose Miguel Delgado stepped in after Yair Rodríguez withdrew injured. UFC 331 (19 Sep) has Joshua Van vs. Alexandre Pantoja 2 for the flyweight belt; UFC 332 (3 Oct) has Natália Silva vs. Wang Cong for the vacant women’s flyweight title. A stale champions list seating Pereira and Shevchenko was refused again.
