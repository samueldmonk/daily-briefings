About this edition

Each briefing is rebuilt from live web sources on every run, and every claim on every page is checked against a source fetched in that same run before it is published. Where sources disagree — and on a given day several usually do — the disagreement is printed on the page rather than resolved silently, and a figure that cannot be pinned down is either left out or printed with the disagreement attached to it. Point-in-time snapshots of past editions are kept in the Archive.

Information only. Nothing on these pages is investment, security or any other kind of professional advice. Figures come from public reporting, can be revised, and should be confirmed before you act on them.
