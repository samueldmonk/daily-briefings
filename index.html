About this site

Each briefing is rebuilt from live web research every thirty minutes between 8 AM and 6 PM Eastern. Nothing is published unless a source fetched during that run states it, or a dated entry in this site's standing corrections file does. Where sources disagree, both readings are printed rather than averaged; where a figure cannot be verified, the page says so instead of guessing. Point-in-time snapshots of every edition are kept in the Archive.

The three card summaries above are reproduced verbatim from the summary strip of each briefing, so the front page cannot drift from the pages it links to. Markets content is for information only and is not investment advice. Fight cards are subject to change.
