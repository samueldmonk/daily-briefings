About this page

Each briefing is rebuilt from live sources every 30 minutes between 8 AM and 6 PM ET. Every claim on every page traces to a source fetched in the run that produced it; anything that could not be verified in that run is dropped, and where credible sources disagree, the disagreement is printed rather than resolved silently. Point-in-time snapshots of previous editions are kept in the Archive.
