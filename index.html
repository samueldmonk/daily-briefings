⛨ The Cyber Wire · The Wire

A booby-trapped web page is now enough to run code on an AI developer's machine

CISA has added an actively exploited flaw in Ray — the open-source engine used to scale AI and machine-learning workloads — to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, giving federal agencies until Thursday, August 20 to patch. CVE-2025-62593 carries a CVSS of 9.4 and needs no credentials and no clicked attachment: a DNS rebinding attack launched from a malicious website or advertisement reaches Ray's unauthenticated endpoints through Firefox or Safari, and can be extended to Ray instances inside the corporate network behind the browser. Alongside it: a crypto hardware-wallet maker has exposed nearly 40,000 customers, and a consumer lender has notified more than 1.2 million.

Futures lower before the bell as oil hits $91 and the long bond touches a 19-year high

US stock futures fell ahead of Tuesday's open as rising US–Iran tensions revived inflation concerns — S&P 500 futures down about 0.5% and Nasdaq-100 futures down roughly 1% at around 8 AM ET, with the VIX up 4.5%. Brent crude hit $91 a barrel after President Trump threatened to bomb Oman if it interferes with US plans for the Strait of Hormuz, pushing the 30-year Treasury yield to 5.31%, its highest in 19 years, and the 10-year to 4.72%. The offset came from earnings: Home Depot beat before the bell with $47.9 billion of quarterly sales and its strongest comparable-sales growth since late 2022.

Makhachev's record night, and the rankings catch up with the fighters who made noise around it

Islam Makhachev's unanimous decision over Ian Machado Garry at UFC 330 in Philadelphia was his 17th straight win inside the Octagon — a new all-time record, passing Anderson Silva — on a sold-out night that drew 19,238 people and a $6,715,776 gate, the highest-grossing event in Xfinity Mobile Arena history. Mackenzie Dern made her first strawweight title defence underneath him. The divisional rankings have now moved: Quillan Salkilld enters the lightweight top 10 at No. 10 after submitting Mateusz Gamrot, and Alexia Thainara arrives at strawweight No. 8. Next up is UFC Sacramento on Saturday.
