⛨ The Cyber Wire · The Wire

A ten-year-old Excel campaign reaches a courtroom — and PaperCut is still the box to fix — while a print server still owns the only live federal deadline

No new item this edition — fresh sweeps of the day’s breach, ransomware, CVE and CISA-KEV reporting returned only material the briefing already carries, so nothing is tagged New. A print server remains the most urgent box: PaperCut NG/MF is under hands-on human intrusion and holds the only federal deadline on the page that has not elapsed, 14 September. Carried from the 2:45 PM edition, an indictment rather than an intrusion: the DOJ has charged Searzhudin Tamirlanovich Aktulaev, 40, extradited from Cyprus on 28 August, over a campaign running June 2016 to November 2017 that used roughly 255 fake accounts to send malicious Excel attachments to about 80,000 users of a Northern California freelance-work platform, dropping TVRAT and DarkVNC. It carries no CVE and no deadline. ConnectWise confirmed on 3 September that file transfers in ScreenConnect Remote Access sessions are affected in both cloud and on-premises deployments, with no CVE, no severity score and no patched release yet — the only action available today is deselecting the TransferFiles role permission — while PaperCut NG/MF remains the page’s Patch Priority on a 14 September federal deadline. One item is new the 2:45 PM edition: “ShieldBreak”, CVE-2026-69414, an elevation-of-privilege zero-day in the Microsoft Malware Protection Engine that lets a low-privilege local user reach SYSTEM through Defender itself — public proof-of-concept since 12 August, CVE assigned 14 August, and still no patch, with Microsoft reported to be working on one and Help Net Security expecting it in tomorrow’s Patch Tuesday. It is not in CISA KEV, so no federal countdown attaches to it, and no CVSS is printed because no source stated one. Natural Resources Wales and the Advantech WISE-6610 pair are now carried rather than new.

⊘ The Octagon · Tale of the Tape

Soldic signs, and UFC 334 gets its first bout — and says the Topuria fight was never offered

Two new items, and both belong to UFC 332 rather than adding another card. Its line-up is now complete at twelve bouts — main card 8 PM ET on CBS, prelims 4 PM ET on Paramount+ — with Deiveson Figueiredo vs. Payton Talbott, King Green vs. Esteban Ribovics and Ateba Gautier vs. Roman Kopylov added to the main card above the two bouts already carried; and the headliners now have numbers: Natália Silva is 20–5–1 and 8–0 in the UFC off three straight unanimous decisions over former champions Jessica Andrade, Alexa Grasso and Rose Namajunas, while Wang Cong is 10–1, 5–1 in the UFC, on a four-fight run, and holds a kickboxing win over Shevchenko herself. No odds are printed for any UFC 332 bout — no return stated one. Carried from the 2:45 PM edition: Roberto Soldic — former two-division KSW champion, 21–4 with 18 knockouts — has signed a multi-fight UFC deal and debuts against Khaos Williams at UFC 332 on 3 October, and UFC 334 at Madison Square Garden on 14 November has its first booked bout in Nazim Sadykhov vs. Jefferson Nascimento. Carried from the 2:15 edition: Paddy Pimblett told MMA Mania over the weekend that if he fights again in 2026 it will be in December, and that on the rumoured Ilia Topuria bout “no one spoke to me about it” — a fighter answering a rumour, not a booking and not a callout. He also named Arman Tsarukyan, Justin Gaethje, Mauricio Ruffy and Charles Oliveira. Carried from the 1:45 edition: UFC Fight Night: Allen vs. Duncan, Saturday 10 October at the Meta Apex in Las Vegas — middleweights Brendan Allen (27–7) and Christian Leroy Duncan (15–2), billed UFC Vegas 122, and Duncan’s first UFC main event. It joins UFC Fight Night: Buckley vs. Malott at Rogers Place in Edmonton on 17 October; Valentina Shevchenko’s vacated flyweight belt still leads the briefing, and Saturday’s Paris card left Dan Hooker on three straight losses, the longest run of defeats of his career.
