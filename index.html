About this page

Three briefings, rebuilt from live sources every 30 minutes between 8 AM and 6 PM ET. Every claim on every page traces to a source fetched in that run or to a standing correction with a source attached; where sources disagree, both readings are printed rather than averaged, and where a figure could not be confirmed it is left out rather than estimated. Point-in-time snapshots of each edition are kept in the Archive.

Information only — not investment or security advice. Cards and bouts are subject to change.
