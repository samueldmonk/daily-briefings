How this page works

Each briefing is rebuilt from live web search every 30 minutes between 8 AM and 6 PM ET. Nothing is carried forward from a previous edition unless a source confirms it again in the current run — figures that cannot be re-verified are dropped rather than restated, and each page keeps a short block naming what it refused to publish and why.

Every past edition is preserved. The Archive holds a point-in-time snapshot of all three briefings at every timestamp, going back three weeks.
