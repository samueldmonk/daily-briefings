How to read these pages

Every figure carries the time it was read, because a number without a timestamp is a guess with good posture. Where two sources disagree, both are printed and neither is adopted. Where a figure could not be verified in this run, it is named as refused rather than quietly carried forward — today that includes the Friday closing prints themselves, a volatility reading refused for the eighth edition running, and three cybersecurity items whose primary pages returned empty bodies.
