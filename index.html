⛨ The Cyber Wire · The Wire

Macs left listening on port 5900 are being rooted and turned into Monero miners

The Netherlands' national cyber centre has warned that CVE-2026-65400 — a critical authentication flaw in the macOS Screen Sharing daemon, CVSS 9.8 — is under active exploitation in the wild. In every confirmed case the attacker reached a Mac with port 5900 exposed to the internet, obtained root, and dropped a Monero miner; Apple fixed it in an emergency update to macOS Tahoe 26.6.1, Sequoia 15.7.9 and Sonoma 14.8.9. The most urgent clock, though, still belongs to Ray: CISA's federal deadline for the actively exploited CVE-2025-62593 falls on Thursday, two days out.

Futures lower before the bell as Brent hits $91 and the long bond sits at a 19-year high

US stock futures fell ahead of Tuesday's open as rising US–Iran tensions revived inflation concerns, with the Nasdaq-100 contract leading the majors lower and the VIX up 4.5%. Brent crude hit $91 a barrel and WTI rose to $84 after President Trump said he intends to inflict more economic pain on Iran and threatened to bomb Oman if it interferes with US plans for the Strait of Hormuz — pressure that has carried the 30-year Treasury yield to 5.31%, its highest in 19 years. The offset arrived at 6 AM: Home Depot beat on both lines with $47.86 billion of quarterly revenue and reaffirmed its full-year guidance.

Makhachev's record night in Philadelphia, and a champion sets a date to come back

Islam Makhachev's unanimous decision over Ian Machado Garry at UFC 330 was his 17th straight win inside the Octagon — a new all-time record, passing Anderson Silva — on a sold-out night that drew 19,238 people and a gate of about $6.7 million, the highest-grossing event in Xfinity Mobile Arena history. Mackenzie Dern made her first strawweight title defence underneath him. Now women's bantamweight champion Kayla Harrison has been confirmed to return before the end of 2026 after the neck injury that scrapped her defence against Amanda Nunes. Next up: UFC Sacramento on Saturday.
