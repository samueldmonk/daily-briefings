Wall Street is treading water just under its records late Tuesday morning. TheStreet's live coverage has the Dow edging higher, an earlier Yahoo Finance read had the Dow up about 0.2% with the S&P 500 adding 0.1% and the Nasdaq up 0.2%, and Trading Economics' CFD board reads the S&P 500 essentially flat around 7,750 (−0.05%) approaching midday — with about 300 S&P 500 members in the green (Bloomberg via live boards).

Crude is the swing factor again. Trading Economics also flags the 10-year Treasury yield nearing a seven-month high.

The setup into Wednesday: all three benchmarks slipped 0.1–0.3% from record territory on Monday (AP), after the S&P 500's Friday record close of 7,757.64 capped its strongest week since April (CNBC) on a July jobs report showing the economy unexpectedly lost 23,000 jobs with unemployment easing to 4.1% (CNBC, NBC News). July CPI lands Wednesday morning — with crude climbing again, it is the week's main test of the market's friendlier-Fed bet.
