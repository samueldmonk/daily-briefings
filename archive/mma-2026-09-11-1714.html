Top Story

Noche UFC returns for a fourth year — Jean Silva headlines Glendale against a short-notice Jose Miguel Delgado

The fourth annual Noche UFC, the promotion's Mexican Independence Day card, takes place Saturday 12 September at the Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona — the UFC's first event at the venue — with a lineup stacked with Mexican and Latin American talent. Prelims start at 2 p.m. ET, the main card at 5 p.m. ET, all on Paramount+.

Top featherweight contender Jean Silva (17-3) headlines. He was booked against former interim featherweight champion Yair Rodriguez, who withdrew injured; Jose Miguel Delgado steps in on short notice with a chance to establish himself at 145 pounds. Silva enters off a win over Arnold Allen and is roughly a four-to-one favourite: Caesars has it Silva −450 / Delgado +350, DraftKings −440 / +340, having opened nearer −425 / +355.

In the co-main event, former flyweight champion Brandon Moreno tries to snap a two-fight skid against Joseph Morales, winner of The Ultimate Fighter 33. All 26 fighters made weight at Friday's official weigh-ins.

Around the Sport

  • Yair Rodriguez's withdrawal reshapes the Noche main event. The former interim featherweight champion pulled out injured, handing Jose Miguel Delgado a short-notice main-event slot against a top contender — and the widest main-event line on any card in the current schedule.
  • Brandon Moreno's stretch. The former flyweight champion has lost two in a row and meets a TUF 33 winner in Morales; a third straight defeat would be the longest skid of his UFC run.
  • Arman Tsarukyan is back. The No. 2-ranked lightweight returns at UFC 331 against Mauricio Ruffy in a five-rounder billed with major title implications — a signal that the queue behind Justin Gaethje is being sorted out in the cage rather than on the microphone.
  • The women's flyweight belt is contested in three weeks. Valentina Shevchenko vacated the title while sidelined for roughly a year; Natália Silva and Wang Cong fight for the vacant belt at UFC 332 on 3 October. The UFC has said Shevchenko is guaranteed a title shot once she is cleared.
  • Contender Series rolls on Tuesdays. Week 5 produced four contracts on 8 September; Week 6 is 15 September at the Meta APEX.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. Jean Silva enters Saturday as a top featherweight contender in a division whose champion, Alexander Volkanovski, is booked to defend at UFC 333 in Abu Dhabi on 24 October. At lightweight, Arman Tsarukyan sits at No. 2 and fights on 19 September. No rankings changes are asserted here beyond what the bookings themselves establish.

Business & broadcast. Noche UFC streams on Paramount+. The notable structural item is UFC 332 on 3 October becoming the first numbered UFC event with its main card on CBS. No viewership, gate or TKO Group financial figures are published on this page — none appeared in any source read this run.
