Housing data (8:30 AM ET). Guidance

Equifax rallies even as it trims its 2026 revenue outlook

The company said 30-year fixed rates have risen about 75 basis points since July, creating a $20 million September revenue headwind (Investrade/Hammerstone). The shares were up 9.6% at Reuters’ 10:31 AM ET read (Reuters).

Braze shares surged 51% to $45.34 after the open (Benzinga, 9:53 AM ET).
