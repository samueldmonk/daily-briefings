Noche UFC · Saturday 12 September

A late call-up headlines Noche UFC as Jean Silva draws a heavy favourite’s price

The UFC’s annual celebration of Mexican and Mexican-American culture returns Saturday to Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona, with a thirteen-bout lineup — the fullest card on the calendar — and a main event that was not the one originally booked. The originally booked opponent, Rodriguez, pulled out with a training-camp injury, and Jose Delgado stepped in on short notice, getting the first chance of his UFC career to headline a card.

Opposite him is Jean Silva, who beat Arnold Allen by decision in his last outing to reassert himself among the division’s best featherweights. The five-round featherweight bout opened with Silva at −450 and Delgado at +350; the line has since sat in a narrow band, quoted at −440 / +340 at DraftKings and −425 / +355 per FightOdds.io.

The card opens a dense stretch: UFC 331 follows on 19 September in Los Angeles with a flyweight title rematch, and UFC 332 on 3 October crowns a new women’s flyweight champion. The UFC has twelve events left on its 2026 calendar, running from Saturday through UFC 335 in Las Vegas on 12 December.
