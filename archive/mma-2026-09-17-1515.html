Top Story

Fight weekFlyweight title

Joshua Van finally gets to settle it with Alexandre Pantoja

Van took the flyweight belt from Pantoja at UFC 323 in December in the least satisfying way available: Pantoja suffered an elbow injury 23 seconds into the fight and it was waved off. Nine months on, Van has one defence to his name — a fifth-round TKO of Tatsuro Taira at UFC 328 — and the rematch is the main event of UFC 331 on Saturday 19 September at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. Pantoja is trying to become the third fighter to hold the flyweight title twice.

The betting market makes it close, which is unusual for a champion’s first real defence of a title won that way: Kalshi has it Van 56% / Pantoja 44%, and the American line is Van −130 / Pantoja +110. The co-main is a genuine lightweight crossroads — Arman Tsarukyan, 10-2 and on five straight since beating Dan Hooker last November, against Mauricio Ruffy, who has wins over Michael Chandler and Rafael Fiziev and says he has grown into a “champion mentality.”

One card change to note: Brian Ortega is off the event, having been scheduled against Renato Moicano.

A first-year featherweight and a Dana White’s Contender Series Class of ’25 graduate. He was battered and nearly finished in the second round against Marwan Rahiki, survived, and took the third outright; UFC.com calls him “already one of the most entertaining all-action talents on the roster.” He has asked for a fourth fight this year in Las Vegas and promised another bonus.

Around the Sport

  • The heavyweight belt is vacant. Tom Aspinall vacated the undisputed title on 14 September over eye complications tracing back to the eye pokes in his no-contest with Ciryl Gane at UFC 321. He is not retiring. Ciryl Gane holds the interim title. Reporting describes an expectation that Gane is elevated to undisputed — an expectation, not an announcement, so the board below still reads vacant.
  • ESPN’s champions page is still seating Aspinall at heavyweight, on a 21 June 2025 title win, three days after he gave the belt up. That is the sixth consecutive run with this regression; the row is refused again. The same page agreed with every other men’s division read this run.
  • Brian Ortega is off UFC 331. He had been scheduled to face Renato Moicano on Saturday.
  • Michael Chandler is predicting a major upset at UFC 331, and Gable Steveson — who gets his first main-card slot since arriving in the UFC — invoked Mike Tyson vs. Peter McNeeley when asked about his opponent.
  • Muhammad Mokaev is publicly frustrated with how the UFC has treated him since the two sides parted ways. For the record: he was released in July 2024 after beating Manel Kape at UFC 304 despite a 7-0 promotional record. The current news is his comments on 16 September vowing a return, not a fresh release.
  • Brandon Moreno snapped a two-fight skid in his first Noche UFC appearance, and Alexa Grasso took her second straight win — UFC.com describes her as the clubhouse leader for the next flyweight title shot.
