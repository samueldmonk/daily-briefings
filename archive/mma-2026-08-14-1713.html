Top Story

Makhachev and Machado Garry hit the mark — UFC 330's main and co-main are official

It was an uneventful morning on the scale in Philadelphia, which is exactly what the UFC wanted. Welterweight champion Islam Makhachev tipped the scale at 170 pounds and challenger Ian Machado Garry weighed an even 170, making Saturday's five-round main event official. They were joined by strawweight champion Mackenzie Dern and Gillian Robertson, both on weight for the co-main title fight.

The ceremonial weigh-ins follow Friday at 6 PM ET at Xfinity Mobile Arena, streaming on the UFC's YouTube channel and its Kick, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram accounts. The card itself streams exclusively on Paramount+, with early prelims at 5 PM ET, prelims at 7 PM ET and the main card at 9 PM ET.

It is Makhachev's first title defence since he took the welterweight belt at UFC 322 in November 2025. Machado Garry arrives on back-to-back wins over Carlos Prates and former welterweight champion Belal Muhammad — a run the betting market has noticed, with the line drifting toward the challenger across the week.
