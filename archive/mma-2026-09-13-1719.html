Top Story

The light heavyweight champion won the belt and tore his ACL doing it — Carlos Ulberg is out until 2027

Ulberg took the vacant light heavyweight title at UFC 327 on 11 April 2026 in Miami, knocking out Jiří Procházka in the first round. He did it on a torn ACL: the injury happened in the opening part of that fight, and he has since had surgery on his right knee. He is expected to miss the remainder of 2026 and does not expect to return until sometime next year.

That leaves 205 pounds in an awkward place. The UFC has not indicated that an interim light heavyweight title will be created during the absence — though CBS Sports reports that possibility is looking more and more likely the longer the champion is out, with Magomed Ankalaev and Procházka among the names in a crowded contender picture. For now the champions board below carries Ulberg with no interim holder, because no interim title has been announced.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. The UFC has released its first official rankings update of the Paramount+ era, reported to carry significant moves for Justin Gaethje and Jean Silva. As of 8 September, Gaethje sits at No. 4 pound-for-pound, having broken into that list after beating Ilia Topuria for the undisputed lightweight title. Natália Silva is No. 4 at women’s flyweight. Jean Silva is No. 6 at featherweight as of 12 September, and there was no movement in the featherweight top 15 — Volkanovski champion, Evloev the No. 1 contender, the two meeting on 24 October.

Business & broadcast. UFC programming now runs through Paramount+: UFC 333 carries no pay-per-view charge, Contender Series Season 10 is a Paramount+ exclusive, and UFC 332 will be the first numbered-event main card to air on CBS.
