Top Story

Gane is no longer interim: UFC elevates him to undisputed champion and books Hokit at the Garden

The UFC has promoted Ciryl Gane from interim heavyweight champion to undisputed champion and booked his first defence of the full title against Josh Hokit at UFC 334, Madison Square Garden, 14 November. The announcement was made during Saturday’s UFC 331 broadcast. Gane was elevated after Tom Aspinall vacated the title while recovering from eye injuries.

Gane and Justin Gaethje are the only fighters to have won interim UFC titles twice. “Bon Gamin” is undefeated in his last four fights — a run that includes the no-contest last year in which his eye pokes caused Aspinall long-term eye damage — and over the last four years he has faced Francis Ngannou, Jon Jones, Aspinall and Alex Pereira.

Hokit went from Contender Series prospect to top-five UFC heavyweight in under a year: an all-time heavyweight classic with Curtis Blaydes at UFC 327 put him on the map, and a dominant win over Derrick Lewis cemented it. CBS Sports notes his remarks on the microphone have ruffled feathers with mainstream media, some fans and even the UFC president.

The co-main is the long-delayed meeting of women’s bantamweight champion Kayla Harrison and Amanda Nunes. The pair were originally booked for UFC 324 in January before a neck injury to Harrison forced the delay; they meet 11 months later. UFC 334 is the promotion’s tenth MSG show — a November tradition that began in 2016 after MMA was legalised in New York, with 2020 lost to the pandemic.

Prospect Watch

ProspectNew

Josh Hokit

From Contender Series prospect to a heavyweight title shot at Madison Square Garden in under a year. The all-time classic with Curtis Blaydes at UFC 327 made his name; the dominant win over Derrick Lewis made the case.

Prospect

Sean Sharaf

A former United States Marine who took his first UFC victory by knocking out the previously undefeated Olympic gold medallist Gable Steveson in 12 seconds — Steveson’s first professional loss, and one of the largest upsets in UFC history by the promotion’s own description.

Prospect

Four contracts on Contender Series

Week 6 of Dana White’s Contender Series Season 10, on 16 September, produced four UFC contracts: welterweight Mayton Perea, middleweights Igor Cavalcanti and Luis Hernandez, and lightweight Akbar Abdullaev. Cavalcanti earned his with a first-round finish and said he was ready to fight immediately.

Around the Sport

  • Tsarukyan made himself undeniable. The knockout of Ruffy moves the division’s No. 2-ranked lightweight to 24–3. He was stunned early and worked past Ruffy’s striking with wrestling before rising from a shot to land the finishing elbow late in the round.
  • No immediate lightweight title fight. Champion Justin Gaethje is expected to remain inactive for the rest of 2026, leaving Tsarukyan with a strong case to be next when he returns — though there has also been talk of the shot going to Ilia Topuria, the man Gaethje took the belt from.
  • Van’s fifth round settled it. Pantoja got ahead early on grappling and took the fourth largely on top control, but the 24-year-old champion was the cleaner striker and dominated the fifth, hurting Pantoja badly to stamp the win.
  • Casey O’Neill’s year. Back from a lengthy injury-induced layoff with a first-round finish in March, she topped it in Los Angeles: taken down and threatened with an anaconda choke, she elevated Moura, twisted her hips and sank a deep armbar.
  • UFC 332 has a vacancy to fill. Valentina Shevchenko’s withdrawal with a back and shoulder ligament injury that sidelines her for about a year is why the women’s flyweight belt is on the line between Silva and Wang Cong on 3 October.

New

UFC 331 by the numbers

The UFC’s own bonus page gives gross total revenue of $8,228,105 on an announced attendance of 19,357 — a sellout, and the highest-grossing event in Crypto.com Arena history. This supersedes the “$8.3 million gate” figure this desk carried in earlier editions. On payouts, MMA Mania reports Pantoja banked $650,000 and Steveson earned $160,000 for the 12-second knockout loss.
