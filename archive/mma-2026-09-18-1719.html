Top story

Twenty-four fighters on weight, and a flyweight title rematch nine months in the making

UFC 331 is tomorrow night — Saturday 19 September — at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, and it cleared its last administrative hurdle this morning. All 24 scheduled athletes made weight, with champion Joshua Van at 125 and challenger Alexandre Pantoja at 125. Ceremonial weigh-ins follow on the evening of Friday 18 September, 5:00 PM PT / 8:00 PM ET, at the arena, streaming on the UFC’s YouTube, Kick, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram channels.

The rematch exists because the first fight barely happened. Van and Pantoja met at UFC 323 and it ended in twenty-six seconds when Pantoja suffered a freak arm injury — a result that left Van with the belt and the open question of whether he had really won it. Van goes in the narrow favourite; Pantoja is trying to become the third fighter to hold the flyweight title twice.

Business & broadcast

Paramount is in year one of a seven-year, $7.7 billion UFC media-rights deal. 16 million subscriber households have watched 180 million-plus hours of UFC on Paramount+ since the start of the year. UFC 331 is a Paramount+ card; UFC 332 on 3 October will be the first numbered-event main card on CBS.
