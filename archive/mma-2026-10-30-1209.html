Top Story

Fight week: Moicano–Nolan tightens to a near pick’em as weigh-ins begin

Official weigh-ins for UFC Vegas 123 run Friday from 12 p.m. ET. It is Nolan’s first UFC main event — he is 5-1 in the promotion on a five-fight win streak, his only UFC loss coming against Nikolas Motta on debut — while Moicano returned to the win column against Chris Duncan earlier this year. The card also features the UFC debut of Oktagon MMA double champion Lucia Szabová, against Tainara Lisboa (Cageside Press).

Renato Moicano (21-7-1) and Tom Nolan (11-1) headline UFC Fight Night at the Meta APEX on Saturday, Oct. 31. Some books have swung toward the veteran: Nolan opened a −170 favorite with Moicano +149 (MMAOddsBreaker), but MyBookie now lists Moicano −126 / Nolan −101 (Fightful, Oct. 29), and KDUS describes the line as “darn near a pick ’em” at Moicano −115 / Nolan −105 (KDUS; same split at VSiN). SportsLine, however, quotes DraftKings with Nolan the −115 favorite and Moicano −105 (SportsLine) — the favorite depends on the book. New Rankings differ by outlet: MMA Weekly lists Moicano No. 8 and Nolan No. 12 (MMA Weekly), while SportsLine’s summary had No. 12 and No. 14. Nolan rides a five-fight win streak, and the bout came together after a cut forced Brian Ortega out of his UFC 331 rematch with Moicano (SportsLine search summary; Cageside Press).

The co-main changed late: unbeaten welterweight Jacobe Smith (13-0) replaces the injured Carlos Leal against Randy Brown, and is a −280 favorite (AgentMMA; Fightful). Elsewhere on the card per MyBookie: Zach Reese −391 vs. Mickey Gall +280, and Luana Santos −350 vs. Yana Santos +250.

UFC Fight Night: Moicano vs. Nolan

Sat Nov 7 · Meta APEX, Las Vegas

UFC Vegas 124 — main event in flux

Sean Brady is out of his welterweight headliner against Gabriel Bonfim; on Thursday he said on the SteadyPicks YouTube channel that a staph infection in his knee left him with very limited mobility (The Big Lead via Yahoo Sports). The withdrawal was first reported by Laerte Viana and confirmed by MMA Fighting ( Yahoo Sports; Sherdog). Viana reports heavyweights Waldo Cortes-Acosta vs. Johnny Walker as the new main event (The Big Lead via Yahoo Sports; MiddleEasy) — Cageside Press says the heavyweight bout is “now penciled in” as the headliner and that Bonfim is expected to be rebooked for a future card; the UFC has not yet announced it (Cageside Press). Walker (23-10, 1 NC) knocked out Mick Parkin with a knee at UFC 332; Cortes-Acosta is 1-2 in 2026, beating Derrick Lewis before back-to-back losses to Alexander Volkov and Curtis Blaydes. Tatiana Suarez vs. Virna Jandiroba is also on the reported card (Cageside Press).

Sat Nov 14 · Madison Square Garden, New York

UFC 334: Gane vs. Hokit

Ciryl Gane’s first defense of the undisputed heavyweight title, against Josh Hokit; co-main Kayla Harrison vs. Amanda Nunes for the women’s bantamweight title. Joselyne Edwards is expected to weigh in as the back-up for Harrison–Nunes, first reported by MMA Fighting (Cageside Press). Odds not confirmed this edition.

Sat Nov 21 · Qatar

New UFC Qatar: Procházka vs. Stirling

Odds not confirmed this edition.
