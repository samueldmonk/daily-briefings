The Lead

As of ~2:08 p.m. ET: Nasdaq +1.02%, S&P 500 +0.55%, Dow −0.25% as chips rebound and Apple weighs

On Yahoo Finance’s market strip at about 2:08 p.m. ET (“U.S. markets close in 1h 53m”; the Dow quote on the same page was stamped “As of 2:07:48 PM EST”), the Nasdaq Composite was up 1.02%, the S&P 500 up 0.55%, the Russell 2000 up 0.79% and the Dow down 0.25% (Yahoo Finance) — little changed from ~1:06 p.m., when Yahoo had the Nasdaq +1.09%, the S&P +0.58% and the Dow −0.29%. At the 9:30 open TheStreet had the S&P 500 +0.28%, the Dow −0.49%, the Nasdaq +0.81% and the Russell 2000 −0.21% (TheStreet). TheStreet Pro’s James “Rev Shark” DePorre said weakness in Apple and Palantir “isn’t spilling over to the rest of the market” (TheStreet). Breadth is thin, though: at 11:45 a.m. ET TheStreet counted just 215 S&P 500 members in the green with the index up 0.41%, crediting tech, industrials and healthcare for the gain; by its 12:51 p.m. update the index was up 0.54%, led by Fiserv (+11.12%), Revvity (+10.89%) and Bloom Energy (+9.23%) (TheStreet).

Chips are doing the lifting. The PHLX Semiconductor Index finished Monday about 22% below its Oct. 6 high — its second bear market since June — but nearly every chip and storage stock in the S&P 500 rose in early trading Tuesday, led by memory and chip-equipment names, Yahoo’s Jared Blikre reported; Yahoo quote chips showed AMD +4.80% and Intel +0.38% at ~2:08 p.m. (and Nvidia +3.15%, Sandisk +3.39% and Micron +2.36% around 12:36 p.m.), after chip and memory stocks lost about $3.7 trillion of market value since Oct. 6 by Yahoo’s calculations (Yahoo Finance; Yahoo Finance). On the other side, Apple fell after a December-quarter gross-margin guide that disappointed investors and weaker-than-expected iPad sales; StockTitan had it down 4.09% before the open, about $202 billion of market value, and down 2.15% at 12:01 p.m. ET — about $107 billion below Monday’s close (StockTitan; TheStreet).

Oil has swung both ways: WTI rose 2.13% to $98.51 early after reports of a U.S. strike on Iran’s Qeshm Island, then turned lower — Yahoo’s Ines Ferré says oil fell for a second day on hopes of U.S.-Iran talks, with Brent down 1% near $103 and WTI near $95 late in the morning (Yahoo Finance; TheStreet; StockTitan); at ~2:08 p.m. Yahoo showed Dec WTI at $96.03 (−0.45%), the 10-year yield at 4.950% (+5.6 basis points) and the VIX down 3.26% at 20.46 (Yahoo Finance). The September JOLTS report showed 7.5 million job openings, from a revised 7.2 million in August, with the hiring rate edging up to 3.4% from 3.3% — still a “low hire, low fire” market (Yahoo Finance). JPMorgan puts 66% odds on a “Blue Wave” in which it expects the S&P 500 to move between −0.25% and +0.50% and long-end yields to decline (TheStreet). Ahead: AMD after the bell and tonight’s midterm returns, which Yahoo says investors will parse for policy signals on pharmaceuticals, housing and AI data centers (Yahoo Finance).

Movers & Drivers

UpNew

Fiserv, Revvity, Bloom lead the S&P at 12:51

TheStreet’s 12:51 p.m. ET midday list had the S&P 500 +0.54%, led by Fiserv +11.12%, Revvity +10.89% and Bloom Energy +9.23%; its 12:17 p.m. losers list was led by Gartner −12.79% (pared from −16.94% at 10:44), with Sysco, Zebra and Ball (−6.00%) also lower. (TheStreet)

PoliticsNew

Polymarket: 76% odds of a Democratic sweep

Polymarket gave Democrats a 76% chance of winning both the House and Senate as of Tuesday morning, Yahoo’s Jared Blikre reports — adding that history says the outcome has shaped stocks for about three months, “then barely at all.” Republicans hold the Senate 53-47; some races may not be called tonight. (Yahoo Finance)

Up

Ibotta, Coinbase, DuPont jump at midday

Ibotta was up 29.18% at 1:01 p.m. ET in regular trading after its revenue beat its own forecast (StockTitan real-time data); CNBC’s midday movers list has Coinbase +8% after a 15% slide over the prior three sessions and DuPont up about 9% on third-quarter results, while Thomson Reuters fell 7.5% on weaker-than-expected earnings. (StockTitan; CNBC)

Crypto

Bitcoin ETFs bleed $1B+ after Binance hack

Investors pulled over $1 billion from U.S.-listed bitcoin exchange-traded products in the two business days after last Thursday’s Binance hack, per Bloomberg data cited by Yahoo; K33’s Vetle Lunde says bitcoin is “rigged for a major move” whatever the midterm outcome. (Yahoo Finance)

Macro

Politics

JPMorgan: modest election reaction

Down

Shopify (SHOP) plunges on GMV miss

Shares fell 16.30% early after Q3 gross merchandise volume of $119.1B missed the ~$120B expected (TheStreet, citing Barron’s); revenue rose 32% to $3.74B and adjusted EPS of $0.49 beat. Down

Apple (AAPL) lower after margin guide

Down 4.09% before the open at 9:00 a.m. ET — about $202 billion of market value — after a holiday-quarter gross-margin forecast that disappointed investors; Reuters said Apple warned memory-chip costs will keep rising. Fiscal Q4 revenue rose 12% to a record $114.5B for a September quarter. (StockTitan)

Down

Palantir (PLTR) falls despite beat-and-raise

−6.22% before the open (about $29B of value): revenue rose 96% to $2.313B, but total contract value grew just 6% to $2.918B (US commercial +11%); CNBC tied the drop to that slowdown, citing William Blair. (StockTitan; TheStreet)
