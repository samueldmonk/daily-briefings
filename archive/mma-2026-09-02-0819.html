Top Story

Five fights, five contracts: Contender Series Week 4 signs everyone who won

Dana White’s Contender Series Season 10, Week 4 ran at the Meta APEX in Las Vegas on Tuesday and produced four finishes and five UFC contracts — one for every winner on the card. Adam Livingston, Gabriel Lorenco, Silvestre Sanchez, Modestino Rodrigues and Adam Darby were all signed.

The fastest of them barely counted as a fight: Rodrigues stopped Brandon Holmes with punches fifteen seconds into the first round. Darby, an Irishman who had spent his entire career with Cage Warriors, took the welterweight headliner over Patrick Rivera by third-round doctor’s stoppage.

Context from UFC.com: the season’s first three weeks produced 15 contracts, two of those winners — Bilal Hasan and Anthony Wint — have already debuted in the UFC and won, and last week delivered what UFC.com calls one of the biggest upsets in Contender Series history, Alex Apodaca beating Bella Mir, a −6000 favourite.
