Top Story

Two books, one verdict: the debutant is a heavy favourite in his own main event

The line on UFC Paris has now been priced by a second sportsbook, and it did not move much. MyBookie opened Salahdine Parnasse −649 / Dan Hooker +420 in a list published at 4:03 AM PT Friday. DraftKings has it Parnasse −625 / Hooker +455. Two independent books, the same story: a fighter making his UFC debut is the shorter price in a main event against a man with 24 professional wins.

New this edition: the method markets are where the value is being argued. Taking Parnasse by knockout shortens to −145, which one preview calls the better price than laying more than six-to-one on the moneyline.

Parnasse, 23-2, is not a developmental-series signing. He is a former two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion who went 14-2 inside that promotion with four defences of the lightweight belt, signed with the UFC in late July 2026 having previously turned the promotion down, and was handed a main event on debut. He is 28. His five-fight streak includes knockouts of Kenneth Cross, Marcin Held and Marian Ziolkowski. He remains a UFC debutant — not a veteran, not ranked, not a contender.

Hooker is 24-14 overall and 14-10 in the UFC, and weighed in at 155 lb. He is reported to need a win after back-to-back setbacks against Arman Tsarukyan and Benoit St. Denis — a detail carried from the previous edition and not re-fetched this run. The order of those two defeats is not stated by any source and is not asserted here.

Around the Sport

  • The light heavyweight division is in open turnover, and the champion is out until 2027. Carlos Ulberg tore the ACL in his right knee in April during the first-round knockout of Jiří Procházka that won him the vacant belt at UFC 327, and told a UFC post-show: “We’re looking at early next year, so 2027 if we’re being smart so we don’t come in too early and reinjure ourselves.” Dana White has floated the possibility of an interim title bout in his absence. CBS Sports, July 28 — not new reporting
  • Khamzat Chimaev has changed his tune about moving up. Immediately after losing the middleweight title to Sean Strickland he announced a move to light heavyweight, citing the weight cut that compromised him. He has since called publicly and repeatedly for an immediate rematch with Strickland instead. CBS Sports, July 28
  • Alex Pereira and Paddy Pimblett have both angled for Madison Square Garden now that UFC 334 has a date there, though no bouts were booked for the card at the time of the announcement.
  • Ulberg’s own matchmaking pitch, posted to Instagram: Magomed Ankalaev vs. Procházka, and Paulo Costa vs. Khalil Rountree Jr. as the wildcard. “By the time the dust settles … I’ll be ready. Whoever earns it. See you in 2027.”

Why this board is not the one the search engines returned

A “current UFC champions September 2026” aggregation this run returned Alex Pereira at light heavyweight and Khamzat Chimaev at middleweight — both wrong, and the forty-second time this desk has caught that same middleweight regression. The same list also swapped the women’s divisions, filing Kayla Harrison under “women’s featherweight” and Valentina Shevchenko under “women’s bantamweight,” and omitted women’s strawweight entirely. It was correct on Aspinall, Makhachev, Gaethje, Volkanovski, Yan and Van, which is exactly what makes these lists dangerous.

Ulberg and Strickland were re-verified this run against fresh reporting, not carried: Ulberg won the vacant belt by first-round knockout of Procházka at UFC 327 on April 11, 2026; Strickland took the middleweight title from Chimaev by split decision at UFC 328 on May 9, 2026. No title bout has taken place since UFC 330 on August 15 — UFC Shanghai on August 29 was a non-title card — so no belt can have changed hands since. The next title fight is Van vs. Pantoja at UFC 331 on September 19.
