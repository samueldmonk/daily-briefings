The Lead

Yields back at 5% pin stocks lower into the second-largest options expiration on record — as of ~3:20 PM ET

The major averages are modestly lower on the last Friday before the quarterly rebalance, giving back part of Thursday's post-Fed bounce as the 10-year Treasury yield climbs back to the 5% line. Direction is consistent across every source read this run: the Dow softest, the Nasdaq the most resilient, small caps worst of all.

Trading Economics live board, read this run: US500 7,621.99 (−15.77, −0.21%), US30 51,560.61 (−217.43, −0.42%), US100 29,460.55 (+13.57, +0.05%), US2000 2,850.70 (−23.93, −0.83%). All four are CFD proxies, per TE's own disclaimer, not official exchange values.

Independent reads this run. Stock Market Watch's session write-up, stamped 12:07 PM: DIA −0.67%, SPY −0.46%, QQQ −0.07%, IWM −0.91%, with TLT −0.67% on the rising-yield move. TheStreet's 11:07 AM entry had the S&P 500 −0.12% with just 119 holdings advancing. At the 9:32 AM open the board was mixed: S&P +0.11%, Dow −0.13%, Nasdaq +0.39%, Russell 2000 +0.39%.

Triple witching, and a big one. Today is the quarterly simultaneous expiration of stock options, index futures and index options. Roughly $7 trillion in US equity options notional expires today — about 25% of total US options exposure and, per the market-preview coverage read this run, the second-largest expiration on record. TheStreet Pro's James "Rev Shark" DePorre: expect heavy volume and sharp swings "particularly in the final hour," and treat it as mechanical — "if the action is volatile into the close, don't read too much into it."

Why yields are the story. Trading Economics: the 10-year "edged up 7bps to 5% on Friday, recovering from an 8bps decline in the previous session," with credit-sensitive names in the red, AI hyperscalers mostly lower amid a debt-issuance spree for capital expenditure, and chip producers rebounding. The benchmark topped 5.04% ahead of Wednesday's FOMC decision, its highest since 2007.

Freshness caveat, stated rather than smoothed. The four TE index values above are identical to the digit with the 2:37 PM read published in this site's earlier edition today, while TE's bond and commodity tables did move between the two reads (the 10-year went 5.0020 → 5.0000; WTI $100.834 → $100.272). The equity CFD table may therefore be cached rather than genuinely flat, so it is presented as a repeat read, not as a fresh 3:20 PM print. Separately, TE's own wrap prose says "the S&P 500 and the Dow dropped 0.3%, while the Nasdaq 100 inched below the flatline," which does not match TE's own table (−0.21% / −0.42% / +0.05%); both are printed and neither is reconciled away.

Movers & Drivers

NewBerkshire Hathaway · BRK.A

Buffett steps down as Berkshire chairman: “Father Time always wins”

Warren Buffett, 96, announced in a letter to shareholders on Friday that he is stepping down as chairman of Berkshire Hathaway, the conglomerate he has led since 1965 (CNBC, carried by TheStreet). He becomes chairman emeritus effective immediately while remaining a director; his son Howard Buffett takes the chairmanship under the long-standing succession plan, and Susan Decker continues as lead independent director. The move comes a little over nine months after Greg Abel took over as CEO. "Father Time always wins," Buffett wrote. "He has, however, been generous with me." No settled share-price move for BRK.A appeared in the sources read this run, so none is published.

Coinbase · COIN

SEC’s five-year “Innovation Exemption” lights up the crypto complex

The SEC issued a five-year conditional "Innovation Exemption" letting eligible venues trade tokenised versions of US-listed stocks without full traditional exchange registration. Two reads, both printed: TheStreet's 10:29 AM entry had Coinbase +6.55%; its 12:26 PM midday winners list had +11.58%. Robinhood ran the same track — +5.94% at 10:29 AM, +7.87% by midday. Neither pair is presented as the figure.

Xenon Pharmaceuticals · XENE

Phase 3 enrolment pause takes a quarter to a third off the stock — how much exactly, sources disagree

Xenon voluntarily paused new patient enrolment in its ongoing Phase 3 trials of azetukalner for focal seizures in epilepsy, and the stock is the session's largest sourced decline. Four reads this run, all printed: −22.24% (TheStreet, 8:38 AM premarket), −29% and −29.1% (TheStreet, 10:29 and 11:44 AM), −23.0% (Stock Market Watch article prose) and −31% at $39.80 on Stock Market Watch's live losers table. The move is unambiguous; the exact percentage is not, so no single figure is asserted.

SpaceX · SPCX

Nasdaq-100 weighting more than doubles at tonight’s close

SpaceX's weighting in the Nasdaq-100 is set to more than double at Friday's closing bell as part of the index's quarterly rebalance, rising to about 2.82% from roughly 1.28% as more shares become publicly tradable after recent lockup expirations. Investing.com, cited by TheStreet, estimates $15.5 billion to $22 billion in programmatic buying; the new weighting takes effect Monday, 21 September. The stock was +0.19% at $155.11 premarket but −2.4% at $151.04 on Stock Market Watch's live actives table, on 52.5 million shares.
