Top Story

Three exploited edge-device flaws, one deadline: federal agencies must patch Cisco, Citrix and Fortinet by 12 September

CISA added the trio to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Wednesday and set a remediation deadline of 12 September 2026 for Federal Civilian Executive Branch agencies — one day from today. All three sit on the network perimeter, and all three are already being used.

The most severe is CVE-2026-20079 (CVSS 10.0), an authentication bypass in the web interface of Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center that lets an unauthenticated remote attacker execute script files and take root on the underlying operating system. Cisco has updated its advisory to say it became aware of exploitation efforts in August 2026, and identified three clusters of post-compromise activity on FMC instances — UAT-12197, UAT-11823 and UAT-11988 — dropping web shells and malware.

CVE-2026-19490 (CVSS 9.3) is an authentication bypass in Citrix NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway that applies when the appliance is configured as an AAA virtual server or as a Gateway. Previdian has logged 56 exploitation attempts against its honeypots since 3 September, 36 of them on 8 September alone. CVE-2025-25249 (CVSS 7.3) is a heap-based buffer overflow in Fortinet FortiOS, FortiSwitchManager and FortiSASE that yields unauthenticated remote code execution.

Two of the Windows zero-days from this week’s record Patch Tuesday were added to the same catalog a day earlier, on 8 September, alongside an Adobe Commerce and Magento template-engine flaw and an N-able N-central static code injection bug.

Patch Priority

Patch this first — deadline tomorrow

CVE-2026-20079 — Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center (on-prem). CVSS 10.0, unauthenticated to root, confirmed exploited by three named post-compromise clusters. The CISA KEV remediation date is 12 September 2026 — 1 day left. Citrix CVE-2026-19490 and Fortinet CVE-2025-25249 share the same deadline. If an FMC, NetScaler or FortiGate is internet-facing and unpatched, treat it as the day’s only priority; hunt for web shells and rotate credentials on anything already exposed.

The PivotC2 operator — a Russian-speaking crew farming FortiGate appliances

SOCRadar attributes the campaign behind CVE-2025-25249 to a Russian-speaking threat actor driven by financial gain, with the earliest evidence of exploitation dating to July 2026. More than 3,000 IP addresses are estimated to have been targeted, resulting in 178 devices infected with a Node.js remote access trojan codenamed PivotC2 — the majority of compromises concentrated in the United States.

The chain starts with a shell script carrying an exploit binary against a vulnerable FortiGate instance, which establishes a reverse shell and runs a single-line JavaScript command via Node.js; that pulls a second-stage payload which is decrypted and executed. PivotC2 then holds a persistent outbound TLS connection to its command-and-control server and offers interactive shells, file transfers, SOCKS5 and HTTP proxy tunnelling, local and remote port forwarding, CIDR-range scanning, and FortiGate-specific configuration harvesting and credential decryption. An auto-mode flag runs a predefined command sequence autonomously on initial infection.

Breaches & Incidents

exploitedNewnetwork edge

Two exploited MikroTik RouterOS flaws get a 13 September federal deadline

CISA added CVE-2026-67277 and CVE-2026-86060 to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue on 10 September, giving agencies until 13 September to apply vendor-recommended mitigations. The first is a missing-authentication flaw (CWE-306) in the bandwidth-test service that can disclose kernel memory and cause a denial of service; the second is improper neutralisation of argument delimiters (CWE-88) that lets an attacker manipulate the trusted RouterOS policy mask and escalate privilege. Both were fixed on 3 September in 6.49.21 and 7.23.4 (long-term) and 7.24.2 (stable).

exploitedNewsupply chain

JFrog Artifactory flaws chained to drop a Rust backdoor

Threat actors are chaining critical and high-severity vulnerabilities in JFrog Artifactory to bypass authentication, gain administrative privileges and deploy a Rust backdoor on vulnerable self-hosted servers, BleepingComputer reports. JFrog Artifactory also appears in CISA’s 2 September KEV batch.

exploitededucation

Hundreds of AI agents drove a PaperCut campaign that hit 395 organisations

A threat actor BleepingComputer describes as likely Russian-speaking used hundreds of AI agents to develop and launch a global exploitation campaign against vulnerable PaperCut NG/MF print-management servers, reaching 395 organisations. Arctic Wolf had previously traced the same flaw pair against education targets from K-12 schools to major universities in the U.S. and Europe.

breachidentity

IDScan confirms a breach behind 153 million stolen driver’s licences

The identity-verification company has confirmed that hackers accessed customer data stored in its cloud platform, days after reports linked it to a database holding more than 153 million driver’s licence scans.

breachhealthcare

AdaptHealth: 4.1 million people exposed in a July attack

The home-medical-equipment provider has confirmed that 4.1 million people had data exposed in a cyberattack that took place in July.

phishingsupply chain

Trezor: 347,000 addresses targeted after its email provider was breached

Trezor says the phishing wave that followed the breach of its third-party email provider Brevo targeted 347,000 email addresses and affected 2,500 users who clicked an embedded malicious link.

exploitedransomware

Cisco Talos: three clusters, ransomware and state-sponsored, on Secure FMC

Talos says two recently patched Secure Firewall Management Center vulnerabilities have been exploited by three separate threat clusters tied to ransomware and state-sponsored operations. Cisco names them UAT-12197, UAT-11823 and UAT-11988, deploying web shells and malware post-compromise.

breachmisconfiguration

Surfshark says an exposed internal test server was breached

The VPN provider disclosed that attackers reached one of its internal test servers after a configuration error exposed it to the internet, affecting internal testing and proxy infrastructure.

enforcement

Conti member sentenced to four years

A Ukrainian national has been sentenced to four years in prison for his role in Conti ransomware attacks carried out between 2021 and 2022.

September’s updates are breaking Remote Desktop and Excel

Windows admins report the September 2026 security updates are causing Remote Desktop Services failures on Windows Server 2019, 2022 and 2025, in some cases requiring a hard reset. Separately, the KB5002914 Office update is breaking copy-and-paste and formula dragging in Excel for some users.

  • 16 September 2026 — 5 days left. CVE-2026-48710 (Kludex Starlette, CVSS 6.5) and CVE-2026-59822 (BerriAI LiteLLM, CVSS 8.8), both from the 2 September batch.
  • 5 September 2026 — overdue by 6 days. The rest of the 2 September batch: CVE-2026-83548 and CVE-2026-83549 (SonicWall SMA 1000), CVE-2026-9586, CVE-2026-82329 and CVE-2026-49869 (Kestra).
  • Added 8 September 2026, no due date read this run. No remediation date appeared in the sources read for this edition, so none is asserted here.
  • Deadlines are assigned per CVE under BOD 26-04, which prioritises remediation by risk. Two vulnerabilities added in the same week can carry very different clocks — the 12 and 16 September dates above are four days apart for batches added a week apart.

Also Moving

  • A Defender zero-day dropped the day Patch Tuesday shipped. The anonymous researcher known as Nightmare Eclipse released ShieldCrash, a bypass for the ShieldBreak privilege-escalation flaw (CVE-2026-69414) that Microsoft had just patched. The proof-of-concept demonstrates an arbitrary file read as SYSTEM on fully patched Windows 10, Windows 11 and Windows Server; the researcher says it does not grant write access. ShieldBreak itself bypassed RoguePlanet, disclosed in June and patched in July.
  • GitLab is urging immediate patching of CVE-2026-85706, which it classes as a maximum-severity path traversal flaw.
  • More than 36,000 exposed Plex servers remain unpatched against recently disclosed flaws.
  • CISA has confirmed ransomware crews are now exploiting a critical WatchGuard Firebox firewall vulnerability it first flagged as actively exploited in December.
  • A new exploit kit called BlueMoon was deployed by multiple cyber-espionage groups, leveraging zero-days in both Microsoft Windows and Google Chrome.
  • N-able has issued a fourth N-central hotfix in five weeks for an unauthenticated RCE flaw, and a separate N-central static code injection bug went into the KEV catalog on 8 September.
