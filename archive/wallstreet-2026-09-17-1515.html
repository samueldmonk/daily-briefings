The Lead

Stocks rebound as yields and oil ease — as of roughly 3:05 p.m. ET

The market took back most of Wednesday’s post-decision slide. On the latest Trading Economics read, the S&P 500 is up 1.12% (+84.90 to 7,636.71), the Dow up 0.73% (+374.13 to 51,836.03), the Nasdaq 100 up 1.67% (+482.91 to 29,427.97), the Russell 2000 up 1.04% and the S&P MidCap 400 up 0.88%. Every one of those rows reconciles: the level, the point change and the percentage agree with Wednesday’s settle, which is the stated reason they are carried. No Nasdaq Composite percentage is published — every figure circulating for it this run was an ETF proxy.

Breadth was positive but not overwhelming: TheStreet counted 259 of the index’s 503 holdings advancing at 10:54 a.m., with strength “anchored in cyclicals and tech.” The Dow had opened +0.48%, or 248 points. Behind the bid: the 10-year Treasury yield eased to 4.95% off the highest level since 2007, and crude fell for a second day as Saudi ship-to-ship cargoes off Oman eased supply fears.

On the Radar

  • The Fed is not done. The 25bp move to 3.75–4.00% was unanimous, and the new projections show 16 of 18 officials expecting at least one further hike this year (Daniela Hathorn, Capital.com). Chair Kevin Warsh: “the plain fact is that inflation is too high and has been for too long.” President Trump has said he wants rates at 1% “or less.”
  • The SEC opened a path for tokenized U.S. stocks on Thursday morning, effective immediately. The five-year “Innovation Exemption” is not a formal rulemaking; two conditions are contested — token holders must retain the same rights as equity holders, and companies must be able to object to tokenization. It lands two days after the Clarity Act failed to advance in the Senate.
  • Oil’s fall has a supply story behind it. Saudi Arabia is offering additional cargoes to Asian refiners through ship-to-ship transfers off Oman’s Sohar port, and Energy Secretary Chris Wright signalled a quicker return to service for the East-West pipeline. Prices remain above $100.
  • The war backdrop. Trump said the U.S. is “hopefully” nearing the end of its nearly seven-month war with Iran and that he has spoken with Tehran “directly.” TheStreet Pro’s James DePorre cautions that a rate rise does not fix a supply-side energy shock.
  • SpaceX (SPCX) was up 1.84% at $153.65 premarket after Cathie Wood argued Starship could generate $10 trillion in annual revenue by 2030; Trading Economics later quoted the stock at $154.93, +2.68%. The next Starship test flight is set for 22 September, expected to deploy Starlink V3 satellites.
