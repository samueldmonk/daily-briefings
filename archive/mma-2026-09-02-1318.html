Top Story

Salahdine Parnasse headlines on debut — and Sherdog calls him the UFC’s most important signing of 2026

Salahdine Parnasse (23-2) makes his Octagon debut on Saturday in the main event of UFC Paris, opposite Dan Hooker (24-14, 14-10 UFC). Sherdog’s own framing this week is an opinion piece titled “Why Salahdine Parnasse could be the UFC’s most important signing of 2026.”

The provenance matters, because it has been reported wrongly before. Parnasse is a former two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion — 14-2 inside KSW with four defences of the lightweight belt — who signed with the UFC in late July 2026 having previously turned the promotion down. He did not come through Dana White’s Contender Series. His U.S. debut came in May 2026 on the Rousey vs. Carano main card, a first-round stoppage of Kenneth Cross for a fifth straight win.

The books have him a heavy favourite at −667, with Hooker at +417 — a line reported to have moved since it opened, though no opening price was sourced this run. Hooker is a debutant’s worst possible first assignment: size, five-round experience and a stopping record, in a division where Parnasse has never fought at this level.
