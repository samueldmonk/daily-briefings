Top Story

Parnasse to 24-2 — a debut main event, a first-round finish, and a call-out he has not been given

Salahdine Parnasse improved to 24-2 by knocking out Dan Hooker at 2:35 of round one in the UFC Paris main event on Saturday 5 September at the Accor Arena — a body kick followed by a finishing sequence on the fence. It was a $100,000 Performance of the Night, and it came on his first appearance in the promotion.

The framing matters here because it has been got wrong before. Parnasse is not a developmental-series graduate: he is a former two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion who signed with the UFC in late July 2026 having previously turned the promotion down, and was handed a main event on debut. He is still a debutant — not a UFC veteran, not ranked, not a contender.

Afterwards he named Max Holloway — “my dream fight is Max Holloway,” alongside “I'm waiting for the next fight. Top five.” That is a call-out and nothing more. No such bout has been announced in anything read for this edition, and this page does not present it as booked.
