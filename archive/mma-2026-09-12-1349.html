Top Story

Noche UFC returns for a fourth year — and an Arizona native steps in to headline it

Noche UFC: Silva vs Delgado takes place today, Saturday 12 September, at Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona. It is the fourth annual edition of the card the UFC stages to mark Mexican Independence Day, and the first to be held at this venue. The event is carried on the books as UFC Fight Night 288 and billed as Noche UFC 4. It airs at a special time: prelims at 2 PM ET / 11 AM PT, main card at 5 PM ET / 2 PM PT, streaming on Paramount+ in the United States.

The headliner is a featherweight bout between Jean Silva (17-3), ranked No. 6 at 145 lb, and Jose Miguel Delgado (12-2). Silva arrives off a decision win over Arnold Allen in January, itself a rebound from the first knockout loss of his career. Delgado is an Arizona native making his first main-event appearance, and he is a replacement for the injured former interim champion Yair Rodriguez. Four of his first five UFC wins include victories over Andre Fili and Austin Bashi. No source read this run states how much notice he was given, so no figure is published.

The co-main pairs two-time flyweight champion Brandon Moreno, on a two-fight skid, with Joseph Morales. Card size is reported two ways: the official event page lists thirteen bouts while a separate listing read this run says sixteen are scheduled. Neither figure is resolved here.

Prospect Watch

Prospect

Tommy Gantt — 12-0 (1 NC)

A Dana White’s Contender Series standout out of Gilroy, California, still unbeaten, and matched against the experienced Drakkar Klose — who fights out of Glendale by way of South Haven, Michigan, and so is effectively at home tonight.

Prospect

Regina Tarin — 8-0, age 21

The Mexico City flyweight opens the card unbeaten, coming off a debut victory in February, against a ranked opponent in No. 15 JJ Aldrich — a substantial step up for a second UFC appearance.

Prospect

Sean King III — 6-0

The undefeated New Orleans featherweight faces Jessie Rosas (8-1). UFC.com describes both as featherweight newcomers.

Prospect

Jose Miguel Delgado — 12-2

Not a prospect much longer. The Arizona native has won four of his first five UFC bouts, including wins over Andre Fili and Austin Bashi, and now headlines in his home state as a replacement for Yair Rodriguez — a +325 underdog with a main-event slot.

Around the Sport

  • Yousri Belgaroui carries a five-fight win streak into his middleweight bout with Djorden Santos, per UFC.com.
  • Rafa Garcia is chasing his second win of 2026 against Rongzhu (28-6), who fights out of Sichuan, China.
  • Tim Elliott, the Ultimate Fighter Season 24 winner, meets Edgar Chairez at a catchweight in the featured prelim. UFC.com states the catchweight but gives no reason for it, and none is inferred here.
  • Brandon Moreno’s two-fight skid is the subtext of the co-main: a two-time flyweight champion trying to stop a slide rather than start a run.
  • The women’s flyweight belt has been vacant since Valentina Shevchenko stepped aside with an injury expected to sideline her for roughly a year. The UFC has said she is guaranteed a title shot once cleared.
  • Noche UFC shares its night with boxing: the promotion is cross-promoting Garcia vs Benn on Paramount+ alongside tonight’s card.
