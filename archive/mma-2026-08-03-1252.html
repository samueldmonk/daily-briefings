Top Story

NewUFC Record

Belgrade breaks the UFC's finish records in the promotion's Balkan debut

Saturday's first UFC event in Serbia turned historic. UFC Belgrade produced 12 total finishes, 10 of them in the first round — the most first-round finishes on a single card and the most finishes on a single card overall in UFC history, with five of those coming on the main card.

Serbian talent found success down the stretch. Aleksandar Rakić earned the lone decision win on the main card, and then Uroš Medić closed the night with the tenth first-round finish of the evening in the main event. Rodriguez landed a hard low kick early that swept Medić to a knee, but "The Doctor" climbed straight back up and landed a left hand that started Rodriguez on a trip to the canvas, finishing at 0:30 of round one.

That is now four straight first-round knockout wins for Medić, who UFC.com says is "knocking on the door of the welterweight Top 10" in one of the deepest divisions in the promotion. The event drew 18,623 fans and $2.5 million in gross revenue — the highest-grossing event in Belgrade Arena history.

Prospect Watch

ProspectNew

Navajo Stirling — 11-0

Three stoppage wins this year, six consecutive wins in the UFC, and 11 wins in 11 starts for the 28-year-old City Kickboxing representative. UFC.com says the Contender Series graduate "should climb into the Top 10" at light heavyweight after stopping Błachowicz.

ProspectNew

Nina Milošević — 9-1

The Serbian bantamweight shined on debut in front of a home crowd, stopping Hailey Cowan with body shots in the first and taking a Performance of the Night bonus. Immediately established as a name to watch in the division.

ProspectNew

Noah Gugnon — 10-2

The 25-year-old from Lyon, France deadened Janičić's lead leg, took the back and laced up a rear-naked choke inside 90 seconds on debut. Now on a six-fight winning streak with every victory inside the distance; UFC.com floats a quick turnaround on the September card in Paris.

RiserNew

Uroš Medić

Four straight first-round knockout wins for "The Doctor," who UFC.com describes as knocking on the door of the welterweight Top 10. He entered the Belgrade main event ranked No. 14, facing No. 15 Daniel Rodriguez.

Around the Sport

  • Rakić snaps a long drought in a new division. After dropping four straight to end his light heavyweight tenure, Aleksandar Rakić's unanimous-decision win over Marcin Tybura was his first victory since besting Thiago Santos at UFC 259 in early 2021 — and it came on his heavyweight debut, in front of his own fans.
  • Urbina's move to 185 pays off immediately. The former TUF finalist halted a two-fight skid with a 61-second finish in his first outing as a middleweight, dropping Čepo with a stiff jab before referee Marc Goddard stepped in.
  • Valentin ties a card record. Robert Valentin's guillotine over Duško Todorović was the record-tying eighth first-round finish of the night, and a second consecutive win for the Swiss fan favourite.
  • Nine bouts official for Vegas 120. The August 8 Gamrot vs. Salkilld card was finalised on July 31 with nine confirmed bouts, per Forbes.
  • Usman Nurmagomedov's free agency is the roster story to watch. Yahoo Sports reports Dana White may already be working on bringing the PFL fighter across, with a free-agency decision expected "very soon." Nothing is signed or scheduled — treat this as reported interest, not a done deal.
  • Medić's homecoming was seven years in the making. He returned to Serbia for the first time in nearly seven years to headline the country's first UFC card, arriving on a three-fight winning streak.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement
  • Welterweight — Medić entered as the No. 14 welterweight against No. 15 Daniel Rodriguez. Yahoo Sports credits his activity and highlight-reel finishes with carrying him into the welterweight Top 15, and UFC.com has him knocking on the door of the Top 10.
  • Light heavyweight — UFC.com projects Stirling into the Top 10 on the strength of his win over Błachowicz; Yahoo Sports says he "will be in the Top 10 come next week," with the win opening up big matchups.
Business & broadcast
  • Belgrade gate — gross total revenue of $2.5 million from an attendance of 18,623, which UFC.com records as the highest-grossing event in Belgrade Arena history.
  • Distribution — both UFC Belgrade and the August 8 Fight Night stream exclusively on Paramount+.
