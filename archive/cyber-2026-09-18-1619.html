Top story

The tracker lists ten identifiers and counts eight — and two of them are rated a flat 10

Eight to ten vulnerabilities crossed from theoretical to confirmed-exploited in the last seven days, across six vendors. The additions read this run: Google Pixel (CVE-2026-58704, improper authorization, 8.8); Cisco Identity Services Engine (CVE-2026-76460, 10.0); Acronis Backup (CVE-2026-87886, 7.8); Cisco Secure Email Gateway (CVE-2026-76461, SQL injection, 9.8); JFrog Artifactory (CVE-2026-42016, incorrect authorization, 8.8 and CVE-2026-42018, improper authentication, 7.5); ConnectWise ScreenConnect (CVE-2026-84869, improper privilege management and missing authorization, 9.9); GitLab CE/EE (CVE-2026-85706, path traversal, 10.0); and two MikroTik RouterOS flaws (CVE-2026-67277, missing authentication for a critical function; CVE-2026-86060, improper neutralization of argument delimiters in a command).

Separately, CISA’s own alert for 18 September adds two Linux kernel flaws — CVE-2026-53266, an out-of-bounds write in the netfilter-bridge ebtables SNAT target, and CVE-2025-39964, a race condition in the AF_ALG crypto user API where two writers on one socket interleave request payloads. Neither appears in the seven-day list on the tracker read this run. That divergence is stated rather than smoothed: two sources, two different pictures of the same week, and no attempt here to decide which catalog view is complete.

BOD 26-04 (“Prioritizing Security Updates Based on Risk”) is the governing directive for the federal remediation dates below; it supersedes the older BOD 22-01 three-week window, which is why a three-day deadline on the Cisco entry is coherent rather than a transcription error.

Patch priority

Cisco Identity Services Engine — CVE-2026-76460, due 19 September 2026 (1 day left)

Cisco rates it CVSS 3.1 10.0 (AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:C/C:H/I:H/A:H). Authentication bypass through incorrect use of privileged APIs in ISE and ISE-PIC; exploited in the wild; emergency fixes are out. It was added to the KEV catalog on 16 September with the federal remediation date shown above.

Provenance, stated plainly: the CVSS and the active exploitation were re-confirmed this run on the live KEV tracker, which lists CVE-2026-76460 at 10.0 and exploited-in-the-wild. The due date itself was not re-fetched this run — direct fetches of cisa.gov’s 18 September alert page returned an empty body for a third consecutive run — so the date is carried from this site’s sourced corrections entry of earlier today. The same date appears in the banner above and the deadline section below, and nowhere is a different one used.

Threat actor spotlight

PeckBirdy — command-and-control behind casino and adult sites

China-aligned threat actors are concealing PeckBirdy command-and-control infrastructure inside low-quality Chinese-language casino and adult websites, which is a genuine detection problem: the domains look like junk traffic rather than tradecraft. Re-confirmed in search this run; first carried here on 17 September from Infoblox via The Register. Not tagged new — two prior hits across 768 archived snapshots.

Breaches & incidents

DNS

BIND 9 — fourteen flaws fixed

The Internet Systems Consortium released BIND 9.20.29 and 9.21.26 to fix fourteen security flaws disclosed on 16 September in its open-source DNS server, one of them affecting any BIND server that answers DNS-over-HTTPS. Not tagged new: “BIND 9” has one prior hit in this site’s archive (17 September), even though the two release numbers have none.

Ransomware

Feral Wolf widens its access routes

The crew has expanded its tradecraft to abuse exposed Atlassian Confluence servers and insecure 1C:Enterprise deployments to reach Russian corporate networks (SecurityWeek, read this run). No victim count or dollar figure was stated and none is published here.

Ransomware

SETTRA rides a legitimate RMM

A newly observed ransomware operation dubbed SETTRA is abusing the legitimate MeshAgent remote monitoring and management platform for persistence — the pattern where the defender’s own management tooling is the persistence mechanism.

Vulnerability

Check Point management servers

A critical flaw in Check Point Security Management and Log Servers lets an attacker with no login credentials run code as root over the network. Carried from this site’s sourced corrections file as CVE-2026-91843, Check Point’s own 9.8, fixed via LivePatch and reachable only through the Trusted Clients setting.

Research

Mandiant’s 2026 AI risk report; PhantomRaven

SecurityWeek’s current front page carries Mandiant’s 2026 AI risk report and a report of PhantomRaven malware used by a bug bounty hunter. Headline level only — no figures from either were stated in anything read this run, so none appear here.
