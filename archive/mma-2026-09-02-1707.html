Top Story

Paris is three days away, and the market has decided the debutant is the safe bet

Salahdine Parnasse walks into the Accor Arena on Saturday for his first UFC fight — as a −600 favourite. Dan Hooker, 24-14 as a professional and 14-10 inside the Octagon, is the +425 underdog in his own opponent's promotional debut. The line opened around −400/+300 and has moved roughly 200 points toward the Frenchman since; DraftKings has it −600/+440 and prices across books run from −500 to −700 and +360 to +450.

That number is not a novelty act. Parnasse is 23-2, a two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion who went 14-2 in that promotion with four defences of the lightweight belt, signed with the UFC in late July 2026 having previously turned the promotion down, and made his U.S. debut in May with a first-round stoppage of Kenneth Cross for a fifth consecutive win. He is a debutant in this promotion and nothing more than that — not a UFC veteran, not ranked — but he did not come through the Contender Series, and this page has corrected that error once and will not repeat it.
