Top Story

Everyone made weight: UFC 330 is set, with two titles on the line in Philadelphia

All fighters made weight on Friday morning, including the four involved in the card's two championship fights. UFC welterweight champion Islam Makhachev (170) faces No. 1-ranked contender Ian Machado Garry (170) in the main event, and strawweight champion Mackenzie Dern (115) sets out for her first title defense against No. 5-ranked challenger Gillian Robertson (115) in the co-main.

Makhachev moved up a division in his last outing, beating Jack Della Maddalena at UFC 322 to take the welterweight belt, and is chasing a seventeenth consecutive victory. Robertson holds UFC female records for most career submissions and most career finishes.

The event streams exclusively on Paramount+ from Xfinity Mobile Arena on Saturday, August 15. Early prelims start at 5 PM ET, prelims at 7 PM ET and the main card at 9 PM ET. Main and co-main are scheduled for five rounds; all other bouts for three.

Around the Sport

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement
  • Ian Machado Garry enters UFC 330 as the No. 1-ranked welterweight contender, per UFC.com's official event billing.
  • Gillian Robertson is the No. 5-ranked strawweight challenger.
  • Quillan Salkilld is now 6-0 in the UFC after submitting Mateusz Gamrot — who entered ranked No. 8 at lightweight — in his first UFC main event.
Business & broadcast
  • Paramount's seven-year, $7.7 billion deal with TKO made Paramount+ the exclusive US streaming home of the UFC, effectively ending the American pay-per-view model; select marquee fights simulcast on CBS.
  • UFC Freedom 250 averaged 7.0 million US viewers — the most-watched UFC event ever domestically — and reached an estimated 34 million total global viewers, per TKO Group.
  • UFC 324 drew 4.96 million views in the Paramount+ debut, the platform's largest exclusive live event since its March 2021 launch.
  • Engagement: since the start of 2026, 16 million subscriber households have watched more than 180 million hours of UFC programming on Paramount+.
