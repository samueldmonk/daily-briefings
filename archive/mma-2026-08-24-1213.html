Top story

Rodrigues wins the war in Sacramento — and asks for du Plessis

Gregory Rodrigues quieted a partisan Golden 1 Center crowd on Saturday night, taking a unanimous decision (48-47, 49-46, 48-47) over local favourite Anthony “Fluffy” Hernandez in a 25-minute middleweight main event. “Robocop” seemed poised to finish in the second after chopping down Hernandez’s lead leg, but Hernandez endured and seized the third before Rodrigues found a second wind in the fourth and defended well down the stretch.

It is Rodrigues’ fourth consecutive win. Yahoo Sports reported he knocked Hernandez down four times and stuffed 26 of 29 takedown attempts; Hernandez has now lost two straight. Both men took home $100,000 for Fight of the Night, and Rodrigues used the cage interview and the post-fight press conference to call for a fight with Dricus du Plessis — “Dricus Du Plessis, where are you?” — arguing it is the fight to make at 185 pounds. Du Plessis, a former middleweight champion, is currently ranked No. 2 in the division.

Sources: UFC.com main card results (E. Spencer Kyte, Aug 23), UFC.com bonus coverage, MMA Weekly, Yahoo Sports/Forbes. Sacramento remains the most recent completed UFC event; no card has run since.

Prospect watch
Prospect

Lucia Szabova — a two-weight OKTAGON champion, reportedly UFC-bound

Bloody Elbow reported on Saturday that the UFC has signed Szabova, whom it bills as an undefeated two-weight world champion. She holds belts at bantamweight and flyweight and has spent the majority of her professional career in OKTAGON at 135 pounds. Her promotional debut is reported to be set for October, against Tainara Lisboa. The UFC has not published a bout announcement, so treat the date and the opponent as reporting rather than confirmed booking.

Prospect

Anthony Wint — 8-0, and a week removed from a contract

Wint earned his UFC deal on the opening episode of Season 10 of Dana White’s Contender Series, then submitted Terrance Chatman in the first round of his promotional debut. There were dicey moments — Chatman threatened a kimura on the way down — but Wint worked free, took top position and clamped on an arm-triangle from knee-on-belly to put Chatman to sleep. He is the second heavyweight with NFL ties in as many years to come to the UFC through Contender Series.

Prospect

Bilal Hasan — contract to debut in a matter of days

Hasan finished Mridul Saikia in under a minute at bantamweight on the opening episode of Season 10 of Dana White’s Contender Series, and White confirmed on the night that he had earned a UFC deal. Days later he was booked for his promotional debut against Nilson Rojas on the UFC Shanghai card this Saturday, one of four contracts handed out in the season’s first week.

Prospect

MarQuel Mederos — 11 straight, unbeaten in the Octagon

Now 4-0-1 in the UFC and riding an 11-fight winning streak overall, which UFC.com notes is tied for the second-longest unbeaten run in the lightweight division alongside Tom Nolan and Arman Tsarukyan, one behind Chris Padilla and Quillan Salkilld. The MMA Lab product called for a top-15 opponent or a date with Beneil Dariush or Chris Duncan.

Prospect

Carli Judice — four in a row, three finishes

“Crispy” has won four straight with three finishes since dropping a split decision in her promotional debut. The Louisiana native closed the distance on Chaves, buried a front kick in her midsection and ended the fight in an instant. She is climbing the flyweight ranks and should be looking at a ranked opponent next.

Prospect

Francesco Nuzzi — signed on a ten-day turnaround

Nuzzi signed with the UFC on August 19 and was slotted straight into the UFC Shanghai card on August 29 — ten days from signature to Octagon walk, among the fastest turnarounds of the year.

Around the sport
  • A finish-filled card. Ten of the 13 Sacramento bouts ended inside the distance, including six finishes in the seven preliminary fights and first-round stoppages from Anthony Wint, Carli Judice and Reinier de Ridder on the main card.
  • De Ridder restarts at 205. After faltering in his last two middleweight appearances, Reinier de Ridder moved up and flattened Roman Dolidze in the first, aided by Dolidze pulling guard to chase leg locks. De Ridder previously won gold in ONE Championship; the light heavyweight division has another contender to consider.
  • Petrino wins ugly. Vitor Petrino swept the cards against Serghei Spivac to go 4-0 since moving to heavyweight, but UFC.com was blunt that the performance “wasn’t the kind that is going to do him any favors.” He is 10 fights into his UFC career.
  • Delphine Benouaich joins the Paris card. Benouaich has signed with the UFC and lands at the Accor Arena on September 5 against Argentina’s Sofia Montenegro at women’s strawweight.
  • Blaydes re-signs. Heavyweight Curtis Blaydes has put pen to paper on a new eight-fight deal, securing his place on the roster against what Bloody Elbow describes as a run of surprise roster removals. He is booked against Waldo Cortes-Acosta on the September 12 Noche UFC card.
  • Road to UFC returns Friday. Shanghai fight week is a back-to-back double: the Road to UFC Season 5 semifinals run Friday, August 28 (5 PM China time), with the 16 surviving Asia-Pacific prospects fighting for a place in the finals and a multi-fight UFC contract. UFC.com says the opening round produced 10 finishes.
  • A Shanghai homecoming. UFC.com describes Saturday’s card as the promotion’s return to Shanghai for a second consecutive year and its first event in the Pudong District since it sold out Mercedes-Benz Arena in 2017. The event is hosted by the Shanghai Municipal Sports Bureau and the Pudong District Government, with China Mobile subsidiary Migu and Orange Lion Sports (formerly Alisports) as co-organizers.
  • The year’s last two numbered cards are official. UFC 334 goes to Madison Square Garden in New York on Saturday, November 14, and UFC 335 closes the year at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas on Saturday, December 12. UFC 334 is being billed as Polymarket UFC 334 after the prediction market became the promotion’s exclusive prediction-market partner — the first time that sponsorship has been folded into an event name. No bouts have been announced for either card. Sports Illustrated’s MMA Knockout reported this morning that headliner options for UFC 334 are thinning, with the men’s flyweight, featherweight and bantamweight titles all already booked — Joshua Van vs Alexandre Pantoja 2 at UFC 331 on September 19 in Los Angeles, and Volkanovski and Yan defending at UFC 333 on October 24 in Abu Dhabi. It floats a rebooked Kayla Harrison vs Amanda Nunes bantamweight title fight as one possibility if Harrison is cleared — speculation, not a booking.
  • Nevada is holding three UFC 329 purses. The Nevada State Athletic Commission has withheld the fight purses of Max Holloway, Paddy Pimblett and Gable Steveson pending disciplinary hearings, after all three climbed out over the cage following their wins — a violation Nevada has enforced more strictly since Khabib Nurmagomedov jumped the fence at UFC 229. Holloway and Pimblett were both shown on the broadcast leaving the cage after first-round stoppages; Steveson was not caught on camera doing so. The hearings will decide whether the withheld money is returned or converted into fines. Pimblett’s manager, Cage Warriors CEO Graham Boyland, texted MMA Junkie: “For the record, this rule needs to be changed.” The precedent is small — Diego Lopes had $5,000 of his $100,000 show money frozen for scaling the cage at UFC 300 and was fined $2,500 — but the amounts withheld here have not been disclosed.
  • Next up. The promotion heads to Shanghai on Saturday, August 29 for Umar Nurmagomedov vs Song Yadong, then Paris on September 5.
