Top Story

GitLab’s CVSS 10.0 file-read bug went from advisory to in-the-wild probing in a day

GitLab urged users on Thursday to patch immediately against CVE-2026-85706, a path traversal in the repository commits API of GitLab CE and EE that lets an unauthenticated user read arbitrary files from the server. GitLab assigned it a CVSS of 10.0. The root cause is improper path confinement combined with missing authentication enforcement on the affected endpoint, which means an attacker outside the intended repository path can pull configuration files, credentials, source code and CI/CD secrets.

Affected releases run from 18.7 through 19.1.7, 19.2 before 19.2.6 and 19.3 before 19.3.2; the fixes are 19.1.8, 19.2.6 and 19.3.2. Opportunistic scanning began almost immediately — in-the-wild probes were observed from 06:00 UTC on 11 September, one day after disclosure.

The reason this matters more than a typical read primitive: a self-hosted GitLab instance is usually the single richest credential store in an engineering organisation. A file read there is rarely the end of the intrusion — it is the step that supplies the keys for the next one.

Patch Priority

If you run Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center, the maximum-severity authentication bypass CVE-2026-20079 carries a CISA remediation deadline of 12 September 2026 (1 day left) — and Cisco has confirmed exploitation dating to August by three separate clusters tied to ransomware and state-sponsored activity. Patch it today. Immediately behind it: the Citrix NetScaler and Fortinet flaws on the same deadline, the two MikroTik RouterOS bugs due 13 September (2 days left), and the GitLab upgrade above, which has no federal deadline but is already being probed.

Threat Actor Spotlight

Newransomwaredouble extortion

The Gentlemen

The group behind this week’s Veradigm claim emerged around mid-2025 and runs a double-extortion model, pairing data theft with encryption across Windows, Linux, NAS, BSD and ESXi systems. Its leak site lists more than 800 victims from 86 countries spanning manufacturing, technology, healthcare, transportation and financial services — a spread that reads as opportunistic targeting driven by whatever access is available rather than by sector.

Two tooling notes from this year: in April 2026 Check Point tied a SystemBC proxy botnet of more than 1,500 hosts to a Gentlemen affiliate, and in June 2026 ESET reported the group using a purpose-built EDR killer called GentleKiller. Defenders should assume endpoint protection is a target of the intrusion, not a backstop against it.

Breaches & Incidents

Newhealthcarethird party

Veradigm: patient data taken through a vendor’s API credentials

The Chicago-based health-tech company, formerly Allscripts, told the SEC that an attacker obtained credentials from a vendor’s environment for a Veradigm API reserved for customer services, then used that access to copy patient data including Social Security numbers for some individuals. Clinical and medical information was not affected, and Veradigm says the credentials gave access only through that limited interface — not to its broader network, servers or databases. The Gentlemen ransomware group claimed the intrusion on 5 September and alleges it holds 3.5 million patient records, threatening to leak them today if no ransom negotiation begins.

Newexposureaviation

220 million traveler records sat exposed in an APIS database

Kinryū Labs found an Elasticsearch cluster carrying an Advance Passenger Information System dataset on 3 June while surveying exposed databases, and disclosed it on 8 September. Records span January 2017 to April 2026 and include names, dates of birth, sex, nationality, passport or travel-document numbers, expiry dates and issuing countries, plus flight numbers and dates, airlines, departure, destination and transit airports, seat assignments and baggage references. The IP address traces to Viettel, Vietnam’s state-owned telecoms provider; two misconfigurations were involved — a direct internet-facing endpoint and a separate cloud path that accepted default credentials.

healthcareShinyHunters

AdaptHealth: 4.1 million people exposed

The home-medical-equipment provider confirmed that data on 4.1 million people was exposed in a cyberattack discovered in July, attributed to the ShinyHunters threat group.

identity

IDScan confirms a breach tied to 153 million licence scans

The identity-verification company confirmed hackers reached customer data in its cloud platform, days after reports linked it to a database holding more than 153 million driver’s licence scans.

phishingsupply chain

Trezor: 347,000 addresses targeted, 2,500 users clicked

After its third-party email provider Brevo was breached, phishing against Trezor customers reached 347,000 email addresses, and 2,500 users clicked an embedded malicious link.

AI-operatededucation

PaperCut: an AI-run campaign that hit 395 organisations

A threat actor, likely Russian-speaking, used hundreds of AI agents to develop and launch a global exploitation campaign against vulnerable PaperCut NG/MF print servers, compromising 395 organisations.

CISA KEV & Federal Deadlines

  • Due 12 September 2026 (1 day left) — CVE-2026-20079 (Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center), CVE-2026-19490 (Citrix NetScaler ADC/Gateway) and CVE-2025-25249 (Fortinet FortiOS, FortiSwitchManager and FortiSASE), all added on 9 September.
  • Due 13 September 2026 (2 days left) — CVE-2026-67277 and CVE-2026-86060 (MikroTik RouterOS), added on 10 September. CISA has flagged CVE-2026-86060 as requiring forensic triage under BOD 26-04.
  • Added 8 September, no due date published in any source read this run: CVE-2026-75650 (Adobe Commerce / Magento template-engine injection), CVE-2026-81963 and CVE-2026-85880 (the two exploited Windows zero-days from this month’s Patch Tuesday) and CVE-2026-86218 (N-able N-central). The date is left blank rather than inferred.
  • Earlier September batches carried forward: the 2 September additions run on two separate clocks — CVE-2026-83548, CVE-2026-83549, CVE-2026-9586, CVE-2026-82329 and CVE-2026-49869 were due 5 September (overdue by 6 days), while CVE-2026-48710 and CVE-2026-59822 are due 16 September (5 days left).
  • Deadlines are assigned per CVE under BOD 26-04. Two vulnerabilities added on the same day can carry very different clocks, as the 12 and 13 September dates above show — read the catalogue entry, not a rule of thumb.
