The Lead

Pre-market, as of ~8:50 AM ET: futures trim gains as claims rise and Philly Fed slumps; Wall Street awaits Anthropic’s debut

Futures. S&P 500 futures were up 0.96%, Dow futures 0.45% and Nasdaq futures 1.77% about 40 minutes before the open, Russell 2000 futures rose 0.47%, and the VIX was down 7.95% at 22.61 (Yahoo Finance quote strip, ~8:50 AM ET) — below the +1.16% / +0.60% / +2.04% readings of ~8:30 AM ET (desk record). Yahoo Finance’s live blog said futures rose as investors awaited Anthropic’s debut, with tech stocks surging in premarket trading, and that it is watching whether Nvidia can shake off five straight days of losses (Yahoo Finance).

Anthropic. The Claude maker priced its IPO at $83 a share and is expected to begin trading as “ANT” after the 9:30 AM ET open, though there is no set time and it is unlikely to open right at 9:30. Its amended S-1 shows sales above $33 billion for the nine months to Sept. 30 (Yahoo Finance).

Walmart. Q3 revenue of $188.8 billion and adjusted EPS of $0.65 topped estimates ($187.2 billion and $0.64, Bloomberg consensus), and it raised its 2026 outlook again; U.S. same-store sales growth moderated to 2.9%, which Yahoo Finance says was as analysts forecast, and e-commerce sales grew 17% (Yahoo Finance). Earlier premarket, shares were near $125.75, down about 1% from Wednesday’s $127.36 close (Blockonomi, desk record).

New Data: initial jobless claims rose to 203,000 in the week ended Nov. 14 (prior week revised to 202,000), above the 197,000 economists polled by Reuters expected; the four-week average rose to 197,750 and continuing claims jumped 70,000 to 1.763 million (AP via ABC News; Reuters via Investing.com). The Philadelphia Fed manufacturing index fell to 6.3 from 20.9 — the lowest since May, per CNBC — well below forecasts; new orders slipped to −0.4 from 4.3 (Newsquawk; CNBC).

Washington: President Trump is departing the APEC summit in China, where meetings with Xi Jinping ended with little change in standoffs over semiconductors, rare earth minerals and tariffs (Yahoo Finance).

Wednesday’s close (desk record): Dow −561.25 (−1.11%), S&P 500 −0.23% (fifth straight loss, below its 200-day average), Nasdaq Composite +0.42%, as health insurers sank on a Medicare Advantage audit proposal and Fed minutes leaned toward more hikes. Markets open at 9:30 AM ET; figures above are pre-market and will move.
