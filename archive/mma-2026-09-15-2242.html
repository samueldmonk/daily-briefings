Top Story

Aspinall's vacated title comes with a prognosis: a fourth surgery if the eye has not healed by January

Tom Aspinall vacated the undisputed UFC heavyweight championship on 14 September 2026, citing ongoing complications with his eyes that have left him unable to obtain medical clearance. He is not retiring and intends to return once cleared. The reporting that followed adds the timeline: he could require a fourth surgery if the eye is not healed by next January.

The injury traces back to the eye pokes in his no contest with Ciryl Gane at UFC 321 in October 2025. The immediate trigger for vacating was a training accident during camp for a Gane rematch set for UFC 334 at Madison Square Garden, after which, in his own framing, the eye went "back to square one."

Interim champion Ciryl Gane — who took the interim belt by knockout of Alex Pereira in round two at Freedom 250 on 14 June 2026 — is expected to be promoted to undisputed. Josh Hokit and Alex Pereira have both been named as top-ranked contenders calling for a shot at Gane, which would put Pereira, a former light heavyweight champion, into a heavyweight title fight. Jon Jones has publicly reacted to the fallout.

Around the Sport

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement
