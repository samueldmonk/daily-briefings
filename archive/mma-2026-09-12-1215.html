Noche UFC lands in Glendale with Jean Silva a four-to-one favourite

The fourth annual Noche UFC takes place tonight, Saturday 12 September, at the Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona, on Mexican Independence Day weekend. Prelims begin at 2 PM ET / 11 AM PT and the main card at 5 PM ET / 2 PM PT, streaming exclusively on Paramount+. The 2 PM ET prelim time now has three independent sources this run (Yahoo Sports, Forbes and MMA Mania); UFC.com’s earlier 1 PM ET listing is printed alongside rather than dropped.

Jean Silva headlines against Jose Delgado, a short-notice replacement after Yair Rodriguez withdrew injured. DraftKings has Silva a −425 favourite with Delgado at +330 — roughly four-to-one — and other books quote −410 / +320. The fight total is set at O/U 1.5 rounds.

The co-main is Manon Fiorot (No. 2) against Alexa Grasso (No. 3) at women’s flyweight — with that belt vacant, a de facto eliminator. Both weighed in at 125.

Friday’s weigh-ins were clean: all 26 fighters made weight. Silva hit 145 and Delgado 145.5; Brandon Moreno and Joseph Morales both came in at 125.5. One face-off produced a near-slap, reported by two outlets under the same headline. No results are published below: this edition was built before the card began.

Around the Sport

  • Jose Delgado is a short-notice replacement. He stepped in for Yair Rodriguez, who withdrew injured — which is part of why the line is as lopsided as it is.
  • Women’s flyweight is vacant and Glendale doubles as an eliminator. Manon Fiorot (No. 2) vs. Alexa Grasso (No. 3) sits directly beneath the Natalia Silva vs. Wang Cong title fight at UFC 332 on 3 October.
  • Joseph Morales, on tonight’s card against Brandon Moreno, is the TUF season 33 winner.
  • UFC 332 will be the first numbered-event main card to air on CBS — a distribution first for a pay-per-view brand that moved to Paramount+ this year.
  • Curtis Blaydes has signed a new eight-fight deal — and fights the first bout of it tonight against Waldo Cortes-Acosta. The No. 10-ranked heavyweight revealed the contract himself in an interview rather than through the promotion; it lands against a run of surprise roster removals. Both men are coming off losses.
  • The roster churn is real on both sides of the ledger. Signings recorded this month include Roberto Soldic, Sean King and Gabriel Lorenco alongside the Contender Series intake; releases include veterans Edson Barboza and Darren Elkins, plus Bruno Lopes and Aoriqileng.
