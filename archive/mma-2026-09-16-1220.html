Fight week: the flyweight line has settled on the champion, but the books do not agree by how much

Joshua Van defends the UFC flyweight championship against Alexandre Pantoja on Saturday at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, a rematch of the December 2025 bout in which Van took the belt by first-round TKO.

The market has moved back and forth all week and now sits on the champion, though the price is worth shopping. Fight-week numbers have LetsBetMD at −130 on Van, BetRivers at −132 and FanDuel at −136; the best plus-money price on Pantoja is +106 at FanDuel. An earlier reading in this series had Van −135 / Pantoja +110 at BetMGM.

No opening price is asserted here, because the sources disagree: DraftKings reports the opener was near pick-’em with Van +100 and Pantoja a light favourite at −120, while an earlier reading in this series recorded a very different open. The disagreement is left on the page rather than resolved.

The co-main may matter more to the division above. Arman Tsarukyan (22-3) returns against Mauricio Ruffy (14-2) with a title shot against lightweight champion Justin Gaethje likely on the line for the winner.

Around the Sport

  • Tom Aspinall has vacated the heavyweight title. He has not competed since the Ciryl Gane bout ended in a no contest last October, when Gane accidentally poked him in both eyes; multiple procedures followed and he still has no medical clearance. He had agreed a fight date and was back sparring when an accident caused further damage to his right eye. “Stepping away from the title is not stepping away from the sport or the dream,” he wrote — he is not retiring.
  • The succession runs through Gane. Gane holds the interim belt from UFC Freedom 250 in June, where he knocked out Alex Pereira. Reporting holds the likely move is to elevate him to undisputed, with the alternative being that his next fight decides the undisputed belt.
  • Valentina Shevchenko was stripped of the women’s flyweight title after withdrawing with a ligament injury to her back and shoulder that will sideline her for a year. Natália Silva and Wang Cong now contest the vacant belt at UFC 332.
  • The Rodríguez withdrawal reshaped Noche UFC — Jose Delgado stepped in opposite Jean Silva on short notice and lost by third-round submission in the main event.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. Natália Silva enters her title shot on a 14-fight win streak, with wins over former champions Rose Namajunas, Alexa Grasso and Jessica Andrade in her last three outings. Wang Cong has won four straight in the Octagon, most recently beating Tracy Cortez at UFC 329 in July. At UFC 331 the co-main pits Arman Tsarukyan (22-3) against Mauricio Ruffy (14-2) with the next lightweight title shot understood to be at stake.

Business & broadcast. UFC 332 will be the first numbered-event main card to air on CBS, with prelims at 4 p.m. ET and the main card at 8 p.m. ET; UFC Fight Night on 26 September streams on Paramount+. Performance bonuses stand at $100,000, raised from $50,000 in January 2026. Dana White’s Contender Series Season 10 has produced 24 contracts across the season’s first half per UFC.com, with four more awarded on 15 September.
