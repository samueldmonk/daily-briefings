Top story

A week of KEV additions that the tracker itself cannot count

Senserva’s live exploited-this-week page, fetched in full this run and stamped updated September 18, 2026, titles itself “8 new exploited CVEs” and repeats that figure in its meta description and its opening line — “8 vulnerabilities crossed from theoretical to confirmed exploited in the wild in the last 7 days, across 6 vendors.” Its own body prose, a few paragraphs down, says CISA “confirmed 10 newly exploited vulnerabilities in the last 7 days, after 9 the week before and 23 across the two weeks before that.” It then lists ten identifiers. This is the third consecutive day this desk has read the page and the contradiction has not self-corrected.

Both counts are printed here and neither is adopted. What is not in dispute is the shape of the batch: Google Pixel, two Cisco products, Acronis Backup, two JFrog Artifactory flaws, ConnectWise ScreenConnect, GitLab and two MikroTik RouterOS bugs. The page names GitLab CVE-2026-85706 as the likeliest to be attacked next, at a FIRST.org EPSS of 12% over the next thirty days.

The page carries its own data notice disclaiming accuracy and directing readers to the authoritative vendor advisory. Where a vendor score was available it is used below; where none appeared, the cell says so.

Patch priority

Patch today — deadline expires

CVE-2026-85046 — Google Chrome, V8 JavaScript and WebAssembly engine. A high-severity type confusion flaw: crafted HTML or JavaScript makes V8 mishandle memory object types, yielding arbitrary read/write and, ultimately, attacker-controlled code execution inside the browser sandbox. CVSS 8.8, exploited in the wild before the fix. Patched in the 3 September stable-channel update — Chrome 152.0.7977.82/.83 on Windows and macOS, 152.0.7977.82 on Linux. CISA added it to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog within hours of Google’s advisory and set federal civilian agencies a remediation date of 18 September 2026 —  . The Cisco date shown in the KEV section below is a separate, later deadline and is labelled as such.

Provenance, stated plainly: the CVSS score, the exploited-in-the-wild status, the fixed build strings and the 18 September federal date all come from secondary reporting read this run (Help Net Security, SOC Prime, tech-insider.org, Security Affairs). cisa.gov’s own alert page returned an empty body for a fifth consecutive run, so the date has not been read from the catalog itself. Sources also disagree on whether this is the sixth or the seventh actively exploited Chrome zero-day of 2026 — both counts appear in results read this run and neither is adopted here.

Threat actor spotlight

Settra

The Settra ransomware crew accounts for four separate victim listings surfaced this run, spread across three countries and three unrelated sectors: Hansler Smith Limited (Canada, 4 September), Teletek Structures Inc. (Canadian cell-tower construction), MedEvolve (US medical billing) and DiaSorin S.p.A. (Italian biotechnology). In each case the group threatens to leak stolen data unless a settlement is reached, and in each case negotiations are described as unresolved.

Every one of those four is an attacker claim on a leak site, not a confirmed or victim-acknowledged breach, and is labelled that way here. No record counts, ransom demands or dollar figures were stated in anything read this run, so none are published. This desk’s standing ledger separately records Settra abusing the legitimate MeshAgent remote-monitoring agent for persistence.

Mass scanning of exposed Vite dev servers

A mass-scanning campaign is targeting Vite deployments to extract cloud credentials from exposed development servers, disclosed 15 September 2026. Separately, credential-harvesting activity observed in August 2026 leveraged CVE-2026-39364 (CVSS 8.2) in Vite, which lets an unauthenticated attacker bypass security restrictions via query-parameter manipulation and leak sensitive data.

CISA KEV & federal deadlines

  • CVE-2026-85046 — Google Chrome V8 zero-day. Added to KEV within hours of Google’s 3 September advisory; federal remediation date 18 September 2026 . This is the deadline used by the Patch Priority box and the threat banner above, and it is the same date in all three places.
  • CVE-2026-76460 — Cisco Identity Services Engine / ISE-PIC, CVSS 10.0, exploited in the wild. Added to KEV 16 September (confirmed from CISA’s own alert index in a prior run), with a remediation date of 19 September 2026 . That date is carried from this desk’s standing ledger and was not re-fetched: cisa.gov returned an empty body again. A later deadline than the Chrome one, and deliberately kept distinct from it.
  • CVE-2026-87886 — Acronis Backup, incorrect default permissions, CVSS 7.8. Added the same day as the Cisco flaw. No due date has been confirmed for it and none is asserted here.
  • Earlier additions in the chain included: 11 September — GitLab CVE-2026-85706 and ConnectWise ScreenConnect CVE-2026-84869, whose 14 September deadline has already passed. 8 September — four flaws: Adobe Commerce/Magento CVE-2026-75650, two Windows bugs CVE-2026-81963 and CVE-2026-85880, and N-able N-central CVE-2026-86218. 2 September — seven flaws.
  • The governing directive for these dates is BOD 26-04, “Prioritizing Security Updates Based on Risk”, which sets per-vulnerability remediation dates rather than the flat three-week window of the older directive it replaced.
