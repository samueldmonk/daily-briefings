Top Story

Shanghai, live: eleven bouts decided, six by finish, and the two that matter most still in the cage

UFC Fight Night: Nurmagomedov vs Song is running right now from the Oriental Sports Center in Shanghai's Pudong District — prelims from 3:00 AM ET, main card from 6:00 AM ET, on Paramount+ in the United States. It is a rare morning card for a U.S. audience and it publishes into the middle of this edition.

The state of play as this page was built: all seven preliminary bouts are official on UFC.com, and four main-card bouts have results in sources fetched this run. Six of those eleven ended inside the distance. Debutants have carried the card — Francesco Nuzzi, Hector Santiago, Bilal Hasan, Liu Ce and Cam Nelson all won in their first or second Octagon appearance, four of them by knockout.

What this page will not do is guess. The co-main event, Yan Xiaonan vs. Denise Gomes, and the five-round bantamweight headliner, Umar Nurmagomedov vs. Song Yadong, were not resulted by any source fetched this run — UFC.com's own main-card page still carried previews for both. The results table below marks them as undecided rather than leaving them out, so the gap is visible. The next edition will carry them.

Venue naming: UFC.com's event card reads “Oriental Sports Center, Pudong District”. Other listings render it “Pudong Development Bank Shanghai Oriental Sports Center” and “SPD Bank Oriental Sports Center”. All three forms are recorded; none is adopted as canonical.

Prospect Watch

prospectNew · 8:40 AM

Bilal Hasan

A Dana White's Contender Series season 10 standout, 9-0 with 1 no contest, fighting out of Everett, Washington by way of Jakarta. He debuted less than three weeks after earning his contract and knocked out fellow unbeaten newcomer Nilson Rojas (9-0 entering) with a straight right at 2:28 of round two — after being rocked early himself.

prospectNew · 8:40 AM

Rei Tsuruya

Two first-round submission wins in three months for the 24-year-old, the latest a rear-naked choke of Kevin Borjas at 4:14 of round one after taking him down inside a minute. He has spoken about wanting a place among the flyweight division's rising names; UFC.com's own write-up calls it mission accomplished.

prospectNew · 8:40 AM

Cam Nelson

The Canadian took the fight on three weeks' notice, out-wrestled Chinese veteran Ding Meng for a unanimous decision, pushed his winning streak to six — and became the first competitor from Prince Edward Island to compete and win inside the Octagon.

prospectNew · 8:40 AM

Francesco Nuzzi

The Italian newcomer needed one minute, stopping local favourite Xiao Long with a torrent of left hands after an early flying knee and an accidental eye poke paused the action. UFC.com calls it the 12th and biggest win of his professional career.

Around the Sport

  • Two fighters missed weight on the same card. Andre Lima missed for the second time in his UFC tenure — he still closed the prelims with a third-round guillotine and moved to 5-1 in the promotion after suffering the first loss of his career in June, but is ineligible for a bonus. Julia Polastri also missed; her head-kick knockout levels her UFC record at 3-3 and continues a pattern of alternating results across her appearances.
  • Short notice, long night for the debutants. Hector Santiago, a 33-year-old Brazilian kickboxer, took his debut on short notice and stopped Road to UFC winner Lawrence Lui at 0:53 of round two. Two of the three Road to UFC winners on the card — Lui and Namsrai Batbayar — lost their debuts.
  • Sean Woodson's return. Competing for the first time since last April, Woodson dropped Jack Jenkins twice in the opening round, lost the second to Jenkins's grappling, and took a split decision. UFC.com puts him back “on the doorstep of the Top 15”.
  • Spelling splits preserved, not normalised. This page carries both Aoriqileng and Qileng Aori, and both Sumudaerji and Su Mudaerji. UFC.com's article text uses the first form of each while its own athlete URL for the flyweight reads su-mudaerji. Anglicised Chinese names appear in more than one order and this page will not guess a fighter's own preference.
