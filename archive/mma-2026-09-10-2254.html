Top Story

Michael Page's UFC run ends on a win — and the promotion lets him walk

Michael “Venom” Page beat Nursulton Ruziboev by unanimous decision at UFC Paris on 5 September, completing his UFC contract. Two days later, UFC CEO Dana White confirmed the promotion would not re-sign the Englishman — “I don't think we're signing him. I think it's been pretty clear that he wasn't being re-signed,” White said, and, to the room, “What's the confusion?” — ending the Octagon run of a fighter Sherdog describes as a former Bellator standout.

The numbers explain the decision better than the noise around it. Page went 5-1 across six UFC appearances and went the distance in every one of them, never delivering the finishes that built his reputation outside the Octagon; several of the fights drew boos. He is 39.

Page's own answer to the criticism was a photograph of himself appearing to push a large rock uphill, captioned: “Me trying to handle the internet's opinions.” Two suitors have already surfaced — Bare-Knuckle Boxing Championship president Dave Feldman wants him back in bare-knuckle, and Jake Paul has pitched Most Valuable Promotions: “Bring MVP to MVP.”
