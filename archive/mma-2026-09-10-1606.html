Top Story

A late call-up headlines Noche UFC as Jean Silva draws a heavy favourite's price

Noche UFC: Silva vs. Delgado lands Saturday, September 12, at the Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona, with a five-round featherweight main event between Jean Silva and Jose Delgado. Delgado is getting his first chance to headline a UFC card, brought in as a replacement after Rodríguez withdrew with a training-camp injury — Wikipedia still carries the event under its original billing, UFC Fight Night: Rodríguez vs. Silva.

The market has not treated the swap kindly to the newcomer. Silva is −440 with Delgado at +340 at DraftKings, and −425 / +355 elsewhere — a band that has held within roughly −425 to −450 across every read this week.

The main card also carries Brandon Moreno vs. Joseph Morales at flyweight, Alexa Grasso vs. Manon Fiorot at women's flyweight, Curtis Blaydes vs. Waldo Cortes-Acosta at heavyweight — a bout originally announced for UFC 331 and moved a week earlier — and Tommy McMillen vs. Marwan Rahiki at featherweight. Prelims start at 2 p.m. ET, the main card at 5 p.m. ET, all on Paramount+.

Around the Sport

  • Contender Series season 10 — the show's tenth season — runs from August to October 2026 across ten weekly Tuesday episodes on Paramount+. Twenty contracts had been awarded across eight divisions by the midway point. Week 6 airs Tuesday, September 15 at 7:00 PM ET; Week 7 follows on September 22.
  • The Kayla Harrison vs. Amanda Nunes women's bantamweight title fight has been postponed again. It was reportedly planned for UFC 331 but is off for undisclosed reasons; it had originally been set for UFC 324 in January 2026 before Harrison withdrew with herniated discs in her neck requiring surgery.
  • Tsarukyan vs. Oliveira fell apart at UFC 331. The bout was first floated as a BMF title rematch, then changed to a non-title contest, then scrapped entirely when Oliveira withdrew on August 5 — days after the death of his longtime American Top Team-adjacent teammate, UFC flyweight Allan Nascimento, at 34. No official reason for the withdrawal was given.
  • UFC Paris was the promotion's 788th event, across 170 cities in 33 countries.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. The UFC replaced its media voting panel with an algorithmic ranking model on June 22. Rankings are recalculated after events and weigh opponent quality, the manner of victory, recency and divisional context; the UFC describes the model as rewarding fighters who compete regularly and as producing more movement inside divisions.

Business & broadcast. Paramount is in year one of a seven-year, $7.7 billion U.S. media-rights deal with TKO Group, covering 13 numbered events and 30 Fight Nights a year on Paramount+, with select numbered events simulcast on CBS. UFC Freedom 250 — the June White House card — drew 17 million total viewers on Paramount+ across the U.S. and Latin America, and an estimated 34 million globally. TKO reported $401 million in first-quarter 2026 revenue, up 12% year over year.
