Top Story

50–45 on one card: Joshua Van removes the doubt against Alexandre Pantoja

UFC 331: Van vs. Pantoja 2 ran on Saturday 19 September at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, and the flyweight champion Joshua Van kept the belt by unanimous decision over Alexandre Pantoja in what CBS Sports called a masterful performance and others an absolute barnburner.

The official cards read 49–46, 48–47 and 50–45, all for Van. Ron McCarthy gave Van every round for the shutout; Sal D’Amato had it 49–46 with Pantoja taking only the fourth; the third judge scored it 48–47. Scorecard correction: this desk’s earlier edition today carried the cards as 50–45 / 49–46 / 49–46, derived from a written description rather than the tabulated scores. The UFC’s own official-scorecards release and the Yahoo Sports and MMA Mania write-ups all give 49–46, 48–47, 50–45. That reading is adopted and the earlier one is withdrawn.

It is Van’s second defence of the title he took from Pantoja by first-round TKO at UFC 323 in December 2025, after stopping Tatsuro Taira in the fifth at UFC 328 in May. The pair also took Fight of the Night.

Around the Sport

  • A refusal this desk withdraws, with the reason. Earlier editions refused a “Khamzat Chimaev vs. Gilbert Burns on Fox Nation” snippet as uncorroborated and as contradicting the middleweight title row. It is now verified — and it was never a UFC fight. Chimaev beat Burns 15–4 in a freestyle wrestling match in the co-main event of RAF 13 on 18 September at the Watsco Center in Coral Gables, Florida, on Fox Nation. Burns’ team appealed; the replay upheld the result. The two first met at UFC 273 in 2022, where Chimaev won a debated unanimous decision and both took Fight of the Night. Nothing about it touches the UFC middleweight title.
  • The heavyweight belt has moved twice in six days without a heavyweight title fight. Tom Aspinall vacated on 14 September over unresolved eye injuries — he is not retiring and intends to return once cleared — and the UFC then elevated interim champion Ciryl Gane to undisputed champion, announced 19 September.
  • Kayla Harrison vs. Amanda Nunes is finally booked for UFC 334, pairing the reigning women’s bantamweight champion with the fighter widely called the sport’s greatest ever. Harrison has made no defences since winning the belt in June 2025; a January defence against Nunes was cancelled after she withdrew for neck surgery.
  • Van’s win was the weekend’s headline, but the schedule is dense: five combat-sports cards ran across 18–20 September.
