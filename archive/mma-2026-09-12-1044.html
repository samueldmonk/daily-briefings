Noche UFC returns to Arizona with a replacement main event and a title-shaping co-main

The fourth annual Noche UFC takes place tonight, Saturday 12 September, at Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona, on Mexican Independence Day weekend. Rising No. 6 featherweight contender Jean Silva headlines against Arizona’s Jose Miguel Delgado — a short-notice replacement after Yair Rodriguez withdrew injured.

The co-main is arguably the more consequential fight. Alexa Grasso, the former flyweight champion and first female Mexican champion, faces France’s Manon Fiorot in a bout the UFC calls of paramount importance to the division’s title picture — pointed, given that the women’s flyweight belt is currently vacant and contested next month. Former flyweight champion Brandon Moreno also returns, against The Ultimate Fighter season 33 winner Joseph Morales.

Start times differ by source: UFC.com has given prelims at 1 PM ET / 10 AM PT with the main card at 5 PM ET / 2 PM PT on Paramount+, while a read this run puts prelims at 11 AM PT / 2 PM ET and the main card at 2 PM MT. Both are printed. It is a full day of combat sports — UFC.com is promoting the card alongside Garcia vs. Benn.

Prospect Watch

Prospect

Salahdine Parnasse

The former KSW two-division champion could hardly have announced himself better: a debut liver-kick TKO of Dan Hooker in the first round, plus a $100,000 performance bonus. He is 24-2, 28 years old, and has won six straight, all by stoppage.

Prospect

Jose Miguel Delgado

Arizona’s Delgado gets a hometown main event on a fortnight’s notice against a top-six featherweight, stepping in for the injured Yair Rodriguez. The books make him a +330 underdog; the upside if he wins is a jump the ranking would ordinarily take a year to produce.

Prospect

The Contender Series class of Week 6

Five bouts at the Meta APEX on Tuesday put ten fighters in front of Dana White with contracts on the line. Perea–Hunt, Cavalcanti–Ravello, Abdullaev–Santos, Guillon–Hernandez and Monteiro–Williams. Nothing has been decided yet.

Around the Sport

  • The women’s flyweight belt is vacant. Valentina Shevchenko vacated after an injury expected to sideline her for roughly a year; Natália Silva and Wang Cong contest the vacant title at UFC 332 on 3 October. The UFC has said Shevchenko is guaranteed a title shot when she is cleared. That is what makes tonight’s Fiorot–Grasso co-main matter.
  • Yair Rodriguez is out injured, which is how Delgado came to headline Noche UFC on short notice.
  • Paris was a French sweep of the bonuses. Three of the four $100,000 performance awards went to fighters competing on home soil, and the card produced no Fight of the Night at all — everything of note ended early.
  • Two title fights are booked for UFC 333, Abu Dhabi, 24 October: Alexander Volkanovski and Petr Yan are both scheduled to defend.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement

Jean Silva enters tonight ranked No. 6 at featherweight. In the co-main, Manon Fiorot is No. 2 and Alexa Grasso No. 3 at women’s flyweight — the winner has the clearest claim on the Silva–Wang victor. At flyweight, Alexandre Pantoja gets an immediate rematch with the man who took his belt in December.

Business & broadcast

Paramount and TKO are in year one of a seven-year, $7.7 billion deal reaching 16 million subscriber households, with 180 million hours of UFC watched on Paramount+ since 1 January and viewership running up to 20× the prior two years’ PPV average. UFC 324 drew 4.96 million views; Freedom 250 averaged 8.2 million and 34 million total globally. TKO’s Q1: revenue $1.6B (+26%), net income $250M, adjusted EBITDA $550M (+32%). The partnership extends to Canada from 2027.
