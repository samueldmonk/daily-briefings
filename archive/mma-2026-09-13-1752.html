Top Story

Noche UFC 4 — Glendale, Arizona

Jean Silva starts slow, gets dropped, and still finds the finish

Silva did not throw a strike for the first half of the opening round and was dropped by a right cross from Jose Miguel Delgado, who had taken the fight on short notice, at home in Glendale, after Yair Rodríguez withdrew injured. The Brazilian woke up in the second. In the third he dropped Delgado with a right hand along the fence, took the back as Delgado panic-wrestled, and sank a one-armed rear-naked choke at 2:57. It leaves him 7-1 inside the Octagon with six finishes and, in UFC.com’s framing, “making a case for a championship opportunity in the future.”

The more consequential result may have come one fight earlier. Alexa Grasso beat Manon Fiorot by unanimous decision, putting the French contender in danger inside the opening 30 seconds, dropping her in the last 30, and holding on through a cut beside her right eye. UFC.com — which calls Grasso a former flyweight champion — describes her as the “clubhouse leader in the chase for the next championship opportunity.” That chase is live precisely because the belt is vacant.

Around the Sport

  • Brandon Moreno ended a two-fight losing run in his first Noche UFC appearance — UFC.com calls it a “much-needed and emotional win” for the former flyweight champion, closed out with a takedown and 90 seconds of top control.
  • Marwan Rahiki fell to 9-1 — his first professional defeat — and still left with $100,000 for a fight in which he very nearly finished an undefeated opponent.
  • Curtis Blaydes is back in the win column and likely back in the heavyweight top five, though UFC.com is blunt that the performance “left a lot to be desired” and expects him matched against a younger contender next.
  • Jose Miguel Delgado took the main event on short notice after Yair Rodríguez withdrew with an injury, ending a planned return from an 18-month layoff.
  • A scheduled newcomer bout between Ramiro Jimenez and Rodrigo Vera was cancelled when Vera was removed over visa issues.

Cross-check refused — four belts, the worst reading recorded

ESPN’s “Current and all-time UFC champions” page, read this run, disagrees with four rows above — more than any previous run has recorded. It seats Alex Pereira at light heavyweight (on an October 2025 result that predates UFC 327), Khamzat Chimaev at middleweight (August 2025, predating UFC 328), Ilia Topuria at lightweight (June 2025, predating Freedom 250) and Valentina Shevchenko at women’s flyweight (September 2024, predating her vacating the title). The lightweight regression is new; it has not been recorded before. All four are refused. The page also lags on three defence counts, listing 0 for Makhachev, Van and Dern, each of whom has one above.

The women’s flyweight vacancy is corroborated independently this run by the UFC 332 listing itself, which states that Silva vs. Wang is for the vacant title, and by UFC.com’s Noche UFC copy calling Alexa Grasso a former flyweight champion.
