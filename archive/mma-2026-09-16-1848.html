Top Story

Fight week is live: media day done, press conference Thursday, Van a slim favourite

Joshua Van and Alexandre Pantoja took questions at UFC 331 media day in Los Angeles and faced off ahead of Saturday night’s flyweight championship rematch at the Crypto.com Arena. Both headliners appeared alongside other fighters on the card, among them former Bellator champion Patricio Pitbull, Olympic gold medallist Gable Steveson, Alonzo Menifield and Marlon Vera.

The pre-fight press conference is Thursday, 17 September, starting at 5:00 p.m. PT / 8:00 p.m. ET, also at the Crypto.com Arena. Eight main-card fighters are scheduled to appear: Joshua Van, Alexandre Pantoja, Arman Tsarukyan, Maurício Ruffy, Patricio Pitbull, Dooho Choi, Alonzo Menifield and Iwo Baraniewski.

The market has Van as the narrow favourite. At DraftKings the champion is −130 and Pantoja is +110 — a tight line for a man who already beat the fighter across from him. The co-main lightweight bout has Arman Tsarukyan at −290 against Maurício Ruffy at +235. The heaviest line on the card belongs to Gable Steveson at −1500 against Sean Sharaf, who returns at +600.

Those co-main numbers are shorter than the ones this page carried an edition ago, when a different book had Tsarukyan at −380 and Ruffy at +305. Both are printed with the book that quoted them rather than averaged, and the DraftKings line above is the one read for this edition.

Around the Sport

  • Dana White has confirmed Michael Page will not return to the UFC. Asked about the speculation, White’s response was “What’s the confusion?”
  • The heavyweight title is vacant. Tom Aspinall relinquished it on 14 September because of ongoing complications with his eyes — multiple surgeries, accidents and infections, and further damage to his right eye after he had agreed a fight date and returned to sparring. He is not retiring and intends to return once cleared. Ciryl Gane holds the interim belt.
  • The women’s flyweight title is also vacant, after Valentina Shevchenko vacated while sidelined by injury. Natália Silva and Wang Cong contest it at UFC 332 on 3 October. The UFC has said Shevchenko is guaranteed a title shot when she is cleared.
  • Carlos Ulberg, the light heavyweight champion, had ACL surgery after winning the vacant belt at UFC 327 and the divisional picture is being described as crowded in his absence.
  • UFC BJJ 11 runs Thursday 24 September on UFC Fight Pass, 8 p.m. ET / 5 p.m. PT.

Rankings & Business

  • Two of the eleven belts on the board are vacant — heavyweight and women’s flyweight — with an interim champion seated at heavyweight. That is an unusually thin championship picture heading into a numbered card.
  • Season 10 of the Contender Series has set a record for contract offers, which is the clearest business signal available this run about how aggressively the UFC is restocking its roster.
