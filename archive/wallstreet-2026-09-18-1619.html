The lead

Buffett hands over the chair on a triple-witching Friday — and the close itself goes unverified

Warren Buffett, 96, is stepping down as chairman of Berkshire Hathaway, the conglomerate he has led since 1965, announcing it in a letter to shareholders Friday. He becomes chairman emeritus effective immediately while remaining a director; his son Howard Buffett replaces him as chairman under the long-standing succession plan, and Susan Decker continues as lead independent director. It comes a little more than nine months after Greg Abel, 64, took over as CEO. “Father Time always wins,” Buffett wrote. “He has, however, been generous with me.”

The day was also triple witching — the quarterly simultaneous expiry of stock options, stock index futures and stock index options, which falls on the third Friday of March, June, September and December. TheStreet Pro’s James “Rev Shark” DePorre warned it “tends to produce heavy volume and sharp swings, particularly in the final hour,” and that the action is mechanical: “If the action is volatile into the close, don’t read too much into it.” No notional size figure was re-sourced this run, so the “$7 trillion / second-largest on record” framing carried in an earlier edition today is dropped rather than repeated.

And here is the problem with this edition. The market closed at 4:00 PM ET and, at verification time just after 4:07 PM ET, no official closing print for any US index could be verified. The live blog that covers the session stopped updating at 12:26 PM. A search-level summary returned “S&P 500 +0.11%, Dow −0.13%, Nasdaq +0.39%” as the closing results — but those three figures are verbatim from that blog’s 9:32 AM Opening Bell post. A second summary offered “Dow closed at 51,681.51, Nasdaq closed at 26,522.55” and the page behind it rendered client-side with no figures at all, its own title stamped for the previous session. Both were refused. What follows are contract-for-difference proxies with their read time attached, which is a weaker thing than a close and is labelled as such throughout.
