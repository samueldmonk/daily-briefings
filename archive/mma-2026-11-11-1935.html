Top Story

UFC 334 fight week: Gane −225 vs. Hokit +185; Harrison −192 vs. Nunes +160

Ciryl Gane (14-2 MMA, 11-2 UFC) defends the heavyweight title against Josh Hokit (10-0 MMA, 4-0 UFC) on Saturday at Madison Square Garden; the main card streams at 9 p.m. ET on Paramount+, prelims at 7 p.m. and early prelims at 5 p.m. DraftKings has Gane −225, Hokit +185 as of Nov. 9 (CBS Sports); MMA Junkie had listed Gane −195, Hokit +160. Gane was elevated to full champion when Tom Aspinall vacated; Hokit’s key wins are Derrick Lewis and Curtis Blaydes (MMA Junkie via Yahoo Sports, desk record).

Co-main: champion Kayla Harrison (19-1, 3-0 UFC) vs. Amanda Nunes (23-5, 16-2 UFC), Harrison −192 / Nunes +160 (DraftKings via CBS Sports; MMA Junkie: −180 / +150). Harrison pulled out of a January date with Nunes to have neck surgery; Nunes returns more than three years after retiring following her UFC 289 title win over Irene Aldana (MMA Junkie, desk record).

Around the Sport

  • White & Trump: ESPN feature headline — “From the Octagon to the Oval Office: White, Trump and a 25-year friendship” (ESPN Staff, headline seen on ESPN this run).
  • UFC 334 back-up: Joselyne Edwards is expected to weigh in as the back-up for Kayla Harrison vs. Amanda Nunes, first reported by MMA Fighting — a surprise pick given Ailin Perez has won seven straight and holds a win over Edwards, though Edwards sits one spot higher in the UFC rankings and has won her last five (Cageside Press).
  • Fight-week schedule (UFC.com): the UFC 334 press conference is Thursday, Nov. 12, at the Infosys Theater at Madison Square Garden — doors 5 p.m. ET, fighters on stage 6 p.m. ET, free and open to the public; ceremonial weigh-ins follow Friday, Nov. 13 (fighters on the scale 5 p.m. ET), after a 4 p.m. ET UFC 335 & UFC 336 press conference; Saturday’s doors open at 4 p.m. ET (UFC.com). MMA Crossfire says Harrison, Nunes, Caio Borralho, Yousri Belgaroui, Uros Medic, Kevin Holland, Bilal Hassan and Luis Gurule are also expected at Thursday’s presser, and that UFC 335 features BMF champion Charles Oliveira vs. Diego Lopes (MMA Crossfire).
  • Fight week is on: Ciryl Gane (14-2 MMA, 11-2 UFC) and Josh Hokit (10-0 MMA, 4-0 UFC) faced off in Times Square on Tuesday, exchanging words as fans cheered, officially kicking off UFC 334 fight-week activities (MMA Junkie via Yahoo Sports, Mike Bohn, Nov. 10).
  • Norma Dumont released: the women’s bantamweight contender (9-4 in the UFC) announced Tuesday night that the UFC has released her despite two fights left on her contract, saying she had been told the promotion likely wouldn’t renew it; she is coming off a close decision loss to Ailin Perez and said she plans to start a family with her partner, UFC middleweight Marco Tulio (Heavy, Adam Martin, Nov. 10).
  • McGregor: ESPN headline — “McGregor insists it has to be Max Holloway for final UFC fight” (ESPN News Services, headline seen on ESPN this run).
  • White at the podium again: ESPN reports the UFC boss joined President Trump at a briefing and said he has “zero political aspirations” (ESPN, headline).
  • Dana White at the White House: the UFC CEO took the podium Monday but turned down President Trump’s apparently joking offer of the press secretary job — “I do not want to be in politics,” White said. Trump had said Saturday he offered White the post, then said Sunday “I was only kidding” (AFP via Yahoo Sports).
  • Walker’s run: consecutive stoppages since moving up to heavyweight; the UFC said the win put him in the division’s top 10 (BJJ TV, desk record).
  • Suarez in line at 115: her third straight win; the UFC suggested a fight with champion Mackenzie Dern could be next (BJJ TV, desk record).
  • UFC 334 prelims include Stephen Thompson vs. Charles Radtke and Jim Miller vs. Terrance McKinney (MMA Junkie, desk record).
