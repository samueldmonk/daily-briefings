Top Story

Van–Pantoja 2 goes to the podium tonight — and the champion is the narrow favourite

The UFC 331 press conference is tonight, Thursday 17 September, at 5:00 p.m. PT / 8:00 p.m. ET, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. Flyweight champion Joshua Van and former champion Alexandre Pantoja headline it alongside main-card fighters Arman Tsarukyan, Mauricio Ruffy, Patricio Pitbull, Doo Ho Choi, Alonzo Menifield and Iwo Baraniewski. It airs on UFC.com, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Kick. Media day has already happened in Los Angeles.

The rematch has an unusual shape. Pantoja lost the title after an injury suffered against Van at UFC 323 — where Van won it by TKO in round one — so the pair run it back on Saturday with the belt on the champion’s waist and the former champion chasing it. Covers has it priced tight: Van −130, Pantoja +110, with Van given a 56% implied probability in its read.

One name came off the card. Brian Ortega will no longer appear at UFC 331. Ortega has answered accusations of ducking; the date of the bout he was scheduled for is not printed here because the sources fetched this run disagree on it.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement

The flyweight picture is the live one: Alexa Grasso beat Manon Fiorot at Noche UFC on 12 September while Natália Silva and Wang Cong prepare to contest the vacant women’s flyweight belt on 3 October. On the men’s side, Brandon Moreno got past Joseph Morales in the same card’s co-main, two days before the division’s champion defends.

Business & broadcast

UFC 332 will be the first numbered event whose main card airs on CBS. Contender Series season 10 streams on Paramount+ on Tuesday nights, and the 26 September Fight Night is a Paramount+ card.
