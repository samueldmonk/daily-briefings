The Lead

Amazon's cloud beat caps a volatile July; markets closed for the weekend

U.S. markets are shut for the weekend, so the most recent session remains Friday, July 31. The major averages finished markedly higher on the last trading day of the month: the S&P 500 added 0.7% to close at 7,489.72, the Nasdaq Composite rose 1% to 25,373.85, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 276.97 points, or 0.53%, to 52,485.03.

The driver was Amazon. AWS reported sales growth of 37% year over year, topping analyst expectations of 31% and marking the unit's fastest expansion since 2021, with CEO Andy Jassy guiding capital spending to $220 billion this year. Wedbush reiterated its outperform rating and raised its target to $310 from $293, calling the quarter "the cleanest beat among the hyperscalers within our coverage."

Apple went the other way, tumbling 7.2% as Services and China revenue fell short. As Capital.com's Kyle Rodda put it, Apple "isn't really a pure AI play and was probably a victim of its own high expectations," having cleared a $5 trillion valuation again going into the print. Rodda credited the broader rally to signs of AI monetisation from Microsoft, reduced pressure on the Fed to raise rates after it held steady on July 29, and no overnight escalation in the U.S.–Iran conflict.

Movers & Drivers

New▲ Gainer

Amazon (AMZN)

Closed up more than 15% — its biggest single-day increase in over a decade — after beating second-quarter expectations. Shares had already jumped 11.51% premarket to $262.60. AWS growth of 37% y/y was the headline; capex guidance of $220 billion for the year was the caveat.

New▼ Decliner

Apple (AAPL)

Fell 7.2% as Services and China revenue disappointed. Apple beat on most major metrics, but weaker-than-expected results in its Chinese business — an area under intense competitive pressure — proved decisive for a stock sitting just below record highs going into the print.

New▼ Decliner

Roblox (RBLX)

Tumbled 22.5% in premarket trading after issuing a weak financial outlook for the next quarter, withdrawing its full-year guidance, and missing key user-growth estimates — the sharpest single-name move on the premarket board.

New▲ Gainer

Newell Brands (NWL) & Monolithic Power (MPWR)

Newell soared 17.5% premarket after the household-products maker beat second-quarter earnings expectations. Monolithic Power climbed 11.4% after the semiconductor company posted better-than-expected Q2 results.

New▼ Decliner

GoDaddy (GDDY) & MasTec (MTZ)

GoDaddy tumbled 16.4% premarket on underwhelming forward revenue guidance and persistent concerns about generative-AI disruption to its web-hosting and design business. MasTec fell nearly 16% after disappointing Q2 results.

Overseas▲ Record

South Korea's Kospi

Climbed 17.9% to 6,695.45 — its largest single-day gain on record, eclipsing a nearly 12% move in October 2008 — as AI-related stocks rebounded. Samsung Electronics surged 28% and SK Hynix soared 30%. The index had fallen more than 17% over the previous three sessions and remains well below the 9,000-plus level it reached in June.
