Top Story

CISA · Known Exploited Vulnerabilities

CISA adds four exploited flaws to KEV and sets a two-day federal patch clock

CISA added four vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Wednesday 9 September and required Federal Civilian Executive Branch agencies to apply the patches by Saturday 12 September — three calendar days from listing. Three of the four hit perimeter security appliances: CVE-2026-20079 (CVSS 10.0) in Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center, CVE-2026-19490 (CVSS 9.3) in Citrix NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway, and CVE-2025-25249 (CVSS 7.3) in Fortinet FortiOS, FortiSwitchManager and FortiSASE. The fourth, CVE-2026-87491, is an out-of-bounds write in Google Chromium’s V8 engine.

Cisco updated its advisory to say it became aware of active exploitation attempts against CVE-2026-20079 in August 2026, without disclosing further detail. The flaw lets an unauthenticated remote attacker bypass authentication on the FMC web interface and execute script files to obtain root on the underlying operating system.

The compressed window is not an anomaly. BOD 26-04, issued 10 June 2026, replaced CVSS-score-based deadlines with a four-variable risk matrix — asset exposure, KEV status, exploit automation potential and technical impact — producing tiered timelines of 3, 14 or 60 days. CISA has cited AI-accelerated exploitation as the rationale, arguing the gap between disclosure and weaponisation has collapsed from months to hours. This week’s two- and three-day deadlines are that top tier in routine use.

Patch Priority

Most urgent today · deadline tomorrow

CVE-2026-86218 — N-able N-central (on-premises). Unauthenticated remote code execution. Added to CISA KEV on 8 September with a federal remediation deadline of 11 September — tomorrow. Affects every on-premises build below 2026.3.1.14 across the 2025.4, 2026.1, 2026.2 and 2026.3 release lines, so servers on older lines are in scope, not just current ones. Huntress said this week it opened an investigation after a customer’s fully patched N-central production environment was compromised on 4 September, and N-able has now issued a fourth N-central hotfix in five weeks.

Immediately behind it: CVE-2026-20079 — Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center, CVSS 10.0, federal deadline 12 September (2 days). Exploitation has been observed since August 2026. If you run either product on-premises and internet-exposed, these are this week’s two jobs.

The PivotC2 operator — turning FortiGate boxes into footholds

SOCRadar attributes a campaign weaponising Fortinet’s CVE-2025-25249 to a Russian-speaking, financially motivated actor, with the earliest evidence of exploitation dating to July 2026. A shell script drops an exploit binary against a vulnerable FortiGate instance, opens a reverse shell and runs a one-line Node.js command, which pulls a second-stage JavaScript payload that decrypts into PivotC2 — a Node.js RAT holding a persistent outbound TLS channel to its C2. Its feature set covers interactive shells, file transfers, SOCKS5 and HTTP proxy tunnelling, local and remote port forwarding, CIDR-range scanning, and FortiGate-specific configuration harvesting and credential decryption. An auto-mode flag runs a predefined command sequence on first infection with no operator present. More than 3,000 IP addresses were targeted; 178 devices were infected, most of them in the United States.

The U.S. Department of Homeland Security confirmed that hackers breached the Homeland Security Information Network, an unclassified information-sharing platform used to support security coordination around the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Huntress investigates a compromise of a fully patched N-central server

Huntress opened an investigation after a customer’s N-central production environment — described as fully patched — was compromised on 4 September 2026. N-able has issued a fourth N-central hotfix in five weeks for the unauthenticated RCE flaw tracked as CVE-2026-86218, whose federal patch deadline falls tomorrow.

CarriedHealthcare

Veradigm discloses a third-party vendor breach exposing SSNs

Disclosed 8 September: stolen vendor credentials were used to reach a Veradigm service-delivery API. Social Security numbers were exposed; no clinical data was. The Gentlemen ransomware group claims more than 3.5 million records — an attacker assertion, not a confirmed figure.

CarriedAI-operated intrusion

PaperCut servers compromised by autonomous AI agents

GreyNoise reported a Russian-speaking actor running hundreds of autonomous AI agents against PaperCut print servers, compromising at least 440 servers across 395 organisations in 48 countries. Infrastructure pivoted on 31 August to CVE-2026-81578 and CVE-2026-82078; the chain runs from initial access to credential theft to domain compromise in minutes.
