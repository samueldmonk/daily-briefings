Top story

Everybody made weight — and the rematch that was decided in 26 seconds finally gets a second act

Joshua Van and Alexandre Pantoja each weighed 125 pounds in Los Angeles on Friday, making Saturday’s flyweight championship rematch official at Crypto.com Arena. All 24 scheduled athletes recorded weights inside their divisional limits.

The rematch has been nine months in the making for a plain reason: the first fight lasted 26 seconds. Van took the belt when Pantoja suffered a freak arm injury that early, which is the kind of ending that settles a title and settles nothing else. Neither man has answered the question the first meeting asked.

The rest of the weigh-in sheet, as it was rendered: Tsarukyan 156 / Ruffy 155; Patricio Pitbull 145.5 / Doo Ho Choi 146; Steveson 240 / Sharaf 241; Menifield 205.5 / Baraniewski 206; Vera 136 / Jourdain 135; Tuivasa 264.5 / Despaigne 263.
