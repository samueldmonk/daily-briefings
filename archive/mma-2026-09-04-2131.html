Top Story

The market has made up its mind

Three more books price Paris — and the debutant is a bigger favourite than the main event usually allows

New Three sportsbooks not previously seen by this desk have now priced the UFC Paris main event, and they cluster tightly: FanDuel has Salahdine Parnasse −620 / Dan Hooker +400; BetRivers is shortest on the Frenchman at −625 while paying the most on the veteran at +410; Caesars offers the softest favourite price at −600 and the longest dog at +430.

Set against the two lines this desk already carried — MyBookie at −649 / +420 and DraftKings at −625 / +455 — the spread across five books is remarkably narrow. Every one of them makes Parnasse at least a −600 favourite. That is an unusual price for a fighter making his promotional debut against a ranked opponent, and it is the story of the card.

New Hooker, ranked #10 at lightweight, “attempts to halt a two-fight skid” on Saturday. Parnasse is a genuine debutant but not a developmental signing: he is a former two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion, 14-2 inside KSW with four defences of the lightweight belt, who signed with the UFC in late July 2026 having previously turned the promotion down — and was handed a main event on debut. His U.S. debut came in May 2026, a first-round stoppage of Kenneth Cross. He did not come through Dana White’s Contender Series.
