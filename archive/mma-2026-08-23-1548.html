Top story

Rodrigues wins the war in Sacramento — and asks for du Plessis

Gregory Rodrigues quieted a partisan Golden 1 Center crowd on Saturday night, taking a unanimous decision (48-47, 49-46, 48-47) over local favourite Anthony “Fluffy” Hernandez in a 25-minute middleweight main event. “Robocop” seemed poised to finish in the second after chopping down Hernandez’s lead leg, but Hernandez endured and seized the third before Rodrigues found a second wind in the fourth and defended well down the stretch.

It is Rodrigues’ fourth consecutive win. Yahoo Sports reported he knocked Hernandez down four times and stuffed 26 of 29 takedown attempts; Hernandez has now lost two straight. Both men took home $100,000 for Fight of the Night, and Rodrigues used the cage interview and the post-fight press conference to call for a fight with Dricus du Plessis — “Dricus Du Plessis, where are you?” — arguing it is the fight to make at 185 pounds. Du Plessis, a former middleweight champion, is currently ranked No. 2 in the division.

Sources: UFC.com main card results (E. Spencer Kyte, Aug 23), UFC.com bonus coverage, MMA Weekly, Yahoo Sports/Forbes.

Prospect watch
Prospect

Anthony Wint — 8-0, and a week removed from a contract

Wint earned his UFC deal on the opening episode of Season 10 of Dana White’s Contender Series, then submitted Terrance Chatman in the first round of his promotional debut. There were dicey moments — Chatman threatened a kimura on the way down — but Wint worked free, took top position and clamped on an arm-triangle from knee-on-belly to put Chatman to sleep. He is the second heavyweight with NFL ties in as many years to come to the UFC through Contender Series.

ProspectNew

Bilal Hasan — contract to debut in a matter of days

Hasan finished Mridul Saikia in under a minute at bantamweight on the opening episode of Season 10 of Dana White’s Contender Series, and White confirmed on the night that he had earned a UFC deal. Days later he was booked for his promotional debut against Nilson Rojas on the UFC Shanghai card this Saturday, one of four contracts handed out in the season’s first week.

Prospect

MarQuel Mederos — 11 straight, unbeaten in the Octagon

Now 4-0-1 in the UFC and riding an 11-fight winning streak overall, which UFC.com notes is tied for the second-longest unbeaten run in the lightweight division alongside Tom Nolan and Arman Tsarukyan, one behind Chris Padilla and Quillan Salkilld. The MMA Lab product called for a top-15 opponent or a date with Beneil Dariush or Chris Duncan.

Prospect

Carli Judice — four in a row, three finishes

“Crispy” has won four straight with three finishes since dropping a split decision in her promotional debut. The Louisiana native closed the distance on Chaves, buried a front kick in her midsection and ended the fight in an instant. She is climbing the flyweight ranks and should be looking at a ranked opponent next.

Prospect

Stan Dorsainvil — short notice, and a day job

Dorsainvil entered the Octagon 5-0 and, per Forbes, took the fight on short notice while working at Home Depot. He came away with a unanimous decision over Gauge Young, showing toughness, power and a rounded overall game. Worth watching with a full camp behind him.

Around the sport
  • A finish-filled card. Ten of the 13 bouts ended inside the distance, including six finishes in the seven preliminary fights and first-round stoppages from Anthony Wint, Carli Judice and Reinier de Ridder on the main card.
  • De Ridder restarts at 205. After faltering in his last two middleweight appearances, Reinier de Ridder moved up and flattened Roman Dolidze in the first, aided by Dolidze pulling guard to chase leg locks. De Ridder previously won gold in ONE Championship; the light heavyweight division has another contender to consider.
  • Petrino wins ugly. Vitor Petrino swept the cards against Serghei Spivac to go 4-0 since moving to heavyweight, but UFC.com was blunt that the performance “wasn’t the kind that is going to do him any favors.” He turns 29 on Friday and is 10 fights into his UFC career.
  • Seven years away. It had been seven years since the UFC last held an event in California’s capital.
  • Blaydes re-signs. Heavyweight Curtis Blaydes has put pen to paper on a new eight-fight deal, securing his place on the roster against what Bloody Elbow describes as a run of surprise roster removals. He is booked against Waldo Cortes-Acosta on the September 12 Noche UFC card.
  • Next up. The promotion heads to Shanghai on Saturday, August 29 for Umar Nurmagomedov vs Song Yadong, then Paris on September 5.
