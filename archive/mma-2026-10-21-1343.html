Top Story

UFC 333 fight week: Volkanovski vs. Evloev is a coin flip, and the champion is promising a finish

Two title fights headline Saturday’s UFC 333 at Etihad Arena in Abu Dhabi. Featherweight champion Alexander Volkanovski, coming off a title defense against Diego Lopes, puts his belt on the line against Movsar Evloev, who is 20-0 after beating Lerone Murphy in March; Kalshi prices the main event at 50% / 50%, per Covers. In the co-main, bantamweight champion Petr Yan defends the title he took from Merab Dvalishvili at UFC 323 last December, with Kalshi at Yan 59% / Dvalishvili 41% (Covers).

“I believe I can get a finish. I believe I can do it inside five,” Volkanovski told MMA Junkie. “Trying to out-grapple me for 25 minutes? I can’t see it… I think I can get my hand raised and I think I can do it in pretty wild fashion.” SB Nation notes Evloev’s 10 UFC wins have all come by decision and that Volkanovski is now 38 (SB Nation via Yahoo Sports).

Fight-week schedule (UFC.com): open workout Wed. Oct. 21, 6 p.m. GST at Yas Mall; press conference Thu. Oct. 22, 5 p.m. GST at Etihad Arena; ceremonial weigh-ins Fri. Oct. 23, 6 p.m. GST; main card 10 p.m. GST Saturday (UFC.com Fight Week Guide).

Around the Sport

  • New “I’m ready for a 25-minute war,” Merab Dvalishvili told Arab News of Saturday’s bantamweight trilogy with champion Petr Yan, which stands at one win apiece: “I’m going to try to finish him every second… If not, then I’m ready to take a decision win because I’m the decision machine.” He named Song Yadong — fresh off a win over Umar Nurmagomedov — as his next target if he regains the belt. Arab News says UFC 333 will be the 24th UFC event staged in Abu Dhabi (Arab News, Oct. 10).
  • Muhammad Mokaev claims the UFC threatens to cut fighters for wrestling, and separately blames TKO and Paramount, not Dana White, for UFC exits, per Sherdog headlines; Dricus du Plessis’ coach says he rejected a UFC 205-pound tournament offer (Sherdog).
  • Evloev’s camp says he has a secret submission planned for Volkanovski: a member of his team told SPORTbible he has three signature techniques — the arm-triangle, the kimura and “a third choke we’ve worked on the most. I won’t reveal it.” SPORTbible notes that if Evloev wins the belt without a stoppage he would become the first men’s champion in UFC history never to have secured a finish in the promotion (SPORTbible, Oct. 21).
  • UFC Hall of Famer Jose Aldo told MMA Fighting he would cut Movsar Evloev if he ran the UFC: “I’d wait for him to lose for the first time and then cut him because he’s a guy who doesn’t sell, a guy who puts on boring fights.” Evloev, 20-0 overall and 10-0 in the UFC, has gone to the judges in every UFC fight and challenges Alexander Volkanovski for the featherweight title Saturday (MMA Knockout on SI, Oct. 20).
  • UFC 333 Embedded episode two, released Oct. 20, follows Losene Keita (who faces Aaron Pico) on a family outing — “It’s the UFC dream” — and Merab Dvalishvili showing a new Las Vegas property where he plans a gym with an Octagon: “training and fighting is number one priority.” Volkanovski has arrived in Abu Dhabi, Evloev discusses managing his weight cut, and Petr Yan closes the episode with gym work (MiddleEasy).
  • Retired lightweight Paul Felder will replace Joe Rogan on color commentary at UFC 333, per MMA Junkie, as Rogan no longer takes overseas assignments; Brendan Fitzgerald joins Din Thomas at the analyst desk, John Gooden handles backstage reporting in place of Megan Olivi, and Bruce Buffer handles walkouts and results (MMA Mania via Yahoo Sports, Oct. 20).
  • Petr Yan told The Ariel Helwani Show on Oct. 19 that his coach’s written plan for Merab Dvalishvili has grown from one page to five; he rejected Dvalishvili calling him rude (“I was just being real”) and declined to name a next challenger, saying he will defend against whoever the UFC considers most deserving (MiddleEasy).
  • Asked after UFC Edmonton whether Middle East events would go ahead after Houthi strikes on Riyadh’s King Khalid airport, Dana White said: “I go to Abu Dhabi on Tuesday; I have like six events in the next 30 days in Saudi, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi.” SPORTbible lists Prochazka vs. Stirling in Doha on Nov. 21 and a Fight Night in Saudi Arabia on Dec. 5, though conflicting reports say the December event could move to Qatar (SPORTbible, Oct. 19).
  • Marc-André Barriault, who had lost five of his previous six, earned his fourth consecutive bonus with the Daukaus finish; Cageside Press suggests Gilbert Urbina next (Cageside Press).
  • Mandel Nallo, 37, earned his first UFC win by second-round TKO of Nate Landwehr (Cageside Press).
  • Canadian UFC fans will get all events on Paramount+ at no extra cost from next year, mirroring the U.S. deal in place since January (MMA Knockout on SI via Yahoo Sports).
  • Dana White on Sean Strickland’s pay complaints: “Sean Strickland just signed a new contract in May… The number that he claimed he made is not true” (Sherdog, Oct. 14).
