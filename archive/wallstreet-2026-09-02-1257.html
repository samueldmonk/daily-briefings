The Lead

The oil rally stalls, and the tape turns green — as of the ~10 AM ET read

A Yahoo Finance quote board fetched during this run showed the S&P 500 +15.23 points, +0.20%, the Dow +277.47, +0.53%, the Nasdaq Composite +7.53, +0.03% and the Russell 2000 +21.95, +0.75%. The VIX was 15.90, −2.69%, gold +0.50%, bitcoin −1.24% and October WTI crude −0.57%. The page was stamped "U.S. markets close in 6h," which places the read at roughly 10 AM ET, not at the moment of the fetch — so that is the time attached to it here.

That is a reversal of Tuesday, when stocks closed lower with the Dow off more than 400 points on the U.S.–Iran conflict, rising oil and surging bond yields. Brent had jumped above $95 a barrel after U.S. airstrikes; President Trump then threatened to hit Iran "much harder" if it retaliated. By Wednesday morning the crude bid had faded, with October WTI slightly lower on the day.

The second leg of the rebound is a rate story. A search summary of Yahoo's Wednesday live blog this run attributes to New York Fed President John Williams the comment that there are "no clear signs right now" that a September hike is needed to bring inflation down. The direct fetch of that live blog returned a cached 5:00 AM version that does not contain the remark, so it is printed as attributed but unconfirmed by a fetched page. The same summary carried index moves of Dow +0.8%, S&P 500 +0.6% and Nasdaq +0.5%; those are not adopted here for the same reason, and the ~10 AM board above is what this edition runs on.

The morning's hard datapoint was labour. ADP reported 38,000 private jobs added in August, below the 47,000 estimate and down from a July figure revised to 46,000 — the slowest month since January. Note that Yahoo's own 5 AM preview listed a different pair, +46,000 expected against +44,000 previously; both are printed, and the post-release figures are the ones used.
