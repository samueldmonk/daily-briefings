The Lead

Alphabet’s worst day drags the Nasdaq 1.4% lower; Amazon, Intel slide after hours (official close; after hours as of ~6:30 PM ET)

US stocks fell Thursday as Alphabet’s Q3 earnings sparked a sell-off in the stock and AI jitters returned. At the close the Nasdaq Composite was down 1.38%, the S&P 500 0.30% and the Dow 0.25%, while the small-cap Russell 2000 rose 1.01% (Yahoo Finance). The Dow had opened down 0.6%. StockTitan, citing CNBC, likewise reports the Nasdaq −1.4% and the S&P 500 −0.3%, ending a three-day gain for the S&P (StockTitan).

Microsoft beat on the top and bottom lines Wednesday night, but Alphabet’s sales came up short; despite 103% cloud growth, concerns about its free cash flow and AI return on investment sank the stock nearly 13%. The sell-off spread to chips (SOXX) and software, with double-digit losses for Fortinet and ServiceNow, while investors rotated into Healthcare, Financials and Real Estate, keeping a lid on overall losses (Yahoo Finance).

Macro: Q3 GDP grew at a 3.4% annualized rate in the advance estimate, beating 3.1% expectations, with business investment up 12.7% and consumer spending up 3.8%. September PCE inflation rose 3.6% year over year (core 3.1%, core +0.3% month over month), all in line — which Yahoo Finance says solidifies expectations for a December rate hike, a day after the Fed held with two dissenters favoring a hike (Yahoo Finance).

Movers & Drivers

EarlierDown ~13%

Alphabet (GOOGL)

Shares dropped nearly 13% after Q3 results: Cloud grew 103%, but sales came up short and free-cash-flow and AI-return concerns weighed. Yahoo Finance said the stock was on track for its worst day since its 2004 IPO, with nearly $500 billion of market value on the line; StockTitan’s data show a 12.64% close lower (Yahoo Finance).

EarlierSoftware

ServiceNow (NOW) & Fortinet (FTNT)

Software stocks “got crushed” after Wednesday-night reports — ServiceNow down 25% and Fortinet down 21% in Yahoo’s ~noon ET update (24/7 Wall St.’s late-session losers board: NOW −26.95%, FTNT −20.09%), with the iShares software ETF (IGV) −4%. Fortinet cut its full-year service-revenue forecast to $5.15–5.17 billion; BofA cut it to Neutral (Yahoo Finance).

EarlierUp 34%

ADT (ADT)

Agreed to be taken private by Thrive Holdings for $10 a share in cash (Yahoo put the deal at $7.6 billion; ADT’s release values it at $15.3 billion including debt, per StockTitan), a 45.6% premium to the Oct. 27 close. The stock soared 34% to about $9.93 (Yahoo Finance).

EarlierUp 25%

Crocs (CROX)

Jumped 25% to its highest price in over two years as HeyDude revenue rose 6.1% to $170 million — its first sales increase in six quarters (Yahoo Finance).

EarlierUp 15%

Carvana (CVNA)

Surged 15% after Q3 revenue rose 42.4% to $8.04 billion, its first quarter above $8 billion; retail units rose 39% to 217,385 (Yahoo Finance).

EarlierDown

Caterpillar (CAT) & Sprouts (SFM)

Caterpillar fell as much as 12% before trimming losses on a modest beat (adj. EPS $7.20, revenue $20.68 billion) and a moderate Q4 sales outlook; Sprouts plunged 22% on a price-cutting strategy expected to weigh on profits into next year (Yahoo Finance).

EarlierUp 6%

Eli Lilly (LLY)

Popped 6% on strong Q3 results for its GLP-1 drugs (Yahoo Finance).

Down 40%

Xerox (XRX)

The day’s biggest loser on StockTitan’s closing board, down 40.32%, after cutting its full-year forecasts for adjusted operating profit and free cash flow; Q3 revenue fell 4.7% to $1.87 billion (StockTitan).

EarlierDeals

DoorDash–Instacart; Marvell–Lumentum

DoorDash agreed to buy Instacart for $17 billion — $65 a share, a 26% premium to the Oct. 22 close. DoorDash closed 5.61% higher (StockTitan). Separately, Marvell closed 13.24% lower and Lumentum 0.61% higher after reports of advanced merger talks, which neither company had confirmed as of the close (StockTitan); Bloomberg said a deal could come as soon as next week (Yahoo Finance).

After-Hours Movers

After hours

Amazon (AMZN) — down about 6–7%

StockTitan data showed Amazon −6.72% after hours at 4:25 p.m. ET, and CNBC’s quote page showed $228.29 after hours, −6.36% from the $243.79 close (search result) (StockTitan; CNBC). Yahoo Finance says Amazon tanked 10% in post-market trading before trimming losses; it held 2026 capex at $220 billion, and trailing-12-month free cash flow swung to a $42.4 billion outflow from a $14.8 billion inflow a year earlier (Yahoo Finance). Q3 EPS of $1.89 missed the $1.96 estimate and revenue of $201.94 billion missed $202.20 billion; AWS revenue grew 39% to $45.98 billion with $17.77 billion of segment operating income, and Jassy projected AWS growth above 40% in both 2027 and 2028 (24/7 Wall St.). Earlier after-hours snapshots: −6.95% (Benzinga) and an initial −8% (24/7 Wall St.). The stock had already fallen about 4.2% in the regular session (24/7 Wall St.).

After hours

Intel (INTC) — down about 17%

Beat third-quarter earnings estimates, but guided Q4 revenue to $15 billion–$16 billion, well below the $17 billion analysts expected, blaming a worse-than-anticipated drop in PC revenue. StockTitan data showed −17.60% at 4:25 p.m. ET; its CFO sees the PC slowdown lasting at least through the first half of next year. The stock fell more than 20% on the news before paring losses (StockTitan; Yahoo Finance).

After hours

Rivian (RIVN) — jumps

Q3 revenue of $2.155 billion beat the $2.02 billion Bloomberg consensus (up 38% year over year); adjusted loss per share of $0.48 vs. $0.61 expected. Rivian raised full-year guidance, citing strong R2 sales (Yahoo Finance).

After hours

Sandisk (SNDK) — up 1.8%

Up 1.80% after hours at 4:25 p.m. ET after fiscal Q1 revenue of $11.0 billion and an eight-for-one stock split announcement (StockTitan).
