Top Story

Nearly 19 million Poles' medical records stolen from a single software supplier

Polish authorities are investigating a major cyberattack on MyDr, a company that supplies the software used to run medical facilities and hold electronic medical records, after personal data belonging to close to 19 million people was reportedly compromised. The stolen database runs to more than two terabytes and covers people who use primary healthcare services; the compromised systems hold prescriptions, appointment records, registered medicines and documents supplied to doctors. Around 12,000 medical facilities are caught up in the incident.

The attackers demonstrated the breach by leaking a senior Polish politician's PESEL national identity number, phone numbers and 25 prescriptions. Poland's Central Cybercrime Bureau is running the investigation, and there is no indication so far that authorities are dealing with an external attack from Russia or any other state. Facilities using MyDr's systems are continuing to operate normally, according to the minister. The Ministry of Digital Affairs is advising citizens to check whether their data appears in the stolen database and, if it does, to block their PESEL number.

Patch Priority

Do this first
CVE-2026-68820 — Microsoft Windows Ancillary Function Driver for WinSock (afd.sys), CVSS 7.0. Federal remediation deadline Tuesday, August 25, 2026 — 8 days away. This is not a theoretical elevation-of-privilege bug: Check Point attributes it to Lazarus Group, which ran it as an unpatched zero-day against defence and aerospace targets before Microsoft fixed it in the August 11 Patch Tuesday release. In those intrusions the flaw carried the FudModule rootkit to SYSTEM privileges and switched off endpoint detection tooling. Patch Windows to the August release, and treat any host that was exposed to the Dream Job lure set as suspect rather than clean. Four other catalog entries below — Metabase, Cisco Secure Firewall, JetBrains TeamCity and Progress LoadMaster — are already past their due dates and should be closed out at the same time.

Threat Actor Spotlight

APT

Lazarus Group — Operation Dream Job, 2026 wave

Check Point Research's August 17 threat intelligence report again flags a new wave of the Lazarus-linked Operation Dream Job campaign, aimed at defence organisations in Europe, India and Brazil. Attackers approach targets with fraudulent job opportunities and deliver malware through trojanised PDF software. The campaign has been active since early 2026 across aerospace, aviation and military technology firms, including organisations working on surveillance sensors, drones and robotics. Operators pose as recruiters, most often over LinkedIn or direct message. The intrusion chain paired the afd.sys zero-day (CVE-2026-68820) with the FudModule rootkit for SYSTEM access and EDR shutdown, while the MISTPEN in-memory downloader routed command-and-control through Microsoft's Graph API and OneDrive so the traffic sat inside normal Microsoft flows.

Breaches & Incidents

Insider threatNew

A North Korean IT worker was on the payroll of a U.S. federal agency

The FBI is investigating how an unidentified federal agency was swept up in the long-running campaign in which North Korean remote IT workers fraudulently obtain jobs at major companies and other organisations. The individual was discovered in July 2026, and Todd Hemmen, deputy assistant director of the FBI's Cyber Capabilities Branch, disclosed the case on July 28 at a Digital Government Institute conference in Washington. The agency has not been named, no details of the worker's duties were provided, and it remains unclear whether any sensitive information was accessed or stolen. The United Nations estimates these remote workers generate between $250 million and $600 million a year for North Korea.

RansomwareNew

Colombia's Ministry of Justice hit days before a presidential transition

Colombia's Ministry of Justice was struck by ransomware on August 2, 2026, affecting part of its technology infrastructure and reducing the availability of several public-facing digital services. Services connected to illicit-drug monitoring and legal processes were among those disrupted. Officials confirmed that some files were encrypted but said no data theft was detected during the incident. The timing — immediately ahead of the presidential transition — is the part defenders should note.

RansomwareNew

Akira claims Minnesota Health Insurance Network

Minnesota Health Insurance Network reported a cybersecurity incident after the Akira ransomware group claimed responsibility for an April 2026 attack. Client personal information reportedly exposed includes passport information, home addresses, phone numbers and email addresses, with similar employee information also allegedly compromised, alongside project information, financial records, contracts and agreements. The organisation had not issued a public statement confirming details at the time of the reports. Akira is one of the most prolific ransomware groups targeting healthcare through 2025 and 2026, typically stealing data before encrypting systems.

Breach

Levi Strauss & Co. — corporate data exfiltrated after three employees were socially engineered

Levi Strauss reported on August 7 that an unauthorised third party reached its systems by social-engineering three employees into granting access to their devices — psychological manipulation rather than a software exploit. The company says "certain corporate information was accessed and exfiltrated," with no evidence to date of consumer data being compromised. Day-to-day operations were not disrupted and Levi Strauss does not expect a material impact on its operations or financial results.

Ransomware

INC accelerating through August via SonicWall SMA 1000 flaws

Resecurity tracks INC as the dominant ransomware actor since the start of August, leaning on vulnerabilities in SonicWall's SMA 1000 series appliances for access. Ransomware.Live counts 885 claimed victims to date. Two SMA 1000 CVEs — CVE-2026-15409 (CVSS 10.0, server-side request forgery) and CVE-2026-15410 (CVSS 7.2, code injection) — sit in the CISA catalog flagged for ransomware use.
