Top Story

Silva wins the vacant women’s flyweight title; UFC 332 sets knockout records

Natália Silva beat Wang Cong by unanimous decision — 48-47, 48-47 and 49-46 — in the UFC 332 main event at the Delta Center in Salt Lake City on Saturday, Oct. 3, to claim the women’s flyweight title Valentina Shevchenko vacated. CBS Sports called it a technical affair in which Silva jumped out to an early lead before a late rally from Wang gave her hope on the scorecards (CBS Sports; Cageside Press).

The undercard was historic. Nine first-round KO/TKOs, and eleven overall, set modern-era records for the promotion, Cageside Press reported; with so many finishes the UFC skipped Fight of the Night and paid four $100,000 Performance of the Night bonuses instead. In the co-main, Payton Talbott stopped former flyweight champion Deiveson Figueiredo with punches in the first round and called out Sean O’Malley; Roman Kopylov opened the main card by knocking out Ateba Gautier, and Esteban Ribovics — who missed weight by half a pound on Friday — stopped King Green in the first (Cageside Press; CBS Sports). Attendance was 16,492 for a $2.7 million gate (Cageside Press).

Also announced Oct. 3: Alex Pereira (13-4) stays at heavyweight and meets Sergei Pavlovich (21-3) in the co-main event of UFC 335 at T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas, on Dec. 12 (Cageside Press).

Prospect Watch

Prospect

Contender Series Week 9: four contracts, all first-round finishes

Dana White signed all four first-round finishers at the Meta APEX on Tuesday: Preston LaGrange (TKO, punches, over Nell Ariano), Sal Everett (KO, punch, over Ozzy Martin), Roque Conceição (submission, triangle choke, over Alexander Chavez) and Mateus Soares (TKO, punches, over Ryuho Miyaguchi). Everett — who worked UFC security for three years before becoming an assistant to White and other executives — moved to 5-0. Alivia Bierley beat the favored Summer Onley by unanimous decision (30-27, 29-28, 29-28) but did not get a contract (CBS Sports; Sherdog). Cageside Press counted 38 contracts through the first eight weeks against Season 5’s mark of 39 (Cageside Press, Oct 5); four more puts Season 10 at 42 by this desk’s count.

Prospect

Imanol Rodriguez stays unbeaten

Rodriguez put Alden Coria away with a body kick and follow-up punches at 3:11 of the first round and earned a $100,000 Performance of the Night bonus (Cageside Press; Uncrowned).

Prospect

Damian Pinas needs 75 seconds

Pinas knocked out Andrey Pulyaev at 1:15 of the first round; Dana White said Pinas “won by a vicious knockout, and he broke his jaw” while announcing his $100,000 bonus (Cageside Press).

Debut

Roberto Soldić wins his UFC debut

The former two-division KSW champion survived an early knockdown, then won a 10-8 second round on the way to a unanimous decision over Khaos Williams in his first fight at welterweight (Uncrowned via Yahoo Sports).

Prospect

Four contracts from Contender Series Week 8, led by George Staines

Dana White signed all four winners: lightweight George Staines, heavyweight Adama Diop, featherweight Ian Escuza and women’s strawweight Aieza Bertolso (Heavy via Yahoo Sports; Cageside Press; Bloody Elbow).

Around the Sport

  • New Oddsmakers are already looking ahead to new women’s flyweight champion Natália Silva’s first defense: Sherdog reports Silva opened as the underdog against two-time champion Valentina Shevchenko, who vacated while injured and is guaranteed a title shot when cleared; no bout is booked (Sherdog via Yahoo Sports, Oct 7).
  • A UFC promo video confirmed Alex Pereira (13-4) vs. Sergei Pavlovich (21-3) for the UFC 335 co-main on Dec. 12 at T-Mobile Arena; Pereira posted an AI-generated video captioned “See you soon,” and Pavlovich replied “Back in action soon.” Pavlovich is on a three-fight win streak, most recently knocking out Tallison Teixeira in 39 seconds at UFC Macau (Sherdog).
  • Former champion Khamzat Chimaev (15-1) offered Sean Strickland $1 million for a rematch — “Strickland I will pay you 1… Send me location and let’s fight” (X, Oct 5) — doubling Imavov’s $500,000 offer; Sherdog says Strickland’s defense against Imavov, first rumored for UFC 335 in December, “now looks set for 2027” (Sherdog, Oct 6).
  • No. 3-ranked middleweight Nassourdine Imavov offered Sean Strickland $500,000 of his own money if Strickland beats him in January — “If it’s really about money, I’ll put $500K of my own money on the line” — and called on the UFC to strip the champion, after Strickland confirmed money was the sticking point in talks; their linked Dec. 12 UFC 335 bout was not finalized. Imavov has won five straight; Strickland beat him by decision in January 2023 (MMA News, Oct 5).
  • Glover Teixeira, who cornered Raoni Barcelos, said tests after Barcelos suddenly lost consciousness during a wrestling exchange in his UFC Vegas 121 headliner against Raul Rosas Jr. on Sept. 26 found swelling in his brain — “a traumatic brain injury” that may have caused him to black out; Barcelos was ahead on the scorecards entering the final round and was taken to a Level 1 trauma center (Sherdog).
  • Payton Talbott called out Sean O’Malley after stopping Figueiredo, pitching a fight to decide the next bantamweight title challenger (CBS Sports).
  • Johnny Walker made his heavyweight debut with a first-round knee KO of Mick Parkin (Cageside Press).
  • Ismail Naurdiev handed Marvin Vettori a fifth straight loss (Cageside Press).
