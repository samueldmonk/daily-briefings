The Lead

Markets closed for Thanksgiving; Wednesday’s late comeback stands as the latest close

Holiday schedule. U.S. stock markets are closed today, Thursday, Nov. 26, for Thanksgiving; they reopen Friday and close early at 1:00 PM ET (StockTitan, citing the NYSE holiday calendar). The live quotes above may show futures or off-hours indications, not a regular session.

Wednesday, Nov. 25 — the latest close. Stocks recovered most of their steep early losses as a pullback in AI stocks eased, AP reports. The S&P 500 fell 0.3% after being down as much as 3.2% earlier in the day; the Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 164 points, or 0.3%; and the Nasdaq composite fell 0.6%. The market pared its losses even as a report raised more worries about high inflation and as President Donald Trump plans to escalate the U.S. trade war with major trading partners. AI stocks retreated for a second day following Monday’s relief rally over the surprise reinstatement of Jensen Huang as Nvidia’s CEO, and Treasury yields fell (AP via ABC News).

The comeback. “Dow wipes out a 1,100-point plunge as AI stocks slide again,” ran one closing headline (NewsCubby headline). Yahoo Finance’s live blog had flagged the Nasdaq-100 on pace for a bear-market close — a drop of 20% or more from its peak — before the averages pared losses to close mixed; Nvidia, down as much as 8% during the session, closed down roughly 4% (Yahoo Finance). By StockTitan’s tally, QQQ (the Nasdaq-100 fund) was down 4.38% at 11:02 AM ET but finished down just 0.65%, while DIA (the Dow fund) turned higher (StockTitan).

Inflation. The PCE price index, the Fed’s preferred inflation gauge, rose 3.8% from a year earlier in October, the highest annual reading since 2023. Core PCE rose to 3.3%, ahead of the 3.2% economists expected, and traders priced a 98% chance of a quarter-point Fed hike in December (Yahoo Finance). The final reading of third-quarter GDP showed 4.3% annualized growth, up from the 3.4% advance estimate and ahead of the 3.5% economists expected (Yahoo Finance).

Desk record: the Nov. 19 edition covered Anthropic’s Nasdaq debut and a session in which the Dow fell more than 1,000 points; those intraday readings are preserved in the Archive and are superseded by the closes above.
