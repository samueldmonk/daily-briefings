The close (Hammerstone Markets closing recap via Investrade, published 4:08 PM ET). “Much ado about nothing summed up today’s stock market action,” the recap said: the Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 195.36 points (+0.38%), the S&P 500 lost 4.34 points (−0.06%), the Nasdaq dropped 120.27 points (−0.44%) and the Russell 2000 fell 12.51 points (−0.45%). Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack said the Fed should move “relatively quickly” to a restrictive stance as inflation becomes more broad-based, and Zelenskiy said it was increasingly clear Russia would not accept the US-proposed energy ceasefire after attacks on six Ukrainian energy facilities.
