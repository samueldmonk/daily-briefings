Top story

Media day in Shanghai: Song Yadong rips his shirt off, and says the winner gets the title shot

The second faceoff of the week was the loud one. Song Yadong and Umar Nurmagomedov came face to face again on Wednesday, at UFC Shanghai media day, and Song pulled his shirt off in front of Nurmagomedov. Song’s line, as reported: “If I win this fight, I will get a title shot.” UFC.com has published the exchange under the title “Song Yadong: ‘With This Fight I Will Get A Title Shot’”. ⚠ This is a distinct event from the Tuesday faceoff in front of the host arena reported by Yahoo Sports and already carried below — two faceoffs, two days, both published. Carried · 10:20 edition

The UFC returns to Shanghai on Saturday, August 29 — for the second consecutive year and the third time overall — and the week has properly begun: Umar Nurmagomedov and Song Yadong had their first fight-week faceoff in front of the host arena on Tuesday, per Yahoo Sports. UFC Fight Night: Nurmagomedov vs Song takes place at the Oriental Sports Center in Shanghai’s Pudong District, streams on Paramount+, and starts at 6:00 a.m. EDT — an early-morning card for a U.S. audience.

⚠ Two different ranking pairs are in circulation and both are published unmerged. UFC.com’s own event announcement headline bills the fight as a “pivotal bantamweight clash between #3 Umar Nurmagomedov and #5 Song Yadong.” A separate read returned this run places them at No. 2 and No. 6 in the latest Meta UFC rankings at 135 pounds. Rankings move week to week and the two statements may simply be snapshots taken at different times; neither is tidied into the other here.

Nurmagomedov (20-1 MMA, 8-1 UFC), whom UFC.com describes as a former title challenger, arrives on a two-fight win streak since coming up short in his title bid against Merab Dvalishvili at UFC 311: he beat Mario Bautista at UFC 321 in October, then outworked Deiveson Figueiredo three months later at UFC 324. Song Yadong (23-9-1 MMA, 12-4-1 UFC), “The Kung Fu Kid,” 28, makes his sixth main-event appearance. He dropped a close decision to Sean O’Malley at UFC 324, then rebounded in May by travelling to Macau and submitting Figueiredo in the second round — his first appearance on Chinese soil since 2018.

Resolved this run — the “two Figueiredo wins” oddity. Previous editions flagged that both main-eventers were credited with a recent win over Deiveson Figueiredo by the same outlet, and published the oddity rather than guess. UFC.com’s fight-by-fight preview settles it: they are two different fights. Nurmagomedov beat Figueiredo on the scorecards at UFC 324 in January; Song submitted him in the second round at UFC Fight Night Macau in May. Both statements were always true.

The stakes are explicit. UFC.com frames the matchup as carrying “massive divisional significance” at 135 pounds, with each man looking to enter the final quarter of the year as “the clubhouse leader in the chase for the next title opportunity” — and states that “the winner will have a legitimate case for being first in line to face the winner of Yan-Dvalishvili 3.” The belt is currently held by two-time champion Petr Yan, with a trilogy bout against Merab Dvalishvili the awaited next chapter. Underneath, the co-main pairs Yan Xiaonan, a fixture in the strawweight title conversation for nearly four years, against Denise Gomes.

The full main card, per UFC.com: Nurmagomedov vs Song; Yan Xiaonan vs Denise Gomes (strawweight, co-main); Aoriqileng vs Kai Asakura; Alex Perez vs Sumudaerji; Liu Ce vs Levi Rodrigues Jr.; Bilal Hasan vs Nilson Rojas. A 12-fight undercard supports it. Yan has never lost in China in the UFC and enters off a decision loss to Virna Jandiroba; Gomes, 26, arrives on a four-fight winning streak, having won six of her last seven. Perez–Sumudaerji is a rebooking after their Macau meeting was halted prematurely by an accidental low blow.

UFC Shanghai — Umar Nurmagomedov vs. Song Yadong

A bantamweight main event with title-shot implications: #3 Nurmagomedov (20-1) against #5 Song (23-9-1), in Song’s sixth main event and his second home-region headliner of the year. Yan Xiaonan vs. Denise Gomes co-headlines at strawweight; Aoriqileng vs. Kai Asakura also features.

Odds: Nurmagomedov −470 / Song +360 (DraftKings), per MMA Junkie’s odds piece. Three further lines stand: −700 / +500 (BetOnline.ag), −500 / +385, and — new this run — −500 / +375, the line the official UFC site is listing. The four are printed unmerged — they are different books at different moments — but they agree on the direction: Nurmagomedov is a heavy favourite everywhere, and no source read this run has him anything but.

● New at 3:50 — a sixth reading of the line, and the first real tale of the tape. LowKick MMA, in a piece by Timothy Wheaton published Aug. 26, 2026 at 8:18 a.m. ET, puts current consensus at Nurmagomedov −500 and Song +380, which it translates to “roughly an 80% market-implied chance for Nurmagomedov leaving Song near 20%.” It also gives the movement, which none of the five earlier lines did: the price has tightened in Nurmagomedov’s direction from early numbers around −380, with Song initially listed near +295, before moving through the −500 / +385 range as fight week arrived. That is the same direction as every other book read this run, and it is now a documented drift rather than a snapshot. The piece’s own preferred angle is Nurmagomedov by decision at −170 rather than laying −500 on the moneyline, with Song by knockout named as the cleanest upset script. ⚠ Published as one outlet’s reading of the market; this desk asserts no price of its own.

● New at 3:50 — the records and the styles, from the same piece. Nurmagomedov is 20-1 as a professional and 8-1 in the UFC; his only defeat came in a five-round title fight against Merab Dvalishvili at UFC 311, and since then he has taken unanimous decisions over Mario Bautista and Deiveson Figueiredo, including a clean 30-27 against Figueiredo in January. He averages more than four takedowns per fifteen minutes in the UFC and has defended every takedown attempted against him apart from Dvalishvili’s. Of his 20 career wins, 11 are decisions, seven are submissions and two are knockouts — a profile that fits the market’s expectation of a controlled decision rather than a finish. Song is 23-9-1 with one no contest, arrives having submitted Figueiredo with a second-round guillotine in May, and carries nine knockout victories; the live counter-argument is that Nurmagomedov has been dropped twice in his last five bouts. One more piece of context this page had not carried: the two were first scheduled to meet in December 2024, and that booking was cancelled because of a Song injury — Saturday is a delayed matchup, not a new one. ⚠ Figueiredo appears on both men’s recent records, in two different fights; that is what the source states and the two are not merged here.

Business & broadcast — the standing blank on this page is filled. Carried · 10:45 edition Every recent edition has said that no viewership figure, gate or TKO Group financial was stated in any source fetched. One now is. TKO Group Holdings chief financial officer Andrew Schleimer told investors on the company’s second-quarter earnings call that UFC Freedom 250 — the seven-fight card staged on the White House South Lawn on June 14 — produced a loss of roughly $30 million, a figure the company had anticipated. Production ran about $60 million, and the promotion sold no tickets, seating around 4,000 invited guests instead. Schleimer tied the card directly to TKO’s adjusted EBITDA margin falling from 59 percent to 52 percent year over year, and told analysts the promotion used the event to sign partners on deals running into 2027 and beyond. Set against that, TKO president and chief operating officer Mark Shapiro said Freedom 250 generated more than $1 billion in earned media value — which is, in the reporting’s framing, the argument for doing it again.
