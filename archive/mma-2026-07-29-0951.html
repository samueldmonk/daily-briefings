Top Story

Ankalaev grinds Guskov into a fifth-round TKO — and puts himself right back in the 205 picture

Former light heavyweight champion Magomed Ankalaev (21-2-1, 1 NC entering, per UFC.com), in his first fight since losing the title to Alex Pereira last October, wore down No. 9-ranked Bogdan Guskov — the short-notice replacement for the injured Khalil Rountree Jr. — for a TKO via punches at 2:41 of Round 5, referee Marc Goddard waving it off as Ankalaev rained ground-and-pound (Bloody Elbow/Yahoo). The early rounds were a staid kickboxing match that drew boos; Ankalaev changed the fight by shooting a takedown to open the fourth and never let Guskov (18-3-1 entering) back up for long after that (Bloody Elbow). The books had him a heavy favorite to the end — CBS Sports' fight-day line was −550/+410 — and the finish keeps the former champ at the front of the post-Ulberg contender queue.

In the co-main, Ramazan Temirov made a statement — he dropped former flyweight title challenger Steve Erceg with the first punch he threw and added two knockdowns in seconds late in the round, a KO at 4:21 of Round 1 (Cageside Press). Earlier, Valter Walker submitted Thomas Petersen with a first-round calf slicer, debuting Muhammad Saidov blew away veteran Dustin Jacoby, Sam Patterson stopped Santiago Ponzinibbio, and No. 8 heavyweight Rizvan Kuniev ground down Tyrell Fortune for a third-round TKO (MyMMANews/Cageside Press). One casualty: the Islam Dulatov–Wellington Turman bout was cancelled after Dulatov fell ill (MyMMANews/LowKick). The bonus checks are in (per Heavy): Kuniev–Fortune took Fight of the Night at $100,000 apiece, Temirov and Walker each earned $100,000 Performance of the Night awards, and five more finishers — Ankalaev, Saidov, Patterson, Sola and Abdul Hussein — collected $25,000 finish bonuses.

Fight Week — Upcoming Cards

Sat Aug 1 · Belgrade Arena, Belgrade

UFC Fight Night: Medić vs Rodriguez

The first UFC event ever held in Serbia. Hometown welterweight Uroš Medić (13-3) is on a three-fight win streak with all three wins coming by first-round knockout, including a destruction of Geoff Neal at UFC Houston that earned a $100,000 performance bonus and a spot in the welterweight rankings; Daniel Rodriguez (20-5) is also on a three-fight streak but has not competed since a July 2025 win over Kevin Holland. Jan Blachowicz vs Navajo Stirling is the co-main. Main card 1:00 PM ET, prelims 10:00 AM ET on Paramount+ (Yahoo Sports/MMA Mania/FIGHTMAG/UFC.com). Odds: Medić is a heavy favorite, with books varying — −315 per VSiN's preview, DraftKings −360 with Rodriguez back at +285 (per SportsLine) — and the line opened as wide as −500/+375 (MMAOddsBreaker/Fight Matrix; re-checked this run).

Sat Aug 8 · UFC Apex, Las Vegas

UFC Vegas 120: Gamrot vs Salkilld

No. 8 lightweight Mateusz Gamrot welcomes unbeaten 5-0 Quillan Salkilld, owner of four first-round KOs and four Performance bonuses — the Australian's biggest step up yet (MMA Mania schedule/Yahoo). The finalized card also features Darren Elkins' farewell fight (Sherdog).

Sat Aug 15 · Xfinity Mobile Arena, Philadelphia

UFC 330: Makhachev vs Machado Garry

Welterweight champ Islam Makhachev defends against Ian Machado Garry as he chases Anderson Silva's 16-fight win-streak record; co-main is Mackenzie Dern's first strawweight title defense vs Gillian Robertson. Odds: Makhachev −320 / Machado Garry +260 (Bovada); DraftKings −310. Dern −205 / Robertson +170.

Sat Aug 22 · Sacramento

UFC Fight Night: Hernandez vs Rodrigues

The West Coast swing continues a packed August: Belgrade, Vegas, Philadelphia, Sacramento, then UFC Shanghai on Aug 29 (MMA Mania/ufctime schedule listings).

Sat Oct 24 · Etihad Arena, Abu Dhabi

UFC 333

Announced during Saturday's card: the UFC returns to Abu Dhabi for a numbered event on October 24, with Abu Dhabi Showdown Week back around it. No bouts announced yet — last October's UFC 321 here was the eye-poke-marred Aspinall–Gane title fight, and an Aspinall–Gane unification rematch at the same venue is the speculation (Cageside Press).

Prospect Watch

Magomed Zaynukov Prospect

The undefeated Contender Series grad won his long-awaited UFC debut Saturday in Abu Dhabi, sweeping the scorecards 30-27 over fellow unbeaten newcomer Damian Rzepecki in a bloody two-way battle — landing hard elbows and takedowns while fighting through an apparent leg issue (SSBCrack News/UFC.com).

Quillan Salkilld Prospect

Unbeaten 5-0 Australian lightweight with four first-round KOs and four Performance bonuses; last out, he TKO'd Beneil Dariush in Round 1 at UFC Perth (May 2). Faces Gamrot in the Aug 8 Vegas headliner — a genuine step-up fight.

Islam Dulatov Prospect

The Contender Series standout's return ended before it began — his bout with Wellington Turman was cancelled Saturday due to illness (MyMMANews). He scored a first-round KO in his lone UFC appearance last year.
