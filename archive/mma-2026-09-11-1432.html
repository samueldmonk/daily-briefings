Fight Week in Glendale: Jean Silva Headlines a Reshuffled Noche UFC

“It’s not the original main event scheduled, but this year’s Noche UFC headliner still has some zing to it,” Cageside Press wrote Friday as the fighters hit the scale in Arizona. Brazilian featherweight Jean Silva returns for a second straight Noche UFC, entering off a win over Arnold Allen; originally booked opposite Yair Rodriguez, “Lord” Silva instead draws the up-and-coming Jose Delgado, who took the fight on short notice.

Silva (17-3) sits No. 6 at 145 pounds in the MMA Fighting Global Rankings, having won six of his seven UFC bouts — and he agrees with the oddsmakers who have installed him as a four-to-one favourite. For Delgado, it is the chance to establish himself as a legitimate player in the division in one night.

The card is the UFC’s first at Desert Diamond Arena, staged for Mexican Independence Day and stacked with Mexican and Latin American talent. Former flyweight champion Brandon Moreno tries to snap a two-fight skid against The Ultimate Fighter 33 winner Joseph Morales in the co-main. But the most consequential bout may be Manon Fiorot vs. Alexa Grasso: both women arrive off stoppage wins, and Cageside Press notes the winner could be next in line for whoever is crowned champion between Natalia Silva and Wang Cong.

Prelims air at 2 PM ET / 11 AM PT, with the main card following at 5 PM ET / 2 PM PT.
