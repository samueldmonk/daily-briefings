Top Story

“We need to bring the money to the show”: Wang Cong calls out Silva at the UFC 332 presser

Natália Silva and Wang Cong closed Thursday’s UFC 332 press conference in Salt Lake City with an intense faceoff, but the bigger moment came when Wang held Silva’s feet to the fire for not doing more to sell their vacant women’s flyweight title fight — she even wrote Silva a note hoping to spice up the ceremonial weigh-in faceoff. “I had my plan let’s heat up the faceoff for the [ceremonial weigh-ins] but she didn’t take a look at it,” Wang said. “We need to bring the money to the show. We need make people hyped. She should do that.”

Silva isn’t biting: “If she wants to get cute, she can get cute by herself. It doesn’t matter. I’m not here to play. This is my dream, I’m here to fight. I’m here to enjoy it” (MMA Fighting via Yahoo Sports).

Official weigh-ins are Friday in Salt Lake City, with 28 fighters stepping on the scale (MMA Knockout on SI). Start times differ by outlet: MMA Fighting lists the official weigh-ins at 11 a.m. ET and the ceremonial weigh-ins at 6 p.m. ET, while MMA Knockout on SI had listed 12:00 PM ET; Silva and Wang must both make 125 pounds or less for the title fight (MMA Fighting via Yahoo Sports). MMA Mania’s live weigh-in page (via Yahoo Sports) says the official weigh-ins stream from the promotion’s host hotel starting at 11 a.m. ET and are expected to last roughly two hours; the Pete Suazo Utah Athletic Commission allows a one-pound allowance in non-title fights, but championship contests “must return exact weights, no exceptions.” New Re-read at about 12:10 PM ET, the live results pages from MMA Mania, MMA Fighting and Cageside Press still listed every bout without weights, so this edition prints none until official numbers are posted (MMA Mania via Yahoo Sports). MMA Fighting notes the bantamweight co-main between two-time flyweight champion Deiveson Figueiredo and Payton Talbott is non-title, so both need to weigh 136 pounds or less (MMA Fighting via Yahoo Sports). Cageside Press notes the card has been “much maligned since it was announced,” but it will crown just the third UFC women’s flyweight champion not named Valentina Shevchenko, who vacated the title due to injury (Cageside Press).

Prospect Watch

Prospect

TUF 34 winners crowned

Valesca “Tina Black” Machado stopped Melissa Amaya by second-round TKO in the women’s strawweight final, and Ilimbek Akylbek uulu won the bantamweight final by disqualification after an illegal knee from Mehemmedeli Osmanli (The Big Lead via Yahoo Sports; LowKick MMA).

Prospect

Rosas Jr., 21, cracks the top 15

Rosas came from behind on the scorecards to stop Raoni Barcelos in the fifth round of his first main event and earn Fight of the Night (LowKick MMA). The sixth straight win moves him to 13-1 (7-1 UFC) and into Sherdog’s bantamweight rankings at No. 14, with Barcelos (22-6) dropping to No. 15 (Sherdog).

Prospect

Luis Hernandez wins debut in 57 seconds

The Miami police officer, a late-notice replacement, choked out Sedriques Dumas with a standing guillotine and took a $100,000 Performance of the Night bonus (LowKick MMA; The Big Lead via Yahoo Sports).

Prospect

Four contracts from Contender Series Week 8, led by George Staines

Dana White signed all four winners: Staines, an undefeated lightweight from Oktagon MMA and Cage Warriors, who finished Loai Abushaar with a slam and punches in the first round — White called it “one of the greatest finishes ever”; heavyweight Adama Diop, who rallied from early trouble to stop Zaurbek Sabanov in the second round, though White said he was on the fence; featherweight Ian Escuza, whose first-round TKO of Luca Borando included an illegal knee to a downed opponent that the commission ruled did not affect the finish; and women’s strawweight Aieza Bertolso, who submitted Camila Reynoso in the first round (Heavy via Yahoo Sports; Cageside Press; Bloody Elbow).
