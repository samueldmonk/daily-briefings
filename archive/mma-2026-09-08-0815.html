Top Story

Parnasse needed 2:35 of his first UFC fight to justify the contract — and he wants Holloway next

The UFC signed Salahdine Parnasse to an exclusive contract this summer, a signing described at the time as a splash: the 28-year-old Frenchman is a former two-division champion in KSW, one of the biggest promotions in Europe. The promotion put him straight into a main event on debut, at home, against a ranked opponent.

It lasted 2:35. At UFC Paris on 5 September at the Accor Arena, Parnasse landed a body kick around halfway through the opening round that got Dan Hooker’s attention, then followed with a flurry of strikes against the fence to send Hooker to the canvas. He collected a $100,000 Performance of the Night bonus for it.

What he asked for afterwards. Parnasse said he is targeting anyone in the division’s top five, with one name in particular: “My dream fight is Max Holloway.” He added, “I’m waiting for the next fight. Top five.” A call-out is not a booking — nothing read here indicates the UFC has matched or scheduled it.
