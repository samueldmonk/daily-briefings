Noche UFC 4 is live in Glendale — thirteen bouts, all on weight, and not one official result posted at build time

The UFC's fourth annual Noche UFC celebration of Mexican Independence Day is under way at Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona — the venue's first time hosting the event. Also carded as UFC Fight Night 288, it airs at a special time: prelims from 2 PM ET, main card at 5 PM ET, on Paramount+ in the United States. Sherdog's weigh-in report confirms all fighters made weight.

The card is thirteen bouts — seven prelims and six on the main card. That number is settled at two independent sources that enumerate rather than summarise: Sherdog's bout-by-bout play-by-play listing, which names all thirteen with weigh-in figures, and a second outlet's card preview headlined "Thirteen Fights." The official event page lists the seven prelims only, so it corroborates that half and no more. The "sixteen" figure carried in earlier editions is retired and appears nowhere on this page but in this sentence.

At the top, fan-favourite Jean Silva (145) meets Jose Miguel Delgado (145.5) over five rounds at featherweight. Delgado took the main event as a short-notice replacement for the injured Yair Rodríguez, and enters on close wins over Austin Bashi and Andre Fili. Silva is coming off a narrow win over Arnold Allen, his only fight since being finished by Diego Lopes at last year's Noche UFC. The books make Silva a clear favourite: −430 at FanDuel with Delgado +320, and −425 / +330 at DraftKings. Silva by KO/TKO is −145; Delgado's shortest path is also KO/TKO, at +700.

No result is published on this page, because none exists in any source read this run. Both live pages were fetched during the prelims: UFC.com's "Prelim Results | Noche UFC" carries all seven prelim bouts as matchup previews with no winner, method or round, and Sherdog's play-by-play shows an "Official Result" heading under every one of the thirteen bouts with all of them blank. See the refusal note below — a search result this run asserted seven prelim winners, and tracing it to source showed where that came from.

Refused This Run

A fabricated result set, traced to its source

A general search for tonight's results returned all seven prelim winners, stated flatly and in the correct bout order. Every one was refused, because the two live pages fetched minutes earlier both showed blank result fields. Following the claim to its source explains it.

The winners came from a page titled "Noche UFC Prediction, Results and Highlights," headlined "What AI Predicted and What Happened?" and published at 2:15 PM ET today — fifteen minutes into the prelims. The page contains no result at all. It contains a prediction model's output: a table giving Regina Tarin, unanimous decision, 55% winner confidence, 47% method confidence, with the note "This is a coin-flip tier call." The "29-28, 29-28, 29-28 unanimous decision" that came back as a result is the model's projected method.

Two things made it read as a result. The page is written throughout in the past tense — "Aldrich entered 15-7," "Tarin entered 8-0" — which is a convention for pre-fight tale-of-the-tape copy, not a report of a completed fight. And the headline and URL both carry the word "results" for search placement on a page published before any result existed.

The durable lesson is that a page can be titled "Results," dated today, written in the past tense, and contain only a forecast. Tense and headline are not evidence. The only acceptable check is a result field on a live page that is actually filled in. Two were checked and both were empty.
