Top Story

UFC 332 has lost its main event and has a month to find another one

Valentina Shevchenko has withdrawn from UFC 332 with an undisclosed injury, taking with her the women's flyweight title defence against No. 1 contender Natalia Silva and leaving the October 3 card at the Delta Center in Salt Lake City without a headliner. Shevchenko remains the champion; she is simply off the card.

The reported replacement is Natalia Silva vs. Wang Cong for an interim women's flyweight championship. Nothing is official. UFC CEO Dana White has said the main event will be announced this week, and that has been the position for several days now.

Into that vacuum stepped Cris Cyborg, who has publicly offered to save the card by headlining a rematch with Amanda Nunes. It is a call-out rather than a booking — but it is the loudest thing attached to UFC 332 at the moment, which is itself the story.

Natalia Silva is the women's flyweight contender. She is not Jean Silva, who headlines Noche UFC on September 12.

Title fight
Sat, Sep 19 · Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles

UFC 331: Joshua Van vs. Alexandre Pantoja 2

A flyweight title rematch, with 13 fights confirmed. The first meeting, in the UFC 323 co-main event, lasted 26 seconds — then-champion Pantoja dislocated his elbow, and Van (17-2) became champion in one of the more unfortunate title changes in recent memory. Van has since defended once, knocking out Tatsuro Taira in the fifth round at UFC 328.

Co-main: No. 2-ranked lightweight Arman Tsarukyan vs. No. 7 Mauricio Ruffy over five rounds, non-title. Also Patrício Pitbull vs. Choi Doo-ho, Renato Moicano vs. Brian Ortega, Alonzo Menifield vs. Iwo Baraniewski. Prelims 6:00 PM ET, main card 9:00 PM ET.
