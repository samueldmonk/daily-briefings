The Lead

Pre-market, as of ~8:46 a.m. ET: Nasdaq futures +0.9%, S&P +0.25%, Dow −0.2% as chips rebound and Apple slides on Election Day

The latest verified snapshot is from before the 9:30 a.m. ET open — the live quotes above show the session itself. On Yahoo Finance’s futures strip at about 8:46 a.m. ET, Nasdaq futures were up 0.91%, S&P 500 futures up 0.25%, Russell 2000 futures up 0.53% and Dow futures down 0.23% — a turn higher from about 7:13 a.m., when Dow futures were −0.94% and S&P futures −0.19%. Yahoo’s quote chips showed AMD +4.05% and Intel +3.99% ahead of the open, after “a turbulent day for chip stocks” on Monday (Yahoo Finance). Bloomberg described futures as struggling for direction as investors held back from adding risk before the midterm vote (Bloomberg, via search).

The biggest single-stock story is Apple. After beating on fiscal fourth-quarter revenue ($114.48 billion) and EPS ($2.10), it guided December-quarter gross margin to 45%–46%, which CFO Kevan Parekh tied primarily to higher memory costs, and Greater China revenue of $17.54 billion fell short of the $18.75 billion expected (Yahoo Finance). TipRanks put the street’s margin estimate at 46.9% (TipRanks). Apple shares were quoted at $328.93 (−2.78%) in overnight trading at 2:10 a.m. ET, and Yahoo’s quote showed them down about 4.6% before the open (Yahoo Finance; Yahoo Finance). Palantir also fell despite a beat-and-raise (Yahoo Finance).

Monday’s close set the backdrop: Dow +0.41%, S&P 500 −0.51%, Nasdaq −1.07% as chip and AI names sold off and front-month WTI fell 8% (Kiplinger). At ~8:46 a.m. ET Yahoo showed Dec WTI down 1.98% at $94.55, the 10-year yield at 4.900% and the VIX down 2.98% at 20.52 (Yahoo Finance). Ahead today: the September JOLTS report, AMD earnings after the bell and, tonight, election results that Yahoo says investors will parse for policy signals on pharmaceuticals, housing and AI data centers (Yahoo Finance).
