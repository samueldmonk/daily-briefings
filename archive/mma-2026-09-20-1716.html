Top story

The heavyweight belt changed hands on a microphone — and Gane’s first defence is already booked

During the UFC 331 main-card broadcast on Saturday 19 September, the UFC announced that Ciryl Gane has been elevated from interim to undisputed heavyweight champion. Gane did not fight on 19 September; the belt moved by announcement, after Tom Aspinall vacated it while still recovering from complications of the eye poke he suffered against Gane at UFC 321. Gane is a two-time interim champion.

The promotion landed less than 24 hours after Dana White had said publicly that Gane would not be elevated without fighting again. The reversal is part of the story, not a footnote to it.

His first defence is set: UFC 334, Saturday 14 November, Madison Square Garden, against the undefeated Josh Hokit — 10-0 as a professional and 4-0 in the UFC, with stoppages of Derrick Lewis and Denzel Freeman and a decision over Curtis Blaydes at UFC 327, three of those wins inside six months. He arrived through the Contender Series in 2025. He is also the fighter Dana White has said he dreads seeing win the title — “I got a lot of thoughts” — after Hokit disparaged Michelle Obama in a post-fight interview on the South Lawn of the White House, which drew White’s own criticism.

Bonuses and payouts

More than $500,000 in bonuses was paid. Fight of the Night went to Van and Pantoja, $100,000 each. Performance of the Night went to Arman Tsarukyan and Casey O’Neill, $100,000 each. Separately, the $50,000 “Proper Chaos” / MobLand bonus went to Sean Sharaf for the 12-second knockout, and Dana White matched it on the spot for $100,000 total — reportedly to help clear a $688,000 debt.

Disclosed purses: Pantoja $650,000, the evening’s largest despite the loss; Van $500,000; Steveson $160,000 for a twelve-second defeat. The event payroll was reported at roughly $4.6 million.
