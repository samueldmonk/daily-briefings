Top Story

Van defends against the man he took the belt from — and the line is almost a coin flip

UFC 331 lands Saturday at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, with Joshua Van putting the flyweight title on the line against Alexandre Pantoja, the champion he beat for it at UFC 323 on 6 December 2025. Covers has Van a −130 favourite against Pantoja at +110 — a 56% implied probability, which is about as close to even as a title rematch gets.

The co-main is the one contenders are watching: Arman Tsarukyan (−290) against Mauricio Ruffy (+235) over five rounds at lightweight, in a division whose belt changed hands in June. Patricio Pitbull also features on the card. The main card starts at 9 p.m. ET on Paramount+.

Michael Chandler has said publicly that he expects a big upset on the card; he does not name the bout in anything fetched this run, so none is attributed to him here.
