Top Story

Tsarukyan takes the eliminator, then calls the champion out — and Dana White will not confirm the shot

Arman Tsarukyan secured the No. 1 contender position at lightweight with a first-round knockout of Mauricio Ruffy in the final seconds of the round at UFC 331 on Saturday 19 September, a bout billed as a title eliminator. He did not wait for the microphone to cool. At the post-fight press conference he said champion Justin Gaethje is ducking him — “he’s trying to avoid me” — arguing that Gaethje has never said his name and had been counting on Ruffy to remove the problem. In follow-up coverage read this run he put it as a choice: fight me or “leave the title”.

What he has not got is a booking. UFC president Dana White would not confirm a title shot for Tsarukyan after UFC 331, with Gaethje’s next move unsettled. One report read this run says the promotion has a Gaethje–Ilia Topuria rematch ready to go — that is reproduced as that outlet’s claim, not as a confirmed booking; Gaethje took the belt from Topuria by fourth-round TKO at Freedom 250 in June.

The other live storyline out of the weekend is settled by comparison. Ciryl Gane is the undisputed heavyweight champion after Tom Aspinall vacated on 14 September with ongoing complications from an eye injury caused by an illegal poke from Gane at UFC 321 in October 2025, and he defends against Josh Hokit at UFC 334, Madison Square Garden, Saturday 14 November — the promotion’s 13th visit to New York City and its first since UFC 322. The co-main is Kayla Harrison vs. Amanda Nunes for the women’s bantamweight title.

The Tsarukyan quote is reproduced in the short form carried in headlines across several outlets read this run. A longer transcription of the same answer appeared in one summary in visibly garbled form; it is not reproduced here.

Rankings & Business

  • Rankings movement. Arman Tsarukyan is described in coverage read this run as having secured the No. 1 contender position at lightweight with his UFC 331 knockout; no official ranking release was stated, so nothing further is asserted. The two moves the results themselves force are at the top: heavyweight has a champion again after a vacancy, and women’s flyweight is contested for a vacant belt on 3 October.

  • Broadcast. UFC 332 on 3 October is the first numbered-event main card to air on CBS, with prelims on Paramount+ at 4 PM ET and the main card at 8 PM ET. No viewership figures were stated in sources read this run and none are printed.
