Top story

Seventeen straight: Makhachev passes Anderson Silva on a sold-out Philadelphia night

Islam Makhachev defended the UFC welterweight title against Ian Machado Garry at UFC 330 on Saturday, August 15, taking a unanimous decision — 49-46, 49-46, 48-47 — at the Xfinity Mobile Arena. It was his 17th consecutive win inside the Octagon, a new all-time record that passes Anderson Silva's 16, and it made him the fifth two-division champion to defend a belt in both divisions. He moves to 29-1; Machado Garry falls to 17-2.

Underneath him, Mackenzie Dern made the first successful defence of the women's strawweight title, outworking Gillian Robertson through the first four rounds for a unanimous decision. Zhang Weili is the No. 1 contender at strawweight.

The night's business matched the sport: a sell-out with 19,238 in attendance and a gate of roughly $6.7 million, making UFC 330 the highest-grossing event in the arena's history — beating the building record by about $2.2 million. It was the promotion's first Philadelphia card in seven years.

Prospect watch

Prospect

Quillan Salkilld — into the top 10

The Australian lightweight enters ESPN's divisional rankings at No. 10, taking the place of the man he beat: a first-round submission of Mateusz Gamrot in his first UFC main event, at UFC Fight Night in Las Vegas on August 8. He is 6-0 in the UFC, and the win earned his third $100,000 bonus of 2026.

Prospect

Alexia Thainara — strawweight No. 8

She enters the women's strawweight rankings at No. 8 after a unanimous decision over former title challenger Amanda Lemos, extending her unbeaten Octagon streak to four. Lemos slips three places to No. 9.

Roster move

DWCS Week 1 signings — and the first is already booked

Dana White handed contracts to four of the five Week 1 winners from Dana White's Contender Series season 10 — a group that includes training partners of Daniel Cormier and Ilia Topuria. UFC.com names the four as Anthony Wint, Bilal Hasan, Tom Pagliarulo and Joseph Kropschot. Hasan is already booked for his promotional debut at UFC Shanghai against Nilson Rojas, days after earning the contract. Week 2 runs tonight.

Around the sport

  • Arman Tsarukyan is back, and it is Ruffy. After the Charles Oliveira booking collapsed, Tsarukyan (23-3 MMA, 10-2 UFC) is officially in the five-round lightweight co-main of UFC 331 opposite Mauricio Ruffy (14-2 MMA, 5-1 UFC). It is Tsarukyan's first fight since his submission win over Dan Hooker in November 2025; Ruffy takes his first five-rounder off back-to-back TKOs of Rafael Fiziev and former title challenger Michael Chandler. Reporting links Tsarukyan's earlier withdrawal to the death of his longtime teammate, UFC flyweight Allan Nascimento.
  • Kayla Harrison will fight again in 2026. The women's bantamweight champion has been confirmed to return before the end of the year after the neck injury that sidelined her and forced the cancellation of a planned title defence against Amanda Nunes — a bout that had been targeted for UFC 331 on September 19 in Los Angeles.
  • Contender Series Week 2 is tonight at the Meta APEX, 7:00 PM ET on Paramount+, headlined by Namo Fazil vs. Kaik Brito at welterweight. Week 1's four contract winners were Anthony Wint, Bilal Hasan, Tom Pagliarulo and Joseph Kropschot.
  • Edson Barboza has retired. The veteran called it a career following his loss at UFC 330, where Esteban Ribovics stopped him in the second round.
  • Jalin Turner wants the BMF belt. After his 39-second knockout of Kaue Fernandes at UFC 330 — one of the night's four $100,000 Performance of the Night awards — Turner said he is interested in a BMF title fight.
  • Din Thomas has retired from cornering following UFC 330, and later apologised amid strong backlash within the MMA community.
  • Usman Nurmagomedov is on the UFC's radar. Asked whether the promotion is interested in signing him, Dana White's answer was "Of course."
  • Philadelphia delivered. UFC 330 was the first card in the city in seven years, sold out the building, and sent both champions home with their belts while a hometown fighter picked up an upset win.
