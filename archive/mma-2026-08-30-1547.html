Nurmagomedov had been a heavy favourite at every book this page recorded, as short as −625; Song had said before the fight that beating him would guarantee a title shot. UFC.com's pre-fight copy listed Nurmagomedov at 20-1, so this is the second defeat of his career — and at 10:50 AM that stopped being a derivation: a post-event report fetched this run states outright that Song “handed the Russian fighter his second professional MMA loss”. The result that moves a second division came in the co-main. Denise Gomes, the No. 14 strawweight and a +125 underdog against a −150 favourite, stopped Yan Xiaonan — No. 4 in the division and a former title challenger — by knockout with elbows and punches at 4:49 of round one. Gomes had rocked her with an overhand right roughly two minutes in; Yan came straight back at her, and the fight stayed competitive until Gomes landed the finishing sequence in the closing seconds of the round. Gomes entered on a four-fight winning streak.

Around it, the card was violent and it belonged to the newcomers. Ten of the thirteen bouts ended inside the distance — three went to the judges. Francesco Nuzzi, Hector Santiago, Bilal Hasan, Liu Ce and Cam Nelson all won in their first Octagon appearance, four of them by knockout; two of the three Road to UFC winners on the card lost their debuts.
