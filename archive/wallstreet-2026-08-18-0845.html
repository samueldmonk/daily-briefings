The lead

Oil at $91 does the talking before the bell — futures lower, long bond at a 19-year high

Markets are closed as this edition is written; the regular session opens at 9:30 AM ET, so what follows is the pre-open picture, not index levels. US stock futures fell on Tuesday as rising tensions between the US and Iran revived inflation concerns, with elevated oil prices adding to the pressure.

Two renderings of the same move, both published as sourced and not collapsed into one. Yahoo Finance's live blog (updated 5:00 AM ET) has Dow futures down 0.2%, S&P 500 futures down 0.5% and Nasdaq-100 contracts down 0.9%. Benzinga, reading the tape later in the pre-market, has the Dow contract flat, the S&P down 0.4%, the Nasdaq-100 down 1.1% and Russell 2000 futures down 0.24%. Yahoo's futures strip, timestamped about 5:08 AM ET, carried S&P futures at 7,729.25 (−39.50, −0.51%), Dow futures 53,492.00 (−52.00, −0.10%), Nasdaq futures 29,762.75 (−333.25, −1.11%) and the VIX at 15.88 (+0.69, +4.54%).

The cause is in the energy complex. Oil climbed to its highest in more than two weeks after President Trump said he intends to inflict more economic pain on Iran and threatened to "bomb" Oman if it interferes with US plans for the Strait of Hormuz. Brent crude hit $91 a barrel and WTI rose to $84, with the US Strategic Petroleum Reserve at its lowest level since 1982. Rising oil, an AI borrowing spree and concerns over government borrowing have lifted bond yields globally: the 30-year Treasury yield reached 5.31%, a 19-year high, and the 10-year sat at 4.72%.

Against that, earnings have kept supporting equities. Home Depot reported before the open and beat on both lines while reaffirming full-year guidance; Toll Brothers and Klarna are also on today's docket.

Movers & drivers

NewPre-market +1.5%

Home Depot (HD) — beat and reaffirm

Shares gained about 1.5% in pre-market trading after fiscal Q2 results topped expectations on both lines: adjusted EPS of $4.92 against $4.73 expected, revenue of $47.86 billion against $47.27 billion forecast. Comparable sales rose 1.7% and US comparable sales 1.3%. Full-year guidance was reaffirmed — adjusted diluted EPS growth of flat to 4.0% from fiscal 2025's $14.69, or $14.69 to $15.28. Earnings call at 9 AM ET.

NewPre-market −9%

Fabrinet (FN) — the day's steepest pre-market fall

Fabrinet dropped more than 9% in pre-market trading, per CNBC's morning movers list — the largest decline among the names it flagged ahead of the open.

DriverBrent $91

Crude — the session's real input

Brent hit $91 a barrel and WTI reached $84, the highest in over two weeks, after Trump's remarks on Iran and his threat to Oman over the Strait of Hormuz. Tesla, separately, dipped 1.2% pre-market. Yahoo's strip put September WTI at 84.15 (+0.41, +0.49%) around 5 AM ET.
