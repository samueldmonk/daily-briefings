Top Story

Paris belonged to the debutant

Salahdine Parnasse stopped Dan Hooker by TKO at 2:35 of round one in the UFC Paris main event on 5 September, in his promotional debut, at home, in a scheduled five-rounder. UFC.com’s own account: known for his grappling, Parnasse instead kept it standing, landed a body kick about halfway through the opening round that “got Hooker’s attention,” and followed with a flurry against the fence. The promotion writes that he “instantly enters the highly touted lightweight Top 15.”

He is not a developmental-series signing and should not be described as one. Parnasse is a two-time KSW featherweight champion and a one-time KSW lightweight champion who signed with the UFC in late July 2026, having previously turned the promotion down, and was handed a main event on debut. Hooker was ranked #10 going in.

The night as a whole did numbers: gross total revenue $4,365,335 and attendance 15,687, sold out — per UFC.com, the highest-grossing event in Accor Arena history.

Bonuses

UFC.com’s own bonus coverage awards four Performance of the Night bonuses — Parnasse, Axel Sola, Losene Keita and Mario Pinto — and no Fight of the Night. The UFC page does not state a dollar amount. The $100,000 figure comes from MMA Mania, Sherdog, LowKick and Heavy, and is reported here as theirs, not asserted. Those same outlets report additional $25,000 finishing bonuses for Delphine Benouaich, Matthieu Duclos, Modestas Bukauskas and Kurtis Campbell; a previous edition of this page dropped that claim because it appeared on no fetched page, and it is included now only because search returns this run state it explicitly — still attributed, still absent from the UFC’s own coverage.
