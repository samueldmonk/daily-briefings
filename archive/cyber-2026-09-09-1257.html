Top Story

StyleSmuggler: a CVSS 10.0 zero-day in Adobe Commerce and Magento, exploited three days before the patch

CVE-2026-75650 is an unauthenticated remote code execution flaw in Adobe Commerce, Adobe Commerce B2B and Magento Open Source, carrying a CVSSv3 score of 10.0 with scope changed — exploitation can reach beyond the vulnerable component. Sansec’s forensics team published the research on 5 September and named it StyleSmuggler; Tenable dates active exploitation to 4 September, three days before a vendor fix existed.

The bug lives in how Magento’s template engine processes style properties. An attacker injects PHP through the styles properties, Magento writes that attacker-controlled content to disk as part of normal operation, and the code executes when the platform renders the “Payment Transaction Failed Reminder” transactional email. There is no authentication gate on that path, and the technique works regardless of session storage backend.

Adobe shipped Hotfix VULN-39341 on 7 September (bulletin APSB26-146). Affected versions: Adobe Commerce 2.4.4–2.4.9, Adobe Commerce B2B 1.3.3–1.5.3, Magento Open Source 2.4.6–2.4.9. Adobe additionally recommends rotating the encryption key and every credential it protects after a compromise — admin passwords, REST/SOAP/GraphQL tokens, OAuth secrets, payment-gateway and database credentials, SSH and deploy keys, extension API keys.

Patching does not remediate an existing compromise. Stores that were online during the three-day window before the hotfix need incident response as well as the fix. Note also that Tenable’s FAQ, published 08:00 ET on 8 September, states the CVE was not yet in KEV — CISA added it later the same day, so that line is stale rather than in conflict.

Patch Priority

Do this first

Apply Adobe Hotfix VULN-39341 to every Adobe Commerce, Commerce B2B and Magento Open Source instance, then assume compromise and hunt. A CVSS 10.0 pre-auth RCE with confirmed in-the-wild exploitation, two distinct operator sets, and a persistent implant is the highest-consequence item on the board. Immediately after it: N-able N-central CVE-2026-86218 (CVSS 10.0, unauthenticated RCE on the N-central server process) — fixed in 2026.3 Hotfix 4, build 2026.3.1.14.

No federal deadline is asserted for either. The CISA alert page for the 8 September batch returned an empty body on fetch this run, so no due date was read first-hand. Search snippets attribute an 11 September due date to CVE-2026-75650 in one place and to CVE-2026-86218 in another, which cannot both be a correct reading of a single CISA statement. The old flat three-week BOD 22-01 arithmetic is also no longer safe to apply, because BOD 26-04 sets risk-tiered remediation deadlines instead. A deadline that cannot be read is not a deadline this page will print.

Threat Actor Spotlight

Unattributed
StyleSmuggler operators

Two separate crews on the same victim stores

There is no public attribution to a named group. Sansec documented at least two distinct operators hitting the same compromised stores with materially different tooling, and treats them as separate campaigns. The first deploys a persistent Linux implant at ~/.local/share/.gvfsd/gvfsd-user that masquerades as a kernel thread ([kworker/u:8:0]) and as the legitimate utilities fc-cache and chronyd, restarted by cron every five minutes — later variants moved to twice an hour — with x86-64 and arm64 builds. The second simply drops a PHP web shell into the product image cache. As of 8 September there was no standalone public proof-of-concept.

Breaches & Incidents

NewIdentity data

FBI probes “Nexus” selling 153 million driver’s licence scans

A dark-web service that surfaced on a Russian cybercrime forum on 31 August offered scans of more than 153 million driver’s licences, 10 million ID cards, 3 million travel documents and 579,000 medical cards covering the U.S. and Canada. Its operators claimed the material came from an ongoing breach at an identity-verification provider; Krebs on Security names IDScan.net. The FBI’s New Orleans field office has opened a formal investigation. Nexus went offline shortly after Krebs published, leaving the message that the service was no longer available.

NewRansomware

An intrusion run end-to-end by AI agents

A ransomware operator used frontier models and agentic attack frameworks to breach an enterprise network in under 10 hours — work The Register reports would normally take human operators around two weeks. AI agents carried out every step of the intrusion, and the attacker left the victim an 80-page security audit.

NewRansomware

Panzer: a new RaaS with 16 victims in 11 countries

A newly identified ransomware-as-a-service operation has claimed 16 victims across 11 countries since its leak site went live on 5 August 2026, spanning technology, manufacturing, government, agriculture, energy, education and retail — technology (4) and manufacturing (3) worst hit. Both data theft and encryption are involved.

CISA KEV & Federal Deadlines

  • 8 September — four added. CVE-2026-75650 (Adobe Commerce/Magento), CVE-2026-81963 (Windows link following), CVE-2026-85880 (Windows heap overflow), CVE-2026-86218 (N-able N-central static code injection). No due date verified this run — no countdown printed. The CISA alert page returned an empty body on fetch, and the two second-hand due-date claims found conflict with each other.
  • 4 September — one added. CVE-2026-85046, Google Chromium V8 type confusion. No due date verified this run.
  • 2 September — seven added. Covering Sangoma Switchvox, Kludex Starlette, Kestra OSS, BerriAI LiteLLM, JFrog Artifactory and SonicWall SMA1000 appliances. No due date verified this run.
  • Why there are no countdowns on this page today. Previous editions ran a three-week clock from the add date under BOD 22-01. BOD 26-04 has since replaced that flat window with remediation deadlines tiered by risk, so the arithmetic that produced those countdowns no longer holds, and CISA’s own pages could not be read this run to supply the real dates. Rather than print a plausible number, this section prints none. Operationally, treat both CVSS 10.0 flaws as due now.
  • Volume check. Twelve CVEs entered the catalogue in the last seven days — a heavy week by any measure.
