Salkilld shocks Gamrot: first-round rear-naked choke crowns a $300K bonus year

Quillan Salkilld made his first UFC main event a short night. The Australian lightweight submitted #8-ranked Mateusz Gamrot with a first-round rear-naked choke at UFC Vegas 120 on Saturday at the Meta Apex in Las Vegas (MMA Mania, Yahoo Sports), running his UFC record to 6-0. The finish earned him Performance of the Night — his third $100,000 post-fight bonus of 2026, pushing his calendar-year award earnings to $300,000 (MMA Mania).

Salkilld entered as a −142 DraftKings favorite despite the ranking gap, and UFC.com's preview had already framed the winner as a title-eliminator candidate in the lightweight division under champion Justin Gaethje.
