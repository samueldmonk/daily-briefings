Top Story

Noche UFC lost its main event — and got a hometown headliner instead

The fourth annual Noche UFC takes over Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona tonight, on Mexican Independence Day weekend. It is not the main event the UFC booked. Jean Silva (17-3) was scheduled to face former interim featherweight champion Yair Rodríguez; Rodríguez withdrew injured, and in stepped Phoenix’s own Jose Miguel Delgado (12-2) — rendered “Jose Miguel Delgado” on UFC.com’s own event and preview pages, and shortened to “Jose Delgado” elsewhere.

Delgado has never headlined and has never faced a ranked opponent. Silva is No. 6 at 145 lb, has won six of his seven UFC fights, and has finished twelve of his seventeen wins by knockout. The books agree emphatically: across six sportsbooks read this run Silva is priced between −425 and −455, with Delgado between +320 and +355.

The rest of the card is deep for a Fight Night. Former flyweight champion Brandon Moreno meets Joseph Morales in the co-main; former women’s flyweight champion Alexa Grasso faces Manon Fiorot; and Curtis Blaydes takes on Waldo Cortes-Acosta at heavyweight. Sixteen fights are scheduled in all, on Paramount+ with no pay-per-view.
