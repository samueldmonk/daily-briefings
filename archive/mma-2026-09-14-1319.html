Top Story

Fight week: the flyweight champion opens as the underdog in his own title defence

UFC 331: Van vs. Pantoja 2 is five days out — Saturday 19 September, Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles — and the opening line has the champion on the wrong side of it. Per MMA Mania’s opening-odds report citing BetOnline, Joshua Van is listed at −105 and Alexandre Pantoja enters as a −115 favourite. A separate headline read this run, from boxingnews.com, states the same thing in words: “Van opens as underdog against Pantoja at UFC 331.”

A third reading is on file from a previous edition — a DraftKings opener of Van +100 / Pantoja −120. All three are printed and none averaged; what all three agree on is the direction. These are opening numbers, not a live line.

Van won the belt from Pantoja at UFC 323 on 6 December 2025 by first-round TKO. Elsewhere on the card, Arman Tsarukyan is a sizeable favourite at −380 over Mauricio Ruffy in a lightweight bout with title implications, and Olympic gold medallist Gable Steveson carries the heaviest line on the card at −1500 against Sean Sharaf (+600).

DWCS alum

Mike Malott, the Contender Series graduate, gets a headline slot

UFC.com’s Edmonton release describes Malott as a Dana White’s Contender Series signee with wins over Gilbert Burns, Kevin Holland and Charles Radtke — a useful reminder of where the 17 October main event came from, and of why the Burns name attached itself to the card in the first place.

Around the Sport

  • The 17 October name dispute is closed. This page has printed both Buckley vs. Malott (schedule listings) and “UFC Fight Night: Burns vs. Malott” (a Wikipedia event title) for two editions without adopting either. UFC.com’s own event page and its own news release both say Buckley vs. Malott, and Rogers Place and ESPN agree. The promotion’s primary listing wins; the Wikipedia title is noted as the contradicted reading rather than silently dropped.
  • Buckley’s résumé, per UFC.com: wins over Colby Covington, Stephen Thompson and Vicente Luque. He is ranked No. 10 at welterweight; Malott is No. 13.

  • Jean Silva’s title call has no booking behind it. He demanded a shot in the cage on Saturday. Nothing read this run states a featherweight title fight has been made, so none is reported here.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. Since 20 June 2026 the UFC has been replacing its traditional rankings with the Meta UFC Rankings, an Elo-based system weighting opponent quality, activity and decay. The most recent release on file is 5 September 2026. No post-Noche release was found this run, so no movement from Saturday’s card is claimed. The only rankings numbers published here are the ones UFC.com states directly for the Edmonton main event: Buckley No. 10, Malott No. 13.

Business & broadcast. UFC 332 on 3 October will be the first numbered UFC event with its main card on CBS — a distribution change that matters more than most of the bouts on it. DWCS Season 10 continues on Paramount+, as does UFC 331.
