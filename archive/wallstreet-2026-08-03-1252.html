The Lead

Iran de-escalation knocks crude down 7% and hands August bulls the open

Stocks opened the month broadly higher. On a Yahoo Finance board reading late Monday morning (roughly 11:10 AM ET), the Nasdaq Composite led at 25,801.98, up 428.13 points or 1.69%; the S&P 500 stood at 7,573.53, up 83.81 or 1.12%; and the Dow at 53,014.36, up 529.33 or 1.01%. The Russell 2000 was up 1.62% and the VIX had eased 1.69% to 15.72. A midday read from 24/7 Wall St. put the S&P's gain at 1.3%.

The catalyst was geopolitical relief. President Trump said he called off a planned attack against Iran in order to negotiate a deal to open the Strait of Hormuz, with talks resuming Monday. "Get to work, everybody, and get it DONE," he posted on Truth Social. Traders unwound last week's energy risk premium accordingly: Brent fell about 6% to roughly $82.95 and WTI dropped nearly 7% to $78.93 in early trading, taking pressure off the inflation narrative and letting the 10-year Treasury yield fall about 7 basis points to near 4.67%.

The data was cooperative too. ISM's manufacturing purchasing managers' index rose 2.3 percentage points above June's figure to an expansionary 55.6%. Attention now turns to a heavy earnings week — Palantir reports Monday, and SpaceX delivers its first quarterly report since going public with the stock hugging an all-time low — before Friday's July jobs report.

Movers & Drivers

New▲ Gainer

Amazon (AMZN)

Added 5.5%, pushing its market capitalisation above $3 trillion, as investors kept rewarding Friday's AWS beat, accelerating AI demand and expanding retail margins. The follow-through extends what was already its biggest single-day gain in over a decade.

New▲ AI Infrastructure

CoreWeave (CRWV) & the AI cloud complex

CoreWeave was the session's biggest mover, up 11% on a 24/7 Wall St. read and 15% on CNBC's midday movers list ahead of its Q2 report, as AI data-centre demand expectations firmed. Nebius advanced more than 13%, Snowflake gained 6%, Oracle climbed 5%, and Vertiv — which makes cooling systems for data centres — popped nearly 7%.

New▼ Decliner

GameStop (GME)

Tumbled 12.59% to $18.99, the day's sharpest single-name decline, after announcing a private exchange of $1.4 billion in convertible senior notes for equity.

NewM&A

AstraZeneca (AZN) / Bristol Myers Squibb (BMY)

AstraZeneca is reportedly in talks about a possible $400 billion combination with Bristol Myers Squibb — a deal that would instantly rank among the largest healthcare mergers on record. BMY traded at $65.07, off 0.38%, suggesting the market is treating the report cautiously.

New▼ Laggard

Memory & storage

The one soft corner of an otherwise broad rally. CNBC's midday movers list had Micron down about 1%, Seagate off 5% and Western Digital down more than 4%. eBay fell 4% after Wells Fargo downgraded it to underweight from equal weight.

NewCrypto

MicroStrategy (MSTR) turns seller

Announced the sale of 1,638 bitcoin at an average price of $63,957, for roughly $104.7 million — notable because MSTR is known almost exclusively as a bitcoin buyer. The move lands with the stock already down 41% this year and 83% below its all-time high. Bitcoin itself traded at $63,800.62, up 1.20%.
