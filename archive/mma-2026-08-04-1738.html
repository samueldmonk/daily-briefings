Fight Week

The oddsmakers have picked a side: Salkilld, 5-0 in the UFC, favored over No. 8 Gamrot on Saturday

Fight week is underway for UFC Fight Night: Gamrot vs. Salkilld, Saturday at the Meta Apex in Las Vegas — and the books are backing the surge. Quillan Salkilld is a −142 favorite at DraftKings, with eighth-ranked Mateusz Gamrot at +120 for the five-round lightweight main event (SportsLine). It's a striking price against a fighter of Gamrot's pedigree, and a measure of how fast the 12th-ranked Australian has moved.

Salkilld is 5-0 in the UFC, most recently flattening longtime contender Beneil Dariush with a first-round TKO in Perth on May 2. Gamrot represents a different kind of test: an elite wrestler with championship-level experience who has spent years in the division's top ten. SportsLine's analyst preview expects heavy grappling exchanges — and the betting total leans toward the fight going long.

A win puts Salkilld's name in the title conversation of a division that just changed hands: Justin Gaethje took the belt from Ilia Topuria at UFC Freedom 250 in June.

Prospect Watch

Prospect

Quillan Salkilld — lightweight

5-0 in the UFC and headlining Saturday as a favorite over a top-ten stalwart. The Australian's May 2 first-round TKO of Beneil Dariush turned a hot start into a genuine contender's résumé; oddsmakers now price him as the division's next problem.

Prospect

Navajo Stirling — light heavyweight

The City Kickboxing product followed a successful rookie campaign with stoppages of Bruno Lopes and Ion Cutelaba this year, then blasted former champion Jan Błachowicz in under three minutes in Belgrade (UFC.com). Pre-fight, UFC.com framed a win as catapulting him "into the thick of the title conversation" at 205.

Prospect

Uroš Medić — welterweight

Belgrade's hometown headliner needed 30 seconds to finish Daniel Rodriguez in his first fight in Serbia in nearly seven years. Now on a three-fight streak, UFC.com says his finishing run has carried him into the welterweight Top 15.
