Top Story

Three days to Philadelphia: Makhachev puts the belt — and a record-tying streak — on the line against Machado Garry

Saturday at Xfinity Mobile Arena, welterweight champion Islam Makhachev (28-1) makes his first title defense against top contender Ian Machado Garry (17-1), who brings five-inch height and four-inch reach advantages — and history rides along: Makhachev's 16-fight win streak is tied with Anderson Silva for the longest in UFC history (SportsLine, CBS Sports). The books have him a heavy favorite, though the line has tightened late in fight week: DraftKings now lists Makhachev −325 (Machado Garry +240), with recent lines from −325 to −340 and the challenger +240 to +270 (MMA News/OddsShark fight-week roundups, this edition) — FanDuel went as high as −390/+280 earlier in the week, when most books sat −340 to −350 (VSiN, Yahoo Sports, Covers). The challenger arrives riding back-to-back wins over Carlos Prates and former champion Belal Muhammad (MMA News). The co-feature is Mackenzie Dern's first strawweight title defense against Gillian Robertson, and the main card streams at 9 PM ET exclusively on Paramount+, with no CBS simulcast (CBS Sports, Paramount+). It is the promotion's first numbered event in Philadelphia since UFC 133 in August 2011 (Wikipedia). CBS Sports frames the stakes in G.O.A.T. terms: Makhachev is one of just nine UFC fighters to hold world titles in two different weight classes, and a win as a roughly 3-to-1 favorite Saturday would put him in the even more exclusive group of champ-champs who have defended titles at both weights — Dana White declared him "headed towards G.O.A.T. talk" after the Della Maddalena win, though Makhachev says chasing Silva's record isn't even on his radar right now.

The prospect pipeline reopened Tuesday night: Dana White's Contender Series premiered its 10th season at the Meta Apex and handed out four UFC contracts in one card. Former FIU linebacker and Jets practice-squad member Anthony Wint needed just 34 seconds to flatten Matt Adams in the heavyweight main event, and flyweight Bilal Hasan — the biggest betting favorite in Contender Series history — stopped Mridul Saikia in 45 seconds (CBS Sports, Cageside Press, MMA News). Full results are in the Last Event table below.

Also on the board: flyweight champion Joshua Van rematches Alexandre Pantoja in the main event of UFC 331 on Sept 19 at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles (UFC.com, ESPN, MMA Weekly) — a rerun of their UFC 323 title fight, which Van won by first-round TKO after the bout was cut short by an arm injury (SI, Yahoo Sports). Arman Tsarukyan also returns on that card (Al Jazeera).
