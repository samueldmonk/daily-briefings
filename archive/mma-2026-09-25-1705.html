Top Story

Fight eve: Rosas Jr. and Barcelos weigh in for both men’s first UFC main event

Weigh-ins are done for tomorrow’s UFC Vegas 121 at the Meta APEX in Las Vegas, where No. 12-ranked bantamweight Raul Rosas Jr. meets No. 13 Raoni Barcelos (UFC.com). It is the first UFC headliner for both: Barcelos gets his first main event sixteen fights into his UFC career and derailed another hot prospect, Payton Talbott, last year (Cageside Press). Both men arrive on five-fight winning streaks and prefer to grind rather than brawl (RG.org). Both headliners made weight without issue — Rosas at 136 pounds, Barcelos at 135 on the dot — and all 24 fighters made weight, including Luis Hernandez, Sedriques Dumas’ late-notice opponent, so the card goes ahead with 12 fights (Sherdog). The 21-year-old Rosas (12-1) is coming off the biggest win of his career, a decision over Rob Font; Barcelos (22-5), 18 years older at 39, has beaten Ricky Simon, Cody Garbrandt, Payton Talbott and Montel Jackson (Sherdog).

Rosas is the betting favorite: Sherdog lists Rosas −175 / Barcelos +150, other books have had Rosas −160 / Barcelos +135, and offshore books opened Rosas at −210 and Barcelos at +177 (MMAOddsBreaker). The main card also carries both finals of The Ultimate Fighter 34 — Mehemmedeli Osmanli vs. Ilimbek Akylbek uulu at bantamweight and Melissa Amaya vs. Valesca “Tina Black” Machado at women’s strawweight — plus Rodolfo Vieira vs. Robert Bryczek and Brady Hiestand vs. Rinya Nakamura. Prelims start at 5 PM ET and the main card at 8 PM ET (UFC.com).

Sources: Cageside Press (weigh-ins & card) · Sherdog (weigh-in results) · UFC.com · RG.org (odds) · RotoWire (odds)
