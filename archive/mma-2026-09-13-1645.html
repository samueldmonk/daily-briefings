Top Story

Silva called for the belt. The rankings answered, and did not move.

Jean Silva closed Noche UFC in Glendale, Arizona by submitting Jose Miguel Delgado with what the promotion calls a slick one-arm choke — a rear-naked choke at 2:57 of round three — then used the cage to demand a title shot: “Whoever holds that belt, the next champion is right here.”

The UFC has since released its first official rankings update of the Paramount+ era, and it is a useful reality check on that demand. Silva is No. 6 at featherweight, confirmed as of 12 September. But there was no movement in the featherweight top 15 in the update: Alexander Volkanovski still holds the belt and Movsar Evloev is still the No. 1 contender. Those two meet for the title on 24 October, and Silva has been explicit that he wants the winner — he has picked Evloev.

The same rankings update is reported to carry big moves for Justin Gaethje as well as Silva. No specific position change for Gaethje was stated in what was read this run, so none is printed here.
