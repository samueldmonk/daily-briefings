Top Story

Rosas Jr. wins a five-round war by late slam TKO; Barcelos stretchered out, now stable

Raul Rosas Jr. beat Raoni Barcelos by fifth-round TKO from a slam at 1:38 — the official result per MiddleEasy, citing UFC’s result and scorecard pages — in Saturday’s UFC Vegas 121 main event at the Meta APEX, capping a back-and-forth bout The Big Lead called an epic war (via Yahoo Sports). Barcelos was ahead on the judges’ scorecards heading into the finish, which came when he suddenly lost consciousness right after Rosas took him down (LowKick MMA). Barcelos was stretchered out and taken to hospital (Yahoo Sports).

In an update posted early Sunday, the UFC said Barcelos was taken immediately to a Level 1 trauma center, where a CT scan and comprehensive assessment were carried out; he is “stable, alert and responsive, with full movement” and will remain under medical supervision (UFC.com). His coach, Davi Ramos, later said “Raoni is doing well” — conscious, talking and moving his limbs (Bloody Elbow; Yahoo Sports). Both fighters earned the $100,000 Fight of the Night bonus (LowKick MMA).

In the co-main event Ailin Perez outpointed Norma Dumont by unanimous decision (29-28 x3), and debutant Luis Hernandez, Sedriques Dumas’ late-notice opponent, submitted him with a standing guillotine in 57 seconds (The Big Lead via Yahoo Sports).

Sources: The Big Lead via Yahoo Sports (results) · UFC.com (Barcelos update) · LowKick MMA (bonuses)
