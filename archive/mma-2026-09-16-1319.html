Top Story

Ortega out of UFC 331 on a freak jiu-jitsu cut; Moicano rebooked for 31 October

Brian Ortega has withdrawn from his scheduled bout with Renato Moicano at UFC 331 because of an injury — a deep cut above his left eye that required stitches and cost him his lightweight debut. Ortega described it as “a freak accident that happened doing jiu jitsu” and said he “wasn’t sparring”.

With fight week already under way three days out from Saturday’s card, no short-notice replacement could be found. Rather than match Moicano with someone new in Los Angeles, the UFC moved him entirely: he now headlines a UFC Fight Night on 31 October at the Meta APEX in Las Vegas against rising lightweight contender Tom Nolan.

Any card listing that still carries Moicano vs. Ortega for 19 September — including this page’s own earlier editions today — is stale from this point on. The bout is off.

Around the Sport

  • A new global promotion. Scott Coker and media executive Peter Y. Levin unveiled Ki MMA at a launch event in Lower Manhattan, targeting a first quarter 2027 start and backed by $60 million in financing announced in May. The inaugural 2027 Ki MMA World Grand Prix will be contested at featherweight across four eight-fighter continental tournaments — North America, Europe, Asia and South America — with each continental champion advancing to a one-night final; the eventual winner must take five bouts, two of them on the final night. All Ki MMA bouts take place in a ring. The ownership group includes skateboarder Tony Hawk and D.C. United owner Steve Kaplan; Richard Chou is head of talent relations and executive matchmaking.
  • Michael Page is out of the UFC and has called the exit “disrespectful” after a 5-1 run, per Sherdog. Jake Paul has publicly pitched bringing him to MVP.
  • Aspinall’s recovery has a timeline now. Tom Aspinall, who vacated the undisputed heavyweight title on 14 September, may require a fourth surgery if his eye has not healed by January. Demetrious Johnson said he would be “shocked” if Aspinall fights again.
  • UFC 331 main card, beyond the top two: Patricio “Pitbull” Freire vs. Doo Ho Choi at featherweight; Iwo Baraniewski vs. Alonzo Menifield at light heavyweight; and Gable Steveson vs. Sean Sharaf at heavyweight, the card’s heaviest line at −1500 / +600 on Sherdog’s 2 September board.
  • Alexa Grasso is the clubhouse leader at 125 lb. Her decision over Manon Fiorot was a second straight win and, per the official recap, sets her up as the leading candidate for the next flyweight title shot — while the vacant belt itself is contested at UFC 332 on 3 October.
  • Off the clock: Alexander Volkanovski choked out 6-foot-8 Philadelphia Eagles offensive lineman Jordan Mailata in a training clip, per Sherdog.
