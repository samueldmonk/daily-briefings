Top Story

Fight week in Los Angeles: Van–Pantoja 2 has turned into a coin toss

UFC 331 runs Saturday, 19 September, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, headlined by a flyweight title rematch: reigning champion Joshua Van against former champion Alexandre Pantoja. Van took the belt from Pantoja at UFC 323 in December 2025, winning by technical knockout 26 seconds into the first round after Pantoja suffered an arm injury.

The betting market has moved a long way. The main event opened with Pantoja at −200 and Van the +170 underdog; it is now effectively a pick ’em at Van −107 / Pantoja −113. The co-main carries the division’s biggest consequence: Arman Tsarukyan vs. Mauricio Ruffy over five rounds at lightweight, with the winner widely expected to face champion Justin Gaethje next. Tsarukyan is around −380, Ruffy near +300.

The main card opens at 9 p.m. ET on Paramount+, with prelims at 7 p.m. and early prelims at 5 p.m.

Around the Sport

  • The heavyweight title is vacant. Tom Aspinall relinquished the undisputed belt on 14 September, unable to obtain medical clearance after double eye surgery in February 2026, an infection in the right eye and a further accidental scrape requiring another operation. Ciryl Gane holds the interim championship, won in June at UFC Freedom 250 over Alex Pereira, and reporting holds the UFC will simply elevate him rather than stage a vacant-title bout.
  • Alexa Grasso is the clubhouse leader at 125 lb. Her unanimous-decision win over Manon Fiorot was her second straight, and UFC.com frames it as setting her up as the leading candidate for the next championship opportunity — a title Natália Silva and Wang Cong contest for the vacant version at UFC 332 on 3 October.
  • Brandon Moreno stopped the slide. The former flyweight champion entered on a two-fight skid and took a split decision from Joseph Morales in his first Noche UFC appearance.
  • Curtis Blaydes is back in the win column after a strategic unanimous decision over Waldo Cortes Acosta, and UFC.com expects him to re-claim a place in the heavyweight top five.
  • Israel Adesanya is not retiring. After a fourth consecutive defeat, he said his “ego is very tied into this; I know what I can do still.” Earlier in 2026 he put his remaining career at fewer than 10 fights and said he could not see past 2027.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. The Tsarukyan–Ruffy winner is expected to get the next lightweight title shot against Justin Gaethje. Grasso has moved to the front of the women’s flyweight queue. Blaydes is expected back inside the heavyweight top five. Jean Silva, now 7-1 in the Octagon with six finishes, made his case for a featherweight title opportunity.

Business & broadcast. UFC 331 streams on Paramount+, with the main card at 9 p.m. ET under the schedule that moved numbered events from 10 p.m. to 9 p.m. Dana White’s Contender Series Season 10 has produced 24 contracts across the season’s first half, per UFC.com.
