Top Story

Paris fight week: Dan Hooker meets Salahdine Parnasse in a main event the debutant was handed on arrival

UFC returns to the Accor Arena on Saturday, September 5 with a 14-fight card headlined by a five-round lightweight bout between Dan Hooker and Salahdine Parnasse. Prelims begin at 12 PM ET / 9 AM PT with the main card at 3 PM ET / 12 PM PT on Paramount+ in the United States — an early start dictated by the Paris clock.

Parnasse is making his UFC debut in a main event. He arrives as a former two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion, 14-2 inside that promotion with four defences of the lightweight belt, who signed with the UFC in late July 2026 having previously turned the promotion down. His U.S. debut came in May 2026 on the Rousey vs. Carano main card, a first-round stoppage of Kenneth Cross for a fifth straight win. He is a debutant — not a UFC veteran, contender or ranked fighter — and he did not come through the Contender Series.

Card change: Mairon Santos has been removed from his featherweight bout with Nathaniel Wood due to illness. Undefeated newcomer Pavel Andrusca steps in and will make his promotional debut.

Around the Sport

  • Salahdine Parnasse’s route to the UFC keeps getting mis-told. He is a KSW champion who signed directly in late July 2026, not a Contender Series graduate. This briefing has corrected that attribution and holds it.
  • Joshua Van’s title reign, in order: he took the belt at UFC 323 after Pantoja suffered an elbow injury 26 seconds into the opening round, then defended it with a fifth-round TKO of Tatsuro Taira at UFC 328 in May. The September 19 rematch with Pantoja is the natural sequel to how the first one ended.
  • The lightweight queue is forming behind Gaethje. Reporting on UFC 331 says both Tsarukyan and Ruffy are eyeing a title shot in early 2027 — with the caveat, stated in that reporting, “should he not retire.”
  • A fabricated résumé was rejected again. A search summary in a recent run described Joshua Van as holding consecutive stoppage wins over Rafael Fiziev and Michael Chandler after losing to Benoît Saint Denis. Those are lightweights; Van is the flyweight champion. Nothing fetched supports any of it, and it is not published.
