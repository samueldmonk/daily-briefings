The Lead

Pre-market (as of ~9 AM ET): futures slide as the bond sell-off persists and Xi lands in Washington

U.S. stock futures fell Thursday morning as inflation fears and rising bond yields outweighed hopes for Middle East diplomacy and for the AI boom to continue its run (Yahoo Finance). Futures on the Dow were down 0.3%, S&P 500 futures 0.6% and Nasdaq-100 contracts 1% (TheStreet). That follows Wednesday’s losses, when the S&P 500 fell 0.75% and the Nasdaq Composite 1.13% (official closes in the Weekly Scorecard).

The 10-year Treasury yield was 5.14% and the 2-year 4.9% at Benzinga’s early check, and CME FedWatch showed a 70.9% probability of a Fed rate hike at the October meeting. WTI crude futures rose 2.38% to around $94.35 a barrel in the early New York session. President Trump’s meeting with Chinese leader Xi Jinping is the key event: Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the U.S. and China will extend their trade truce — the “Busan Agreement,” which had been scheduled to end November 10 — until January 10 (Yahoo Finance). On the data front, 197,000 Americans applied for unemployment benefits last week, the fewest since mid-July and down from a revised 198,000 (AP via U.S. News).

Opening-bell index moves were not yet reported in sources fetched this run — watch the live quotes above. Sources: Yahoo Finance (Sept 24 live) · TheStreet (Sept 24) · Benzinga (pre-market) · AP via U.S. News (jobless claims)
