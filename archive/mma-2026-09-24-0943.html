Top Story

Tsarukyan’s elbow KO puts him next in line — but Gaethje says he’s done for the year

Arman Tsarukyan finished Mauricio Ruffy with elbows at 4:56 of round one at UFC 331 on September 19, his sixth straight UFC win, and says he was told before and after the fight that his next bout will be for lightweight gold. Champion Justin Gaethje, however, told Sports Illustrated he “still can’t punch anything” and that both hands remain hurt from his June win over Ilia Topuria, adding he will “enjoy being champion for the rest of the year” after fighting twice in the first half of 2026 — meaning he is not expected to compete again until 2027. Tsarukyan is skeptical a fight will happen soon, joking Gaethje might retire rather than face him. Gaethje’s manager, Ali Abdelaziz, responded only that he has “some ideas.” Bookmakers have already posted odds on a hypothetical matchup: Tsarukyan (24-3) a −450 favorite over Gaethje (28-5) at +350.

Sources: Yahoo Sports · Bloody Elbow

Prospect Watch

Prospect

Six contracts from Contender Series Week 7

At the September 22 taping, Dana White awarded UFC contracts to winners Alvi Dasuyev, Marcos Degli, Piero Guaylupo and Damian Piwowarczyk — plus Callum Connor and Jaden Ortega despite their losses, after their bouts were called instant classics. Degli submitted Paris Moran via rear-naked choke to extend his win streak to 13; Piwowarczyk stopped previously unbeaten Emilio Quissua by first-round TKO. No contract was awarded in the headliner, won by Norbert Novenyi Jr. over Theo Haig.

Source: Cageside Press · DraftKings Network

Around the Sport

  • Alexander Volkov, passed over for Ciryl Gane’s first undisputed defense, sarcastically praised the UFC on The Ariel Helwani Show for giving Josh Hokit the UFC 334 title shot: “It’s a noble thing for UFC to do for him with his mental problem.” Hokit earned the slot with 2026 wins over Derrick Lewis and Curtis Blaydes (CBS Sports / BJPenn.com).
  • Justin Gaethje says he will not fight again until 2027, citing lingering hand damage from his June win over Ilia Topuria at Freedom 250.
  • Arman Tsarukyan has been told his next fight is for the title but is publicly skeptical Gaethje will take it soon; sportsbooks have already posted a −450/+350 line on the hypothetical matchup.
  • Dana White’s Contender Series season 10 continues weekly on Paramount+ through October, with 28 total contracts targeted for the season.

Rankings & Business

Business & broadcast: UFC segment revenue rose 29% to $535.7 million in TKO Group’s second quarter of 2026 on the strength of the Paramount media deal; TKO’s first-quarter 2026 revenue reached $1.597 billion, up 26% year over year, with UFC and WWE both contributing. Freedom 250 (June 2026) drew roughly 17 million Paramount+ viewers in the U.S. and Latin America, with international audiences pushing the estimated global total to about 34 million — among the most-watched events in UFC history. Paramount’s seven-year rights deal with TKO, averaging $1.1 billion annually, carries all 13 numbered events and 30 Fight Nights on Paramount+, with select numbered events simulcast on CBS.

Sources: TKO Group investor release · Hollywood Reporter
