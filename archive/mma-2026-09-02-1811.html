Top Story

A debutant headlines Paris as a heavy favourite, and the veteran opposite him is the one being written off

UFC Fight Night: Hooker vs. Parnasse — UFC Fight Night 287 — lands Saturday, September 5, at the Accor Arena in Paris, with 14 fights on the card and the whole show streaming on Paramount+. Prelims start at 12:00 PM ET, unusually early for a U.S. audience, with the main card at 3:00 PM ET, to suit the French time zone.

Salahdine Parnasse gets a main event on debut. He is 23-2, a former two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion who went 14-2 inside that promotion with four defences of the lightweight belt, and he signed with the UFC in late July 2026 having previously turned the promotion down. His U.S. debut came in May 2026 on the Rousey vs. Carano main card, a first-round stoppage of Kenneth Cross for a fifth straight win. He has not fought in the UFC before.

Opposite him, Dan Hooker is 24-14 as a professional and 14-10 inside the Octagon — a record, not a fight count. He is the seasoned name and the underdog.

The market has moved steadily one way. Parnasse opened around −400 with Hooker at +300; the line now sits at −667 / +417 at one major book, −600 / +440 at DraftKings, and roughly −599 / +400 elsewhere, with another book at −600 / +430. With the vig removed, the −667 line implies 81.8% for Parnasse against 18.2% for Hooker.

One card change, sourced from UFC.com. Mairon Santos is out of his featherweight bout with Nathaniel Wood due to illness. Undefeated newcomer Pavel Andrusca replaces him on debut — and his finishing rate is newly sourced this run: he has finished seven of his eight wins, five of them in the first round. Also on the card, carried from this desk’s record of an earlier fetch rather than re-sourced this run: an all-French lightweight co-main between Farès Ziam and Axel Sola, plus Daniil Donchenko vs. Punahele Soriano.
