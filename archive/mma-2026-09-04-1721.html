Top Story

The market says the debutant wins — and it is not close

MyBookie’s numbers for UFC Paris landed this morning, and they read strangely for a main event featuring a fighter who has never set foot in the Octagon. Salahdine Parnasse opens at −649; Dan Hooker, returning to the Octagon with a 24-14 record, is a +420 underdog in his own comeback fight.

F4W, publishing the MyBookie line, calls Losene Keita (−399) over Muhammad Naimov (+284) the only other bout that comes anywhere near that split — though the same list prices Kurtis Campbell at −410 over Trevor Peek (+290), a shade wider still. Elsewhere the card is tight: Fabia Sintes (−101) vs Michael Aljarouj (−126), Nora Cornolle (−126) vs Klaudia Sygula (−101) and Luis Felipe Dias (−110) vs Matthieu Duclos (−115) are all effectively pick’ems.

Parnasse arrives as a former two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion who signed with the UFC in late July 2026 after previously turning the promotion down, and was handed a main event on debut. His last outing was not in the UFC at all — he fought on the Most Valuable Promotions Rousey vs Carano card in May. The main event is confirmed: Hooker weighed in at 155 pounds.

Song’s knockout handed Umar Nurmagomedov the second loss of his career. The 28-year-old landed a heavy right hand as Nurmagomedov changed levels for a takedown — moments after Song had opened a bad cut under his own right eyebrow on an accidental clash of heads. Coming after his submission of Deiveson Figueiredo in Macau, UFC.com suggests the win could push Song into the No. 1 contender position at bantamweight.

Prospect Watch

Prospect

Bilal Hasan

Won on debut — KO of Nilson Rojas at 2:28 of round two, after being put on the deck by a push kick moments earlier. The 25-year-old flyweight is still unbeaten, came through Dana White’s Contender Series, took home a $100,000 Performance of the Night bonus, and called out Andre Lima on the way out.

Prospect

Liu Ce

Also won on debut, and violently: the Chinese kickboxer hurt Levi Rodrigues Jr. repeatedly before spinning him to the canvas with a right hand at 4:26 of round one, earning a share of Fight of the Night. A fan favourite in the region and a new dangerous striker at light heavyweight.

Prospect

Kai Asakura

Not a newcomer, but reborn at 135 — now 2-0 with two highlight-reel finishes since dropping to bantamweight, most recently a head kick and follow-up strikes that stopped Aoriqileng 34 seconds into round two. The 32-year-old is, in UFC.com’s words, too big a name to keep being brought along slowly.

Around the Sport

  • Dana White on the biggest upset in Contender Series history: Bella Mir entered her August 25 bout at the Meta Apex as a −6000 favourite — the largest in the show’s history — and lost a 29-28 unanimous decision to Alex Apodaca. White admitted at the post-fight press conference that he had been “extremely biased” about her, having been close to Mir for over four years and having talked her into going to college for a wrestling background rather than turning pro early.
  • Denise Gomes has won five straight after crushing Yan Xiaonan with a lead elbow and follow-up shots late in round one. Yan had previously only lost to champions and contenders.
  • Sumudaerji beat Alex Perez by unanimous decision in their rematch, the biggest win of his career; he is 6-2 with one no contest over his last nine flyweight appearances and should enter the rankings.
  • UFC officials have included WWE in plans for the expansion of the Las Vegas campus, per F4W/Wrestling Observer.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. Song Yadong is positioned for a run at No. 1 contender at bantamweight after stopping Umar Nurmagomedov. Denise Gomes and Sumudaerji both project to climb after wins over ranked opposition (Yan Xiaonan, Alex Perez respectively).
