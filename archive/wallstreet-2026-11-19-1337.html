The Lead

Early afternoon (~1:32 PM ET): losses deepen after Anthropic’s debut — Dow −1.59%, S&P 500 −1.26%, Nasdaq −1.31%

New Early-afternoon slide (~1:32 PM ET, latest verified reading). Yahoo Finance’s live blog now headlines “Dow drops 800 points, S&P 500 and Nasdaq tank as Anthropic stock surges in public debut.” Its quotes as of 1:32 PM ET had the Dow down 796.92 points (−1.59%) and the Nasdaq Composite down 1.31%; its quote strip had the S&P 500 down 1.26%, the Russell 2000 down 1.65% and the VIX up 14.30% at 28.07. ANT was at $97.33, up 17.27% from its $83 IPO price, as of 1:32 PM ET. Yahoo Finance’s Jared Blikre reports that ANT slid to about $97 by its 30th minute of trading, 13% below its $111.50 first trade — erasing nearly $200 billion in market value on the 13.1 billion implied shares outstanding Yahoo counts — and that no big US IPO since 2008 was further below its first trade 30 minutes in, in a Yahoo Finance review of 1-minute data for 21 of them; the stock was still well above its IPO price (Yahoo Finance).

Midday sell-off (~12:31 PM ET, desk record). “US stock losses accelerated on Thursday as Anthropic (ANT) shares opened 34% above their IPO price,” Yahoo Finance reports; its live blog said the Dow fell 2%, or over 1,000 points, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite each down 1.5% as selling picked up. Its quotes as of 12:31 PM ET had the Dow down 618.97 points (−1.23%), the S&P 500 down 0.62% and the Nasdaq Composite down 0.32%; the quote strip had the Russell 2000 down 1.96% and the VIX up 15.61% at 28.39. Chip stocks erased their gains shortly after Anthropic started trading: Nvidia and Broadcom slid into the red and Micron and SK Hynix lost their gains, while Intel and Qualcomm were among the few semiconductor names in the green. Amazon and Alphabet — the largest public companies with disclosed Anthropic stakes — dipped, with Amazon down 0.9% and Alphabet paring its gain to 0.7%, and the S&P technology sector ETF (XLK) fell more than 1%. ANT itself was at $100.54, up 21.13% from its IPO price, as of 12:31 PM ET; the first trade gave Anthropic a market capitalization of over $1.5 trillion, and retail investors received a 15% allocation in the deal (Yahoo Finance).

Anthropic opens at $111.50 (desk record). ANT’s first trade came at $111.50 a share, after the record IPO, per CNN and Benzinga — a 34.3% premium to the $83 offering price. NBC News headlined that the stock “jumps by more than 30% as trading begins.” Yahoo Finance’s live-blog headline now reads “Dow, S&P 500, and Nasdaq slide as Anthropic stock surges in public debut” (Yahoo Finance). Needham started coverage at buy with a $150 target, and non-founder staff can sell up to 10% of their shares on day one (The Next Web).

Late morning (~11:31 AM ET, desk record). Yahoo Finance’s live blog says the market’s opening enthusiasm “fizzled by midmorning, and stocks are now trading at session lows.” Its quotes as of 11:31 AM ET had the Dow down 513.32 points (−1.02%), the S&P 500 down 0.25% and the Nasdaq Composite up 0.27%; its quote strip had the Russell 2000 down 0.78% and the VIX up 3.79% at 25.49. AMD dropped as much as 9% after Hunterbrook Capital opened a short position, citing the chipmaker’s close ties to China as Washington weighs tighter chip-export restrictions; UnitedHealth fell 5% as a rotation out of health care continued, putting it on track for an 18% slide over five days; and tech was the lone sector in the green. Nvidia was up 1.5% as it tries to snap a five-day losing streak (Yahoo Finance).

Late morning (~10:52 AM ET, desk record). The opening rally has reversed. Yahoo Finance’s quote strip had the Dow down 497.68 points (−0.99%), the S&P 500 down 0.42%, the Nasdaq Composite up just 0.06% and the Russell 2000 down 0.87%, with the VIX up 5.10% at 25.81 (Yahoo Finance). CNBC’s live blog headline reads “Dow falls 500 points, Nasdaq gives up gain as Anthropic IPO fails to spark AI rebound” (CNBC). Nvidia shares jumped more than 3% at the open, per Yahoo Finance; no fetched source gives its current move.

Before the first trade (desk record). Shares had been indicated to open as high as $105, nearly 27% above the IPO price (Yahoo Finance, ~10:48 AM ET); CEO Dario Amodei and president Daniela Amodei rang the Nasdaq opening bell (Yahoo Finance).

Early trade (~9:40 AM ET, desk record). The Nasdaq Composite was up 1.60%, the S&P 500 up 0.83% and the Dow up just 0.03% about ten minutes into the session, per JournalArta’s market snapshot (data as of 14:41 GMT, possibly delayed up to 15 minutes) — the Nasdaq leading on enthusiasm for AI shares ahead of Anthropic’s debut while the Dow hovered near flat. Just after the 9:30 AM ET bell, CNBC’s live blog had the Nasdaq up about 1.5%, the S&P 500 about 1% and the Dow up about 230 points (desk record), so the blue chips had already given back most of their opening gain by then. The S&P 500 entered the day on a five-session losing streak (desk record).

Pre-market (desk record). S&P 500 futures were up 0.96%, Dow futures 0.45% and Nasdaq futures 1.77% about 40 minutes before the open, Russell 2000 futures rose 0.47%, and the VIX was down 7.95% at 22.61 (Yahoo Finance quote strip, ~8:50 AM ET) — below the +1.16% / +0.60% / +2.04% readings of ~8:30 AM ET (desk record). Yahoo Finance’s live blog said futures rose as investors awaited Anthropic’s debut, with tech stocks surging in premarket trading, and that it is watching whether Nvidia can shake off five straight days of losses (Yahoo Finance).

Anthropic. The Claude maker raised $81.3 billion selling 980 million Class A shares at $83, topping SpaceX’s record $75 billion raise, and has the option to issue an additional 147 million shares within 30 days (Yahoo Finance IPO live blog). Bloomberg reported gray-market data indicating an open 20%–26% above the IPO price (The Next Web) — an indication, not a trade. CBS News puts the IPO’s valuation at $1.5 trillion, says the $83 price was raised from an original $70–$78 range, and notes a further $26 billion Anthropic has said it will raise from companies including AMD, Broadcom, Google, Microsoft and Nvidia, for roughly $107 billion in total (CBS News). The stock is expected to begin trading as “ANT” after the 9:30 AM ET open, though there is no set time and it is unlikely to open right at 9:30. Its amended S-1 shows sales above $33 billion for the nine months to Sept. 30 (Yahoo Finance).

Walmart. Q3 revenue of $188.8 billion and adjusted EPS of $0.65 topped estimates ($187.2 billion and $0.64, Bloomberg consensus), and it raised its 2026 outlook again; U.S. same-store sales growth moderated to 2.9%, which Yahoo Finance says was as analysts forecast, and e-commerce sales grew 17% (Yahoo Finance). Earlier premarket, shares were near $125.75, down about 1% from Wednesday’s $127.36 close (Blockonomi, desk record).

Data: initial jobless claims rose to 203,000 in the week ended Nov. 14 (prior week revised to 202,000), above the 197,000 economists polled by Reuters expected; the four-week average rose to 197,750 and continuing claims jumped 70,000 to 1.763 million (AP via ABC News; Reuters via Investing.com). The Philadelphia Fed manufacturing index fell to 6.3 from 20.9 — the lowest since May, per CNBC — well below forecasts; new orders slipped to −0.4 from 4.3 (Newsquawk; CNBC).

Washington: President Trump is departing the APEC summit in China, where meetings with Xi Jinping ended with little change in standoffs over semiconductors, rare earth minerals and tariffs (Yahoo Finance).

Wednesday’s close (desk record): Dow −561.25 (−1.11%), S&P 500 −0.23% (fifth straight loss, below its 200-day average), Nasdaq Composite +0.42%, as health insurers sank on a Medicare Advantage audit proposal and Fed minutes leaned toward more hikes. Futures figures above are pre-market; the latest index reading is as of ~1:32 PM ET and will move.
