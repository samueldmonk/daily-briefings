Top Story

Heavyweights take over a busy November: Vegas 124 Saturday, Gane–Hokit at MSG on Nov. 14

Waldo Cortes-Acosta and Johnny Walker meet in a five-round heavyweight main event at UFC Vegas 124 on Nov. 7, replacing Gabriel Bonfim vs. Sean Brady after Brady pulled out (MMASucka; BoxingNews; Cageside Press). One week later, Ciryl Gane makes the first defense of the undisputed heavyweight title against Josh Hokit — reported as 10-0 and fourth-ranked — atop UFC 334 at Madison Square Garden, with Kayla Harrison vs. Amanda Nunes for the women’s bantamweight title in the co-main (Cageside Press; Heavy; Field Level Media, via search).

The most recent card remains Halloween’s UFC Vegas 123, where Tom Nolan finished Renato Moicano by TKO (punches and elbows) at 1:28 of Round 3 in his first UFC main event, after referee Dan Miragliotta stood the fighters up in Round 2 with Moicano in control on the ground (UFC.com; Yahoo Sports). Heavy called the stand-up a “questionable call” (Heavy).
