The Lead

Jobs blowout meets a tech bounce: Nasdaq futures +1.5%, Dow futures −0.9% (as of 8:30 AM ET)

New U.S. employers added a surprising 165,000 jobs in October, far above the 65,000 economists expected, and the unemployment rate dipped to 4.1%, the AP reported. Revisions added a combined 84,000 jobs to August and September; 421,000 people entered the labour force; and average hourly wages rose 0.4% from September — the biggest monthly gain since January — though only 3.1% from a year earlier. “This was a blowout jobs report,” said Edward Jones’ Angelo Kourkafas. Traders now see a 77% chance of a December Fed rate hike, up about 10 percentage points from Thursday, per CME FedWatch (AP via CP24/BNN Bloomberg, 10:09 AM ET).

New Before the bell, futures were split: as of 8:30 a.m. ET, Nasdaq-100 futures were up 1.49% and S&P 500 futures 0.17%, while Dow futures were down 0.93% (TipRanks, 8:30 AM ET). The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.85% as Treasury prices slipped (Coinpedia, 7:21 AM ET). TipRanks attributes the rebound to tech and AI names recovering after Thursday’s selloff pushed the Nasdaq-100 into correction territory. Live quotes above show where the cash session stands now; no verified post-open index snapshot was available for this edition.

Thursday’s damage, for context: the S&P 500 fell 4.36%, the Nasdaq Composite 5.52% — closing more than 10% below its recent high, in correction — and the Dow 3.44%, the indexes’ biggest one-day losses since April 2025; all 11 S&P sectors fell (TipRanks; official closes in the Weekly Scorecard, JournalArta). For the week, TipRanks puts the Nasdaq down 4.4%, the S&P 500 down nearly 3% and the Dow down just 0.5% heading into Friday (TipRanks).

Overnight abroad: South Korea’s Kospi plunged more than 6% to 5,059.69 (−11.7% on the week) and Japan’s Nikkei 225 fell 5.9%, entering bear-market territory; the Hang Seng fell 1.27% and the Shanghai Composite 1.10% (TipRanks).

Movers & Drivers

New

Macro · 165K jobs, 4.1% unemployment

Professional and business services added 70,000 jobs, healthcare nearly 42,000 and factories 19,000, while hotels, restaurants and bars cut 10,600. The AP says the strength gives the Fed leeway to focus on inflation and raise rates in December (AP).

New

Chips & neoclouds rebound premarket

Iren (IREN) rallied after strong fiscal first-quarter revenue, lifting peers CoreWeave (CRWV) and Nebius (NBIS); Micron (MU), SanDisk (SNDK) and AMD also rebounded in early trading (TipRanks).

New

Moderna pops on Q3 beat

Moderna (MRNA) rose after market-beating third-quarter results and a raised full-year sales-growth outlook (TipRanks).

Cloudflare (NET) jumps on guidance

Shares rose more than 17% in extended trading Thursday after Q3 revenue grew 39% to $782.5 million and Q4 revenue guidance of $845.0–$846.0 million beat the $798.2 million expected (Investing.com); the stock was still jumping before Friday’s open (TipRanks).

DraftKings (DKNG) sinks

Down about 18% after hours Thursday on a third-quarter miss and cut full-year guidance (Investing.com); shares were still sinking Friday morning on a wider-than-expected loss (TipRanks).
