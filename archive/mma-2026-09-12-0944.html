Top Story

Noche UFC returns to Arizona with a short-notice main event

The fourth annual Noche UFC runs tonight at the Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona, on Mexican Independence Day weekend. Thirteen fights — a seven-fight preliminary card from 2 PM ET, a six-fight main card from 5 PM ET — all streaming on Paramount+. Main-event walkouts are expected around 6:30 PM ET, depending on how the night runs. (An earlier read this weekend put the card at sixteen fights; two reads today give thirteen, and the 7+6 split reconciles to that.)

In the main event, featherweight Jean Silva (17-3), No. 6 in the division, meets Arizona’s own Jose Miguel Delgado (12-2, 4-1 in the UFC). Delgado is the replacement: Yair Rodríguez withdrew with a training-camp injury, and Delgado took the fight on twenty-four days’ notice. He is unranked and has never faced a ranked opponent.

The books make it lopsided. Silva runs from −410 to −455 across the seven reads taken, with Delgado between +310 and +355; the shortest price this run, −410, is quoted alongside a 77% win probability, and the same read gives Silva a 63% chance of winning by knockout or TKO. Twelve of Silva’s seventeen wins are by knockout, and the method props reflect it: Silva by KO/TKO −145, by decision +460.

No result from tonight is published here. The prelims have not opened at the time of this edition.

Business

UFC Paris set an arena record

Per UFC.com’s own bonus page: gross revenue $4,365,335 from an attendance of 15,687, a sell-out and the highest-grossing event in Accor Arena history.

Tonight’s card streams under the first year of Paramount’s seven-year, $7.7 billion rights deal with TKO Group, and the audience figures behind that deal were re-read this run. Paramount+ says 16 million subscriber households have watched more than 180 million hours of UFC programming since the start of the year, and that viewership runs as much as 20 times the pay-per-view average of the previous two years. The first card under the deal, UFC 324, drew 4.96 million views; UFC Freedom 250 averaged 8.2 million and reached an estimated 34 million total global viewers, with 17 million on Paramount+ across the US and Latin America. TKO posted first-quarter revenue of $1.6 billion, up 26%, net income of $250 million and adjusted EBITDA of $550 million, up 32%. The partnership extends to Canada from 2027.
