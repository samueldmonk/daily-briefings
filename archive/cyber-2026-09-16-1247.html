Top Story

Two unauthenticated RCE chains in The Events Calendar put 200,000+ WordPress sites one comment away from takeover

SecurityWeek reported at 7:32 a.m. ET this morning that more than 200,000 WordPress websites are potentially exposed to takeover via two critical vulnerabilities in The Events Calendar, a plugin with over 600,000 active installations. All plugin versions before 6.17.3.1 are affected by both code-injection bugs, each of which leads to remote code execution and complete compromise of the WordPress installation. WordPress security firm Defiant found them with Wordfence Argus.

CVE-2026-78159 (CVSS 9.8) is an unauthenticated code injection caused by insufficient validation: under certain conditions an attacker can inject a plain-array payload that bypasses checks and executes during the processing of single-event HTML, including the comment area. StellarWP, the plugin’s developer, patched it on 25 August in version 6.17.3.1.

CVE-2026-78006 (CVSS 9.8) is an unauthenticated PHP object injection issue, exploitable when comments on events are enabled and visible. Insufficient protections in a plugin function can be bypassed by a commenter without authentication or approval, because the injected code is delivered to the vulnerable function before moderation occurs. StellarWP fixed it in version 6.17.4.1, released 10 September.

The exposure math: WordPress’s own data shows roughly 240,000 sites running versions prior to 6.17, meaning they are affected by both flaws. Between 10 and 14 September the plugin was downloaded just over 300,000 times, which suggests roughly half of installations may still be vulnerable to CVE-2026-78006. SecurityWeek notes it is unclear exactly how many sites are exploitable, because both chains require that comments be enabled.

Neither flaw is in the CISA KEV catalog and no in-the-wild exploitation was stated in anything read this run, so no federal clock attaches — which is why the Patch Priority box below still points at the KEV deadline that expires today.

Patch Priority

Today is the deadline: Starlette CVE-2026-48710 and LiteLLM CVE-2026-59822 — 0 days left

Federal civilian agencies have until 16 September 2026 — today — to remediate CVE-2026-48710 (Kludex Starlette, CVSS 6.5) and CVE-2026-59822 (BerriAI LiteLLM, CVSS 8.8), both added in CISA’s 2 September batch. The Hacker News states the extended deadline explicitly: agencies have time until September 16, 2026 to address the Starlette and LiteLLM flaws. Next up, tomorrow: Cisco Secure Email Gateway CVE-2026-76461 (CVSS 9.8), actively exploited, with no workarounds — fixed in AsyncOS 15.5.5-0141, 16.0.4-302 and 16.5.0-780.

Threat Actor Spotlight

Iran · state-linked

CHOSEN BRICK — spyware aimed at dissidents, activists and journalists

The UK’s National Cyber Security Centre, the FBI and the Netherlands’ AIVD issued a joint advisory on 15 September exposing CHOSEN BRICK, a Windows-only spyware family Iranian state cyber actors have used against dissidents, activists and journalists in the UK, US and Netherlands since at least 2025. Delivery is spear-phishing over messaging platforms including WhatsApp and Telegram, with lures tailored to each target — in one case fake MRI test results. Once installed it collects contacts, emails and social-media messages, captures screen content, accesses the device microphone, and survives a reboot.

Breaches & Incidents

Healthcare · 280,000

Premier Medical Group breach exposed patient records

Hackers accessed the personal and medical information of 280,000 patients of Premier Medical Group, a Hudson Valley multi-specialty provider. Systems were disrupted in June and attackers accessed files on 14 June; the investigation concluded 14 July and notification began 21 August. Exposed fields may include names, contact details, dates of birth, health insurance information, provider names, internal patient identifiers, dates of service, medication information and treatment or diagnostic information. No ransomware or extortion group has claimed responsibility.

Exploited in the wild

WSO2 API Manager JWT bypass under live attack

CVE-2026-5430 (CVSS 9.8), an improper verification of a cryptographic signature in WSO2 API Manager, has come under active exploitation. JWT authentication accepts tokens signed with unsupported algorithms and approves them anyway, yielding account takeover. watchTowr honeypots captured forged tokens carrying baked-in administrator privileges on 13 September. Affected: API Manager 4.1.0 through 4.6.0; advisory WSO2-2026-5328.

Exploited in the wild

WooCommerce Wholesale Lead Capture abused for file upload

Threat actors are exploiting CVE-2026-27540 (CVSS 9.8) in WooCommerce Wholesale Lead Capture, a premium WordPress plugin with more than 6,000 active installs. The flaw is an arbitrary file upload from missing file-type validation in the AJAX action wwlc_file_upload_handler, present in all versions up to and including 2.0.3.1. Wordfence has blocked more than 100,000 exploit attempts since June 2026.

Chrome and Firefox ship 115 fixes between them

Google released Chrome 153 fixing 42 vulnerabilities, three of them rated Critical; Mozilla fixed 73 bugs in Firefox. SecurityWeek tallies the combined total at 115. Outlets differ on the Chrome breakdown — some headline 42 fixes with three critical, others five Critical and 41 high-severity defects. The disagreement is printed rather than resolved.
