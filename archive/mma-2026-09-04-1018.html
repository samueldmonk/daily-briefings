Top Story

Paris is official: the whole 14-bout card made weight, and the UFC’s own running order settles a split this page has carried for two editions

The weigh-in is done and the card is intact. UFC.com published its Official Weigh-In Results page for UFC Fight Night: Hooker vs Parnasse this morning, listing all 14 bouts across main card and prelims. Dan Hooker and Salahdine Parnasse each weighed 155 pounds with no issue for the five-round main event, and every other athlete on the card also made weight — all 14 fights are official.

This closes an overclaim the last two editions deliberately refused to make. The 0917 and 0946 runs both declined to say the card had made weight: the Cageside Press article headlined “Weigh-In Results” was fetched in full and contained no weights at all, only a bout listing, and Parnasse’s 155 was the single weight this page would assert. That refusal was correct, and it is now resolved by sources rather than assumed away.

It also resolves the card-order split. UFC.com’s running order puts Campbell–Peek and Keita–Naimov on the MAIN CARD and Charriere–Lima on the PRELIMS. That is an authoritative primary source, independent of the Cageside listing that carried the same order — which is exactly the second, non-identical corroboration the 0946 edition said it did not yet have.

Prospect Watch

ProspectDebut

Matthieu Letho Duclos (10-3) — UFC debut at home

The French middleweight out of Arras debuts on the Paris prelims against Luis Felipe Dias (18-5, São José dos Campos, Brazil). Duclos lost his Contender Series appearance in Season 7 and has posted four straight wins since to earn the call in his home country. Dias earned a contract last season and followed it with a first-round KO on debut in Macau in May, stopping YiSak Lee.

ProspectShort notice

Pavel Andrusca — undefeated, and moving up

The promotional newcomer stepped in for Mairon Santos, who withdrew with an unspecified health problem, and faces Nathaniel Wood on the Paris prelims. Andrusca is moving up from bantamweight to featherweight to take it.
