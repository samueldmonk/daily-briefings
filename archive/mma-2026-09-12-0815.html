Top Story

Noche UFC lost its main event — and got a hometown headliner instead

The fourth annual Noche UFC takes over Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona tonight on Mexican Independence Day weekend. It is not the main event the UFC booked. Jean Silva (17-3, out of São Paulo) was scheduled to face former interim featherweight champion Yair Rodriguez; Rodriguez withdrew injured, and in stepped Phoenix’s own Jose Miguel Delgado (12-2) on twenty-four days’ notice.

Delgado is 4-1 in the UFC and has never faced a ranked opponent. Silva is No. 6 at 145 lb in the MMA Fighting Global Rankings, has won six of his seven UFC fights, and has finished twelve of his seventeen wins by knockout. The books read it accordingly — see the odds spread below.

The card is stacked with Mexican and Latin American talent beyond the headliner. Former flyweight champion Brandon Moreno meets The Ultimate Fighter season 33 winner Joseph Morales in the co-main at 125 pounds, and Manon Fiorot faces former women’s flyweight champion Alexa Grasso in a bout with real title implications while that belt sits vacant.

Prospect Watch

ProspectDebutNew

Salahdine Parnasse

A former KSW two-division champion who took a five-round main event on home soil as his UFC debut — and finished Dan Hooker inside a round. The 28-year-old has now won six straight, all by stoppage. UFC.com has him stepping straight into the lightweight top 15. Known for his grappling, he chose to kickbox with Hooker, hurt him with a body kick and finished on the fence.

ProspectLightweightNew

Axel Sola

Third Octagon appearance of 2026 and the knockout of his career — a single left hand that ended Farès Ziam before he hit the canvas, 1:40 into the first. That is back-to-back first-round finishes and a second straight win in Paris, after opening the year with a Fight of the Night performance opposite Mason Jones.

ProspectFeatherweightNew

Losene Keita

The former Oktagon MMA double champion got his first UFC win in emphatic fashion, dropping Muhammad Naimov with a jab and finishing with a right hook before scaling the Octagon wall to take a front-row seat. He called out Lerone Murphy in a bilingual post-fight interview.

ProspectMiddleweightNew

Daniil Donchenko

The Ultimate Fighter winner swept the cards against Punahele Soriano to extend his winning streak to eight with a third consecutive UFC victory. The 25-year-old called for a matchup with Daniel Rodriguez before the end of the year.

Around the Sport

  • Grasso–Fiorot is a title eliminator in everything but name. With the women’s flyweight belt vacant and Natália Silva vs. Wang Cong already booked for it at UFC 332, tonight’s winner has the clearest path to whoever comes out of Salt Lake City.
  • 12 September is a full day of combat sports. UFC.com is billing Noche UFC alongside Garcia vs. Benn on the same date.
  • Arizona fighters are on the main card. Cronkite News notes the Glendale event features Arizona-based fighters on the main card, with Delgado himself out of Phoenix — the local angle the UFC leans on for Noche.
  • Dana White’s Contender Series season ten continues. Week five ran Tuesday 8 September at the UFC Apex, with four contracts awarded — Quentin Pasley, Isaac Moreno, Martin Kozák and Christian Natividad.

Rankings & Business

  • Rankings movement: Jean Silva enters tonight ranked No. 6 at featherweight in the MMA Fighting Global Rankings; Jose Miguel Delgado is unranked and has never fought a ranked opponent. UFC.com places Salahdine Parnasse in the lightweight top 15 off his debut win.
  • UFC Paris gate: gross total revenue of $4,365,335 from a sold-out attendance of 15,687 — the highest-grossing event in Accor Arena history, per UFC.com’s official bonus coverage.
  • Broadcast: Noche UFC streams on Paramount+. UFC 332 on 3 October will be the first numbered event with its main card on CBS — a milestone for the Paramount era of UFC broadcasting.
