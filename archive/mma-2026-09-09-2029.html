Top Story

“Tom Aspinall is hurt” — White gives no return date, and says the division moves on without him

Asked on 9 September for an update on the heavyweight champion’s health, UFC chief executive Dana White said: “Tom Aspinall is hurt, y’know, until we hear differently. So … the heavyweight division will just keep rolling.” That second clause is the news. It signals the UFC is prepared to book a heavyweight title fight — interim, vacant or otherwise — without waiting.

Aspinall has not competed since the No Contest against Ciryl Gane at UFC 321 last October, when he was poked in the eye. That is roughly 11 months out, and he is approaching an unwanted Jon Jones inactivity mark. There had been positive noises in recent months until his new manager, Eddie Hearn, revealed he still needed further testing before he could fight.

Aspinall vs. Gane 2 had been discussed for November’s Madison Square Garden card — on this reporting, that rematch now appears unlikely. No return is treated as booked.

The previous edition also carried an athletic-commission medical-clearance detail attached to a specific November date. That detail could not be re-confirmed in anything read this run, so it has been removed rather than carried forward.

Fight Week — Upcoming Cards

Updated
Sat 12 September · Desert Diamond Arena, Glendale, AZ

Noche UFC: Jean Silva vs. Jose Miguel Delgado

The fourth annual Noche UFC, commemorating Mexican Independence Day, and the promotion’s third visit to Glendale — its first since UFC 263 in June 2021. The man Delgado replaced is Yair Rodríguez, a former interim featherweight champion, who withdrew with an injury after being booked to end an 18-month layoff. Delgado — who is from Arizona — stepped in on late notice and gets the first headline slot of his UFC career. Prelims 2 p.m. ET, main card 5 p.m. ET, Paramount+.

Odds: Silva −450 / Delgado +350 at Caesars; −428 / +324 as an average across 20 books tracked by UFCalendar. The line opened near −425 and has traded in a −400 to −450 band. Silva’s implied win probability sits around 80–82%.

Around the Sport

  • Aspinall’s layoff is now the heavyweight division’s central problem. Eleven months without a defence, no timeline, and an interim champion in Ciryl Gane already sitting in the wings — the same man Aspinall fought to a No Contest.
  • Michael Page’s UFC run appears over. White’s “I don’t think we’re signing him” follows the unanimous-decision win over Nursulton Ruziboev in Paris. His UFC record reads 5-1 across six appearances in two reads and 4-1 in a third; 5-1 is carried as better supported and the conflict is stated rather than smoothed.
  • A blank on this page is now filled. Previous editions said only that Jose Miguel Delgado “stepped in on late notice” and declined to name who had withdrawn, because a single read gave only a surname. Two further reads this run identify him as Yair Rodríguez, out with an injury. That turns Saturday’s headliner from a routine short-notice booking into a local fighter replacing one of the division’s most recognisable names on a Mexican Independence Day card.
  • Hawaii is being teased as a first-ever market. The UFC has never held an event in Hawaii despite it being one of the promotion’s biggest markets, and White has said an inaugural Hawaii card will follow not long after a new complex opens. It is carried here as a tease, not a booking: no date, no venue and no card have been stated, and nothing beyond that is asserted.
  • Roster churn cuts both ways. Curtis Blaydes signed a new eight-fight deal and debuts it Saturday, against a reported run of surprise roster removals in 2026.
