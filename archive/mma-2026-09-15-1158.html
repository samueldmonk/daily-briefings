Top Story

Title vacated · heavyweight

Aspinall gives up the heavyweight belt over an eye that never healed

Tom Aspinall announced on Monday 14 September that he has vacated the UFC heavyweight title, because he could not be medically cleared to compete. The cause is ongoing complications with his eyes, which he traces to his bout with Ciryl Gane at UFC 321 in October 2025. Aspinall said he has had “multiple surgeries, accidents and infections”, and that when he was back sparring and ready to compete, an accident caused further damage to his right eye. His own word for it was “I’m absolutely gutted”.

He framed the decision as a release rather than a retirement announcement, saying he asked the UFC to let him make the statement himself because he did not want to hold the heavyweight division up. That is the practical effect: the division has been frozen behind a champion who could not be cleared, and it is now unfrozen.

What happens next is expected, not done, and the Champions Board below reflects that. Interim heavyweight champion Ciryl Gane is expected to be promoted to undisputed champion. Until that is announced, the heavyweight row reads vacant with Gane holding the interim belt — which, with women’s flyweight also vacant after Valentina Shevchenko relinquished that title earlier this month, leaves two belts off the board.

Around the Sport

  • Ortega is out of UFC 331, and it is the second time. UFC officials announced Monday 14 September that Brian Ortega had been pulled from his bout with Renato Moicano with an injury, four days before the card. Bloody Elbow carries his statement under “This one hurts.” The injury has not been formally disclosed. The pair were previously matched at UFC 326 and Ortega withdrew then too. Ortega posted on social media after the news.
  • Jones and Makhachev are trading shots, from Thailand. Islam Makhachev said he does not consider Jon Jones one of the greats and does not count his twenty-fight unbeaten run in the Octagon; Jones responded on X and has since stood by the remark. Jones is currently in Thailand filming a Russian reality television show with Nate Diaz, and in a clip from it says Makhachev is “new in the sport still” — which Jones explains as an attempt to clarify something Diaz had said to a Russian speaker.
  • Nate Diaz is keeping his options open. He is reported to be planning to “fight everywhere” for the foreseeable future, and is still hoping for a Conor McGregor trilogy bout.
  • One claim refused this run. A search summary asserted that UFC 335 took place today, 15 September, at the Meta Apex. It is not published: UFC 331 is on 19 September and UFC 332 on 3 October, so a card five numbers further on cannot have happened today, and nothing else read supports it. The Apex event scheduled for tonight is the Contender Series.
