Top Story

A screenshot tool leaked 23.62 million accounts — and half a billion pointers to private images

Helpfeel, the Japanese operator of the screenshot-sharing service Gyazo, disclosed a breach exposing 23.62 million user records and roughly 490 million image metadata records. Cybernews and The Hacker News both report the intrusion dates to 11 September, and that attackers exploited a bug in Gyazo's image upload server that let them execute arbitrary commands.

The user records include names, email addresses, password hashes, device IDs, login sessions and some connected-account tokens. The metadata set is the more unusual half: it covers images mostly dated January 2019 or earlier and includes the IDs that make up Gyazo image links — which is why the company has temporarily disabled viewing of certain images, since that metadata could help an attacker reach pictures their owners believed were private.

Gyazo will require password changes and is advising users to change reused passwords elsewhere. Neither source fetched this run names a threat actor, states a ransom demand, or gives a figure for how many of the 490 million metadata rows map to images still hosted — so none of those is printed here.

Patch Priority

Today · CVE-2026-76461 · Cisco Secure Email Gateway

The single most urgent item for defenders today is CVE-2026-76461 in Cisco Secure Email Gateway. CISA's remediation deadline for federal civilian agencies is 17 September 2026 — that is today, 0 days left.

Newly sourced this run: the flaw carries a CVSS of 9.8. It lives in the email-parsing logic of Cisco AsyncOS, where insufficient validation lets a specially crafted email carry SQL statements that the gateway processes — ending in arbitrary SQL execution and then operating-system command execution as root, with no authentication. Cisco PSIRT says it became aware of attacks exploiting the flaw in September 2026.

Affected: AsyncOS 16.5, 16.0, and 15.5 and earlier, on on-premises physical and virtual appliances. Fixed releases: 15.5.5-014, 16.0.4-302, 16.5.0-780. Immediately behind it sits the CVSS 10.0 Cisco ISE bypass, CVE-2026-76460, due 19 September (2 days left).

Threat Actor Spotlight

RansomwareCritical infrastructure

Medusa: 67 claimed victims, and an EDR killer in the toolkit

Active since mid-2022, Medusa has hit hundreds of critical-infrastructure organisations and claims 67 victims as of 2026, concentrated in government, retail and manufacturing. Its tradecraft leans on exploiting remote-access services and deploying EDR killers, and it stages Rclone inside Windows Defender exclusion paths so that exfiltration does not trip detection.

Espionage

UTA0560 chained three zero-days to drop GRIMWEDGE

Volexity attributes a spear-phishing campaign against multiple NGOs on 1 September 2026 to a China-linked cluster it tracks as UTA0560, delivering a JavaScript backdoor called GRIMWEDGE. The chain runs CVE-2026-85046 for arbitrary read/write inside the V8 sandbox, CVE-2026-87491 to escape the browser sandbox, then CVE-2026-85880 to inject into the Chrome process. A second cluster, JungleBamboo, uses SUPERSTOMP to install the LONGTALE extension.
