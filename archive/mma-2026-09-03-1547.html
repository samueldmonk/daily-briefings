Top Story

UFC 332 is four weeks out with no main event, and Cris Cyborg has offered to save it

Valentina Shevchenko has withdrawn from the October 3 card at the Delta Center in Salt Lake City with an undisclosed injury, leaving the promotion without a headliner. The reported replacement is Natalia Silva vs. Wang Cong for an interim women’s flyweight title — Wang is the No. 6-ranked contender in the division. Nothing is official; UFC CEO Dana White has said the main event will be announced this week.

Into that gap stepped Cris Cyborg, who publicly offered to headline the card against Amanda Nunes, writing to Nunes on social media: “tired of waiting for @KaylaH? Feel like saving an @ufc event?” Nunes knocked Cyborg out at UFC 232 eight years ago to become a two-division champion. Nunes has not responded.

Should the interim bout be made, it would set up a title fight for Shevchenko on her return that could let her avenge a 2015 kickboxing loss to Wang Cong — as reported, though the two have never met in MMA.
