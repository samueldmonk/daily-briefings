Fight week in Philadelphia: Makhachev defends against Machado Garry — and Van–Pantoja 2 is official for September

UFC 330 fight week is underway (UFC.com). Saturday at Xfinity Mobile Arena, welterweight champion Islam Makhachev defends against #1-ranked contender Ian Machado Garry — Makhachev is a −340 favorite at DraftKings, with Machado Garry at +270, a line that has moved toward the champion through fight week (SportsLine, CBS Sports). Arman Tsarukyan also returns on the card (Al Jazeera).
