Top Story

Hernandez vs. Rodrigues headlines a live card in Sacramento — and the first results are in

UFC Fight Night 285 is under way at the Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, California. Prelims opened at 5:00 PM ET / 2:00 PM PT with seven fights; the six-fight main card follows at 8:00 PM ET / 5:00 PM PT, streaming on Paramount+.

The five-round middleweight main event pits Anthony Hernandez (15-3, 1 NC) of Dunnigan, California — ranked No. 6 — against Brazil's Gregory Rodrigues (19-6), ranked No. 11. Hernandez is looking to get back on track after an eight-fight win streak ended against Sean Strickland in February; Rodrigues is chasing a fourth straight victory, having knocked out Bruno Ferreira at UFC 326 in March. UFC.com lists the line at Hernandez −165 / Rodrigues +140.

The co-main event is a heavyweight bout between Moldova's Serghei Spivac (18-6), ranked No. 6, and Brazil's Vitor Petrino (14-2), at Spivac +130 / Petrino −155.
