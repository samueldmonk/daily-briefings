Top Story

Heavyweight · injury

Aspinall faces a possible fourth eye surgery, and a January verdict on whether he fights again

Tom Aspinall vacated the UFC heavyweight title on Monday 14 September because he could not be medically cleared. Reporting read this run fills in what was behind that decision. He had been in camp for a rematch with Ciryl Gane, set for UFC 334, when a training accident caused his already-injured right eye to deteriorate again and derailed the return — a development Ariel Helwani reported.

If the eye does not recover as hoped, he could need another operation: a third on the right eye and a fourth overall. The instruction he has been given is to let it heal — rest, avoid getting hit or scraped — and return to his doctor in January 2027 for reassessment, in the hope that there is enough time for the cells to regenerate. That is a verdict several months away, not a date on a calendar.

All of it traces to the eye pokes he sustained at UFC 321 in Abu Dhabi against Ciryl Gane in October 2025. Aspinall’s own account of the vacating, reported Monday, was “I’m absolutely gutted”, and he asked the UFC to let him make the statement himself so as not to hold the division up: “I know what it’s like to have your career put on hold and I want the other fighters to have the opportunity to compete for the title.”

What happens next is expected, not done. Interim heavyweight champion Ciryl Gane is widely expected to be promoted to undisputed champion, but the UFC has made no statement on it. The Champions Board below therefore reads heavyweight vacant, with Gane holding the interim belt, and will keep reading that way until the promotion says otherwise.

Around the Sport

  • Dana White says Ilia Topuria is ready to fight — but nothing is booked. White stated the UFC does not have Topuria on the schedule right now, that Topuria is ready and the promotion is ready for him. Asked whether a Justin Gaethje rematch or a Paddy Pimblett bout is likelier, he said “both sound good to me”, calling the rematch “incredible” and the Pimblett fight “amazing”.
