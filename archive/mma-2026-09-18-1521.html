Top Story

All 24 make weight in Los Angeles — and Dana White puts a one-off $50,000 bonus on the card

Every one of the 24 scheduled athletes on UFC 331 made weight in Los Angeles on Friday morning, including both men in the flyweight title rematch: Joshua Van 125 and Alexandre Pantoja 125. Down the card, as the weigh-in sheet renders them: Arman Tsarukyan 156 / Mauricio Ruffy 155; Patricio Pitbull 145.5 / Doo Ho Choi 146; Gable Steveson 240 / Sean Sharaf 241; Menifield 205.5 / Baraniewski 206; Vera 136 / Jourdain 135; Tuivasa 264.5 / Despaigne 263. The ceremonial weigh-ins follow on Friday at Crypto.com Arena at 5:00 PM PT / 8:00 PM ET, streamed on the UFC's YouTube channel and on its Kick, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram accounts.

Separately, Dana White has announced a one-event $50,000 "Proper Chaos" bonus for UFC 331, tied to the Paramount+ series MobLand, whose second season premieres Friday. White will personally choose the recipient, and the UFC has not announced a formal scoring system for the award — so it sits alongside, not inside, the usual Performance and Fight of the Night structure.

Fight Week — Upcoming Cards

Sat 19 Sep · Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles

UFC 331: Van vs. Pantoja 2

The flyweight title rematch. Joshua Van defends against Alexandre Pantoja, the man he took the belt from at UFC 323 in December; their first meeting ended inside the opening round. Co-main is a five-round lightweight fight between Arman Tsarukyan (23-3) and Mauricio Ruffy (14-2). Also on the card: Olympic gold medallist Gable Steveson's second UFC appearance, and former two-division Bellator champion Patricio Pitbull against Doo Ho Choi. Early prelims 5:30 PM ET, prelims 7:00, main card 9:00, Paramount+.

Odds: Van −130 / Pantoja +110 (Covers, 56% implied for Van); CBS Sports via DraftKings has Van −135. Co-main: Tsarukyan −305 / Ruffy +240.

Sat 26 Sep · Meta APEX, Enterprise NV

UFC Fight Night: Rosas Jr. vs. Barcelos

Raul Rosas Jr., 21, takes his first UFC main event against Raoni Barcelos — an 18-year age gap in the headliner. The card also carries the TUF: Team Cormier vs. Team Bisping bantamweight and women's strawweight finals, plus Nakamura vs. Hiestand, across 12 bouts. 8:00 PM EDT, Paramount+. No betting line for the headliner appeared in any source read this run, so none is printed.

Sat 3 Oct · Delta Center, Salt Lake City

UFC 332: Silva vs. Wang

Natalia Silva vs. Wang Cong for the vacant women's flyweight title, the belt Valentina Shevchenko gave up while sidelined by injury. Thirteen fights, and the promotion's first numbered main card on CBS. No odds were sourced this run and none are printed.

Sat 24 Oct · Etihad Arena, Abu Dhabi

UFC 333: Volkanovski vs. Evloev

Featherweight champion Alexander Volkanovski defends against Movsar Evloev, with the Petr Yan vs. Merab Dvalishvili bantamweight trilogy bout in the co-main. Nine fights. No odds were sourced this run and none are printed.

Around the Sport

  • Michael "Venom" Page has been released. His contract was not renewed after his UFC Paris decision over Nursulton Ruziboev, leaving him 5-1 in six UFC fights with no finishes (Bloody Elbow leaked-email report, 7 September). Page says he now has offers from every other promotion, Rizin among them.
  • Other September roster departures from the tracker read this run: Aori Qileng, released 5 September (bantamweight); Michael Page, contract not renewed 7 September; Bruno Lopes, released 10 September (light heavyweight).
  • The heavyweight title is still vacant. Tom Aspinall vacated it on 14 September over ongoing complications with his eyes — multiple surgeries, accidents and infections, and further damage to his right eye after he had agreed a fight date and returned to sparring. He is not retiring and intends to return once cleared. The root cause traces to the eye pokes in his no-contest with Ciryl Gane at UFC 321 in October 2025. Gane holds the interim belt.
