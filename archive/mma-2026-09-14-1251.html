Top Story

Glendale, Arizona

Noche UFC posts the UFC’s highest domestic Fight Night gate — and Jean Silva asks for the belt

Saturday’s Noche UFC: Silva vs. Delgado at Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale drew ticket sales of $4,436,470 in front of more than 16,928 people — the UFC’s highest domestic Fight Night gate, and the arena’s highest-grossing single-day event ever, passing Drake’s record. It was the fourth annual Noche UFC, staged as part of a seven-event deal between TKO Group Holdings and the Arizona Sports & Events Alliance.

In the main event, Jean Silva started slowly and warmed into the fight before submitting Jose Miguel Delgado in the third round at 2:57 — a one-armed choke — then demanded a title shot. He took a $100,000 Performance of the Night bonus with him.

Around the Sport

  • Two belts are frozen by injury. Light heavyweight champion Carlos Ulberg tore his ACL in the April knockout of Jiří Procházka that won him the title and is expected to miss the rest of 2026, targeting a first defence in early 2027 — likely against Magomed Ankalaev or Paulo Costa. Valentina Shevchenko was forced out with a ligament injury to her back and shoulder that sidelines her for a year, vacating the women’s flyweight title.
  • Natália Silva arrives at UFC 332 on a 14-fight win streak, with wins over former champions Rose Namajunas, Alexa Grasso and Jessica Andrade in her last three outings. Wang Cong has won four straight in the Octagon, most recently beating Tracy Cortez at UFC 329 in July.
  • The rankings are now a model, not a panel. Since 20 June 2026 the UFC has been replacing its traditional rankings with the Meta UFC Rankings, an Elo-based system weighting opponent quality, activity and decay for long layoffs or outdated wins. The most recent release was 5 September 2026, after UFC Fight Night: Hooker vs. Parnasse.
  • Alexa Grasso beat Manon Fiorot by unanimous decision on Saturday — a result that lands in a women’s flyweight division whose belt is on the line three weeks later.
