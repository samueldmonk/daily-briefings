Fight week in Shanghai: Umar Nurmagomedov and Song Yadong meet with the next title shot on the line

UFC Fight Night: Nurmagomedov vs. Song takes place Saturday, August 29 at the Pudong Development Bank Shanghai Oriental Sports Center, streaming exclusively on Paramount+ in the United States. UFC.com frames it as a critical matchup for both men, each looking to head into the final quarter of the year as the clubhouse leader in the chase for the next title opportunity.

Umar Nurmagomedov (20-1, fighting out of Dagestan, Russia) is on a two-fight win streak since challenging for the bantamweight title, and holds wins over former UFC flyweight champion Deiveson Figueiredo, former title challenger Cory Sandhagen and Mario Bautista. Song Yadong "The Kung Fu Kid" (23-9-1, fighting out of Heilongjiang, China) makes his sixth main-event appearance following a submission win over former UFC flyweight champion Deiveson Figueiredo at UFC Fight Night Macau in May. The two have had their first fight-week faceoff.

The stakes are spelled out on both sides. Nurmagomedov is working his way back toward a title shot after losing to Merab Dvalishvili at UFC 311; Song is China's highest-ranked male UFC contender, and a win over Nurmagomedov would be the biggest of his career. A decisive result either way carries title-shot implications at 135 pounds.

The co-main event is a women's strawweight bout between Yan Xiaonan, China's first female UFC athlete and a former strawweight title challenger, ranked No. 4, and the surging No. 13 Denise Gomes. Because of the time difference, the card runs in the American morning: prelims at 3 a.m. ET, main card at 6 a.m. ET — 3 p.m. and 6 p.m. China Standard Time. Fight week is a back-to-back double: the Road to UFC season 5 semifinals run on Friday, August 28 at 5 a.m. ET / 5 p.m. CST, and UFC.com has now published official weights for the athletes competing at the Oriental Sports Center on August 28. The 9:05 edition said those weights were "due on August 28"; they have since been released, and the wording is corrected rather than left standing.

Rankings, unresolved: UFC.com's own preview headline bills the fight as "#3 Umar Nurmagomedov and #5 Song Yadong," while a summary of the same coverage describes them as ranked No. 2 and No. 6 at 135 pounds. Both readings are printed; neither is adopted, and no numeric rank is asserted here for either man.

Week 3 — five fights, five contracts, and a record upset

All five winners on August 25 left with UFC deals. In the featured bout, bantamweight Alex Apodaca beat Bella Mir — daughter of former UFC heavyweight champion Frank Mir — by unanimous decision, 29-28 on all three cards. Mir had been a −6000 favourite and Apodaca a +1200 underdog, described as the biggest upset in Contender Series history. Joining her on the roster: Ronald Humphrey (first-round submission of Alexis Miranda), Sean Clancy Jr., Nick Galanti and Guilherme Uriel, the quartet all winning by finish.
