Top Story

One email, root on the gateway: Cisco's Secure Email Gateway zero-day is being exploited now

Cisco published an advisory on 14 September 2026 for CVE-2026-76461, a SQL injection flaw in Cisco AsyncOS Software for Cisco Secure Email Gateway carrying a CVSS v3.1 base score of 9.8. An unauthenticated, remote attacker can execute arbitrary commands with root privileges on an affected appliance.

The trigger is the appliance doing its job: reporting says the flaw is reached by sending a specially crafted email through a vulnerable gateway, so exploitation needs no access to an administrative interface and no credentials. Cisco confirmed attackers had already used it as a zero-day. CISA added it to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog the same day, 14 September, and set federal civilian agencies a remediation date of 17 September 2026.

Fixed releases named in reporting on the advisory: 15.5.5-0141, 16.0.4-3021 and 16.5.0-780. All deployed versions are described as affected if not moved to one of those. Senserva's tracker records the CVE being scored on 15 September (CVSS 0.0 → 9.8, EPSS first set at 2.2%) and revised to EPSS 2.0% on 16 September; it currently sits at #3 on that tracker's hottest-CVE list.

Patch Priority

Overdue: CVE-2026-76461 — Cisco Secure Email Gateway (AsyncOS)

Federal remediation date 17 September 2026, now past due. Move to AsyncOS 15.5.5-0141, 16.0.4-3021 or 16.5.0-780 on every Secure Email Gateway appliance and verify the deployment, then treat unpatched appliances as potentially already compromised rather than merely exposed — Cisco has confirmed pre-patch exploitation. This is the same date carried in the KEV section below.

Threat Actor Spotlight

RansomwareUAT-11988

A Qilin affiliate that walked in through a firewall manager

Cisco Talos has confirmed that two patched Secure Firewall Management Center (FMC) flaws — CVE-2026-20079, a maximum-severity authentication bypass at CVSS 10.0 allowing an unauthenticated remote attacker to execute script files and obtain root, and CVE-2026-20316, a static-credential issue permitting login as a low-privileged account — have been exploited by three separate threat clusters. One of them, tracked as UAT-11988, logged in with the static credentials, ran domain enumeration and credential theft, deployed antivirus killers, and ultimately dropped the Qilin ransomware family. Talos places the start of in-the-wild exploitation in August 2026, with other activity overlapping the Russian military-linked Sandworm group and the deployment of Cyclops Blink malware. Compromised FMC devices were used for web shells, credential theft, reverse shells and proxies.

Breaches & Incidents

NewBrowser / AI

BragJack turns a browser extension against the AI assistant beside it

Forever Security researcher Gal Weizman demonstrated a single extension, with browser-specific rules, seizing the trusted channels used by AI assistants across five Chromium environments: Chrome, Edge, Opera Neon, Perplexity Comet and Claude in Chrome. The technique, Prompt Forcing, does not hide instructions in web content; it supplies commands straight to privileged browser components using content scripts and the declarativeNetRequest API. Demonstrated impact: reading local files, browsing history and screenshots, and forcing agents to exfiltrate email summaries. The work earned over $20,000 in bounties and two CVEs.

New23M records

Gyazo: 23 million user records exposed

Japanese software company Helpfeel notified users of its Gyazo screenshot-and-clip sharing service on 18 September 2026 that a breach had compromised 23 million user records. Attackers are reported to have gained unauthorised access by exploiting a vulnerability in the service's image upload server.

Network edge

Three clusters, one firewall manager

The Cisco FMC exploitation detailed above is an incident as well as an actor story: Talos describes web shells, stolen credentials, reverse shells and proxies planted on compromised management appliances, with ransomware in one cluster and state-linked implants in another. The management plane, not the firewall data plane, was the way in.

Pattern

Two Cisco appliance zero-days inside three days

CISA added CVE-2026-76461 (Secure Email Gateway) on 14 September and CVE-2026-76460 (Identity Services Engine, CVSS 10.0) on 16 September. Both are Cisco appliances that sit at a trust boundary, both are confirmed exploited, and both federal deadlines — 17 and 19 September — have now passed.
