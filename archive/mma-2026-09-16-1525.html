Top Story

Fight week in Los Angeles: Joshua Van defends the flyweight title against Alexandre Pantoja on Saturday

UFC 331 lands at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles on Saturday 19 September, with prelims at 6:00 p.m. ET and the main card at 9:00 p.m. ET on Paramount+. The champion is a narrow favourite in the rematch: Van −132, Pantoja +113 — the BetRivers number on Forbes’ 15 September fight-week board, re-confirmed in odds coverage read this run.

The first meeting was over almost before it started. Van took the belt at UFC 323 in December 2025 by technical knockout 26 seconds into round one, after Pantoja sustained an arm injury — a result that leaves the rematch carrying most of the questions the first fight never got to ask.

The week’s schedule is set: the pre-fight press conference is Thursday 17 September at 8:00 p.m. ET at the arena and on the UFC’s YouTube channel, and ceremonial weigh-ins follow Friday 18 September at 8:00 p.m. ET. In the co-main, Arman Tsarukyan meets Maurício Ruffy at lightweight over five non-title rounds.

Around the Sport

  • The heavyweight title is vacant. Tom Aspinall vacated on 14 September over ongoing eye problems that trace to the eye pokes in his no-contest with Ciryl Gane at UFC 321; he is not retiring and intends to return once cleared. Gane holds the interim belt after his second-round knockout of Alex Pereira at Freedom 250. Reporting expects Gane to be promoted to undisputed, but the UFC has made no announcement, so the board below keeps the undisputed title vacant.
  • UFC 332 will be the first numbered event whose main card airs on CBS — a broadcast first for a numbered UFC event.
  • The women’s flyweight title is vacant and Natália Silva vs. Wang Cong will fill it on 3 October. Valentina Shevchenko vacated while injured; the UFC has said she is guaranteed a title shot when cleared.
  • No fresh pound-for-pound movement was returned this run, and none is asserted. The most recent reading carried on this site is dated 25 August 2026.
