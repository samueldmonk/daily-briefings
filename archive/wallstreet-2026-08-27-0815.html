The Lead

New · 8:06 AM ETPre-open

Nvidia's blowout quarter puts a bid under tech futures before the open

As of roughly 8:06 AM ET, ahead of Thursday's open, S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 futures were higher while Dow futures slipped after Nvidia's second-quarter report landed Wednesday evening. Bloomberg reported Nasdaq 100 contracts up about 1% in early Thursday trade after CFO Colette Kress signalled strong sales growth into fiscal 2028.

The quarter itself, from Nvidia's own filing: revenue of $96.2 billion, up 106% year over year and 18% from the prior quarter; Data Center revenue of $89.0 billion, up 117%; GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins both 75.0%; and diluted EPS of $2.46 GAAP / $2.22 non-GAAP. Management guided third-quarter revenue to $108 billion, plus or minus 2%, with non-GAAP gross margin near 74% — a figure that excludes any Data Center compute revenue from China, where Hopper shipments were under 1% of Data Center revenue in the quarter. CNBC reported CEO Jensen Huang forecasting roughly 70% revenue growth in fiscal 2028, well above estimates.

That follows a flat-to-lower Wednesday session: the S&P 500 closed at 7,675.70, down 0.02%, with the Dow off 0.08% and the Nasdaq Composite down 0.16%.

NewSoftware

Salesforce (CRM) — double-digit after-hours pop

Revenue of $11.35 billion, up 11% year over year and ahead of a $11.32 billion consensus. Full-year fiscal 2027 revenue guidance was raised to $46.1–$46.4 billion from $45.9–$46.2 billion. Agentforce and Data 360 annual recurring revenue reached nearly $3.9 billion, up more than 210%; Agentforce ARR alone topped $1.5 billion, up more than 240%. The stock traded to $231.70 after hours, +$26.08 / +12.68%; a separate tally put the move at +11.8%.
