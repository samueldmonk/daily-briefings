Top Story

CISA adds two actively-exploited Linux kernel flaws, both due 21 September

CISA added two vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog today on evidence of active exploitation, and both carry a federal remediation due date of 21 September 2026 — a three-day window, the same shape as Wednesday's Cisco addition (added 16 Sep, due 19 Sep). The Acronis entry added the same day has no due date confirmed by anything read this run, so none is stated anywhere on this page.

CVE-2026-53266, a Linux kernel out-of-bounds write, sits in the ebtables SNAT target: an ARP sender-hardware-address rewrite can write directly into a nonlinear socket-buffer fragment backed by a splice-imported file page. A proof of concept is available, and CVE Security lists the score as CVSS 8.8.

CVE-2025-39964 is a race condition in the kernel's cryptographic user API: the AF_ALG datapath allowed two writers on the same socket, so request payloads from concurrent writes interleave unpredictably, which can corrupt cryptographic results or cause denial of service. The fix adds a ctx->write field marking exclusive write ownership so concurrent writes to one af_alg socket are disallowed.

Severity conflict, printed rather than smoothed: for CVE-2025-39964 the sources read this run disagree — CVE Security lists 7.8 (High) and Strix lists 3.3. Neither is presented here as the score. The governing directive is BOD 26-04, "Prioritizing Security Updates Based on Risk," which supersedes BOD 22-01's fixed three-week window and is what makes a three-day deadline coherent rather than a transcription error.

Patch Priority

Patch Cisco ISE before tomorrow — CVE-2026-76460, CVSS 10.0, exploited in the wild  

The single most urgent action for defenders today remains CVE-2026-76460, an authentication bypass in Cisco Identity Services Engine and ISE-PIC arising from incorrect use of privileged APIs. Cisco rates it CVSS 3.1 10.0 (AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:C/C:H/I:H/A:H) and has issued emergency fixes. CISA added it to the KEV catalog on 16 September and set federal remediation for Friday, 19 September 2026. ISE sits in front of network access for the whole estate, so an authentication bypass here is a bypass of everything behind it.

Threat Actor Spotlight

NewChina-aligned APT

PeckBirdy — espionage C2 hidden behind casino and adult sites

PeckBirdy is a JavaScript-based command-and-control framework used by China-aligned APT groups since 2023, with its infrastructure concealed inside low-quality Chinese-language casino and adult websites (Infoblox research, reported by The Register on 15 September). Campaigns have hit corporate and government organisations across Asia, with education, IT, banking, financial services and government among the observed sectors. In one campaign attackers injected scripts into gambling sites that loaded PeckBirdy and served fake software-update pages to lure downloads; the framework lets operators remotely deliver and execute JavaScript and abuse Windows living-off-the-land binaries including MSHTA and Windows Script Host. Detection note: Infoblox telemetry found just over 3% of enterprise customers resolved at least one PeckBirdy C2 domain — well beyond the campaign's apparent Asian victim focus — and says resolving three to ten distinct PeckBirdy C2 domains is a meaningful signal that a network may be compromised.

Breaches & Incidents

Law enforcement

US seizes NightmareStresser, one of the longest-running DDoS-for-hire services

The FBI seized two domains behind NightmareStresser under court authorisation on 15 September, in a takedown reported this week and accused of facilitating hundreds of thousands of attacks worldwide. FBI Anchorage led the seizure alongside the Royal Canadian Mounted Police's Federal Policing Northwest Region, as part of the long-running international Operation PowerOFF campaign against booter and stresser infrastructure. Research published by Searchlight Cyber in 2023 found the service had more than 566,000 registered users, 52 servers and 28 attack methods, with attacks priced from €25 to €19,999 (about $28.70 to $22,900). The DOJ had previously seized another NightmareStresser domain in 2022. Not tagged New: this site first carried the item in its 17 September 12:01 PM edition.

NewSurveillance data

Flock camera data reaches reporters after a camera is physically removed

A group calling itself stegan0gram physically removed a Flock automated licence-plate-reader camera, accessed the data on it and shared the findings with media. Wired and 404 Media reported that the camera's software can generate dozens of images from a single vehicle, and that in 21 days the camera photographed 50,200 vehicles and generated 1.6 million photos — capturing details as fine as an American flag patch on a motorcyclist's bag. Flock told The National News Desk that "the unauthorized removal and tampering of a Flock camera is illegal," and said it did not have enough detail to assess the claims being made. The incident turned on physical removal and local storage rather than a compromise of Flock's cloud platform.

Advisory

Windows 11 domain sign-ins break after the September updates

Microsoft published a temporary fix on Wednesday for a known issue that stops Windows 11 users from logging in with valid domain credentials after installing the September 2026 security updates. Not an intrusion, but a live operational hazard for any organisation that has already deployed this month's cumulative update — and one worth checking before rolling the Linux-kernel and Cisco fixes above into the same maintenance window.
