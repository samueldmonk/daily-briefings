Top Story

Silva submits Delgado, then aims past the whole division: “There's a new champion in town”

Jean Silva def. Jose Miguel Delgado, submission (rear-naked choke), 2:57 of round three — the Noche UFC main event, Saturday 12 September, Desert Diamond Arena, Glendale, Arizona. Delgado, who is from Arizona and unranked, had stepped in on less than a month's notice for an injured Yair Rodriguez.

Silva did not wait for a matchmaker. He is ranked No. 6 at featherweight in both the Meta UFC rankings and the media rankings, and he had already said before the fight, “Is there someone else to fight instead of me? Then I'm next.” Afterwards he aimed the message forward: “This message is not for the division, it's not for anybody out there, it's for the next champion. Whoever holds that belt, the next champion is right here. There's a new champion in town.”

He also made a prediction against the reigning champion. Asked who wins Alexander Volkanovski vs. Movsar Evloev at UFC 333 on 24 October in Abu Dhabi, Silva picked the undefeated Russian: “I think Evloev wins. It'll be me and him. But if Evloev pulls out, then it's me and Volkanovski. It doesn't matter. I'm next.”

The queue is contested. Aljamain Sterling, who wants the same shot, has called Silva out for a title eliminator at UFC 334 at Madison Square Garden. Silva's reply, per MMASucka: “Aljo, you're soft.”

Prospect Watch

NewRecord

Tommy McMillen broke Nate Diaz's three-round striking record — in his second UFC fight

McMillen landed 252 significant strikes on 517 attempts against Marwan Rahiki, breaking the UFC's three-round promotional record previously held by Nate Diaz. The total ranks third all-time in UFC history behind Max Holloway's 290 against Calvin Kattar and Rob Font's 271 against Marlon Vera — both of which took five rounds. He did it after being dropped early, rallying to the unanimous decision, and took Fight of the Night for it.
