Top Story

Three days out from Paris, and the UFC has handed a debutant the main event

France's Salahdine Parnasse gets a main event on his promotional debut on Saturday, September 5, against the veteran Dan Hooker, at the Accor Arena in Paris. It is a rare assignment for a newcomer, and the market has taken it seriously: Parnasse opened around −400 and has been bid to as short as −667.

He arrives with a real résumé rather than a developmental one. Parnasse is a former two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion who signed with the UFC in late July 2026, having previously turned the promotion down. He did not come through Dana White's Contender Series — a misattribution this desk published three times in a single edition and has since corrected. He remains a UFC debutant: not a veteran, not a contender, not ranked.

The card also lost a bout this week. Mairon Santos has been removed from his featherweight fight with Nathaniel Wood because of illness. His replacement is Pavel Andrusca, an undefeated UFC newcomer who has finished seven of his eight wins, five of them in the first round.

Start times are unusual for a European card: prelims at 12 PM ET / 9 AM PT, main card at 3 PM ET / 12 PM PT, with the whole card streaming on Paramount+.

Standing correction

Darby's opponent was Patrick Rivera — not the referee

A search summary once rendered Darby's Week 4 win as a TKO "over Kerry Hatley." Hatley is the referee. Darby (8-1) beat Patrick Rivera (13-5); Hatley called in the cageside doctor, who stopped it midway through round three. The correction is restated here rather than silently applied, because the fabricated version was grammatical, named a real person and got the round right.
