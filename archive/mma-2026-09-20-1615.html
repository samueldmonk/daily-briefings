Top Story

The heavyweight belt changed hands without a punch being thrown

During the UFC 331 main-card broadcast on Saturday 19 September, the promotion announced that Ciryl Gane has been elevated from interim to undisputed heavyweight champion, and booked his first defence against Josh Hokit to headline UFC 334 on Saturday 14 November at Madison Square Garden in New York.

The elevation follows Tom Aspinall’s vacating of the title on Monday 14 September over complications from the eye injuries he suffered against Gane in October 2025 — a title fight ruled a no contest after a double eye poke in round one. Aspinall’s right eye is reported to be back to “the same as day one,” with no timetable for a return.

The reversal is part of the story. The promotion landed less than 24 hours after Dana White told Sportsnet on Friday that Gane would not be elevated without fighting again. Gane became a two-time interim champion in June with a knockout of Alex Pereira. Also announced for UFC 334: Kayla Harrison vs Amanda Nunes as the co-main event, and White has publicly aired his misgivings about the possibility of Hokit walking out of MSG with the belt — “I got a lot of thoughts.”

Bonuses & Payouts

Over $500,000 paid

Post-fight bonuses

Fight of the Night: Joshua Van and Alexandre Pantoja, $100,000 each. Performance of the Night: Arman Tsarukyan ($100,000) and Casey O’Neill ($100,000) for her first-round armbar. Sean Sharaf received the $50,000 “Proper Chaos” / Mobland bonus, which Dana White then doubled on the spot to $100,000. $25,000 finish bonuses went to Patricio Pitbull, Marlon Vera, Ryan Gandra and Joanderson Brito.

Disclosed purses

The money on the night

Alexandre Pantoja took the biggest disclosed purse of the evening at $650,000, despite losing. Champion Joshua Van earned a flat $500,000. Patricio Pitbull and Marlon Vera were next at $400,000 apiece. Gable Steveson earned $160,000 for a fight that lasted 12 seconds.

Attributed, not asserted

The Sharaf upset

Sharaf, 0-2 in the UFC going into Saturday, knocked Steveson out with the second punch he threw, a left hook, 12 seconds in. ESPN calls it the third-fastest finish in UFC heavyweight history and the first MMA defeat for the 2020 Olympic wrestling gold medallist. A separate account called it “the biggest upset in UFC history” — that superlative is carried as one outlet’s characterisation, not as fact. Bloody Elbow reports White doubled the bonus to help Sharaf clear a $688,000 debt.

Prospect10-0

Josh Hokit — from Contender Series to a title shot at MSG

Unbeaten at 10-0 with four wins in four UFC appearances since earning his contract on Dana White’s Contender Series. In November he headlines Madison Square Garden for the undisputed heavyweight title. White has said out loud that he dreads the possibility of Hokit winning it.

Week 6 ran Tuesday 15 September at the Meta APEX. Four of the five winners were handed contracts; every fighter who scored a stoppage got one, while the lone unanimous-decision winner was offered a return to the series instead. Week 8 falls on 29 September.
