Top Story

UFC 333: Volkanovski stops unbeaten Evloev in Round 2 to retain featherweight title

Volkanovski retains. Alexander Volkanovski defeated Movsar Evloev by second-round TKO at 4:54 in the UFC 333 main event at Etihad Arena, Abu Dhabi — the official result per MMA Fighting. Evloev had moments early, landing a left hand and knees in the clinch and briefly getting the champion down in the first, and opened the second with a right hand and a takedown; Volkanovski rolled to top position, then clipped Evloev with a right and a left on the feet, hurt him with another right, dropped him with a left to the chin and finished with ground and pound (MMA Fighting via Yahoo Sports). Evloev entered 20-0, having won all 10 of his fights inside the Octagon (UFC.com; Uncrowned via Yahoo Sports). The win is Volkanovski’s ninth in UFC featherweight title fights, passing José Aldo, and his first finish since his 2023 win over Yair Rodriguez (MMA Mania via Yahoo Sports); ESPN, which lists the records at 29-4 and 20-1, notes it was his second featherweight title defense of the year and that he outlanded Evloev 45-37 in total strikes per UFC Stats (ESPN). Pre-fight, The National had noted a win would carry the 38-year-old past Aldo (The National, Oct. 21). Sherdog’s recap confirms the stoppage — referee Marc Goddard stepped in at 4:54 of the second round as Volkanovski swarmed Evloev with punches — and lists the champion at 29-4 and Evloev at 20-1; Volkanovski also earned a $100,000 Performance of the Night bonus and teased that he won’t retire after the win (Sherdog recap; Sherdog bonuses).

Yan defends. Bantamweight champion Petr Yan beat Merab Dvalishvili by unanimous decision in their trilogy bout, with all three cageside judges scoring it 48-47. Dvalishvili took the first two rounds with takedowns and heavier output; Yan came alive in the third, hurting Dvalishvili with punches and a strike to the body, then outstruck him and stuffed his takedowns over the fourth and fifth to win three rounds to two (Heavy). UFC.com’s official results confirm the identical 48-47 cards and that Yan “successfully defended the UFC bantamweight title” (UFC.com).

Earlier on the main card. Lone’er Kavanagh stopped Ramazan Temirov by first-round TKO at 4:28 at flyweight: after Temirov caught him with a right hand, Kavanagh answered with a left uppercut and a flurry of punches that put Temirov down before the referee stepped in (SSBCrack News). Before that, Alexander Volkov beat Rizvan Kuniev by unanimous decision (29-28, 30-27, 30-27) at heavyweight, nearly finishing a rear-naked choke at the final buzzer, then asked Dana White for a title shot after his third straight win (MMA Junkie via Yahoo Sports) (Sherdog play-by-play). Aaron Pico opened the main card by stopping Losene Keita via TKO (punches) at 1:14 of the first round at featherweight — per Sherdog, the first legitimate stoppage loss of Keita’s 20-fight career (Sherdog play-by-play).

Prelims. Dominick Reyes closed the prelims by stopping Azamat Murzakanov in the first round at light heavyweight: after a left head kick landed only as a glancing blow, Reyes dropped Murzakanov with a straight left and finished with a series of punches until the referee stepped in — officially TKO (head kick and punches) at 2:40 of round one (Sherdog). Before that, undefeated light heavyweight Abdul Rakhman Yakhyaev stopped Nikita Krylov in the first round: after losing his grip on a D’Arce choke, he landed a knee to the head with Krylov down against the cage, then follow-up punches to end the fight — officially TKO (knee and punches) at 2:38 of round one (Sherdog). Earlier on the 12 p.m. ET prelims, Abus Magomedov submitted Cam Rowston with a guillotine choke at 1:44 of the first round at middleweight, and Grant Dawson beat previously unbeaten Nurullo Aliev by unanimous decision (29-27, 29-27, 29-27) at lightweight after Aliev was docked a point for strikes to the back of the head (UFC.com). Earlier, Colby Thicknesse beat debuting Asaf Chopurov — 11-0 and a former UAE Warriors bantamweight champion entering the night — by split decision (29-28, 29-28, 28-29) at bantamweight, his third straight win, closing out the early prelims (UFC.com) (Cageside Press). Before that, Abubakar Vagaev took a unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28) over Jacob Malkoun at middleweight, although all three Sherdog scorers had Malkoun ahead 29-28. Daniel Santos edged Bolaji Oki by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28) at featherweight. Iasmin Lucindo opened UFC 333 with a unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27) over Nicolle Caliari at a 120.5-pound catchweight after Caliari missed weight by 4.5 pounds, and Kody Steele stopped Noah Gugnon by TKO (punches) at 3:54 of the first round, per Sherdog’s official results (Sherdog play-by-play). Main card order: Pico–Keita, Volkov–Kuniev, Kavanagh–Temirov, then the two title fights (Uncrowned via Yahoo Sports).

Around the Sport

  • Volkanovski “weighing the options”: the 38-year-old said he has no plans to retire after the win, which The National says was his sixth title defence across two reigns and took him past José Aldo for the most title wins in featherweight history, with nine. Besides a possible move up to face lightweight champion Justin Gaethje, Jean Silva or the winner of Aljamain Sterling vs. Kevin Vallejos are potential 145-pound opponents. “I’m in a pretty good position when you’ve got the president saying that you could pretty much do whatever you want,” Volkanovski said (The National, Oct. 25).
  • “Maybe we keep going”: Volkanovski, 38, brushed off retirement questions after improving to 29-4 — “I said if I didn’t take too much damage, we’ll probably keep going, so maybe we keep going” — and said he had promised his wife a quick win after their wedding anniversary on Wednesday. Evloev: “He’s like good wine; older, stronger, better, he’s truly a legend.” It was Volkanovski’s first stoppage win in six fights stretching back to 2023 (ABC News/AAP, Oct. 25).
  • Evloev responds: “Today wasn’t my day… Congratulations to Volk. He was the better man today. Thank you to the UFC for giving me the opportunity to fight for the title,” Movsar Evloev wrote on X after his first career loss; he is now 10-1 in the UFC after 10 straight wins (Heavy).
  • White on Volkanovski: “the guy is an absolute stud, coming in and beating an undefeated, younger (fighter)… He was the aggressive fighter… this guy’s just (expletive) built different,” Dana White told reporters after UFC 333; the 38-year-old has won three straight (Heavy).
  • Volkanovski wants a Gaethje send-off: after the win the 38-year-old floated a non-title “superfight” with lightweight champion Justin Gaethje as a possible retirement fight for both, saying he does not want to hold either division up; Dana White: “When you’ve accomplished what he has accomplished, there’s not much we’re going to say no to with him” (MMA Mania via Yahoo Sports). White also called him “an absolute stud.” Evloev: “He’s truly a legend. I didn’t prove I’m the best in the world. Maybe I’ll try again” (ESPN).
  • Makhachev out until at least May: per the UFC, the welterweight champion was expected to defend at UFC 336 but is unavailable, and will then observe Ramadan beginning in February — hence the Prates–Morales interim bout, which will serve as UFC 336’s co-main event (ESPN).
  • Kavanagh enters the title picture: ESPN says Lone’er Kavanagh’s first-round finish of Temirov puts him into flyweight title talk (ESPN headline).
  • Volkanovski not done: the featherweight champion teased after the win that he won’t retire, per Sherdog (Sherdog). Abus Magomedov, meanwhile, called out an ex-training partner, “Robocop,” after his UFC 333 win (Sherdog headline).
  • Who’s next for Yan? Top contender Song Yadong was cageside and made it known he wants the next bantamweight title shot, while Sean O’Malley — who owns a win over Yan — also called the champion out (Forbes via Yahoo Sports, Oct. 24).
  • UFC 336 headliners announced (Oct. 24): flyweight champion Joshua Van defends against Manel Kape, and Carlos Prates meets unbeaten Michael Morales (19-0, 7-0 UFC) for the interim welterweight title — the winner earning the right to face Islam Makhachev — at Scotiabank Arena, Toronto, on Jan. 30, 2027 (UFC.com). It follows the hoped-for Makhachev–Prates title fight falling through; Ariel Helwani on X, Oct. 23: “Date doesn’t work for Makhachev anymore. Unclear why” (Athlon Sports, Oct. 23).
  • Dana White on Farid Basharat’s release: “There’s definitely a reason” (Cageside Press).
  • “UFC Wanted 205 lbs Grand Prix, Says Coach of Dricus du Plessis” (Heavy).
  • Steven Koslow revealed he tore his meniscus ahead of UFC Edmonton (Cageside Press); Su Young You targets Alexandre Topuria next (Cageside Press).
  • Jasudavicius “inches closer to title” after the Blanchfield win (Cageside Press).
