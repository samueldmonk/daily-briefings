Top Story

The rarest booking in the sport: a debutant headlining his own arrival — and Parnasse made weight first

Salahdine Parnasse hit 155 pounds on the dot Friday morning ahead of the five-round main event, and Dan Hooker made the same mark; neither had any issue. Every other athlete on the card also made weight, so all 14 fights are official. That is stated this run by three independent outlets — UFC.com’s official weigh-in results page, Sherdog and Yahoo Sports.

The framing that is new this run is what the booking itself means. Parnasse “joins a rare class of UFC debutants who headlined their first event,” and is described as one of the promotion’s biggest free-agent entrances in recent years. The 28-year-old arrives on a five-fight win streak that includes knockouts of Kenneth Cross, Marcin Held and Marian Ziolkowski, and is a former KSW double champion at featherweight and lightweight.

Hooker arrives from the opposite direction. He is reported to be in need of a win after back-to-back setbacks against Arman Tsarukyan and Benoit St. Denis. The order of those two defeats is not stated by any source fetched this run, so it is not asserted here.

The market has an opinion. Parnasse is −667 and Hooker +417 at one book, with −649 / +420 and −600 / +440 elsewhere — roughly 81.8% / 18.2% with the vig removed. The opener was −357 / +275. A debutant is a heavy favourite over a man with thirty-eight professional fights.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY. UFC.com renders every individual weight as an empty field, so Hooker’s and Parnasse’s 155 lb remain the only weights asserted anywhere on this page. That every other athlete made weight is reported, not a figure this page has seen.

Refused this run, for the fortieth time: Khamzat Chimaev at middleweight

A “current UFC champions September 2026” aggregation returned this run listed Khamzat Chimaev as middleweight champion, dating the reign to August 16, 2025. He is a former champion. Sean Strickland took the belt by split decision at UFC 328 on May 9, 2026 at the Prudential Center in Newark — scorecards 48-47, 47-48, 48-47 — as a +400-and-up underdog, becoming a two-time middleweight champion and the first man to defeat Chimaev. That was re-verified against fresh event reporting this run, not carried from memory. The aggregation was correct in every other cell — Aspinall plus Gane interim, Ulberg, Makhachev, Gaethje, Volkanovski, Yan, Van, Harrison, Shevchenko, Dern — which is exactly what makes this error so durable. It also carried a “Women’s Featherweight — vacant” row, which is not one of the eleven active belts on this board and has not been added.
