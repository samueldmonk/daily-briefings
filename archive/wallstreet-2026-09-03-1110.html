The Lead

Stocks open higher as Waller opens the door to a September hold — readings as of ~10:05–10:30 AM ET

Fed Governor Christopher Waller said Thursday that next week’s inflation report will largely determine whether he supports a rate increase later this month. “If this continues in the data due over the next two weeks, I would be inclined to support holding the target for the federal funds rate at its current setting,” he said — while adding that if the August figures show recent progress has reversed, a hike at the September 15–16 FOMC meeting could be appropriate. August inflation data lands September 11.

Markets moved on it: Treasury yields dropped to session lows and equity futures rose after the remarks, with traders dialling back September hike expectations to roughly a coin flip in fed funds futures and swaps. The tone reads as more dovish than Chair Kevin Warsh’s Jackson Hole speech, which had pushed hike odds higher.

Index readings differ by source and none is adopted here. The Yahoo Finance live blog has the S&P 500 +0.57%, the Dow +0.80%, the Nasdaq +0.64% and the Russell 2000 +1.13%; a second return gives the Dow +0.8% with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite +0.5%; a third has the S&P 500 +0.3% and the Dow +0.6%; a fourth puts the Nasdaq Composite +1%. Every reading agrees on direction — higher. They are not all the same moment: some are pre-open readings and some are taken after the bell, and the sources do not all quote the same index, which is enough to account for the spread.

Movers & Drivers

NewM&AAI

Nvidia buys Hugging Face

Nvidia confirmed a definitive agreement for the open-source AI platform Hugging Face. The headline number is reported several ways and none is adopted here: Bloomberg and CNBC say about $13 billion / almost $13 billion; Fox Business and Variety say $12.9 billion; Investing.com gives $12.93 billion plus roughly $1 billion in employee retention, about $14 billion all in; CNBC breaks it out as roughly $11.9 billion to shareholders plus up to $1 billion in equity-based retention awards. Jensen Huang says Hugging Face will “remain an open platform for the entire AI ecosystem.” The platform counts more than 18 million developers, researchers and creators sharing 3 million+ models, 500,000 data sets and 1 million applications. It is Nvidia's second-biggest purchase, after $20 billion for Groq assets at the end of last year. The stock was +0.6% pre-open on one reading and +1% premarket on another; JPMorgan reaffirmed Overweight and a $320 price target.

Earnings beatSoftware

Snowflake — a 24% move before the bell

Shares were up more than 24% pre-market Thursday. That is a different window from Wednesday's after-hours move, reported at 22% by CNBC and 23% by Yahoo — two windows, not a range. Q2: adjusted EPS $0.62 against a $0.45 consensus on revenue of $1.55 billion versus $1.48 billion expected, up 35%; product revenue +37% to $1.49 billion. The FY2027 product-revenue guide went to $6.07 billion from $5.84 billion. AI products accounted for “approximately half of the acceleration”: coding assistant Cortex Code passed 9,100 accounts after adding more than 2,000 in the quarter, and enterprise chatbot CoWork reached 5,800 accounts. At least 22 brokerages raised targets, Wells Fargo to a Street-high $525.

GuidanceSemis

Broadcom — a record quarter, a guide the market didn't like

Fiscal Q3 revenue of $29.6 billion, up 86% year over year, with AI semiconductor revenue of $16.7 billion, up 221% y/y and 54% q/q, and record free cash flow of $13.7 billion. The fiscal Q4 guide is $34.8 billion — a 93% annual increase, but below a $35.03 billion consensus — with semiconductor revenue of $26.1 billion and AI semiconductors of $21.7 billion (+236%). Longer term the company points to $58 billion of AI revenue in fiscal 2026 and roughly $115 billion and $230 billion in fiscal 2027 and 2028. The stock read -2.5% premarket on CNBC's list and -0.66% in a later quote; two windows, neither adopted.
