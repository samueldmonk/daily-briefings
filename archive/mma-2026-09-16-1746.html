Top Story

Van defends against Pantoja on Saturday — and the market has flipped since the first fight

UFC 331: Van vs. Pantoja 2 is set for Saturday, 19 September 2026 at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. It is the promotion’s sixth visit to Los Angeles and its first since UFC 227 in August 2018 — an eight-year gap.

The main event is a UFC flyweight championship rematch. Reigning champion Joshua Van faces former champion Alexandre Pantoja, whom he beat for the belt at UFC 323 in December 2025 by technical knockout 26 seconds into the first round.

Which makes the betting line the interesting part. Van is a narrow favourite: −135 with Pantoja +110 at BetMGM; −130 at LetsBetMD, −132 at BetRivers, −136 at FanDuel; Forbes reports −132 / +113. A champion who needed 26 seconds the first time is priced barely better than a coin flip the second.

The direction of travel explains it. The early market had made Pantoja the slight favourite at −120, with Van at +100; fight-week money has moved toward Van. The market opened by discounting a 26-second finish and has spent the week partially conceding it.

In the co-main, Arman Tsarukyan meets Mauricio Ruffy, with Tsarukyan chasing the title shot that has repeatedly slipped away from him.

Around the Sport

  • UFC 331 is a homecoming of sorts. Six visits to Los Angeles in the promotion’s history, and the last one was UFC 227 in August 2018.
  • The flyweight belt has turned over quickly. Van took it from Pantoja in 26 seconds in December 2025, defended it against Tatsuro Taira in May, and defends again on Saturday against the man he took it from.
  • Two vacant belts. Heavyweight sits vacant, and the women’s flyweight title is contested at UFC 332 on 3 October after Valentina Shevchenko withdrew with a ligament injury to her back and shoulder expected to sideline her for a year.
  • Marlon Vera is booked against Charles Jourdain at UFC 331. Vera is a former bantamweight title challenger — he has not held the belt.
  • Contender Series season 10 has set a record for contract offers, with four more signings on 15 September alone.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. Natália Silva carries a 14-fight win streak into her title shot, with wins over three former champions — Namajunas, Grasso and Andrade — in her last three outings. Wang Cong has won four straight in the Octagon, most recently over Tracy Cortez at UFC 329 in July. Arman Tsarukyan returns at UFC 331 still seeking a title shot.

How this board was derived, and one source problem worth naming. Every belt here is re-derived from the most recent title-changing event rather than copied from any standing list. ESPN’s “Current and all-time UFC champions” page, read this edition, returned its divisions shifted by one row — it labelled Carlos Ulberg (11 April 2026) as heavyweight and Sean Strickland (9 May 2026) as light heavyweight. The names and the dates are right; the division labels are not. Ulberg won the light heavyweight belt at UFC 327 on 11 April and Strickland won middleweight at UFC 328 on 9 May. The same page states the heavyweight division is vacant, which is carried here. That reading is a cross-check, never the source of truth. Earlier runs this month saw the same page seat Alex Pereira at light heavyweight and Khamzat Chimaev at middleweight on results that predate UFC 327 and UFC 328; neither appears on this board.
