Top Story

GitLab patches a CVSS 10.0 unauthenticated file read — and watchTowr saw probes the same morning

CVE-2026-85706 is a path-traversal issue in GitLab's repository commits API that lets an unauthenticated user read arbitrary files from the server. GitLab attributes it to “improper path confinement and missing authentication enforcement in the repository commits API.” It affects Community and Enterprise Edition from 18.7 before 19.1.8, 19.2 before 19.2.6 and 19.3 before 19.3.2.

Exposure-management firm watchTowr reported active in-the-wild probes from 06:00 UTC on 11 September, saying an external attacker can read log files and GitLab configuration files to obtain credentials and secrets. Jake Knott, its head of threat intelligence, notes exploitation needs only one public project to exist, and calls this the second critical GitLab flaw in recent weeks after the GraphQL code-injection bug CVE-2026-19478.

The same patch releases (19.3.2 / 19.2.6 / 19.1.8) also fix CVE-2026-87719 (CVSS 9.9), an insecure-deserialization bug in GitLab EE that could let an authenticated user with Duo Chat access read Advanced Search instance configuration and credentials via a crafted GraphQL subscription argument.

Detection tip carried from the source, not invented: Knott suggests reviewing logs for HTTP POST requests to /api/v4/projects/{id}/repository/commits/ URIs containing file.Path parameters.

Patch Priority

Do this first — deadline tomorrow
Patch CVE-2026-85706 on every self-managed GitLab CE/EE instance, or remove public access if it is not required. CISA added it to the KEV catalog on 11 September and set the FCEB remediation deadline at 14 September 2026 1 day left. CISA also flagged it as requiring forensic triage under BOD 26-04, which reflects that vulnerable servers may already have been read before patching — so patching alone does not close the incident.

Threat Actor Spotlight

NewIran-nexusEspionage

Nimbus Manticore — recruiter lures that ship cross-platform RATs

Kaspersky attributes two previously undocumented malware families, NodeRabbit and PollCat, to the Iranian group Nimbus Manticore — also tracked as Iranian Dream Job and Mirage Kitten. Both are cross-platform RATs written in Node.js and JavaScript, delivered inside trojanized coding-challenge archives sent through LinkedIn and other job platforms.

The first NodeRabbit sample came off a system in Afghanistan, with later sightings in Egypt and Ethiopia. In the Afghan case the actor posed as a talent-acquisition specialist at a major technology company; the archive's server.js, described in the instructions as “bug-free,” imported a trojanized npm package (colorized_terminal 2.1.0) bundled in node_modules rather than published to npm, launching the implant as a detached process. NodeRabbit supports 11 commands and beacons to three Azure-hosted C2 addresses; PollCat supports 22.

Persistence is per-OS — a Run key on Windows, cron on Linux, a launch agent on macOS — and one variant impersonates a Microsoft Edge update, another Intel's Driver & Support Assistant. PollCat also hunts folders matching 24 hard-coded security and software vendor names, inventorying only the folder root.

Breaches & Incidents

NewCredential theftSupply chain

JetBrains Cadence breached through its own unpatched TeamCity

JetBrains is telling Cadence users to revoke and rotate every credential after attackers exploited CVE-2026-63077 (CVSS 9.8), the TeamCity deserialization flaw, to reach its environment. The intrusion ran 8–24 August 2026 and was discovered on 23 August. Confirmed accessed: usernames, real names, email addresses, last-login timestamps and last-accessed IPs; a full 2024 Cadence server backup; multiple AWS IAM users and secrets extracted from that backup, including IAM users belonging to JetBrains employees; and files in S3 buckets in JetBrains AWS accounts.

Source code synchronised from PyCharm projects may also have been reached. CISA added CVE-2026-63077 to KEV on 5 August; JetBrains conceded the server “should have been patched” under its own vulnerability response and did not say why it was not. api.cadence.jetbrains.com is now offline and all plugin access tokens invalidated.

NewCourt recordsThird party

Thomson Reuters C-Track: sealed court data may be in the haul

Thomson Reuters disclosed on 2 September that an unauthorised party obtained files from C-Track, the court case-management platform sold by its West Publishing unit, in March 2026. West Publishing discovered the activity on 30 June. A subset of records may hold names, Social Security numbers, driver's licence numbers, dates of birth, medical and health-insurance information; the notice adds that “certain confidential, redacted or sealed information may have been impacted for certain affected courts.”

The West Publishing notice lists 24 court bodies in 11 states and the US Virgin Islands; a separate Canadian notice names three Ontario courts. Minnesota is absent from that list, yet the Minnesota Judicial Branch said on 2 September its appellate data was exposed, terminated Thomson Reuters' access and required password changes. Montana and Alabama describe backup data on vendor servers — Alabama says the backup was one it “had neither requested nor known about” — while the Supreme Court of Ohio says it was told the access took place on the production platform. Montana dates unauthorised access to that storage as 1 March–29 June. As of 3 September no party had published a victim count, the method, or the actor.

NewChina-nexusNetwork infrastructure

Fire Ant ran purpose-built implants on IOS XR routers

Sygnia reports that the China-nexus actor Fire Ant, first documented in 2025, ran implants on Cisco IOS XR routers that suppressed syslog delivery, filtered show command output and supported a hidden GRE tunnel. The actor captured packets from routers and uploaded them to external FTP servers, and ran connection attempts and port scans against connected systems associated with critical infrastructure.

The investigation started from a tunnel interface active on a router with no running configuration or commit history to explain it — which, Sygnia wrote, meant the device's operational state “could no longer be trusted to match the configuration and audit records.” Sygnia did not identify the initial access vector or name a vulnerability, and this briefing does not supply one.

Active exploitationCarried

GitLab probes began within hours — and mass exploitation is the expected next step

watchTowr's read is that “the transition of this vulnerability to indiscriminate mass exploitation is likely not far away, and defenders have limited time to act.” The appeal is structural rather than incidental: unauthorised GitLab access yields source code, CI/CD secrets, credentials, and the ability to inject code into build pipelines and poison anything downstream.

CISA KEV & Federal Deadlines

  • CVE-2026-85706 (GitLab CE/EE path traversal, CVSS 10.0) — added 11 September, due 14 September 1 day left. Flagged as requiring forensic triage under BOD 26-04.
  • CVE-2026-85046 (Google Chromium V8 type confusion, CVSS 8.8) — added 4 September, due 18 September 5 days left. A fourteen-day window.
  • CVE-2026-85880 (Windows heap-based buffer overflow), CVE-2026-81963 (Windows link following) and CVE-2026-86218 (N-able N-central static code injection) — added 8 September, due 22 September 9 days left.
  • CVE-2026-75650 (Adobe Commerce / Magento template-engine injection, Adobe CVSS 10.0) — added in the same 8 September batch but due 11 September overdue by 2 days. The batch split three days versus fourteen; the CISA-stated date per CVE is the only safe one to use.
  • CVE-2026-87491 (Chromium V8, fixed in Chrome 153.0.8010.36) — due 23 September 10 days left.
  • The old three-week rule is gone. BOD 26-04, issued 10 June 2026, supersedes and revokes BOD 22-01 and replaces the uniform window with a 16-tier risk matrix keyed to public exposure, KEV membership and whether exploitation can be fully automated; the most dangerous tier is three days plus mandatory forensic triage. Agencies must be operating to these timelines by 7 December 2026. A same-week deadline is now normal and must not be “corrected” upward.
  • KEV entry requires evidence of exploitation, so a severe flaw with no confirmed exploitation carries no federal deadline at all. CVE-2026-20212 — unauthenticated root RCE at 9.8 — is not in KEV, because Cisco reports no known malicious use. Absence from KEV is not a severity judgement.
