The Lead

At the bell (~4:01 PM ET): Dow +0.69%, S&P 500 ~+0.6%, Nasdaq ~+1% — SpaceX relights the AI trade

NewAt the bell. Yahoo Finance’s live page, stamped 4:01 PM EDT, showed the Dow at 52,857.04, up 361.16 points (+0.69%) — consistent with Tuesday’s 52,495.88 close — with the S&P 500 up roughly 0.6% and the Nasdaq Composite about 1%. Its headline now credits a SpaceX surge of “nearly 8%” (Yahoo Finance, 4:01 PM). Exact S&P 500 and Nasdaq closing levels were not confirmed at publication.

Final-hour read. With “U.S. markets close in 16m” showing, Yahoo Finance’s market strip had the Nasdaq Composite up 271.44 points, or 1.03%, at 26,696.26; the S&P 500 up 43.58, or 0.57%, at 7,756.15; and the Dow up 280.40, or 0.53%, at 52,776.28 — each reconciling with Tuesday’s closes. The Russell 2000 was up 0.30% and the VIX down 3.00% at 16.79 (Yahoo Finance, ~3:44 PM ET). Intraday readings, not closes — the official close could not be confirmed at publication; it will appear in the Weekly Scorecard next edition.

What drove it. SpaceX reported Q3 revenue of $14.6 billion, up 161%, with its Super Intelligence unit (formerly the AI division) bringing in $7.56 billion, and guided Q1 2027 revenue to $33–$37 billion versus estimates near $23 billion; JPMorgan more than doubled its price target to $500. Its gain was its biggest one-day move since Aug. 7 (Benzinga). Elon Musk said SpaceX and Tesla have formed special committees to discuss a potential merger (Yahoo Finance).

NewOil headline. Saudi Arabia’s state news agency reported an attack on an Aramco oil field in the Eastern Region this afternoon; Brent jumped more than $1.50 a barrel within minutes and was on track for its highest close since May 19, while stocks “barely flinched,” Yahoo’s Jared Blikre wrote (Yahoo Finance).

Up next. Tesla and Meta report after the bell, kicking off Big Tech earnings. Analysts expect Tesla adjusted EPS of 45 cents on $28.06 billion of revenue; IBM, Lam Research and Texas Instruments also report (Benzinga).

Movers & Drivers

▲ SPCX

SpaceX +8% to +10% on a blowout Q3

Shares jumped about 10% by midday, adding roughly $200 billion in market value, per Benzinga at 1:02 PM ET (Benzinga); Yahoo’s post-bell headline puts the gain at nearly 8% (Yahoo Finance).

New▲ Cyber

Zscaler, Okta, Cloudflare, CrowdStrike +5%–7% on PHP2Shell

A critical bug in code behind an estimated 70% of websites sent security stocks higher — roughly three to five times their typical daily moves; SentinelOne and Tenable +3%–4%, HACK and CIBR ~+2%; Palo Alto and Fortinet only +1%–2% (Yahoo Finance).

▲ SMCI

Super Micro +9.6% on a $4 billion guidance lift

Fiscal Q1 revenue outlook raised to $18.5–$19.5 billion from $14.5–$15.5 billion; gross-margin forecast 12.0%–12.5% (Benzinga). It jumped as much as 12% intraday; full results Nov. 3 (Yahoo Finance).

▼ NFLX

Netflix −4% to −5%: soft Q4 revenue guide

Q4 revenue guidance of $13.4 billion trailed the $13.5 billion consensus despite an EPS beat; Needham cut to Hold (Benzinga). Yahoo flagged its lowest close since August 2024 in sight, ~51% below its June 2025 record (Yahoo Finance).

▼ T

AT&T −6% on the Starlink threat

Revenue beat and a higher EPS outlook were overshadowed by SpaceX’s mobile ambitions; CEO John Stankey: “I would not over-rotate on what I would call one carrier of low-band spectrum” (Yahoo Finance) (Benzinga).

New▼ WING

Wingstop −6.6%; Abbott −3.5%, DexCom −5.9%

Wingstop slid after a StoneX target cut; Abbott cut its comparable-sales outlook and plans a nutrition spin-off, dragging glucose-monitor rival DexCom (Benzinga).

New▲ ECHO

EchoStar +8.1%, Galaxy Digital +8%

EchoStar, which holds a SpaceX stake, rose in tandem; Galaxy rallied as Bitcoin climbed (Benzinga).
