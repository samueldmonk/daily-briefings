Top Story

Fight eve in Paris: the full card is priced, and Parnasse is a big favourite on debut

UFC Fight Night: Hooker vs. Parnasse goes Saturday, September 5, at the Accor Arena in Paris — 14 fights, prelims at 12 PM ET and the main card at 3 PM ET on Paramount+.

The line has moved a long way and is quoted differently everywhere, so all five readings are printed and none is adopted: Parnasse −649 / Hooker +420 and Parnasse −600 / Hooker +440 this edition, against −620 / +400, −667 / +417, and an opening line of −357 / +275. The direction is unambiguous even if the number is not.

Salahdine Parnasse is 23-2 and making his Octagon debut in a main event. He arrives as a former two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion — 14-2 inside that promotion with four defences of the lightweight belt — who signed with the UFC in late July 2026 after previously turning the promotion down. His U.S. debut came in May 2026, a first-round stoppage of Kenneth Cross for a fifth straight win. He did not come through Dana White's Contender Series.

Dan Hooker (24-14, 14-10 in the UFC — 38 pro fights) is the veteran opposite him, and the reason a debutant is being handed a five-round main event at all.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. ESPN's divisional rankings update this week is headlined by Song Yadong's rise at bantamweight on the back of the upset knockout of Umar Nurmagomedov.

Business & broadcast. Freedom 250 drew roughly 34 million total global viewers (viewers watching at least one minute — not an average), with 17 million across the US and Latin America, an 8.2 million average across US+LatAm and a 7.0 million US average, making it the most-watched UFC event ever domestically. Paramount is in year one of a seven-year, $7.7 billion US rights deal. For comparison, UFC 324 in January 2026 drew a 4.96 million average-minute audience, 7.18 million households and a 5.93 million peak concurrent.
