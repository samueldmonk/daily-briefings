Wall Street closes higher as tech rebounds — and DICK’S Sporting Goods ends the worst day in its history

Wall Street’s main indexes closed higher on Tuesday, Reuters reported at the bell, “as technology stocks recovered from a selloff” ahead of Nvidia’s Wednesday report, “while investors found some relief in drops in oil prices and bond yields.” The settled board: S&P 500 +0.32%, Nasdaq Composite +0.66%, Dow Jones Industrial Average +0.30% — the Dow’s third straight winning session. What moved it. Longer-dated Treasury yields fell as oil dropped to a one-week low and traders weighed Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent’s decision to expand Treasury buybacks. “There’s a lot of push and pull — in the bond market, geopolitics, oil,” Joe Quinlan, head of market strategy for Merrill and Bank of America Private Bank, told Reuters; “but we’re pretty constructive on the outlook (for the) next 12, 18 months on the U.S. economy; therefore, we are constructive on the markets as well.” Most megacaps closed higher, including Nvidia, Meta and Micron, and the broader chips index recovered after a 2.7% fall on Monday. Advanced Micro Devices rose after a Raymond James upgrade; Moderna surged after Barclays raised its target price; DICK’S Sporting Goods cratered after cutting its annual forecasts; Nike dipped; and Target fell after apologising for and pulling a Halloween costume criticised for evoking blackface.
