Top Story

The Octagon returns to China: Umar Nurmagomedov meets Song Yadong in a five-round bantamweight main event

UFC Fight Night 286 runs Saturday, August 29 from the Pudong Development Bank Shanghai Oriental Sports Center (listed by some outlets under its short form, SPD Bank Oriental Sports Center). The main card starts at 6:00 a.m. ET, with prelims from 3:00 a.m. ET.

The headliner puts No. 2-ranked Umar Nurmagomedov (20-1 MMA, 8-1 UFC) against No. 6-ranked Song Yadong (23-9-1 MMA, 12-4-1 UFC, 1 NC) over five rounds. The stylistic question is narrow and clear: whether Song's takedown defence can keep the fight standing and let his boxing set the pace, or whether Nurmagomedov can get it to the mat and impose positional control.

The co-main is a women's strawweight bout between China's Yan Xiaonan and Brazil's Denise Gomes.

⚠ Not asserted: no source seen this run states that the winner receives a title shot. Both men are described as making a case for one — that is a characterisation of stakes, not a booking.

Around the Sport

  • Contender Series Season 10 is running hot. Ten contracts were awarded across the first two weeks — four in Week 1 (Anthony Wint, Bilal Hasan, Thomas Pagliarulo, Joe Kropschot) and six in Week 2 — covering every division on the men's side of the sport except bantamweight.
  • Week 1's two fastest finishes set the tone: former linebacker Anthony Wint stopped Matt Adams at heavyweight in 34 seconds, and Bilal Hasan finished Mridul Saikia at bantamweight in under one minute.
  • Week 2 moved to the newly renovated Meta APEX in Las Vegas, on a five-fight card.
  • Islam Makhachev's welterweight reign continued at UFC 330 (Aug 15, Xfinity Mobile Arena, Philadelphia) with a unanimous-decision win over Ian Machado Garry — a win that also set a record for the most consecutive victories inside the Octagon.
