Paris is live: the main card is under way, and a debutant is a heavy favourite in his own main event

UFC Fight Night: Hooker vs Parnasse is running at the Accor Arena in Paris on a special schedule — prelims from 12 PM ET, main card from 3 PM ET, streaming on Paramount+ in the United States. The prelims are complete enough to report and the main card has begun; the main event had not taken place when this edition was assembled. It is the promotion’s fifth Accor Arena event since 2022, and the headline act is unusual: Salahdine Parnasse, making his UFC debut, is booked in a five-round main event against No. 10 ranked lightweight Dan Hooker.

The books have made the newcomer a very short price. Returns this run give Parnasse −550 at Bet365 and DraftKings, −620 at FanDuel and −625 at BetRivers, with Hooker +400 to +410, and put Parnasse’s implied win probability at 85.7% to 86.2% across three books. That is a striking number to hang on a fighter who has never been inside the Octagon — and it has moved: earlier editions of this briefing recorded DraftKings at −625/+455, which is now −550/+410.

Parnasse is not a developmental signing. He is a two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion who went 14-2 inside that promotion with four defences of the lightweight belt, signed with the UFC in late July 2026 having previously turned the promotion down, and was handed a main event on debut. He remains a debutant — unranked, with no UFC record — and this desk does not describe him as a contender.

Business & broadcast

The Paramount era is the structural story of the year. TKO Group and Paramount’s exclusive rights agreement is valued at $7.7 billion over seven years — roughly $1.1 billion annually — and it eliminates the pay-per-view model: 13 numbered events and 30 Fight Nights go out on Paramount+ at no additional cost to subscribers, with select events also on CBS.

On the numbers so far: more than 10 million households have watched over 100 million hours of UFC programming on Paramount+, which the announcement frames as viewership more than 15× the average pay-per-view event of the past two years. TKO has since announced an expansion of the Paramount partnership into Canada beginning in 2027, alongside multi-territory expansion into Latin America and Australia.

No gate figure, attendance number or viewership total for the Paris card was returned this run, and none is published.
