The Lead

Wall Street grinds higher as of 1:15 p.m. ET, waiting on Warsh

On a 1:15 p.m. ET reading from Trading Economics, the S&P 500 is up 0.43%, the Nasdaq 100 up 0.97% and the Dow barely above flat at +0.03%. That S&P figure is arithmetically consistent with Tuesday’s verified close of 7,585.73, which is why it is published; the level itself is left to the Weekly Scorecard.

An hour earlier the picture was the same shape but slightly smaller: around 1:00 p.m. ET the S&P 500 was up 0.3% and the Nasdaq Composite up 0.7%, with the Dow wavering near the flat line. Both readings are printed with their own timestamps rather than blended, and note that the Composite and the Nasdaq 100 are different indices.

Everything hinges on 2:00 p.m. ET, when the FOMC releases its decision, statement and updated Summary of Economic Projections, with Chair Kevin Warsh’s press conference at 2:30 p.m. CME FedWatch odds on a quarter-point move from 3.50%–3.75% to 3.75%–4.00% were cited at 92.5% by TheStreet at 11:10 a.m., 92% by Yahoo Finance and 92.9% by CNBC. All three are printed with their own attribution rather than averaged. It would be the first hike since July 2023.

Two things have leaned against the tape all week: a bond sell-off that carried the 10-year Treasury yield to a 19-year high on Tuesday, and crude still above $100 a barrel. Both eased today, and the chip complex supplied the bid.

No policy outcome is published on this page. This edition was built at about 1:20 p.m. ET, before the decision, the projections or the press conference. Nothing here asserts what the Fed did or what Chair Warsh said; the next edition will carry the outcome.

Transport · down · Wed morning

J.B. Hunt tumbles 12.64% on an earnings warning

J.B. Hunt Transport Services (JBHT) fell 12.64% after warning of a decline in earnings and flagging rising operating costs, per TheStreet’s Wednesday-morning movers post. It had been down 12.10% in premarket trade. Old Dominion Freight Line (ODFL) fell 4.34% premarket on concerns about slowing trucking momentum, rising operating costs and elevated valuations.

Optics · up · Wed morning

Coherent and Lumentum both up 6%

Coherent (COHR) climbed 6% as buyers returned to optical and photonics names following a recent sector-wide pullback, and Lumentum Holdings (LITE) rose 6% in the same rebound. Separately, Intel (INTC) rose about 5% and SK Hynix about 2% on the Reuters report that the two are discussing U.S. memory-chip manufacturing — talks one source described as exploratory, with no decisions made.

Space · up · Wed morning

SpaceX gains 5.29% ahead of a first orbital Starship test

SpaceX (SPCX) rose 5.29% after the company said Starship Flight 14 is scheduled for its first-ever orbital test flight on 22 September. Pending regulatory approval the mission is expected to last nearly ten hours, flying about 275 kilometres above Earth and completing about six orbits before splashing down in the Pacific west of Chile, deploying the first batch of Starlink V3 satellites.

Energy · down · Wed morning

Diamondback drops 8% as crude backs off

Diamondback Energy (FANG) fell 8% amid concerns over inflation, rising Treasury yields and geopolitical risk in crude markets; APA Corp. (APA) fell 5.2% after surging to a new 52-week high in the previous session. Energy names were among the most-represented on the midday losers list as oil eased.
