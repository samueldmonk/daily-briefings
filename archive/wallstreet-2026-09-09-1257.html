The Lead

Oil back over $100 drags stocks lower for a third session — Dow −413 points as of 12:07 p.m. ET

Brent crude futures passed $100 a barrel for the first time since July on Wednesday, and Wall Street went with it. The Associated Press put the Dow down 413 points, or 0.8%, as of 12:07 p.m. Eastern, with crude the proximate cause.

The escalation is military. The U.S. struck five Iranian tankers on Tuesday, sinking one, after Iran fired ballistic missiles at a U.S. Navy warship; Houthi attacks have separately damaged Saudi energy infrastructure and cut its capacity. Traders are pricing prolonged disruption to Persian Gulf fuel exports, and Goldman Sachs said intensifying shipping attacks raise the probability of Brent clearing $120.

An earlier, un-timed read of the session gave the S&P 500 −0.24%, the Dow −0.58%, the Nasdaq −0.35% and the Russell 2000 −0.52%. The 12:07 p.m. Dow figure is the later of the two and the only one with a stated time, so it leads; both are shown rather than blended. Against Tuesday’s close of 52,786.07, 413 points is 0.78% — self-consistent with the reported 0.8%.

NewEarnings
Casey’s General Stores · CASY

Down more than 10% on a beat that wasn’t enough

The convenience-store chain beat on both earnings and revenue in its fiscal first quarter, but same-store sales grew more slowly than a year ago and prepared food and dispensed beverage, grocery and general merchandise, and fuel gallons all missed estimates. Shares fell more than 10% premarket.

NewEvent risk
Apple · AAPL

Slightly lower into the “Surprise and shine” event

Shares were modestly lower premarket as traders waited on Apple’s 10:00 a.m. Pacific event — the first headlined by new chief executive John Ternus, who took over from Tim Cook on 1 September. The iPhone 18 Pro, 18 Pro Max and a foldable are expected.
