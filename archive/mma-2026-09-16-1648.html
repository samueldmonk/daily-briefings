Top Story

Van is the favourite in the rematch — but the line moved to get there

UFC 331 is locked in for Saturday, 19 September at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, with flyweight champion Joshua Van defending against Alexandre Pantoja in a rematch of their UFC 323 title fight. Van is favoured at −130 on LetsBetMD, −132 on BetRivers and −136 on FanDuel — a tight line for a champion who already beat the man across from him.

The detail worth holding onto is the direction of travel. Despite Van winning the first fight, Pantoja opened as a small betting favourite for the rematch; the market has since shifted to Van. That is a market re-pricing an outcome it initially discounted — the first meeting ended in injury, and the opening line appears to have read that as an asterisk before moving back.

The method market prices it as a fight that goes long or ends by strikes: Van by decision +390, Van by KO/TKO +175, Van by submission +1600; Pantoja by decision +460, Pantoja by KO/TKO +850, Pantoja by submission +290. The shortest price on the board after Van straight-up is Van by KO/TKO; on Pantoja’s side the market’s preferred route is the submission at +290, shorter than either his decision or his knockout price.

The penultimate bout has Arman Tsarukyan against Maurício Ruffy at 155 lb. Also on the main card: Patricio Pitbull vs. Doo Ho Choi at 145 lb, Renato Moicano vs. Brian Ortega 2 at 155 lb, and Gable Steveson vs. Sean Sharaf at 265 lb.
