The Lead

As of ~10:40 AM ET, Wed. Oct. 21: Nasdaq +0.47%, S&P 500 +0.12%, Dow −0.04%

New Latest read (~10:40 AM ET). “US stocks were mixed on Wednesday,” Yahoo Finance reported. Its market strip, read with “U.S. markets close in 5h 20m” showing (about 10:40 AM ET), had the Nasdaq Composite up 123.29 points, or 0.47%, at 26,548.11; the S&P 500 up 9.52 points, or 0.12%, at 7,722.09; and the Dow down 19.75 points, or 0.04%, at 52,476.13 — all three reconcile with Tuesday’s closes. The Russell 2000 was up 0.42% at 2,785.04, the VIX 17.35 (+0.23%) and the 10-year yield 5.11% (Yahoo Finance live, ~10:40 AM ET). Intraday readings, not closes.

Earlier (10:08 AM ET). The Nasdaq Composite was up 0.58% at 26,578.18, the S&P 500 up 0.10% at 7,720.02 and the Dow down 0.13% at 52,427.22, using Yahoo Finance data compiled by TechStock². It was a narrow advance: six of 11 sector funds fell, with technology (XLK) +0.84% and industrials (XLI) +0.70% against health care (XLV) −0.94% and communication services (XLC) −0.79%; the equal-weight S&P 500 ETF was up just 0.03% (TechStock², 10:08 AM ET). At about 9:30 AM ET the Nasdaq had been up 0.69%, the S&P 500 0.29% and the Dow 0.20% (Yahoo Finance).

What’s driving it. SpaceX’s results “renewed enthusiasm for the AI trade ahead of earnings from Tesla and Meta,” Yahoo Finance’s Ines Ferré and David Hollerith wrote. Musk said on SpaceX’s earnings call late Tuesday that merging SpaceX with Tesla would be a good idea and that both companies have formed special committees to study a deal. Tesla and Meta report after Wednesday’s close, kicking off Big Tech earnings. Netflix shares dropped 4% after its Q4 revenue forecast missed estimates and it lowered its 2026 ad-revenue guidance. Separately, Anthropic could publicly file its S-1 as soon as Friday, according to people familiar with its plans. Brent hovered at $108 a barrel and WTI traded close to $97 (Yahoo Finance).

Earlier (6:07 AM ET). Futures were mixed to slightly higher as investors weighed a heavy slate of earnings, the AI trade and Middle East uncertainty: Dow futures had ticked down 66 points (0.1%), S&P 500 futures were up 6 points (0.1%) and Nasdaq 100 futures up 87 points (0.3%), Investing.com reported (Investing.com, 6:51 AM ET). Futures readings, not index levels.

Tuesday recap. The Dow closed up 529.85 points, or 1.02%, at 52,495.88; the S&P 500 rose 0.28% to 7,712.57; and the Nasdaq Composite slipped 0.02% to 26,424.82, as a health-care rally, 3M’s post-earnings jump and softer oil lifted blue chips (CNBC).

Movers & Drivers

New

SpaceX +8.7% at 10:40 AM; Berkshire Hathaway hits a record

SpaceX was at $160.61, up $12.82 (+8.67%), and Tesla at $383.15 (+2.53%) as of 10:40 AM ET. Berkshire Hathaway’s Class B stock closed at a record Tuesday — topping its May 2, 2025, high by two cents, its first record since Warren Buffett announced he would hand over the CEO job — and both share classes hit intraday records Wednesday morning. Berkshire is on pace to rise in 14 of its last 15 sessions, including its first 10-day winning streak since 2018, and is up about 7% since the S&P 500’s Oct. 6 record while the index has slipped, per Yahoo Finance’s Jared Blikre (Yahoo Finance).

SpaceX +11.9%, Super Micro +11.5% by 10:08 AM; AT&T −5.2%

At 10:08 AM ET SpaceX had extended its gain to 11.86% at $165.32 and Super Micro was up 11.50% at $36.21; Reuters credited their results for the firmer open. Capital One was up 3.75%, while AT&T fell 5.24% and Netflix was down 3.15% at $66.53 — its Q4 EPS forecast of 67 cents trailed the 73-cent estimate, per Benzinga, and Needham’s Laura Martin cut the stock to Hold from Buy (TechStock²).

SpaceX +7.5% at the open; Musk floats a Tesla merger

SpaceX was at $158.93, up $11.14 (+7.54%), as of 9:30 AM ET after third-quarter revenue and profit topped estimates; Tesla was up 3.02% at $385.00. Musk said both companies have formed special committees to study a merger. SpaceX’s AI segment, renamed “Super Intelligence,” brought in $7.6 billion — more than half its $14.6 billion in Q3 revenue. Every stock in Yahoo’s basket of newer space names was higher premarket. Up to 1.3 billion SpaceX shares unlock Thursday, the biggest release yet (Yahoo Finance).

Super Micro +12.7% premarket on a big guidance raise

Super Micro Computer jumped 12.7% premarket to $36.69 after forecasting quarterly revenue and profit margins comfortably above estimates. SCHMID Group soared 12.5% after lifting its 2026 order-intake target to more than €150 million, and DigitalOcean climbed 3.7% on a Piper Sandler upgrade to Overweight (price target cut to $126 from $142) (Investing.com).

Netflix −6%, Intuitive Surgical −7.9% premarket

Netflix dropped 6.0% premarket after trimming its full-year ad forecast and issuing a softer Q4 outlook; it is down more than 26% year to date. Intuitive Surgical fell 7.9% to $403.60 despite beating estimates (adjusted EPS $3.00), weighed by a $450 million pre-tax charge tied to a da Vinci legal settlement that cut GAAP net income to $523 million. Alpha Teknova plunged 30.7% to $5.12 after pricing an 8 million-share offering at $5.00 (Investing.com).

Capital One +3.2%, Elevance +1.8% on Q3 beats

Capital One rallied 3.2% premarket after Q3 adjusted EPS of $6.69, about 22% above estimates; net interest margin rose 40 basis points sequentially to 8.41%. Elevance Health rose 1.8% after adjusted EPS of $6.99 vs. a $4.70 consensus on revenue of $50.4 billion, and raised its full-year adjusted EPS floor to at least $29.00 (Investing.com).

Chart of the Day — SpaceX (NASDAQ:SPCX), up 8.7% at 10:40 AM ET

SpaceX, the session’s headline mover, was up 8.67% at $160.61 at 10:40 AM ET, off the +11.86% ($165.32) TechStock² logged at 10:08 AM. Yahoo’s Jared Blikre is watching whether it holds $150 through the day, with $172 — which capped the stock in July and October — the next potential wall (Yahoo Finance); the chart is live.
