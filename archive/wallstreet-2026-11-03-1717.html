The Lead

Closing bell: S&P 500 +0.36%, Nasdaq +0.83%, Dow −0.38% as chips rebound and Apple weighs

The S&P 500 rose 27.52 points, or 0.36%, to 7,568.92, snapping a three-day losing run; the Nasdaq Composite added 0.83% to 25,372.01 and the Russell 2000 gained 0.27%, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average lost 197.26 points, or 0.38%, to 51,786.72 (Yahoo Finance market strip after the close, Dow “At close: 4:09:44 PM EST”; corroborated by CNBC) (Yahoo Finance; CNBC). The split held all day: at the 9:30 open TheStreet had the S&P +0.28%, the Dow −0.49% and the Nasdaq +0.81%, and by 1:57 p.m. it called the Dow “the odd one out,” the only major index in the red, with consumer names and financials responsible for most of its decline (TheStreet). Breadth was thin — at 11:45 a.m. ET just 215 S&P 500 members were rising with the index up 0.41% (TheStreet).

Chips did the lifting. The PHLX Semiconductor Index had finished Monday about 22% below its Oct. 6 high — its second bear market since June — and nearly every chip and storage stock in the S&P 500 rose in early trading Tuesday, Yahoo’s Jared Blikre reported; AMD closed up 3.65% at $483.90 ahead of its report (Yahoo Finance). Apple fell after a December-quarter gross-margin guide that disappointed investors and weaker-than-expected iPad sales, and Palantir slid despite a beat-and-raise as investors focused on slowing contract bookings (TheStreet; StockTitan).

After the bell, AMD reported record third-quarter revenue of $13.4 billion, up 45%, with non-GAAP EPS of $2.09 and Data Center revenue up 97% to a record $8.5 billion, and guided fourth-quarter revenue to about $17 billion, plus or minus 3% (AMD); CNBC reported the stock jumped 7% in extended trading, against expectations of $1.95 a share on $13.09 billion of revenue (CNBC, via search). In rates and commodities, Yahoo’s post-close strip showed the 10-year yield at 4.952% (+5.8 basis points), December WTI at $96.57 (+0.11%) and the VIX at 20.80 (−1.66%); Yahoo said oil trimmed losses in the afternoon, with Brent near $104 and WTI near $96 (Yahoo Finance). Tonight: midterm returns, which Yahoo says investors will parse for policy signals on pharmaceuticals, housing and AI data centers, and Super Micro’s fiscal first-quarter report (Yahoo Finance).

Politics

Polymarket: 76% odds of a Democratic sweep

Polymarket gave Democrats a 76% chance of winning both the House and Senate as of Tuesday morning, Yahoo’s Jared Blikre reports — adding that history says the outcome has shaped stocks for about three months, “then barely at all.” Republicans hold the Senate 53-47; some races may not be called tonight. (Yahoo Finance)

Up

Ibotta, Coinbase, DuPont jump at midday

Ibotta was up 29.18% at 1:01 p.m. ET in regular trading after its revenue beat its own forecast (StockTitan real-time data); CNBC’s midday movers list has Coinbase +8% after a 15% slide over the prior three sessions and DuPont up about 9% on third-quarter results, while Thomson Reuters fell 7.5% on weaker-than-expected earnings. (StockTitan; CNBC)

Crypto

Bitcoin ETFs bleed $1B+ after Binance hack

Investors pulled over $1 billion from U.S.-listed bitcoin exchange-traded products in the two business days after last Thursday’s Binance hack, per Bloomberg data cited by Yahoo; K33’s Vetle Lunde says bitcoin is “rigged for a major move” whatever the midterm outcome. (Yahoo Finance)
