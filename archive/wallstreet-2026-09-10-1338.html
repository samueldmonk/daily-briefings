The Lead

Thursday session · as of ~11:38 AM ET

Fourth straight decline takes shape as crude tops $100 and wholesale inflation runs hot

Stocks fell again on Thursday morning, with the S&P 500 down about a third of a percent as of 11:38 AM ET and roughly 60% of U.S. issues in the red — a fourth consecutive down day in the making. A separate midday read had the index off about half a percent. At the opening bell the S&P 500 was down 0.59%, the Dow 0.35%, the Nasdaq 0.97% and the Russell 2000 1.32%.

The driver is oil. West Texas Intermediate surged 4.2% to $100.10 a barrel and Brent jumped 3.6% to $105.37 in morning trade — the highest since May — on escalating fighting between the United States and Iran and the risk of further disruption through the Strait of Hormuz. Multiple American military aircraft were damaged in Iranian strikes on Muwaffaq Salti Air Base in Jordan overnight Tuesday into Wednesday, CBS News reported; one A-10 lost a wing and roughly eight F-15s took light damage.

Inflation data cut both ways. August’s producer price index rose 0.4% month over month, matching the Dow Jones consensus, but the annual rate climbed to 5.4% from 4.8% in July — slightly above forecasts. Odds of a Fed rate hike next week rose to nearly 70% on CME Group’s FedWatch gauge. The August CPI, out Friday at 8:30 AM ET with consensus at 0.4% monthly and 3.4% annual, is the swing factor.

Elsewhere: the European Central Bank raised its key deposit rate 25 basis points to 2.5%, as fully priced in; existing home sales fell 2% in August to a 3.98 million annual pace, the lowest since June 2025; and the 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.91%, its highest since 2023.

Movers & Drivers

New−14%

Cooper Cos. (COO) — guidance cut and a shelved sale

Down nearly 14% mid-morning (and 18.1% in the premarket) after a disappointing strategic review, slashed guidance, a revenue miss and a wave of analyst downgrades. Q3 adjusted EPS of $1.15 beat the $1.12 consensus, but revenue of $1.07bn missed the $1.1bn estimate. Q4 adjusted EPS guidance of $1.05–$1.09 sits well below the $1.19 consensus. The company will retain CooperSurgical, concluding the offers it received were not in shareholders’ best interest.

New−7.3%

Freeport-McMoRan (FCX) — copper retreats from records

Down 7.3% as copper prices pulled back from record highs, after an 8.8% premarket drop. Southern Copper (SCCO) fell 7.1% amid the same decline and sector-wide profit-taking.

New−5.7%

Intel (INTC) — profit-taking after a multi-session rally

Off 5.7% as investors took profits following a large multi-session run, amid a broader semiconductor selloff driven by rising 10-year Treasury yields and surging oil. Nvidia slipped 1.4%, weighing on the Nasdaq.

New+4.7%

Reddit (RDDT) — Piper Sandler user data

Up 4.7% after new Piper Sandler data showed an 8% month-over-month increase in the platform’s user base in August. Charter Communications (CHTR) rose 4.5% on a technical rebound after Wednesday’s sharp selloff.

New+4.1%

Elevance Health (ELV) — guidance reaffirmed

Up 4.1% after the health benefits company said it plans to reaffirm its full-year 2026 earnings and benefit expense guidance in upcoming meetings with investors and analysts.

NewEarnings

Macy’s (M) — beat and raise, shares still slip

Down 1.4% premarket despite adjusted EPS of 63¢ against a 37¢ consensus and net sales up 1.1% to $4.87bn. Comparable sales rose 2.7%, with Bloomingdale’s up 11.3% and the namesake Macy’s business up just 1.1%. Full-year adjusted EPS guidance was raised to $2.15–$2.35, including a benefit from $116m of tariff refunds received so far.

On the Radar

  • August CPI, Friday 11 September, 8:30 AM ET. Dow Jones consensus calls for a 0.4% monthly rise and 3.4% over twelve months; economists expect core prices up 0.2%. Fed Governor Waller said last week he would vote to hold at 0.2% and support a hike above it — a single tenth of a percentage point is the pivot.
  • Oracle and Adobe report after Thursday’s close. Consensus for Oracle is $1.73 EPS on $19.13bn revenue, implying roughly 28% growth; for Adobe, $6.07 EPS on $6.69bn. Oracle settled at $162.52 on 8 September, down 32.7% year over year; Adobe finished at $257.26, down 27.3%.
  • The Fed meets next week with the target range at 3.50%–3.75% and Chair Kevin Warsh under what one TheStreet Pro contributor called a market "double-dog dare" to deliver the hike bond pricing implies.
  • Existing home sales fell 2% in August to a 3.98 million annual pace, the lowest since June 2025 and down 1.2% year over year. Inventory of 1.62 million homes is a 4.9-month supply, the highest in more than a decade; the median price rose 1.6% to $429,100.
  • Fiscal politics entered the tape. President Trump proposed sending every American adult $5,000 — a "Trump Dividend" — if Republicans retain the House and Senate; Reuters estimated the cost at about $1.35 trillion. The proposal would require congressional approval and no funding mechanism was given.
  • SpaceX (SPCX) slipped 0.52% to $146.78 as up to 319 million previously locked-up shares became eligible for sale following the 90-day lockup expiry after its June IPO.
