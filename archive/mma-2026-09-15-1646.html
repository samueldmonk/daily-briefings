Top Story

Heavyweight · announced Monday 14 September

Aspinall’s title is off the board, the injury traces back to Gane, and a fourth eye surgery is on the table

Tom Aspinall has vacated the UFC heavyweight championship. The cause is the right eye he injured against Ciryl Gane at UFC 321 in October 2025, and the trigger was a training accident: he was in camp for a Gane rematch set for UFC 334 at Madison Square Garden when the eye, in the reporting’s phrase, went “back to square one.” He could require a fourth surgery if the eye is not healed by next January.

The consequence for the division is immediate. Interim champion Ciryl Gane is expected to be promoted to undisputed champion, which is why the Champions Board below reads VACANT at heavyweight rather than carrying a name — the promotion is expected, not yet done. Top-ranked contenders Josh Hokit and Alex Pereira are both calling for an undisputed title bout against Gane.

That makes two belts off the board this month: Shevchenko’s women’s flyweight title, vacated in early September and contested at UFC 332 on 3 October, and now heavyweight, vacated on the 14th. Jon Jones has posted several reactions to the Aspinall news, and Gane offered what one outlet characterised as a two-word response.
