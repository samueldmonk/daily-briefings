The Lead

Pre-market, as of ~6:18 AM ET: Nasdaq futures −0.8%, S&P futures −0.3%, Dow futures +0.1% as Nvidia CEO turmoil hits tech

Pre-market. With U.S. markets opening in 3 hours 12 minutes (≈6:18 AM ET), Yahoo Finance’s strip had S&P 500 futures down 0.34%, Nasdaq futures down 0.77% and Dow futures up 0.11%, with Russell 2000 futures up 0.04% and the VIX up 4.69% to 18.31 (Yahoo Finance). Yahoo’s text: futures on the S&P 500 fell 0.3% and contracts on the Nasdaq-100 dropped 0.7%, “backsliding” from Wednesday’s chip-led rally, while Dow futures rose 0.1% (Yahoo Finance). Futures are not the open — the first official read comes at 9:30 AM ET.

Why it’s down: reports of a second affair by Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang — with an executive at the company — “tanked the chipmaker’s stock, dragging the tech sector down with it.” Nvidia tumbled 5.6% premarket as its board decided whether to oust Huang; the board is reportedly meeting to determine whether to remove him, the Wall Street Journal reports, and the New York Times reports Huang also had a relationship with Nvidia executive Kimberly Powell, who joined in 2008 and heads its healthcare business. Some chipmakers, including Intel and Micron, fell, while AMD rose; stocks in Asia held steady (Yahoo Finance). No board decision had been announced as of this edition.

Wednesday’s close (for reference): Nasdaq Composite +1.60%, S&P 500 +1.31%, Dow +0.49% and Russell 2000 +0.88% as CoreWeave revived the AI trade (TheStreet; Kiplinger — desk record). Levels are in the Weekly Scorecard.

Today: initial jobless claims (189,000 last week) and existing home sales; earnings from Cisco, Applied Materials, Brookfield, Nu Holdings, Nebius, Cerebras Systems, Korea Electric Power, QXO, Dillard’s and others; Yahoo Finance’s Invest event starts at 8 a.m. ET with AMD CEO Lisa Su and Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan among the speakers (Yahoo Finance).

Movers & Drivers

Down New

Nvidia −5.6% premarket as board weighs Huang’s future

Nvidia tumbled 5.6% in premarket trading as the company’s board decided whether to oust Jensen Huang, raising questions over who could succeed him; Yahoo notes Nvidia’s web of AI tie-ups makes the potential market fallout hard to gauge (Yahoo Finance). An Investing.com headline reads “Nvidia shares slump 7% as report says board preparing to oust CEO Jensen Huang” (Investing.com, headline). Nvidia closed Wednesday up 2.9% (desk record).

Down New

Nebius down 10%+ premarket despite a Q3 beat

Nebius posted a loss of $0.13 a share vs. a consensus $0.63 loss; revenue surged 571% to $980.8 million vs. $907.2 million expected, and it guided fiscal 2027 revenue to $12–13 billion vs. $11.98 billion consensus — yet shares were down over 10% premarket. Quarterly capex surged 664% to $7.3 billion; cash stood at $7.4 billion (Investing.com, 7:39 AM ET — Investing.com). The stock had closed up 15.44% Wednesday (Investing.com).

Mixed New

Chips split: Intel, Micron lower; AMD higher

Some chipmakers, including Intel and Micron, fell in premarket trading while Nvidia rival AMD rose (Yahoo Finance).

Up

Wednesday: Apple sets a record — $5.16 trillion valuation

Apple (+1.96%) notched a fresh record before the close, eclipsing $355.42 and lifting its valuation to $5.16 trillion, its highest ever; the shares are up 31.1% this year (TheStreet, 3:48 PM ET — TheStreet).

Up

Wednesday: CoreWeave +24.7% — best day since June 2025

Shares jumped 24.7%, their biggest one-day percentage gain since June 3, 2025, after Q3 showed a narrower-than-expected loss of $1.27 a share on revenue of $3.66 billion; CEO Michael Intrator said CoreWeave “will not use the unsecured debt market to fund” the business. The stock had been down nearly 32% year to date coming into the session (Kiplinger). 24/7 Wall St.’s 4:10 PM ET movers board had it at $60.51, +24.2% (24/7 Wall St.).

Up

Wednesday: Closing gainers: SanDisk +13.3%, Bloom +12.5%, Dell +11.9%, Arm +11.9%

CoreWeave led five double-digit gainers at the close as AI-infrastructure names “took the whole session”; on the downside GoPro fell 18.3% to $0.69, NetEase lost 4.4% and nothing else fell more than 4.4% (24/7 Wall St., 4:10 PM ET — 24/7 Wall St.).

Up

Wednesday: Nvidia +2.9% despite Baird downgrade

Wall Street bought the dip: Nvidia closed up 2.9% even after Baird cut it to Neutral and Morgan Stanley removed it from its Top Pick list while keeping Overweight. The stock was down 9.5% for the week coming into the session (Kiplinger).

Up

Wednesday: Costco +2.0% on $30 special dividend

Costco’s board approved a $30-a-share special dividend — roughly $13.3 billion — plus a $4 billion buyback; Mizuho’s David Bellinger said it is well above the $18–$20 he had forecast (Kiplinger).
