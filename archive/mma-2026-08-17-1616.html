Top Story

Makhachev passes Anderson Silva — 17 straight, and a first welterweight defence

Islam Makhachev defended the UFC welterweight championship against Ian Machado Garry on Saturday at Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia, taking a unanimous decision by scores of 49-46, 49-46 and 48-47. The win was his 17th consecutive victory inside the Octagon, a new record for the most consecutive UFC wins, passing the 16-fight mark set by Hall of Famer Anderson Silva. It also made him only the fifth two-division champion to record successful title defences in both divisions.

The champion controlled the opening round with his grappling and caught Machado Garry with a right hand and a left high kick out of the clinch in the second that put the Irishman down momentarily. Machado Garry out-landed him in the third, and landed the better strikes across the later rounds, but Makhachev returned to his wrestling in the fourth and took the challenger's back in the fifth, holding the position to the horn. Machado Garry falls to 17-2. Makhachev remains atop the men's pound-for-pound rankings.

Prospect Watch

Anthony Wint — heavyweight, 30

Wint ran through Matt Adams in 34 seconds on the Contender Series in Week 1 of Season 10, improving his undefeated professional record to 7-0 and earning a contract on the spot. The turnaround has been quick: he is already booked to make his promotional debut against fellow newcomer Terrance Chatman at UFC Sacramento this Saturday, August 22.

Bilal Hasan — flyweight

Hasan finished Mridul Saikia in under a minute at bantamweight on Contender Series Week 1 to earn his contract. He has since been booked to debut at UFC Shanghai on August 29 against Nilson Rojas.

Prospect

Donte Johnson — middleweight, 27

A Contender Series Season 9 alum, "Lockjaw" is now three-for-three in the UFC and nine wins deep on his overall streak after detonating a straight left on Eric McConico 1:38 into the first round at UFC 330. The Wisconsin native took a $100,000 Performance bonus for it and trains out of Xtreme Couture in Las Vegas.

Around the Sport

  • Dana White turned one winner away. Undefeated lightweight Abe Alsaghir improved to 9-0 in front of the UFC president on Contender Series Week 1, taking a split decision over Fabrizio Escarrega across three rounds — but White disagreed with the result and made clear he would not be adding Alsaghir to the roster. Four of the five winners on the night were signed.
  • Contender Series returns tomorrow. Season 10 continues with Week 2 on Tuesday at Meta Apex, streaming on Paramount+. Week 1 on August 11 produced four contracts — Anthony Wint, Bilal Hasan, Tom Pagliarulo and Joseph Kropschot.
  • Edson Barboza retired in the cage. After Esteban Ribovics stopped him in the second round at UFC 330, Barboza took off his gloves and laid them in the centre of the Octagon, closing a 16-year UFC career.
  • Jeremiah Wells is at a crossroads. The lone Philadelphian on the card came from two rounds down to choke out Myktybek Orolbai, then said he is likely to call it a career unless he gets a top-15 opponent next time out.
  • A rare finish. Charles Johnson's Scottish twister on Eduardo Chapolin was the second of its kind inside the Octagon this year.
  • Neil Magny, still going. The 39-year-old collected his 25th UFC win, and has now won three of his last four.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. Makhachev holds the top spot in the men's pound-for-pound rankings after breaking the consecutive-wins record at UFC 330. Mackenzie Dern's standing rose on the back of her first successful strawweight defence. Umar Nurmagomedov carries a two-fight win streak into the Shanghai headliner following his bantamweight title challenge, with wins over Deiveson Figueiredo, Cory Sandhagen and Mario Bautista on his record; Song Yadong's résumé includes wins over Henry Cejudo, Marlon Moraes and Chito Vera.
