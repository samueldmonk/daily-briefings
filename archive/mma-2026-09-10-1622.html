Top Story

“The heavyweight division will just keep rolling” — Dana White stops waiting on Tom Aspinall

UFC CEO Dana White has confirmed the promotion will move the heavyweight picture forward without its undisputed champion. “Tom Aspinall is hurt,” White said. “Until we hear differently, the heavyweight division will just keep rolling.”

Aspinall has not fought since UFC 321 in October last year, when his bout with Ciryl Gane was ruled a no contest after Gane inadvertently poked him in both eyes in the first round. He underwent surgery in February for damage to both eyes and remains sidelined nearly a year on. Earlier in 2026 he said he would be ready to face Gane in an undisputed title fight in Paris in September; that did not happen.

In the meantime Gane finished Alex Pereira to claim the interim heavyweight title at UFC Freedom 250 in June. Pereira is now pushing for an immediate shot at Gane, and Josh Hokit is demanding a title opportunity after going 4-0 since joining the roster last year. White’s language keeps every option open — interim, vacant or otherwise — without setting a date.

UFC 331 — Joshua Van vs. Alexandre Pantoja 2

Van defends the flyweight belt in a rematch of UFC 323, where he won the title 26 seconds into round one after Pantoja injured his arm. Co-main is Arman Tsarukyan vs. Mateusz Ruffy over five rounds, a lightweight title eliminator whose winner is expected to face Justin Gaethje; it was booked on 5 August after Charles Oliveira withdrew. Around the Sport

  • Michael “Venom” Page will not be re-signed. Dana White: “I don’t think we’re signing him. I think it’s been pretty clear that he wasn’t being re-signed.”
  • Valentina Shevchenko vacated the women’s flyweight title after a ligament injury to her back and shoulder that will keep her out for roughly a year. She had been due to defend against Natália Silva; the UFC has said she is guaranteed a title shot when cleared. Any “current champions” list still seating her at 125 lb is out of date.
  • Hawaii is back on the board. White has said the UFC will plan events in Hawaii once the New Aloha Stadium Entertainment District in Halawa, Oahu — a 31,000-seat multi-purpose venue capable of hosting combat sports — opens in the first quarter of 2029.
  • Twelve events remain on the 2026 calendar, running from Noche UFC in Glendale on 12 September through UFC 335 in Las Vegas on 12 December.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. The media voting panel that set the UFC’s divisional rankings was replaced by an algorithmic model on 22 June 2026, recalculated after each event.

Business & broadcast. This is year one of Paramount’s seven-year, $7.7 billion agreement with TKO, covering 13 numbered events and 30 Fight Nights a year on Paramount+, with select numbered events simulcast on CBS — UFC 332 on 3 October will be the first numbered main card to run on the network. UFC Freedom 250, the June White House card, drew 17 million viewers on Paramount+ across the U.S. and Latin America and roughly 34 million globally. TKO reported $401 million of Q1 2026 revenue, up 12% year over year.
