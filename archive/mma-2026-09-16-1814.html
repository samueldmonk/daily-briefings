Top Story

The books can’t split Van and Pantoja — and Van won the first fight in 26 seconds

UFC 331 takes place Saturday 19 September at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, with Joshua Van defending the flyweight title against the former champion Alexandre Pantoja. The odds are the story. Per BetOnline, the reigning champion is a slight underdog at −105 against Pantoja at −115; at DraftKings the line is inverted, Van −130 and Pantoja +110. A near pick’em in a rematch of a fight the champion finished in 26 seconds is an unusual market.

The co-main is the more lopsided book: Arman Tsarukyan is a −380 favourite over Maurício Ruffy at +305 in a 155-pound bout. Further down the card, Gable Steveson is priced at −1500.

The card also lost a fight this week: Brian Ortega is off UFC 331, and has publicly pushed back at those accusing him of ducking. He had been scheduled to face Renato Moicano.

Around the Sport

  • Brian Ortega is off UFC 331 and has answered the ducking accusations aimed at him. He had been booked against Renato Moicano.
  • Alex Pereira mocked Ciryl Gane’s promotion to the top of the heavyweight division following Tom Aspinall’s title vacating. Pereira lost the interim heavyweight bout to Gane at Freedom 250 in June.
  • The heavyweight belt is still vacant. Aspinall vacated the undisputed title on 14 September over ongoing eye complications that have kept him from being medically cleared. He is not retiring and intends to return once cleared; Gane holds the interim title.
  • UFC 332 is a broadcast milestone — the first numbered-event main card to air on CBS, alongside Paramount+.

Rankings & Business

  • Rankings movement: a Van win on Saturday would be his second flyweight title defence of 2026, after the TKO5 of Tatsuro Taira at UFC 328 in May. A Pantoja win returns the belt to the man Van took it from in 26 seconds.
  • Broadcast: UFC 332’s main card on 3 October will be the first numbered UFC event main card carried on CBS.
  • Betting market: the sportsbooks are split on the UFC 331 main event — BetOnline has the champion as a slight dog, DraftKings has him a −130 favourite. Gable Steveson is the card’s heaviest chalk at −1500.
