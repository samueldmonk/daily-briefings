Top Story

A UFC debutant is a −600 favourite in his own main event

Salahdine Parnasse (23-2) makes his UFC debut in Paris this afternoon as the headliner — and the market is treating him as something close to a lock. Five sportsbooks price him between −600 and −649, an implied win probability reported at 84.3%, against Dan Hooker (24-14, 14-10 in the UFC), the #10-ranked lightweight, who comes in on a two-fight skid. Parnasse did not come through Dana White's Contender Series: he is a former two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion, 14-2 inside that promotion with four defences of the lightweight belt, who signed with the UFC in late July 2026 after previously turning the promotion down — and was handed a main event on debut. His U.S. debut came in May 2026, a first-round stoppage of Kenneth Cross. He is still a debutant in this promotion, and the odds are the story precisely because of that.

Around the Sport

This is July 2026 reporting, restated here as standing context rather than as news.
