Top Story

CISA adds exploited VMware ESX and Linux kernel flaws to KEV

On Wednesday, CISA added CVE-2026-41709, a Broadcom VMware ESX insufficient-logging flaw, after CrowdStrike revealed that the China-nexus actor Warp Panda had weaponized it as a zero-day in early 2026 in attacks targeting the US legal sector. The bug (CVSS 2.7) lets a malicious actor who already has admin access perform certain operations without them being logged; Broadcom addressed it in a July 2026 advisory. Federal civilian agencies are directed to apply fixes by Nov. 11 (The Hacker News).

The same update added CVE-2026-46300, the Linux kernel flaw known as Fragnesia, which affects the XFRM ESP-in-TCP subsystem and lets unprivileged local attackers modify read-only file contents in the kernel page cache to gain root. It was discovered by William Bowling of the V12 security team; Wiz published a technical analysis. CISA set a federal deadline of Oct. 31; SecurityOnline reports CISA says attackers chain it with the PHP2Shell flaws to implant PHP webshells and run commands as root (SecurityOnline; Security Affairs).

CrowdStrike says Warp Panda — also tracked as Armory Castle (formerly UNC5221), Clay Typhoon and VerdantBamboo — exploits internet-facing edge devices, pivots to vCenter to deploy malware such as BRICKSTORM, harvests cloud session tokens and registers rogue MFA devices (The Hacker News).

Patch Priority

Due today: PHP — CVE-2026-109618 / CVE-2026-109619 (“PHP2Shell”), actively exploited remote code execution. CISA’s catalog lists both as added Oct. 26 with a federal due date of Thursday, Oct. 29, 2026 (0 days left) (HighCircl, quoting the CISA catalog; BleepingComputer). Upgrade to PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35 and restart PHP workers; CISA advises taking PHP 8.1-and-older systems offline if they cannot be patched (HighCircl).

Then: Linux kernel — CVE-2026-46300 (“Fragnesia”), local privilege escalation to root. In CISA’s KEV since Oct. 28 with a federal due date of Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026 (2 days left) (Security Affairs; CISA KEV entry). Apply your distribution’s kernel update. Next: VMware ESX CVE-2026-41709 — fixed builds include ESXi80U3j-25429389 and ESXi-9.0.2.0100-25595025 per Broadcom’s advisory as listed by The Hacker News — due Nov. 11 (13 days left).

Threat Actor Spotlight

China-linked APT

Volt Typhoon’s “Silent Train”

Lumen’s Black Lotus Labs has tracked a covert relay network it calls Silent Train for three years and ties it to Volt Typhoon. Sessions start on a rented server outside the US and hop through compromised SOHO routers and IoT devices — about four devices on average, up to six — before a final US device near the target; Lumen counted more than 27 device classes. Its investigations revealed at least four confirmed zero-days (Versa Director; N-able N-central CVE-2025-8875/-8876; Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN CVE-2026-20127; VeloCloud Orchestrator CVE-2026-16812) and a likely fifth in Quest KACE. Victims span telecoms, government, defense and critical infrastructure; Hawaii, California and Texas were the top locations (Security Affairs).

Breaches & Incidents

Data breach

ENGIE: French customers’ data exposed via partner sales tool

Attackers accessed names, postal and email addresses, phone numbers, customer and contract numbers and energy supply identifiers through a tool partners use to sell ENGIE offers. ENGIE says its internal systems and banking data were not affected; it has not said how many customers were hit, and no group has claimed it (Security Affairs).

Law enforcement

Hive/Nefilim affiliate sentenced to 70 months

A Brooklyn federal judge sentenced Ukrainian national Artem Stryzhak, who pleaded guilty in December 2025. As a Hive affiliate he and co-conspirators hit seven victims during the pandemic; a reward of up to $11 million remains for alleged Nefilim admin Volodymyr Tymoshchuk (Security Affairs).

Exposure

Ransomnews: 484,142 open cloud buckets

The index counted 18.05 billion files, including 80,495 .env files (67% last modified in 2026) and about 4.7 million database and dump files. Amazon S3 accounts for 71.9% of the buckets; Azure Blob is over-represented among buckets named for backups, exports and payroll (Security Affairs).

Ransomware

GMO Internet (Japan): data leaked after ¥8 billion demand

GMO warned Onamae.com cloud customers of a suspected ransomware threat after attackers leaked data and demanded about ¥8 billion, and later confirmed alleged company and customer information appeared online (Cybersecurity News).
