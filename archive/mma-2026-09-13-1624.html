Top Story

The bonuses are settled, McMillen is 12–0 — and a record this site printed belongs to a different fight

Noche UFC, Saturday 12 September at Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona, produced three bonus awards, all confirmed: Performance of the Night to Jean Silva and to Sean King III, and Fight of the Night to Tommy McMillen vs. Marwan Rahiki. Yahoo Sports and LowKick put the figure at $100,000 each; the UFC’s own bonus page names the winners and prints no dollar amounts, so the number is attributed to the outlets, not the promotion.

The correction. The previous edition of this page stated that McMillen “landed 252 significant strikes on 517 attempts against Marwan Rahiki, breaking Nate Diaz’s three-round UFC record.” That is wrong on the opponent. Reporting read this run states McMillen entered the Rahiki fight 11–0 with 10 finishes, having already set the three-round UFC record of 252 significant strikes in a previous bout. Against Rahiki the line is different and smaller: 89 significant strikes to Rahiki’s 79, a 128–80 edge in total strikes, five takedowns from five attempts, 4:53 of control time, and a knockdown in the third before he pressed on the ground and threatened submissions to close out the decision.

What the Rahiki fight actually was: a scare. McMillen was hurt early — Yahoo’s headline calls it weathering an early storm — then came back to take it, moving to 12–0. The Great Falls, Montana native afterwards declared himself the “People’s Champ” and asked for tough fights.
