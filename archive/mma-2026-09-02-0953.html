Top Story

UFC.com puts its own name to all fourteen Paris bouts, three days out

The promotion has published its fight-by-fight preview of Saturday’s card at the Accor Arena, the Octagon’s fifth straight September visit to Paris. It is a 14-fight card, and having the running order from UFC.com rather than from coverage of it matters: several bouts this page carried secondhand earlier in the week are now confirmed by the promotion, and the ones that are not confirmed are visible as absences rather than hidden inside a list.

The main event is Dan Hooker vs. Salahdine Parnasse at lightweight. Hooker, a top-15 fixture for close to a decade, enters his fourth UFC main event after consecutive losses to Arman Tsarukyan and Benoît Saint Denis. Parnasse is 28, from Aubervilliers, and carries a 23-2 record with fifteen finishes into his promotional debut; UFC.com describes him as “long considered one of the best fighters competing outside of the UFC” and notes his last defeat came when he tried to win a belt in a third weight class under the KSW banner.

One caveat this page is keeping in the open. The preview was published August 31 and last modified early on September 1, and it lists the prelim bout as Nathaniel Wood vs. TBA. Later that same day UFC.com separately announced that Mairon Santos withdrew ill and Pavel Andrusca replaced him. The later announcement supersedes the earlier preview — a primary source is not automatically the current source, and the timestamps are what decide it. That finding was recorded in the 8:48 AM edition and is carried, not new to this one; what this run adds is that the replacement bout is at featherweight, and that the card, the venue, the fourteen-bout count and the daytime start times all came back unchanged on a fresh read.

SEPT 5 · ACCOR ARENA, PARIS · 14 FIGHTS

UFC Fight Night: Hooker vs. Parnasse

Dan Hooker (#10) vs. Salahdine Parnasse, five rounds at lightweight. Parnasse is a promotional newcomer — a former two-time KSW featherweight champion and one-time KSW lightweight champion, 23-2 with fifteen finishes, handed a main event on debut. UFC.com’s own card lists the fight at Hooker +400 / Parnasse −550. Across other books this desk has fetched, Hooker has been available from +360 to +450 and Parnasse from roughly −500 to −700, DraftKings at −600 / +440, off an opening line near −400 / +300. The range is printed and no average is taken.

A refusal this page made earlier this morning is now resolved, and the way it was resolved is the point. The 8:19 AM edition declined to print UFC.com odds because the promotion’s card renders a bout as “+275 odds -350” between two names without saying which price belongs to which man, and layout order alone is not a key. This run the same page was read across all fourteen bouts at once, and five of them can be checked against independent quotes fetched separately: Ziam–Sola, Page–Ruziboev, Keita–Naimov, Charriere–Lima and Donchenko–Soriano all resolve the same way — the first price belongs to the first-named fighter, and in every case the favourite/underdog assignment matches the outside book. Five independent confirmations establish the convention, so the main-event line can now be assigned. The ambiguity was in the single row, not in the page; reading the whole table dissolved it.

Main card — UFC.com, with its prices (3 PM ET, Paramount+): Hooker +400 / Parnasse −550 (LW) · Farès Ziam −145 / Axel Sola +125 (LW) · Michael “Venom” Page (#15) −175 / Nursulton Ruziboev +145 (MW) · Daniil Donchenko −245 / Punahele Soriano +200 (WW) · Kurtis Campbell −390 / Trevor Peek +310 (FW) · Losene Keita −360 / Muhammad Naimov +280 (FW).

A correction to this page. The 9:18 AM edition placed Charriere vs. Lima on the main card and Campbell vs. Peek on the prelims. UFC.com’s card, re-read primary this run, has them the other way round: Campbell–Peek is the sixth main-card bout and Charriere–Lima opens the prelims. Corrected above.

The line that stands out is not the main event. Pavel Andrusca is a −130 favourite over Nathaniel Wood — a promotional newcomer, brought in when Mairon Santos withdrew with an unspecified health problem, priced above an opponent with far more UFC experience — and UFC.com’s card shows no divisional rank beside either man in that bout, so neither is described here as ranked. Nothing fetched this run explains why the market sees it that way, and no explanation is invented; the price is simply reported. Start times remain unusual for a UFC card: prelims 12 PM ET / 9 AM PT, main card 3 PM ET / 12 PM PT. In the co-main, Ziam entered 2026 on a six-fight winning streak that Tom Nolan halted in June; he is 2-0 in Paris and knocked out Matt Frevola on his last appearance there. Sola most recently submitted Ismael Bonfim in the first round in July.

Replacement booking

SEPT 12 · DESERT DIAMOND ARENA, GLENDALE, ARIZONA

Noche UFC: Silva vs. Delgado

A replacement booking this page has carried since the switch: former interim featherweight champion Yair Rodríguez was originally scheduled opposite Jean Silva and withdrew with an injury, and Jose Miguel Delgado stepped in — the first main event of Delgado’s UFC career. The card below is UFC.com’s own running order rather than a secondhand listing — both men are listed at #6. It was first read primary in the 8:19 AM edition and is re-confirmed here, not new to this one.

Main card per UFC.com: Silva vs. Delgado; Brandon Moreno vs. Joseph Morales (flyweight); Tommy McMillen vs. Marwan Rahiki (featherweight); Manon Fiorot (#2) vs. Alexa Grasso (#3), women’s flyweight; Waldo Cortes Acosta (#5) vs. Curtis Blaydes (#6), heavyweight; David Martínez (#8) vs. Dan Ige, bantamweight. Main card 5 PM ET, Paramount+. The prelim start time is disputed across this desk’s reads — UFC.com gave 2 PM ET, a schedule listing fetched this run gives 1 PM ET; both printed, neither adopted. A secondhand listing put Kelvin Gastelum opposite Yousri Belgaroui; UFC.com’s own card says Djorden Santos, and the promotion’s page governs — a refusal first made in the 8:19 AM edition and re-confirmed here.

One wrinkle worth flagging for anyone searching this card. Event listings fetched this run still brand the show “Noche UFC: Rodriguez vs. Silva,” carrying the name of a fighter who has withdrawn. An event title is marketing that was printed before the card changed; it is not a source for who is fighting. The bout this page publishes is the one on UFC.com’s own card: Silva vs. Delgado.

ProspectDebut

Michael Aljarouj & Fabia Sintes

Two newcomers meet each other on the Paris prelims. “The Soldier” Aljarouj, fighting at home in France, carries a 13-3 record with one no contest and a seven-fight unbeaten streak. Sintes has won seven straight and most recently won and then twice defended the WOW flyweight title. At most one of the two streaks survives Saturday, which is the arithmetic a matchmaker accepts when booking two prospects against each other.
