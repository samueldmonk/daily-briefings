Top Story

Aspinall vacates the heavyweight title, and the belt is Gane’s to inherit

Tom Aspinall has vacated the UFC heavyweight championship, announced 14 September 2026, after continuing eye problems left him unable to obtain medical clearance. The sequence, as reported: double eye surgery in February 2026, then an infection in the right eye, then an accidental scrape of that same eye, which required another surgery.

That leaves Ciryl Gane, who won the interim heavyweight title in June at UFC Freedom 250 on the White House South Lawn by beating Alex Pereira, as the obvious beneficiary. Reporting this week holds that the UFC can simply elevate Gane to undisputed champion rather than stage a vacant-title bout, and that this is the most likely immediate move — making him the clear favourite to finish 2026 holding the belt. Until the promotion says so, this page lists the undisputed title as vacant and Gane as interim champion.

Sat 19 Sep · Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles

UFC 331: Joshua Van (c) vs. Alexandre Pantoja 2

Flyweight title rematch of their UFC 323 meeting in December 2025, which ended when Van stopped Pantoja 26 seconds into round one on a Pantoja arm injury. Odds: Van −130 / Pantoja +110 (DraftKings); BetOnline has it the other way, Van −105 / Pantoja −115. Note the move — Pantoja opened as the small favourite, and the fight-week line has crossed over to Van. Co-main: Arman Tsarukyan vs. Maurício Ruffy at 155 lb, with Tsarukyan a sizable −380 favourite. Also on the card, Gable Steveson vs. Sean Sharaf at heavyweight carries the card’s heaviest line at −1500 / +600, and Robelis Despaigne vs. Tai Tuivasa. Thirteen bouts are currently scheduled. Main card 9:00 PM ET, prelims 7:00 PM ET, early prelims around 5:00 PM ET.

Sat 3 Oct · Delta Center, Salt Lake City

UFC 332: Natália Silva vs. Wang Cong, vacant women’s flyweight title

Promoted to the main event after Valentina Shevchenko was stripped of the belt, sidelined for up to a year by a ligament injury to her back. Silva is 20-5-1 overall and 8-0 in the UFC, her last three wins unanimous decisions over former champions Jessica Andrade, Alexa Grasso and Rose Namajunas. Wang is on a four-fight winning streak including two wins this year, over Eduarda Moura and Tracy Cortez, and holds a kickboxing win over Shevchenko. Live on CBS and Paramount+. One schedule aggregator has dated this card 26 September; the promotion’s own event page says 3 October, and that is what is printed here.

Around the Sport

  • Two belts are unoccupied at once. Heavyweight went vacant on 14 September when Aspinall gave it up; women’s flyweight went vacant when Shevchenko was stripped after a back ligament injury expected to sideline her for up to a year.
  • Light heavyweight is frozen until 2027. Carlos Ulberg won the title on 11 April 2026 with a first-round knockout of Jiří Procházka, then had surgery six days later on a torn ACL. He is targeting an early-2027 first defence.
  • The UFC 331 main-event line has crossed over. Pantoja opened as a small favourite; by fight week DraftKings had Van at −130 and Pantoja at +110, while BetOnline still had Pantoja narrowly ahead at −115. Both readings are printed rather than reconciled.
  • Paramount+ is now the through-line. DWCS streams there, UFC 332 airs on CBS and Paramount+, and UFC 333 has a Paramount+ lineup.
  • Arman Tsarukyan’s co-main is a gatekeeping fight — described in the fight-week coverage as continuing his march back toward a second lightweight title shot.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. Natália Silva enters her title shot 8-0 in the UFC with three consecutive unanimous-decision wins over former champions Andrade, Grasso and Namajunas. Wang Cong arrives on a four-fight streak with 2026 wins over Eduarda Moura and Tracy Cortez. Movsar Evloev remains undefeated heading into UFC 333.

Business & broadcast. UFC 332 is a CBS and Paramount+ broadcast; DWCS Season 10 streams on Paramount+ at 7:00 PM ET on Tuesdays; the series has produced 24 contracts through its first half, plus four more in Week 6.
