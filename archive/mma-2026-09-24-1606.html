Top Story

Gaethje: Tsarukyan is “next in line” — but defend-or-retire call waits until after the holidays

Justin Gaethje says Arman Tsarukyan, who knocked out Mauricio Ruffy with elbows in the first round at UFC 331, is the man he will fight “if and when I defend this championship” — but the 37-year-old champion told the Flagrant podcast he hasn’t decided whether to defend or retire. “I have a deep bruise in my knee. Still can’t punch with my hand,” he said, three months after his fourth-round stoppage of Ilia Topuria at Freedom 250. “I’m just going to enjoy being the champion for the rest of the year. I’m going to make a business decision and a life decision whenever the time comes.” Asked whether he was posturing for a bigger payday, Gaethje replied: “This has never been about money for me. It’s about health.” On the same show he said Topuria is undersized for 155 pounds, will never regain the lightweight belt and should go back down to featherweight.

Sources: MMA Junkie via Yahoo Sports · Bloody Elbow

SEPT 26 · META APEX, LAS VEGAS

Rosas Jr. vs. Barcelos

UFC Vegas 121. Raul Rosas Jr. headlines for the first time against Raoni Barcelos; both arrive on five-fight win streaks. The Ultimate Fighter finals are on the main card: Valesca Machado vs. Melissa Amaya (women’s strawweight) and Mehemmedeli Osmanli vs. Ilimbek Akylbek Uulu (bantamweight). Odds: Rosas Jr. −188 / Barcelos +146 (FanDuel); −190 / +150 (BetRivers).

OCT 3 · DELTA CENTER, SALT LAKE CITY

UFC 332: Silva vs. Wang Cong

Natália Silva and Wang Cong meet for the vacant women’s flyweight title after Valentina Shevchenko withdrew with a back and shoulder ligament injury. Main card also features Deiveson Figueiredo vs. Payton Talbott, King Green vs. Esteban Ribovics, Roman Kopylov vs. Ateba Gautier and Khaos Williams vs. Roberto Soldić — the first numbered-event main card to air on CBS.

OCT 17 · ROGERS PLACE, EDMONTON

Buckley vs. Malott

Welterweights Joaquin Buckley and Mike Malott headline the UFC’s return to Edmonton.

OCT 24 · ETIHAD ARENA, ABU DHABI

UFC 333: Volkanovski vs. Evloev + Yan vs. Dvalishvili 3

Featherweight champion Alexander Volkanovski defends against Movsar Evloev, and bantamweight champion Petr Yan meets Merab Dvalishvili in a trilogy bout.

NOV 14 · MADISON SQUARE GARDEN, NEW YORK

UFC 334: Gane vs. Hokit + Harrison vs. Nunes

Ciryl Gane makes his first defense of the undisputed heavyweight title against Josh Hokit; Kayla Harrison defends the women’s bantamweight title against Amanda Nunes.
