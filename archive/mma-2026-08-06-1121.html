Fight Week

The oddsmakers have picked a side: Salkilld, 5-0 in the UFC, favored over No. 8 Gamrot on Saturday

Fight week is underway for UFC Fight Night: Gamrot vs. Salkilld, Saturday at the Meta Apex in Las Vegas — and the books are backing the surge. Quillan Salkilld is a −142 favorite, with eighth-ranked Mateusz Gamrot at +120 for the lightweight main event (SportsLine/Docs Sports). It's a striking price against a fighter of Gamrot's pedigree, and a measure of how fast the 12th-ranked Australian has moved.

Salkilld is 12-1 overall and 5-0 in the UFC since debuting last year, most recently flattening longtime contender Beneil Dariush with a first-round knockout in Perth on May 2. UFC.com's fight-by-fight preview notes the Contender Series graduate has four first-round finishes and four Performance of the Night bonuses in that five-fight run, while Gamrot — 26-4 with one no contest (Docs Sports) — brings elite grappling and key wins over Rafael dos Anjos and Arman Tsarukyan; the preview frames the winner as a candidate for a title eliminator next. Prelims start at 5 PM ET Saturday, the main card at 8 PM ET on Paramount+ (Yahoo Sports/Wikipedia).

A win puts Salkilld's name in the title conversation of a division that just changed hands: Justin Gaethje took the belt from Ilia Topuria at UFC Freedom 250 in June. CBS Sports calls Saturday the chance for Salkilld "to continue his meteoric rise" in his first UFC main event.

Prospect Watch

Prospect

Quillan Salkilld — lightweight

12-1 overall, 5-0 in the UFC, and headlining Saturday as a favorite over a top-ten stalwart. The Australian's May 2 first-round knockout of Beneil Dariush turned a hot start into a genuine contender's résumé; oddsmakers now price him as the division's next problem.

Prospect

Navajo Stirling — light heavyweight

Took the Błachowicz fight as a short-notice replacement — less than two weeks out — and battered the former titleholder for a first-round TKO at 2:56 of the Belgrade co-main, the biggest win of his career. His perfect record now stands at 11-0 (Yahoo/MMA Weekly).

Prospect

Ty Miller — returns Saturday

The undefeated prospect is back at UFC Vegas 120 opposite Billy Ray Goff, having made a strong first impression with a first-round knockout at UFC 324 in January (AgentMMA). A second statement win on a card headlined by the division's hottest riser would put two lightweight names on the same fast track.

Debut

Jessie Rosas — short-notice debut

The UFC announced the debut of Jessie Rosas, older brother of bantamweight prospect Raul Rosas Jr., who steps in on short notice to fight at featherweight on Saturday's card (AgentMMA). The Rosas family project expands — and short-notice debuts have made careers in this building.
