The Lead

As of 12:00 PM ET: Nasdaq +1.98%, S&P 500 +1.32%, Dow +0.10% as tech keeps leading

24/7 Wall St.’s 12:00 PM ET midday snapshot had the S&P 500 up 1.32%, the Dow Jones Industrial Average up 0.10% and the Nasdaq Composite up 1.98%, with the S&P 500 “trading at 7,620 at midday” after adding to gains through the first half of trading — the Nasdaq “more than doubling” the S&P’s advance while the Dow is “essentially flat” (24/7 Wall St.). At 9:30 AM ET the same desk had the S&P 500 +0.78%, Dow −0.14% and Nasdaq +1.31% (24/7 Wall St.).

Earlier, Yahoo Finance’s market strip, with U.S. markets closing in 5 hours 6 minutes (≈10:54 AM ET), showed the S&P 500 at 7,603.10, up 82.17 points or 1.09%; the Nasdaq Composite at 25,510.46, up 444.59 or 1.77%; and the Dow at 52,141.10, down 90.79 or 0.17% — all three reconcile exactly with Tuesday’s official closes. Yahoo’s Nasdaq quote stamped 10:55:05 AM ET read 25,512.72, +1.78%, and its Dow quote at 10:55:10 AM ET 52,137.76, −0.18%. The Russell 2000 was up 0.56% at 2,837.08 and the VIX down 8.28% to 17.85 (Yahoo Finance). That extends the gains from Yahoo’s ≈10:24 AM read (S&P 500 +0.80%, Nasdaq +1.16%, Dow −0.27%). At the 9:30 AM ET bell, Reuters had the Dow up 0.05% at 52,259.02, the S&P 500 up 0.97% at 7,593.95 and the Nasdaq Composite up 1.54% at 25,451.05 (Reuters via Investing.com); TheStreet’s 9:33 AM read had the S&P 500 +1.05%, Dow +0.16%, Nasdaq +1.64% and the Russell 2000 −0.68% (TheStreet). Before the bell Nasdaq futures had been up 1.9%, S&P 500 futures 0.9% and Dow futures 0.2% (Investopedia via Yahoo Finance).

Why it’s up: chip stocks surged after CoreWeave’s quarterly results eased worries about AI demand and financing. Nvidia rose for the first time since CEO Jensen Huang admitted to an affair with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy — it was up 4.00% at $175.54 as of 10:55 AM ET — though Yahoo notes the stock is still down about 14% in November and has lost about $700 billion in market value; the bounce came despite a Baird downgrade to Neutral (Yahoo Finance). TheStreet says Nvidia’s two-day sell-off had erased more than $430 billion in market value (TheStreet). Investopedia said the market has been especially volatile in the week since Democrats’ decisive midterm victory, which sparked investor concerns that AI development could stall under new leadership in Washington and key statehouses (Investopedia).

Rates backdrop: the stock market is open for Veterans Day but the bond market is closed. On Tuesday the 10-year Treasury yield briefly spiked above 5% again as traders recalibrated bets on higher rates after the hot October CPI; Fed Governor Michelle Bowman said, “There is still work to do to bring inflation closer to 2%” (Yahoo Finance). It is a quiet day for earnings and economic data, with an MBA mortgage-rate update due (Yahoo Finance).

Movers & Drivers

Oil risk New

Iran’s Qalibaf: Hormuz “will not return to pre-war state”

An 11:42 AM ET headline relayed by @DeItaone: Iran’s Qalibaf, in an interview with Al Jazeera, said the Strait of Hormuz will not return to its pre-war state and that it is part of Iran’s seven conditions — which 24/7 Wall St. says caps any relief rally in crude and keeps a risk premium under energy names and tanker rates (24/7 Wall St.).

Up

10:47 AM movers: CoreWeave +20.74%, Bloom +14.02%, Lumentum +12.30%

TheStreet’s 10:47 AM ET market-movers update had CoreWeave up 20.74% after strong Q3 revenue growth and a raised outlook; Bloom Energy up 14.02% as AI-infrastructure names rebounded on CoreWeave’s results; and Lumentum up 12.30%, recovering from losses that followed Marvell’s all-stock deal (4.5 Marvell shares per Lumentum share) (TheStreet).

Down

Down

SpaceX −1.41% ahead of Starship Flight 15

Airbnb −2.15% on OpenAI travel deal

Morgan Stanley pulls Nvidia from Top Picks; Baird cuts target to $200

Baird’s downgrade to Neutral came with a price-target cut from $500 to $200. Morgan Stanley’s Joseph Moore removed Nvidia as a Top Pick under the firm’s stop-loss policy after a 25%+ drop since Oct. 2, while keeping an Overweight rating and $300 target. Nvidia has hired Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to investigate whether company secrets were compromised (TheStreet).

Up

AMD jumps 13% after analyst resets target

Iran war costs and threats weigh in the background

Insurer Allianz Trade said the war with Iran has caused more than $200 billion in physical damage across Persian Gulf nations and estimated the total regional economic cost could reach $790 billion through 2030; Iran’s Mohsen Rezaei vowed Tehran would not allow removal of “even a single particle” of enriched nuclear material, casting doubt on diplomacy. Separately, CNBC reported Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent was moved to a military base over Iranian threats against his life (TheStreet, 7:51 and 9:52 AM ET — TheStreet).

Up

CoreWeave jumps after beat-and-raise

Shares popped 13% after the Nvidia-backed cloud provider’s better-than-expected revenue outlook, per Yahoo’s main live-blog text, and a later Yahoo update said the stock “jumped 19%”; TheStreet’s 10:47 AM ET update had it up 20.74% (TheStreet) (Yahoo Finance).
