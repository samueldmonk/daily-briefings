Fight week

UFC 331 is five days out — and the flyweight champion is the one who opened as the underdog

UFC 331: Van vs. Pantoja 2 lands Saturday 19 September at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, and the headline oddity is the headline fight. Joshua Van defends the flyweight title in a rematch with Alexandre Pantoja — the man he took the belt from at UFC 323, in a fight that ended in injury — and on the opening lines it is Pantoja who is the favourite at −115, with the reigning champion Van at −105. A rematch where the champion won the first fight and still opens as the underdog is the market saying, in the only language it has, that it thinks the first result came with an asterisk.

The rest of the card is built on lines that are anything but close. Arman Tsarukyan meets Mauricio Ruffy at lightweight in the co-main as a sizable favourite at −380. Olympic gold medallist Gable Steveson takes his second UFC fight against Sean Sharaf carrying the heaviest line on the card at −1500. And Robelis Despaigne faces Tai Tuivasa in the other heavyweight bout.

The odds dispute, resolved but not erased. This site has carried two contradictory readings of the main event: a UFC listing at Van −115 / Pantoja −105, and a DraftKings opener at Van +100 / Pantoja −120. This run's reading — Van −105 / Pantoja −115, with the champion explicitly described as the underdog — agrees with DraftKings on direction and sits between the two on price. The direction is now supported by two independent reads and one contradicting it; this page publishes Pantoja as the slight favourite and prints the disagreement rather than hiding it. These are opening odds, not a live line — verify a current price before it means anything to you. Not printed: a start time for the card, which was not stated in anything read this run. The countdown above targets 6:00 p.m. ET on 19 September as a placeholder and does not assert a bell time.

Around the Sport

  • The heavyweight belt is on the table, and Dana White said so out loud. Asked on Saturday night after the Ryan Garcia vs. Conor Benn fight whether Tom Aspinall might be asked to vacate, White said the UFC will “definitely be talking about that,” said he does not personally know where Aspinall stands medically, and that it should be cleared up soon. He named Shevchenko's situation as the precedent. Aspinall (15-3 MMA, 8-1 UFC) reaches one year without a fight on 25 October; his last appearance was a no contest in round one on illegal eye pokes from Ciryl Gane, followed by surgery on both eyes and no return timeline. A discussion is not a decision — no belt has changed, and the Champions Board below seats Aspinall accordingly. Carried from Sunday's edition; not re-verified this run.
  • Noche UFC's gate was the biggest domestic Fight Night the UFC has run. $4,436,470 in ticket sales with attendance above 16,928, and the all-time top-grossing event at Desert Diamond Arena, passing Drake's 5 September 2023 show — the first single-day event to take that building record. Carried from Sunday's edition; not re-verified this run.
  • A milestone in passing: as of UFC Fight Night: Silva vs. Delgado, the promotion has held 789 events in 170 cities across 33 countries.
  • Interim light heavyweight remains a rumour, not a booking. Carlos Ulberg won the vacant belt at UFC 327 on a torn ACL, had surgery on his right knee, is expected to miss the remainder of 2026 and does not expect to return until 2027. The UFC has not indicated an interim title will be created. Magomed Ankalaev — No. 1 in the official LHW rankings, last seen stopping Bogdan Guskov in round five at UFC Abu Dhabi on 25 July 2026 — and Jiří Procházka head the queue. Kamaru Usman has floated Chimaev vs. Ankalaev for an interim belt; that is Usman's suggestion, not a UFC booking. Carried on file; not re-verified this run.
