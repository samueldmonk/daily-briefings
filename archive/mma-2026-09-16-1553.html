Top Story

Fight week in Los Angeles: Joshua Van defends the flyweight title against Alexandre Pantoja on Saturday

UFC 331 lands at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles on Saturday 19 September, with prelims at 6:00 p.m. ET and the main card at 9:00 p.m. ET on Paramount+. The champion is the favourite in the rematch: Van −130, Pantoja +110 at DraftKings, with Van described as the favourite by a healthy margin across the books read this run — a reversal, since Pantoja opened as a small favourite before the line moved.

The first meeting was over almost before it started. Van took the belt at UFC 323 in December 2025 by technical knockout 26 seconds into round one, after Pantoja sustained an arm injury — fight-week previews this run describe it as an elbow. Either way the rematch carries most of the questions the first fight never got to ask, and the line-move case rests on Van’s subsequent showing against Tatsuro Taira against Pantoja’s layoff.

The week’s schedule is set: the pre-fight press conference is Thursday 17 September at 8:00 p.m. ET at the arena and on the UFC’s YouTube channel, and ceremonial weigh-ins follow Friday 18 September at 8:00 p.m. ET. In the co-main, Arman Tsarukyan meets Maurício Ruffy at lightweight over five non-title rounds.

Around the Sport

  • The heavyweight title is vacant, and a challenger is putting his hand up. Josh Hokit says he is ready to face Ciryl Gane for the heavyweight title now that Tom Aspinall has relinquished the belt.
  • Aspinall vacated on 14 September over ongoing eye problems that trace to the eye pokes in his no-contest with Gane at UFC 321; he is not retiring and intends to return once cleared. Gane holds the interim belt after his second-round knockout of Alex Pereira at Freedom 250. The UFC has made no announcement promoting him, so the board below keeps the undisputed title vacant.
  • Conor McGregor is making progress in his recovery from the knee injury that forced an anticlimactic end to his Octagon return. No return date was stated in what was read this run.
  • Scott Coker and media executive Peter Levin are unveiling a new global mixed-martial-arts promotion, Ki MMA, at a launch event in Lower Manhattan.
  • UFC CEO Dana White has confirmed the promotion will not be re-signing Michael “Venom” Page: “I don’t think we’re signing him. I think it’s been pretty clear that he wasn’t being re-signed.”
  • UFC 332 on 3 October will be the first numbered event whose main card airs on CBS.

Rankings & Business

  • Rankings movement: no ranked-list changes were stated in any source read this run, so none are published — but Saturday’s Noche UFC win moved Alexa Grasso back into flyweight title contention: her bout with Manon Fiorot was an officially designated 125-pound title eliminator, and the belt is vacant until Natália Silva and Wang Cong meet on 3 October. The live question at heavyweight is whether Gane is elevated from interim to undisputed — reporting expects it, but there has been no announcement.
  • Business & broadcast: UFC 331 streams on Paramount+ (prelims 6 p.m. ET, main card 9 p.m. ET); UFC 332 takes a numbered main card to CBS for the first time; and Ki MMA is being launched as a new global promotion by Scott Coker and Peter Levin.
