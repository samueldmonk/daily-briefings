Top Story

Makhachev passes Anderson Silva — 17 straight, and a first welterweight defence

Islam Makhachev defended the UFC welterweight championship against Ian Machado Garry on Saturday at Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia, taking a unanimous decision by scores of 49-46, 49-46 and 48-47. The win was his 17th consecutive victory inside the Octagon, a new record for the most consecutive UFC wins, passing the 16-fight mark set by Hall of Famer Anderson Silva. It also made him only the fifth two-division champion to record successful title defences in both divisions, and moved his overall professional record to 29-1.

The champion controlled the opening round with his grappling and caught Machado Garry with a right hand and a left high kick out of the clinch in the second that put the Irishman down momentarily. Machado Garry out-landed him in the third, and landed the better strikes across the later rounds, but Makhachev returned to his wrestling in the fourth and took the challenger's back in the fifth, holding the position to the horn. Machado Garry falls to 17-2. Makhachev remains atop the men's pound-for-pound rankings.

Around the Sport

  • The Oliveira rematch is off — and Tsarukyan got Ruffy instead. A second meeting between Arman Tsarukyan and Charles Oliveira had been reported as finalised for the UFC 331 main event on September 19, with talk of Oliveira's BMF title being on the line. The promotion pivoted away from it. Tsarukyan now meets Mauricio Ruffy in the co-main event on that card; reporting through the negotiation described Tsarukyan seeking guarantees from the promotion before agreeing to face Oliveira, and separately said he would not be permitted to challenge for the BMF belt. Tsarukyan beat Oliveira when the two met at UFC 300; the method was not stated in the sources checked for this edition, so it is not printed here.
  • Dana White turned one winner away. Undefeated lightweight Abe Alsaghir improved to 9-0 in front of the UFC president on Contender Series Week 1, taking a split decision over Fabrizio Escarrega across three rounds — but White disagreed with the result and made clear he would not be adding Alsaghir to the roster. Four of the five winners on the night were signed.
  • Contender Series returns tomorrow — and the card is set. Season 10, Week 2 runs Tuesday, August 18 at Meta Apex in Las Vegas with five fights, streaming on Paramount+. The headliner is a welterweight bout between Namo Fazil and Kaik Brito, a former two-time Oktagon welterweight champion who previously appeared on Contender Series Season 7. Also booked: Douglas Rodrigues vs. Trent Miller, Logan Paxton vs. Cristian Perez, Mahamed Aly vs. Alik Lorenz, and Taner Trembley vs. Roman Puga. Week 1 on August 11 produced four contracts — Anthony Wint, Bilal Hasan, Tom Pagliarulo and Joseph Kropschot.
  • Edson Barboza retired in the cage. After Esteban Ribovics stopped him in the second round at UFC 330, Barboza took off his gloves and laid them in the centre of the Octagon, closing a 16-year UFC career.
  • Jeremiah Wells is at a crossroads. The lone Philadelphian on the card was down 20-18 on the cards heading into the third before rocking Myktybek Orolbai and immediately wrapping up his neck to put him to sleep — one of the year's bigger upsets. He then said he is likely to call it a career unless he gets a top-15 opponent next time out.
  • A rare finish. Charles Johnson's Scottish twister on Eduardo Chapolin was the second of its kind inside the Octagon this year.
  • Neil Magny, still going. The 39-year-old collected his 25th UFC win, and has now won three of his last four.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. Makhachev holds the top spot in the men's pound-for-pound rankings after breaking the consecutive-wins record at UFC 330. Mackenzie Dern's standing rose on the back of her first successful strawweight defence. Umar Nurmagomedov carries a two-fight win streak into the Shanghai headliner following his bantamweight title challenge, with wins over Deiveson Figueiredo, Cory Sandhagen and Mario Bautista on his record; Song Yadong's résumé includes wins over Henry Cejudo, Marlon Moraes and Chito Vera.

Business & broadcast. UFC 330 drew 19,238 spectators and generated $6,715,776 in gate receipts, making it the highest-grossing event in Xfinity Mobile Arena history. "We shattered the arena record by two million dollars," UFC CEO Dana White said at the post-fight press conference, adding: "We're in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania with a Russian Muslim and an Irish kid, and we do a $7 million gate and we shatter the record here. The biggest event that's ever been held here, we beat it by $2 million." It was the promotion's first Philadelphia card in seven years — the last was UFC on ESPN: Barboza vs. Gaethje in March 2019, when the building was known as the Wells Fargo Center. The event streamed on Paramount+, as do Dana White's Contender Series Season 10 and the coming Sacramento and Shanghai Fight Nights.

No television or streaming viewership figure for UFC 330, and no TKO Group financial disclosure tied to the event, appeared in sources checked for this edition — so none is published here.
