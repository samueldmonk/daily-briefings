Fight Week

The oddsmakers have picked a side: Salkilld, 5-0 in the UFC, favored over No. 8 Gamrot on Saturday

Fight week is underway for UFC Fight Night: Gamrot vs. Salkilld, Saturday at the Meta Apex in Las Vegas — and the books are backing the surge. Quillan Salkilld is a −142 favorite, with eighth-ranked Mateusz Gamrot at +120 for the lightweight main event (SportsLine/Docs Sports). It's a striking price against a fighter of Gamrot's pedigree, and a measure of how fast the 12th-ranked Australian has moved.

Salkilld is 12-1 overall and 5-0 in the UFC since debuting last year, most recently flattening longtime contender Beneil Dariush with a first-round knockout in Perth on May 2 — his fifth straight UFC win in barely fifteen months. Prelims start at 5 PM ET Saturday, the main card at 8 PM ET on Paramount+ (Yahoo Sports).

A win puts Salkilld's name in the title conversation of a division that just changed hands: Justin Gaethje took the belt from Ilia Topuria at UFC Freedom 250 in June.

Prospect Watch

Prospect

Quillan Salkilld — lightweight

12-1 overall, 5-0 in the UFC, and headlining Saturday as a favorite over a top-ten stalwart. The Australian's May 2 first-round knockout of Beneil Dariush turned a hot start into a genuine contender's résumé; oddsmakers now price him as the division's next problem.

Prospect

Navajo Stirling — light heavyweight

Took the Błachowicz fight as a short-notice replacement — less than two weeks out — and battered the former titleholder with slicing elbows for a first-round TKO at 2:56 of the Belgrade co-main, the biggest win of his career. His perfect record now stands at 11-0 (Yahoo/MMA Weekly).

Prospect

Uroš Medić — welterweight

Belgrade's hometown headliner needed 30 seconds to finish Daniel Rodriguez in the UFC's first event in Serbia — after eating a low kick that swept him to a knee, he got up and dropped Rodriguez with a left. It was his fourth straight first-round knockout win (Bloody Elbow/MMA Mania).
