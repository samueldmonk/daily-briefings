Top Story

Makhachev passes Anderson Silva — 17 straight, and a first welterweight defence

Islam Makhachev defended the UFC welterweight championship against Ian Machado Garry on Saturday, August 15, at Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia, taking a unanimous decision by scores of 49-46, 49-46 and 48-47. The win was his 17th consecutive victory inside the Octagon — a new record for the most consecutive UFC wins, passing the 16-fight mark set by Hall of Famer Anderson Silva. It also made him only the fifth two-division champion to record successful title defences in both divisions, and moved his overall professional record to 29-1.

The champion controlled the opening round with his grappling and caught Machado Garry with a right hand and a left high kick out of the clinch in the second that put the Irishman down momentarily. Machado Garry out-landed him in the third and landed the better strikes across the later rounds, but Makhachev returned to his wrestling in the fourth and took the challenger's back in the fifth, holding the position to the horn. Machado Garry falls to 17-2. Makhachev remains atop the men's pound-for-pound rankings.

Underneath him, Mackenzie Dern made the first defence of the women's strawweight title, beating Gillian Robertson by unanimous decision on identical scores — 49-46, 49-46 and 48-47. Both champions retained, and the card produced five knockouts and two submissions.

Prospect Watch

ProspectNew

Quillan Salkilld — into the lightweight top 10

The Australian has established himself as a legitimate rising contender in the UFC's stacked lightweight division after disposing of veteran Mateusz Gamrot by first-round submission in the main event of UFC Fight Night in Las Vegas on August 8 — his first UFC main event, and a rear-naked choke. The win jumped Salkilld into the division's top 10, taking Gamrot's place at No. 10. He is 6-0 in the UFC, and the performance earned his third $100,000 bonus of 2026.

ProspectNew

Alexia Thainara — enters at No. 8 at strawweight

The strawweight contender continued her ascension with a unanimous decision over former title challenger Amanda Lemos, extending her unbeaten streak inside the Octagon to four. Thainara enters the strawweight rankings at No. 8, leapfrogging Lemos, who falls three spots to No. 9.

Roster move

Contender Series — four more signings

Dana White signed four of the five winners from Week 1 of Season 10 of Dana White's Contender Series. Reporting on the group notes it includes training partners of Daniel Cormier and Ilia Topuria.

Rankings & Business

Rankings movement. Two fighters broke through in the latest divisional rankings update: Quillan Salkilld enters the lightweight top 10 at No. 10, displacing the man he beat, Mateusz Gamrot; and Alexia Thainara enters the women's strawweight rankings at No. 8, leapfrogging Amanda Lemos, who drops three places to No. 9. Islam Makhachev remains the men's pound-for-pound No. 1 after his record-setting 17th straight Octagon win.

Business & broadcast. UFC 330 drew 19,238 spectators and $6,715,776 in gate receipts — the highest-grossing event in Xfinity Mobile Arena history. Dana White said at the post-fight press conference that the card beat the arena's previous gate record by roughly two million dollars.
