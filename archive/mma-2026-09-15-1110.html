Top Story

Withdrawal · fight week

Ortega out of UFC 331 again, and Moicano gets Halloween instead

Brian Ortega has been pulled from his UFC 331 bout with Renato Moicano with an injury, UFC officials announced on Monday — days before the card. Ortega released a statement on the withdrawal; Bloody Elbow carries it under the line “This one hurts.” The injury has not been formally disclosed, though video circulated of Ortega at supper with friends appearing to carry a significant injury over one eye.

It is the second time this booking has fallen through. Ortega and Moicano were previously matched at UFC 326 earlier this year, and Ortega withdrew then too, with lingering injuries cited.

Moicano has not been left without a payday. He now headlines a UFC Fight Night at the Meta Apex on 31 October against Tom Nolan — a main-event slot on Halloween in place of a main-card spot in Los Angeles. UFC 331 loses one bout from its main card four days out; the rest of the card, including the flyweight title rematch on top, is unaffected.

Around the Sport

  • A fifth bonus, named after a TV show. New Dana White has announced that one of the 26 fighters then scheduled at UFC 331 will receive an extra $50,000, on top of the usual four $100,000 Fight Night awards. It is called the “proper chaos bonus”, in honour of the Paramount+ series MobLand, whose second season premieres the Friday before the card. White did not give criteria, saying the winner is at his discretion.
  • The heavyweight division is still short a champion. Tom Aspinall vacated the undisputed title on 14 September because of ongoing eye complications; he is not retired and intends to return once cleared. Ciryl Gane holds the interim belt.
  • Two belts are vacant at once. Aspinall’s at heavyweight, and women’s flyweight since Valentina Shevchenko vacated while sidelined roughly a year. The latter is settled at UFC 332 on 3 October.
  • UFC 331 is on Paramount+, part of the promotion’s move onto the Paramount platforms this year; UFC 332’s main card is the first numbered-event main card to run on CBS.

Rankings & Business

  • Rankings movement. The flyweight picture is unusual: the champion and the man he took the belt from meet again four days from now, so the division’s top two rows resolve on Saturday rather than drift. At welterweight, Islam Makhachev’s UFC 330 decision over Ian Machado Garry was his 17th straight UFC win, a record for consecutive Octagon victories.
  • Bonus structure at UFC 331. Four standard awards at $100,000 — one Fight of the Night, two Performance of the Night — plus the discretionary $50,000 addition. The 26-fighter figure is the one given when the bonus was announced on 14 September, before the Ortega withdrawal.
  • Broadcast. Paramount+ carries UFC 331; CBS takes the UFC 332 main card on 3 October, with prelims at 4 PM ET on Paramount+ and the main card at 8 PM ET.
  • On fighter pay, White has said bonuses will rise under the Paramount television deal, without giving figures. No dollar amount for that increase has been stated in anything read this run, so none is published.
