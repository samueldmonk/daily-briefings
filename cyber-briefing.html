Top Story

North Korea-linked hackers hit Binance for a record $6.3 billion

Binance, the world’s largest crypto exchange, was hit late Thursday by what investigators say is the biggest crypto hack in history, with an estimated $6.3 billion in digital assets affected; about $3 billion has been “frozen on-chain so far.” Binance temporarily suspended withdrawals, says it “will cover all losses” from its Secure Asset Fund and company reserves, and CEO Yi He said withdrawals should resume by Sunday (Yahoo Finance).

TRM Labs attributed the theft “with high confidence” to TraderTraitor, the North Korean group behind last year’s Bybit hack; Elliptic also linked it to North Korea and estimated about 44% of stolen assets are frozen — nearly all stablecoins — with attackers still controlling over $3 billion that issuers cannot freeze. Tether froze more than 1.567 billion USDT and Circle another $800 million. The firms say the attackers appear to have compromised Binance’s wallet-signing infrastructure; Binance’s preliminary investigation points to a compromised engineer’s laptop (Yahoo Finance). TheStreet, citing CNBC, quotes Binance saying it “may involve a compromised DevOps engineer’s laptop” that let an attacker reach a service container and submit unauthorized withdrawals (TheStreet).

Update Friday: Binance co-CEO Richard Teng said the company is “committed to covering all hacked losses through our company funds,” that the hack was not carried out by one person, and that while the initial investigation pointed to a compromised laptop, “further digging revealed additional points of breach.” “This was a complex attack,” he said; “the attacker also used a self-destruct mechanism that destroyed tooling and tracers” (Yahoo Finance live blog).

Patch Priority

GitLab CE/EE — CVE-2026-97229 (CVSS 10.0), unauthenticated remote code execution, actively exploited. CISA added it to the KEV catalog on Oct. 29 with a remediation deadline of Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026 (2 days left) (GBHackers, citing the catalog entry). A crafted regular expression in a CI/CD configuration can let an unauthenticated attacker run code on the server. Upgrade self-managed instances to 19.4.2, 19.3.5 or 19.2.8 (released Oct. 12); GitLab.com is already patched and GitLab Dedicated customers need no action. New THN, reporting the KEV addition, says the Alpine attacker crashed GitLab worker processes eight times on Oct. 20–21 and later re-entry attempts on Oct. 25–26 were blocked; Alpine found no evidence of success but rebuilt the instance from scratch (The Hacker News). Alpine Linux says an attacker tried the flaw on its own GitLab server a week after the fix; because GitLab’s fix patches the Ruby it bundles, servers running their own Ruby also need Ruby 3.3.13, 3.4.12, 4.0.9 or later — “upgrading GitLab is not enough” (The Hacker News) (IT-Connect, citing GitLab’s bulletin).

Then: Linux kernel — CVE-2026-46300 (“Fragnesia”), local root, due Saturday, Oct. 31 (1 day left) (Security Affairs). Overdue: PHP “PHP2Shell” CVE-2026-109618/-109619 was due Oct. 29 — upgrade to PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35 (HighCircl).

Threat Actor Spotlight

North Korea

TraderTraitor

TRM Labs attributes the $6.3 billion Binance theft “with high confidence” to TraderTraitor, the same North Korean group it blames for the February 2025 Bybit hack ($1.5 billion, the previous record). Chainalysis data cited by Yahoo Finance put North Korean crypto theft at $2 billion in 2025 and $6.75 billion since 2016 through the end of last year (Yahoo Finance).

Breaches & Incidents

Exploited

Drupal Commerce Core access bypass exploited

Drupal warns that CVE-2026-118539, a “highly critical” missing-authorization flaw in Commerce Core (versions up to 2.41 and 3.0.0–3.3.13), is being “actively exploited in the wild”; it “may financially impact the vendor.” Upgrade to Commerce Core 3.3.13 or 8.x-2.41 (The Hacker News).

Exploited

Cisco ASA/FTD VPN flaw crashes firewalls

Cisco patched CVE-2026-76418 (CVSS 8.6), exploited in the wild: an unauthenticated remote attacker can force a reload (DoS) of Secure Firewall ASA and FTD devices via the Remote Access SSL VPN when secondary authentication is configured (The Hacker News). New CISA added it to the KEV catalog on Oct. 29 alongside the GitLab flaw, with agencies told to apply fixes by Nov. 1; Cisco has not identified the attackers or the number of targeted organizations (The Hacker News).

Exploited

PHP2Shell now hitting Palo Alto management interfaces

Palo Alto Networks says threat actors are exploiting PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618/-109619) against internet-exposed management interfaces (The Hacker News).

Zero-day

Ivanti Connect Secure zero-day chain (UTA0576)

Volexity traced exploitation by an unknown actor it calls UTA0576 to Oct. 3 against a fully patched appliance. Ivanti’s Oct. 29 advisory update names CVE-2026-104935 (auth bypass/RCE, CVSS 8.6), CVE-2026-111357 (privilege escalation, 8.8) and CVE-2026-118527 (4.3); the attacker stole credentials and queried LAPS passwords and BitLocker keys. Fixed in 25.1.1.3 and 22.8R2.6 (GBHackers).

Ransomware

C3RB3R ransomware wave hits Synology NAS

Owners report files renamed .L0CK3D with a read-me3.txt note. Qihoo 360 attributes the attacks to C3RB3R, a Cerber variant, exploiting DSM flaws CVE-2026-13639 and CVE-2026-13684 (CVSS 9.8, patched Sept. 18); Synology also warned that attackers are exploiting CVE-2024-10441 on outdated DSM. Update to DSM 7.4-90075, 7.3.2-86009-4, 7.2.2-72806-9 or 7.2.1-69057-12 (IT-Connect).

Data breach

ENGIE: French customers’ data exposed via partner sales tool

Names, contact details, customer and contract numbers and energy supply identifiers were accessed through a partner sales tool; ENGIE says internal systems and banking data were not affected (Security Affairs).

Law enforcement

Hive/Nefilim affiliate sentenced to 70 months

Ukrainian national Artem Stryzhak was sentenced in Brooklyn; a reward of up to $11 million remains for alleged Nefilim admin Volodymyr Tymoshchuk (Security Affairs).
