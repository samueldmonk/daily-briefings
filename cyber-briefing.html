Top Story

PHP2Shell: emergency PHP patches for two pre-auth RCE flaws, with attacks already logged

The PHP team released 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35 to fix CVE-2026-109618 (RCE via out-of-memory corruption) and CVE-2026-109619 (RCE via stale global state). Every PHP installation serving web requests is affected — PHP-FPM, Apache mod_php (greatest danger), php-cgi, FrankenPHP and LiteSpeed — and a working exploit shows a single unauthenticated request can trigger the attack before any application code runs (Help Net Security).

Details leaked on Oct. 20, a day ahead of the embargo; Cloudflare logged the first attacks at 07:43 UTC on Oct. 21, hours before the ~14:00 UTC patch window, and has pushed WAF rules to all customers — which its CTO calls “a STOPGAP only.” Reuters reported cybersecurity-agency websites in the Netherlands and Finland went dark as a precaution (Investing.com via Yahoo).

PHP 8.1 and earlier get no official fix; the team published minimal source backports for 7.4, 8.0 and 8.1. The UK NCSC urges taking offline any system that can’t be fixed right away. “It’s one of the biggest vulns in years,” said Kevin Beaumont (Help Net Security). PHP says there is no complete mitigation short of updating (php-src releases).

How exposed is the long tail? Security firm Mallory counted about 884,000 internet-facing hosts whose HTTP banner reported PHP 8.1 or older to Shodan on Oct. 20 — 59% of the roughly 1.5 million hosts that reported any version — a count of banners, not confirmed affected systems. Earlier on Oct. 21, watchTowr’s FAQ listed exploitation as none reported and Huntress said it had seen none in partner environments as of Oct. 20 (The Hacker News).

Attempts confirmed, no successes yet. watchTowr now says the first in-the-wild exploitation attempts hit its sensors around the time the patches landed but none has succeeded — “Whilst nobody has been successful to date, we don’t expect that will last long,” said head of threat intelligence Jake Knott. Hadrian reported php2shell “exploited in the wild at scale,” mostly via PHP’s session upload-progress path, and Patchstack, Cloudflare and Vega also saw attempts. The flaws affect nearly all PHP releases back to 5.4 and can also be used to read process memory holding credentials and API keys. Wiz, OpenAI and Calif found them in August; the out-of-memory-to-RCE issue was reported on Aug. 11 (SecurityWeek).

Patch Priority

Patch now: PHP — CVE-2026-109618 / CVE-2026-109619 (CVSS v4.0 9.2). Upgrade to 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35; check your distro/LTS vendor for packages; consider taking unpatchable systems offline (Help Net Security). Not yet in CISA’s KEV, so no federal deadline applies.

Federal deadline: Plex Media Server CVE-2026-96656 and CVE-2026-96654 (the Plex2Shell chain) plus SiteGuard Server Edition CVE-2026-106931 — due Friday, Oct. 23, 2026 (1 day left). Fixed in Plex Media Server 1.43.3.10861 (THN).

Threat Actor Spotlight

Ransomware

Qilin — riding a Palo Alto VPN bypass into networks

Unit 42 updated its threat brief on Oct. 19 naming Qilin and Inc among operators exploiting CVE-2026-0257, a PAN-OS GlobalProtect authentication bypass patched in May; ReliaQuest also tracks Qilin and Settra. In one case attackers went from the VPN gateway to vCenter, disabled ESXi security controls and pulled credentials offline from a domain controller’s virtual disk. ReliaQuest: “Patching alone may not remove access established before remediation” — end active GlobalProtect sessions after patching (Help Net Security).

Breaches & Incidents

New Espionage

China-based BRONZE SANDCASTLE sideloads backdoors via Sandboxie

Sophos CTU and Sophos Labs say the China-based group abused legitimate Windows applications — Sandboxie repeatedly, plus Java, Krita, NVDA, LINE and Sysinternals utilities — to sideload malicious DLLs that decrypt a modified Meterpreter-based backdoor from config.ini and run it in memory, hitting organisations in the Philippines, Taiwan, Afghanistan and Namibia (South Korea likely) between August 2025 and August 2026. Operators created Volume Shadow Copies and copied ntds.dit, the Active Directory database; payload version 1.1.7 also pulled encrypted configuration files from GitHub repositories. The research does not report a compromise of Sandboxie’s website or official downloads (Hackread, Oct. 21).

Extortion

Feds suspect ransomware negotiators helped ShinyHunters

U.S. investigators suspect executives at two ransomware negotiation firms kept unusually close ties to ShinyHunters, including one unnamed executive at a large U.S. consulting firm who allegedly helped run the group’s extortion site, shinywire.com — active from about May 3 to Oct. 16, 2026, with pages for nearly 100 organisations — per KrebsOnSecurity’s sources. No charges have been announced against that executive. Separately, Canadian negotiator Edward Dubrovsky was arrested in Pennsylvania on Oct. 8 on federal extortion and conspiracy charges; his complaint is sealed and he has not been convicted. The FBI says ShinyHunters has obtained more than $70 million from victims in 2026 (Hackread, Oct. 19).

Ransomware

The Gentlemen ransomware kills Defender, wipes shadow copies

Acronis’ Threat Research Unit says the Go-based ransomware-as-a-service operation, which emerged in 2025 and targets Windows, Linux, NAS and BSD systems, disables Defender real-time protection, stops backup-related services, deletes Volume Shadow Copies and clears logs before encrypting with ChaCha20; a separate EDR killer abuses vulnerable drivers (one linked to CVE-2025-70795). The group claims more than 800 victims — its own claim, not independently confirmed (Cyber Security News, Oct. 21).

Supply chain

LiteLLM 1.107.0 on PyPI shipped a credential stealer and RAT

The release was compromised on Oct. 21 across all seven wheel files and also reached Docker images (tags including v1.107.0, latest, main-latest and main-stable, per Wiz). Startup files dropped into site-packages run the stealer whenever Python starts, targeting SSH keys, AWS/Google Cloud/Azure credentials, browser passwords and AI-tool tokens. PyPI removed the version; Aikido says anyone who installed it should assume credentials were exposed. Wiz lists 1.106.2 as the latest safe version — rotate secrets from a clean system and pin images to verified digests (Cyber Security News).

Ransomware

CISA, FBI: SafePay has hit at least 600 organizations

An Oct. 20 joint advisory (AA26-293A) with the FBI, Secret Service and German and British partners says SafePay actors have compromised at least 600 organizations across critical-infrastructure sectors since August 2024. Named entry points include CVE-2020-3259, CVE-2023-20269, CVE-2023-27532, CVE-2024-40711 and CVE-2024-40766; operators stop Volume Shadow Copy and reset backup NAS devices to factory settings before encrypting with ChaCha20 and RSA. CISA urges offline, immutable backups and MFA (GBHackers).

Policy

DHS orders DeepSeek out of federal civilian systems

Federal civilian agencies must remove DeepSeek products — and third-party products or services “developed using DeepSeek implementations” — and may no longer buy from the Chinese AI developer or its affiliates, under an order from Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin dated July 24, 2026, issued under the Federal Acquisition Supply Chain Security Act of 2018 and published in the Federal Register on Oct. 20. The notice does not define “DeepSeek implementations” (The Hacker News, Oct. 21).

Espionage

Vasilek backdoor hid in VMware Tools during two-year healthcare intrusion

Russian security firm Solar attributes a nearly two-year intrusion at a Russian healthcare organization to Partisan Zmiy (also known as the Cyber Partisans), a Belarus-aligned hacktivist group active since at least 2020 whose Vasilek backdoor uses Telegram for command-and-control and exfiltration; the group has also staged destructive attacks with a wiper called Pryanik (The Hacker News, Oct. 21).

Ransomware

Exposed INC / The Gentlemen affiliate servers show AI-assisted intrusions

SOCRadar found seven exposed servers tied to INC and The Gentlemen affiliates holding 8,257 AI messages across 133 OpenCode sessions, stolen credentials and 46 INC encryptor builds prepared for six victims; operator records name 62 organizations (36 in the U.S.) and claim exploitation of cPanel CVE-2026-41940, SimpleHelp CVE-2024-57727 and FortiClient EMS CVE-2026-35616 — figures from the attackers’ own records, not independently verified. Session logs show humans approving each AI step (Hackread).

Supply chain

ConnectWise pulls Cloud Backup after Microsoft 365 abuse

A compromised ConnectWise Cloud Backup credential was used to reach customers’ Microsoft 365 tenants; Microsoft says attackers likely abused permissions such as Directory.ReadWrite.All. Huntress saw unauthorized sign-ins in 162 tenants after attacks began around Oct. 15. Cloud Backup, SaaS Security and Cloud Management are suspended with no restoration timeline; ConnectWise says there is “no indication” backup data was compromised (The Record, Oct. 19).

Supply chain

OVHcloud CLI releases swapped for an info-stealer

A hacker used a developer’s GitHub account to replace binaries on five releases (v0.11.0–v0.15.0) on Oct. 19; CEO Octave Klaba urges affected users to change every secret on the machine. OpenSourceMalware ties it to GlassWorm (Help Net Security).

Supply chain

AOMEI Backupper update servers hijacked (UNC7017)

Google/Mandiant attribute minutes-long tampering windows (May 28, July 16, July 20) to UNC7017, delivering the CROSSROAD downloader and SHINYROAD backdoor; infected machines stay compromised after AOMEI’s server fix (BleepingComputer) (CSA).

Ransomware

GlobalProtect intrusions across sectors since June

Healthcare, education, government and energy victims; attackers chained Veeam CVE-2023-27532 in some cases (Help Net Security).

Exploitation

Plex2Shell used to drop miners and proxy malware

Researcher boredhackerblog found the chained Plex flaws weaponized to deploy cryptocurrency miners and proxy malware, prompting CISA’s KEV listing (THN).
