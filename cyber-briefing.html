Top Story

NewSupply chain

Optimizely flaw used to plant malware on sites of Zoom, BBC, CNN, HSBC and 170+ others

An attacker exploited a vulnerability in Optimizely's web experimentation platform to inject malicious JavaScript into customer projects; Optimizely says it fixed the flaw and removed the code. Researcher Scott Helme (Report URI) found the code served to visitors of 178 organisations on Nov 16 — a kit that swapped crypto wallet addresses, skimmed payment cards, stole cookies and screenshots, and delivered the Coruna and DarkSword iOS exploit kits. Attacker wallets received about $7,150 before being emptied; only 24 affected organizations (15%) had a Content Security Policy strict enough to block it. Hudson Rock traced poisoned scripts to Kissmetrics, StorifyMe and minute-ly too (Help Net Security).

Patch Priority

Synology DSM — CVE-2024-10441 (CVSS 9.8): unauthenticated remote code execution in the system plugin daemon. Added to CISA KEV Nov 18 with BOD 26-04 forensic triage; federal deadline Nov 21, 2026. Update to DSM 7.2.2-72806-1 / 7.2.1-69057-6 / 7.2-64570-4 / 7.1.1-42962-7 / 6.2.4-25556-8, BSM 1.1-65374 (SecurityOnline, citing CISA).

Threat Actor Spotlight

China-nexus

CL-STA-1238 — autonomous AI intrusions with "Cairn"

Unit 42 says Chinese-speaking operators ran 25 operations against at least 19 organizations in Aug–Sep 2026 using a customized fork of the open-source Cairn AI pentest framework (13 on Zhipu GLM 5.2, 8 on DeepSeek V4 Flash, 4 on DeepSeek V4 Pro), gaining access or data at 10. Targets: semiconductor supply chain, defense policy, and civil society; one victim was a U.S. teachers' union where the framework logged access to more than one million member records. Indicators: 154.44.31[.]4 and 82.29.129[.]54 (Help Net Security).
