Top Story

SonicWall SSL-VPN MFA bypass under exploitation; federal agencies have until Nov. 20

CISA added SonicOS flaw CVE-2026-19868 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Nov. 6, giving federal civilian agencies until Nov. 20 to fix it (Security Affairs; SecurityWeek, via search). The bug lets a user holding valid credentials for an alternate authentication method bypass SAML authentication controls on the SSL-VPN service — and may also bypass SAML-enforced MFA. It is not a credentialless bypass: attackers need usable credentials for another login method exposed by the SSL-VPN (GBHackers).

SonicWall rates it CVSS 7.1; NVD also lists a 7.5 from CISA (SecurityWeek, via search). Advisory SNWLID-2026-0014, published Nov. 6, covers seven SonicOS flaws, topped by CVE-2026-107155 and CVE-2026-107154 (both 8.8). Affected: Gen7/NSv on SonicOS 7.3.3-7015 or earlier and Gen8 on 8.2.2-8015 or earlier; fixed in 7.3.4-7013 and 8.2.3-8015 or later. There is no workaround (GBHackers).

Threat Actor Spotlight

Ransomware

Hawkmoth (Anubis RaaS)

Symantec attributes Anubis ransomware to a group it tracks as Hawkmoth, which runs a ransomware-as-a-service operation that emerged in December 2024 as a rebrand of Sphinx; it has claimed about 96 victims in the past 12 months, 52% of them in the U.S. In an October 2026 intrusion at a U.S. organization, attackers planted a previously undocumented custom backdoor, Lindnor, on two Microsoft SQL Servers, dropped at least 13 security-killing executables and five vulnerable signed drivers (BYOVD) within roughly an hour, and configured AnyDesk to survive Safe Mode boots — but the encryptor was blocked and there is no evidence any files were encrypted (GBHackers, citing Symantec).

Breaches & Incidents

Exploited

QNAP: NAS devices breached via Plex2Shell

QNAP says some users’ Plex Media Server installs on its NAS devices appear to have been compromised through Plex2Shell, a chain of four Plex flaws (CVE-2026-96652, -96654, -96655, -96656); QTS and QuTS hero themselves are not affected. Users running a vulnerable, internet-exposed Plex server should treat the NAS and its content as compromised: update to Plex 1.43.3.10861, check for signs of compromise, clean-reinstall the OS, reset Plex and NAS credentials, and stop forwarding port 32400 if remote access isn’t needed. CISA added all four flaws to KEV on Oct. 20 with a three-day deadline (BleepingComputer, Nov. 6).

Crypto / supply chain

Web3Auth flaw used to drain Keplr wallets

An attacker exploited a security issue in Web3Auth’s infrastructure to steal crypto from some Keplr wallets created with Google or Apple sign-in; Keplr called it a “private key breach.” Web3Auth used the same route to move at-risk funds into recovery wallets — Bitquery counted at least $6.01 million from more than 44,000 wallets — and GoPlus estimates at least about $1 million was stolen. Recovery-phrase and hardware wallets are not affected; Keplr has paused Google/Apple sign-in, and Web3Auth paused its embedded wallet service (The Hacker News, Nov. 9).

ICS / OT

CISA, FBI warn on refrigeration and cold-chain attacks

CISA and the FBI, with HHS, USDA and DOT, published a joint fact sheet on Nov. 5 warning of ongoing attacks on industrial refrigeration and cold-chain monitoring systems. Attackers could force units into continuous heating or defrost cycles or block operators from seeing temperatures; the agencies urge taking controller panels off the public internet, replacing default passwords and setting safe operating limits with alerts (GBHackers).

Data breach

Iris Ohyama: data on ~7.7M customers may be exposed

A breach of a third-party cloud service may have exposed data on about 7.7 million customers of the Iris Plaza platform; a spokesperson said the company had received no direct communication from the attacker (BleepingComputer coverage, via search — BleepingComputer).

Financial

Crédit Mutuel Arkéa: up to $133M in fraudulent transactions

Attackers used a cyberattack on the French bank to carry out fraudulent transactions of up to $133 million, and the bank suspended some systems (BleepingComputer listing, via search — BleepingComputer).

Ransomware

Akira claims Ligne Roset; unverified

Akira claimed in a Nov. 6 statement to have taken about 150GB of internal files from the French furniture brand, including employee passports and financial documents. No data has been published, the claim is unverified and the company has not confirmed an incident (Interior Daily).

Botnet

Google disrupts 1.3M-device x.cpe botnet

Google says it is highly confident TV-box maker Videostrong deliberately built in the backdoor and sold criminal access, and banned it from certified Android. The backdoor stays in firmware and owners cannot remove it themselves (THN).

Espionage

APT29-linked chain puts old Apple flaw in KEV

CISA added Apple CVE-2024-27834 (CVSS 5.5) to KEV on Nov. 4 after Sekoia tied a watering-hole campaign to APT29 with “moderate to high confidence”; agencies are to patch by Nov. 18 (THN).
