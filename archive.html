About the archive

  • Snapshots are written automatically at the end of every run and are never hand-curated.
  • Filenames follow section-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM.html, timestamped in Eastern time.
  • Corrections issued after a snapshot published are not backported into it — that is the point of a snapshot.

Archived editions are historical records. Market levels, CVE deadlines, fight cards and champions listed in an older snapshot may since have changed. Always read the current briefing for live information.
