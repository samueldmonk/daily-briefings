UFC 334 fight week: Gane −195 vs. Hokit +160; Harrison −180 vs. Nunes +150

Ciryl Gane (14-2 MMA, 11-2 UFC) defends the heavyweight title against Josh Hokit (10-0 MMA, 4-0 UFC) on Saturday at Madison Square Garden; the main card streams at 9 p.m. ET on Paramount+, prelims at 7 p.m. and early prelims at 5 p.m. MMA Junkie lists Gane −195, Hokit +160. Gane was elevated to full champion when Tom Aspinall vacated; Hokit’s key wins are Derrick Lewis and Curtis Blaydes, and he won four bonuses in his first three UFC fights (MMA Junkie via Yahoo Sports).

Co-main: champion Kayla Harrison (19-1, 3-0 UFC) vs. Amanda Nunes (23-5, 16-2 UFC), Harrison −180 / Nunes +150. Harrison has been out 17 months — she pulled out of a January date with Nunes to have surgery on herniated discs in her neck; Nunes returns more than three years after retiring following her UFC 289 title win over Irene Aldana (MMA Junkie).

Around the Sport

  • Walker’s rise: a question-mark kick opened the finish; Walker has now won by stoppage in consecutive fights since moving up to heavyweight, and the UFC said the win punched his ticket to the division’s top 10 (BJJ TV). He is 24-10 (1 NC) as a pro (RotoBaller, via search).
  • Suarez in line at 115: her third straight win; the UFC suggested a fight with champion Mackenzie Dern could be next unless Zhang Weili returns to the division (BJJ TV).
  • Vegas 124 prelims: Jose Souza KO’d Jonny Parsons (uppercut, R1 4:16); Austin Bashi submitted short-notice replacement Carlos Calderon (rear-naked choke, R1 1:07); Andre Petroski stopped Mantas Kondratavicius (R2 4:39) to end a three-fight skid; Thomas Petersen rallied to stop Gabriel Lorenco (R3 4:47) (BJJ TV).
  • UFC 334 prelims include Stephen Thompson vs. Charles Radtke and Jim Miller vs. Terrance McKinney (MMA Junkie).
