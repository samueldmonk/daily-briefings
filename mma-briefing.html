Top Story

UFC 333: two title fights, and the main event is a coin flip

Featherweight champion Alexander Volkanovski, coming off a title defense against Diego Lopes, puts his belt on the line against Movsar Evloev, who is 20-0 after beating Lerone Murphy in March. Kalshi prices it 50% / 50%. In the co-main, Petr Yan defends the bantamweight title he reclaimed from Merab Dvalishvili at UFC 323 last December, at 59% / 41% (Covers, updated Oct. 19).

Opening sportsbook lines had Volkanovski a slight −115 favourite with Evloev −105 (DraftKings) (Heavy) (DraftKings Network). Also on the card: Alexander Volkov vs. Rizvan Kuniev (53% / 47%), Azamat Murzakanov vs. Dominick Reyes (64% / 36%) and Aaron Pico vs. Losene Keita (50% / 50%) (Covers).

Around the Sport

  • The National’s picks (Steve Luckings, Oct. 22): Volkanovski to retain on the judges’ scorecards and Dvalishvili to reclaim the bantamweight title; Temirov by KO over Lone’er Kavanagh, Volkov by decision over Kuniev, and Keita by KO over Aaron Pico. The paper notes Losene Keita was drafted in on short notice after an injury to Arnold Allen, that there are nine fights on the card, and that neither Yan nor Dvalishvili has fought since their December meeting (The National).
  • Done: the UFC 333 pre-fight press conference went ahead Thursday morning at Etihad Arena (scheduled 9:00 a.m. ET); after taking questions from the media in front of fans, Volkanovski, Evloev, Yan and Dvalishvili all faced off (MMA Junkie via Yahoo Sports, Oct. 22; DraftKings Network).
  • Michael Bisping picks Volkanovski to retain: “I think he fights a little bit smarter … and I think he walks away as still the featherweight champion of the world.” On Evloev: “he’s got a lot less miles on the clock and of course, he’s never been beaten” (MMA Sucka, Oct. 21).
  • At Wednesday’s UFC 333 media day, Volkanovski called Evloev’s wrestling “one of the best in the UFC, definitely the best in our division. But again, it’s just not enough for me,” adding: “I believe if we went five rounds of no strikes and just wrestling, grappling, I’ll beat him there.” MMA Fighting notes he won 15 of 18 UFC bouts over the past decade and conceded only 10 takedowns in featherweight fights, winning every one of them (MMA Fighting via Yahoo Sports, Oct. 21).
  • Evloev’s camp says he has three signature submissions — the arm-triangle, the kimura and “a third choke we’ve worked on the most. I won’t reveal it.” Every one of his 10 straight UFC wins has come by decision; José Aldo has called him “boring” (SPORTbible).
  • Volkanovski to MMAjunkie: “I’m saying I’ll finish him inside the five rounds for sure” — and if Evloev tries to out-grapple him, “for 25 minutes, I can’t see it” (Heavy via Yahoo Sports, Oct. 19).
  • Volkanovski, 38, says he is targeting a quick finish at UFC 333; Saturday is his 12th straight title fight and sixth featherweight defense across two reigns, and a win would pass José Aldo for most featherweight title-fight wins, with nine. Evloev (20-0): “I have been waiting seven years since I signed with the UFC for this moment” (The National, Oct. 21).
  • Dana White on Farid Basharat’s release: “There’s definitely a reason” (Cageside Press).
  • “UFC Wanted 205 lbs Grand Prix, Says Coach of Dricus du Plessis” (Heavy).
  • Steven Koslow revealed he tore his meniscus ahead of UFC Edmonton (Cageside Press); Su Young You targets Alexandre Topuria next (Cageside Press).
  • Jasudavicius “inches closer to title” after the Blanchfield win (Cageside Press).
