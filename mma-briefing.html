Top Story

“Big Train” arrives: Nolan stops Moicano in Round 3 on Halloween

Australia’s Tom Nolan finished Renato Moicano by TKO (punches and elbows) at 1:28 of Round 3 at Meta APEX — the biggest win of his career, in his first UFC main event (UFC.com). Moicano controlled the early going, taking Nolan’s back and locking up a body triangle, but referee Dan Miragliotta stood the fighters up in Round 2; Nolan then dropped Moicano, nearly finished him before the bell, and closed it out early in the third after sweeping to top position (UFC.com; Yahoo Sports).

Heavy called the stand-up a “questionable call” (Heavy). Nolan moves to 12-1 with six straight wins at lightweight, while Moicano has lost three of his last four, per Yahoo Sports. Nolan also earned a Performance of the Night bonus (UFC.com).
