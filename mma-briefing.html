Top Story

New

Gane edges Hokit in a record slugfest at MSG; Harrison spoils Nunes' return

Ciryl Gane defended his heavyweight title for the first time with a unanimous decision over Josh Hokit at UFC 334 inside Madison Square Garden on Nov 14 — all three judges 48-47. Gane, elevated from interim champion after Tom Aspinall vacated in September, landed 237 significant strikes, a record for a UFC heavyweight fight (Yahoo/ESPN coverage). Hokit was challenging in just his fifth UFC fight. In the co-main, Kayla Harrison submitted Amanda Nunes by rear-naked choke early in Round 4 to keep the women's bantamweight belt (ESPN).
