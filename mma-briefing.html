Top Story

Stirling knocks out Procházka in Doha — another ex-champ down

Navajo Stirling (12-0) knocked out Jiří Procházka (32-7-1) with a right hand at 4:58 of the second round in the main event of UFC Fight Night at ABHA Arena, Doha, on Saturday, Nov. 21 (UFC.com scorecards; ESPN). It was his second straight finish of a former champion after knocking out Jan Blachowicz in Belgrade in August, and he is now 7-0 in the UFC with four knockouts. Procházka had success with the jab and bloodied Stirling’s right eye, but Stirling broke him down with leg kicks before slipping a left hook and landing a short right cross. “He was better. Congrats,” Procházka said — the fifth knockout loss of his 14-year career (ESPN).

ESPN’s Brett Okamoto writes the result “creates an interesting predicament at light heavyweight”: Stirling will likely crack the top 5 while City Kickboxing teammate Carlos Ulberg holds the belt and has yet to return from ACL surgery (ESPN). Stirling earned a $100,000 Performance of the Night (FightBook MMA, citing UFC’s official bonus coverage).

Ortega retires: former featherweight title challenger Brian Ortega (16-6), 35, announced his retirement after a lopsided decision loss to Dan Hooker at lightweight (30-27 x3) (ESPN; FightBook MMA).

Around the Sport

  • No Kiwi title fight: City Kickboxing coach Eugene Bareman says light heavyweight champion Carlos Ulberg and Navajo Stirling will never fight each other — “boys from the same gym don’t fight.” ESPN says the UFC may look to have Ulberg defend against Magomed Ankalaev once he is fit to return, or Stirling could face Ankalaev, perhaps for an interim title, if Ulberg’s return is delayed (ESPN, Nov. 25).
  • Smith charges dismissed: the Sarpy County Attorney’s Office dismissed three felony charges against former UFC light heavyweight Anthony Smith, arrested in July over an alleged domestic violence incident in Nebraska (ESPN, Brett Okamoto, Nov. 25).
  • UFC 336 adds: “Steveson, Boser, Barriault Among Additions to UFC 336” (Cageside Press, Nov. 23, headline).
  • Ortega retires: Brian Ortega, who fought Alexander Volkanovski and Max Holloway in UFC title fights, retired after losing to Dan Hooker in Doha: “I started this because I came from nothing … I healed. So, as of right now, I think that’s it” (ESPN).
  • Albazi bonus: Amir Albazi submitted Alessandro Costa with a rear-naked choke at 4:34 of Round 3 for a $100,000 Performance of the Night (FightBook MMA).
  • Fiorot suspended: UFC fighter Manon Fiorot was banned for three months for violating the anti-doping policy (ESPN, headline).
  • Jandiroba released: the UFC released Virna Jandiroba, a former title challenger and the current No. 3-ranked strawweight, coming off a decision loss to Tatiana Suarez on Nov. 7; she said the decision caught her “off-guard” and that she does not intend to retire (ESPN, Nov. 18).
  • Brawl sentences cut: an Abu Dhabi court reduced sentences for eight people over the Nurmagomedov–Dvalishvili hotel brawl (ESPN, headline).
  • Rematch talk: Nunes wants a rematch after her submission loss (ESPN, headline); Brett Okamoto writes Harrison and Gane could be in line for rematches (ESPN).
  • Wonderboy retires: Stephen Thompson capped his career with a spinning-kick knockout at UFC 334 (ESPN).
  • Crossover: an Overeem–Manuwa boxing match was added to the Dec. 24 Paul–Doumbé card (ESPN).
